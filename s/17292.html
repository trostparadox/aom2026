Enabling AI Proactivity: Leadership Sensemaking, Ambidexterity, and Psychological Safety

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 9
OB

People

Authors: Bilqees Ghani Bano Institute of Business Management; Shoaib Amjad Hussain Forman Christian College (A Chartered University); M Abdur Rahman Malik

Session overview

AI adoption is reshaping work, yet implementation often under delivers due to human and organizational factors. Integrating leadership and sensemaking literature, we argue that employees seek interpretive cues from leaders, whose narratives shape meaning-making during times of change. Accordingly, we propose AI-Leadership Sensemaking as an important enabler for employees to understand, respond to, and experiment with AI-enabled change. We propose that AI-Leadership Sensemaking is positively related to AI-driven Proactive Work Behavior, and that this relationship is mediated by Ambidextrous Work Behavior—employees’ capacity to balance exploitation and exploration in everyday tasks. Finally, we discuss how Psychological Safety Climate strengthens the positive relationship between AI-Leadership Sensemaking and Ambidextrous Work Behavior by enabling employees to engage in AI-related risks. We will test the model using a three-wave, time-lagged survey of employees in AI-enabled roles.
