Session overview

In recent years, the level, speed, and complexity of turbulence appear unprecedented. Economic volatility, climate change, geopolitical conflicts, global pandemics, cyber threats, populist politics, shifting trade alliances, and rapid technological disruption combine to produce a world in which uncertainty is the new normal rather than the exception. How prepared are organizations, their leaders, and organizational members to successfully navigate such a turbulent environment? What can they learn from scholars and their research conducted in the fields of international business, leadership, organizational behavior, organization theory, and change? A group of interdisciplinary scholars from different regions and countries will share and discuss insights from their recent research regarding concepts, strategies, and practices in dealing with and navigating different kinds of turbulent situations and environments.
