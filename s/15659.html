Session overview

This panel symposium explores how emotions shape creativity and innovation in AI-augmented work. As generative AI becomes embedded in creative processes, human–AI collaboration introduces new emotional experiences that influence idea generation, refinement, and implementation. Panelists examine the role of mixed emotions, emotional ambivalence, and emotion regulation across stages of creative work, and discuss how AI can both enable and disrupt these dynamics. The symposium advances a process perspective on the emotional foundations of creativity in the age of AI.
