The Role of Succession Planning in Driving Digital Transformation and Family Business Performance

ENT
Giao Thi Xuan Dam (VinUniversity); VU ANH DZUNG
This conceptual study develops an integrative framework linking succession planning (SP), digital transformation (DT), and family business performance (FBP). Drawing on stewardship theory, the resource-based view, the dynamic capabilities framework, and the socioemotional wealth perspective, the paper argues that succession planning acts as both a governance mechanism and a strategic capability that enables digital renewal. SP is conceptualized through three dimensions - structural, governance, and relational - that collectively prepare successors to sense, seize, and transform digital opportunities. DT is positioned as a dynamic capability that transmits the strategic and relational benefits of succession toward improved performance outcomes. The study contributes to family business research by demonstrating how legacy preservation and technological adaptation can be reconciled through intergenerational leadership renewal. The proposed framework is being operationalized for empirical validation through a survey of approximately 300 Vietnamese family businesses, conducted in collaboration with the Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry.
