Session overview

Impactful health care management research benefits from deep, sustained partnerships with provider organizations to generate questions that are both theoretically novel and operationally relevant. However, while scholarly publications report the final findings of such studies, the relational strategies and reciprocal exchanges required to maintain these collaborations is rarely documented. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) provides a forum for scholars to examine varied engagement models and share diverse strategies for building trust and managing the reciprocity essential to long-term collaboration. Using an interactive “World Café” format, four co-organizers will lead structured discussions on 4 distinct topics: (1) cultivating research opportunities through executive education and clinical engagement; (2) navigating the technical and ethical trade-offs of Electronic Health Record (EHR) data access; (3) integrating practitioners and managers as active colleagues on research teams; and (4) using participatory implementation science to co-create organizational change. Participants will discuss practical insights on delivering tangible value back to partner organizations—such as tailored reports, technical advice, and workforce development. This workshop is designed for HCM Division members at all career stages, offering an opportunity to scaffold productive dialogue and build relationships. Attendees will leave with a broader toolkit for managing the distinctive challenges of conducting management research in healthcare delivery settings.
