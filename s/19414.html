Session overview

This workshop invites participants to reflect upon common challenges experienced during mid-career stages of the academic life cycle. The mid-career stage comes with a set of challenges after having established a career in academia. After a successful launch of this workshop series at AOM 2025, in this second edition of the format, we will particularly focus on the following categories of challenges: (1) Coping with rejections and the related opportunity costs in terms of academic work and private life (holidays, maternity leaves etc.); (2) Managing workload and when/how to say ‘no’ to roles/jobs that typically accumulate during this career stage (reviewing requests, administrative and managerial roles etc.); (3) Balancing family life, especially when living abroad from family, with the demands of academic life that often includes non-standard hours, travel etc. This session is organized as moderated panel discussion with plenty opportunities for the audience to ask questions and meet with panelists in more informal conversation settings at the end of the session.
