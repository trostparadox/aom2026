Session overview

We acknowledge an astounding number of pertinent research projects being conducted worldwide by researchers. We also acknowledge that many of these scholars, particularly those from underrepresented regions, can benefit from greater support to publish their work in prestigious and widely recognized academic journals. Yet, researchers outside of major academic centers often face difficulties in reaching wider audiences and making a larger impact in their field. This PDW aims to address this issue by providing valuable assistance to such researchers. Through practical guidance and support, the workshop aims to equip participants with the necessary tools and strategies to achieve successful publication outcomes, thereby enhancing the visibility and recognition of their work.
