Session overview

Over the past several decades, strategic leadership has evolved into a broad and dynamic area of scholarly inquiry, generating rich insights into executives, top management teams, and the contexts that shape their actions. As the field approaches this point in its development, it offers a timely opportunity to take stock of how foundational ideas have developed, how theoretical and methodological efforts have diversified, and how changes in organizations and society continue to open new avenues for inquiry. This moment invites reflection on what has guided the field’s trajectory so far and consideration of where future work can be most impactful. This symposium brings together leading scholars to 1) revisit the origins and early conceptual foundations of strategic leadership; 2) assess the key developments and turning points that have shaped the field’s evolution; and 3) discuss emerging opportunities for future scholarship, including new data, methods, and research directions that can further enrich our understanding of executive influence.
