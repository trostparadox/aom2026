Development and Validation of a Multidimensional Measure for the Triphasic Model of Poise

Authors: Yunita Sofyan; Matej Cerne School of Economics and Business, University of Ljubljana; Amadeja Lamovšek School of Economics and Business, University of Ljubljana; Irfan Jo Not Associated; Ivan Radevic University of Montenegro

Session overview

We examine how the meta-skill of Poise—the triphasic capacity to integrate past experience, engage fully in the present, and detach from future outcomes— functions as a self-regulatory resource that supports well-being, performance, career and relationship. Integrating Temporal Self-Regulation Theory with Eastern philosophy—Buddhist, Daoist, Confucianism, and Javanese, we position Poise as a cultivable skill that strengthens the psychological connectedness between one’s past, present, and future self, thereby promoting adaptive functioning. We develop and validate the Triphasic Poise Scale to measure this construct using multiple cross-cultural samples. Results indicate strong psychometric properties, including convergent and discriminant validity, and proved that Poise uniquely predicts key outcomes—including emotional well-being, work performance, career and relational satisfaction— beyond established constructs such as mindfulness, grit, and temporal focus. This research contributes to the integration of Eastern and Western perspectives on adaptive functioning and offers a validated measure for studying purposeful engagement across time.
