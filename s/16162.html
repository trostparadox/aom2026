Session overview

This PDW addresses the pressing challenges of accessing Chinese data and publishing management research with China as the research context. As geopolitical tensions and policy changes continue to reshape the landscape for China-focused scholarship, researchers face growing uncertainty around data access, fieldwork, and collaboration with China-based colleagues. This PDW will examine how these evolving conditions impact scholarly work and propose practical solutions to navigate these challenges. Our distinguished speakers bring extensive experience in publishing high-impact research on Chinese organizations and have successfully navigated data access barriers across both qualitative and quantitative methodologies. The PDW will connect participants with scholars who share a common interest in the Chinese context and will consist of three parts: (1) an overview of the issues faced by scholars; (2) two panel discussions with leading experts on overcoming data access challenges, validating Chinese data, and publishing findings in top management journals; and (3) individual roundtable sessions providing interactive, personalized guidance for participants' research projects.
