Price of Popularity? How Technological Positioning Shapes the Performance of Innovative Ventures

Author: Diqiu Wang The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Session overview

This study investigates how the structural dimensions of innovation differentially condition performance of innovative ventures. Drawing on the distinction between core concepts and architectural linkages, this study proposes a bifurcated valuation framework in which these dimensions resolve different forms of uncertainty and exert differential effects on venture performance. The findings provide a structural explanation of how the paradox of legitimacy and competitive pressure manifests across comparative niches and shed light on the social construction of technological trajectories.
