Hosts: Stephanie L. Wang Indiana University Bloomington; Klaus Meyer; Nandini Lahiri American University; Ilya Cuypers Singapore Management University

Session overview

This joint social event, sponsored by the Organizational Behavior (OB) Division Global Committee and the International Management (IM) Division, invites scholars to connect aboard a sunset river tour on the Ben Franklin Yacht. Designed as an inclusive and informal networking space, the event creates opportunities for international scholars, newcomers, and globally minded colleagues to build meaningful professional relationships beyond formal sessions. The gathering encourages cross-regional exchange and community-building within the broader AOM network. Boarding & Departure Details: Boarding begins at 5:30pm. The boat departs promptly at 6:00pm. This event requires separate registration and ticket purchase. Participants must register in advance and pay the event fee (55.00 USD)
