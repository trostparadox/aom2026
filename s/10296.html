Generative AI Helps Close Socioeconomic Status-Based Gap in Professional Networking

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 7
OB

People

Authors: Ruixi (Raye) Zhu Boston University; Evan P. Apfelbaum

Session overview

Research shows individuals who have lower socioeconomic status (SES) face significant disadvantages in career advancement, yet little research has looked at how emerging generative AI (GenAI) technologies may offer new pathways to address SES-based career disparities. This research examines whether GenAI can alleviate SES-based disparities in the effectiveness of email networking – a crucial, early-stage gateway to professional opportunities. In an online experiment and a field experiment (N = 1,201), participants wrote networking emails targeting professionals who currently hold their “dream job” either with or without the assistance of GenAI. We found that the networking emails written by lower (versus higher) SES participants are rated as less effective without the use of GenAI, but that with its use, this gap is significantly reduced. This research advances our understanding of SES-based barriers in early career stages while, importantly, demonstrating how accessible GenAI tools can potentially mitigate entrenched status-based inequalities in professional advancement opportunities, bringing them one step closer to their aspired professional careers.
