Beyond High-Performance Work Systems: Global Compliance Work Systems Under Institutional Corruption

Authors: Richard Posthuma The University of Texas at El Paso; Mansura Nusrat The University of Texas at El Paso

Session overview

Corruption in international business environments poses persistent ethical and compliance challenges for multinational enterprises (MNEs. Because corruption ultimately manifests through human behavior, firm-level prevention requires HR system architectures that shape employee motivation, discretion, and accountability. Addressing this gap, we develop a theory of Global Compliance Work Systems (GCWS), a compliance-constraining alternative to performance-maximizing HR system architectures, designed to operate under conditions of elevated institutional corruption.
