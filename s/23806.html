Emotional Dynamics of Playfulness and Knowledge Sharing: Team Resilience under Performance Pressure

OB
KAI YAO (Fudan University); huang xinyi (Fudan University); yan xinyue
Drawing from the Job Demands-Resources (JD-R) theory and Knowledge Management perspective, this study constructs a moderated mediation model to examine how the dynamics of playfulness influence knowledge sharing within R&D teams of high-tech startups. Results from a two-wave survey of 101 R&D teams in China reveal that dynamics of playfulness positively promotes team knowledge sharing by enhancing team resilience. Furthermore, team performance pressure strengthens both the direct effect of playfulness dynamics on team resilience and its indirect effect on knowledge sharing. Similarly, team knowledge integration capability not only amplifies the positive relationship between team resilience and knowledge sharing but also enhances the indirect effect of dynamics of playfulness. These findings offer novel insights for startups by underscoring the value of cultivating playful dynamics as a socio-emotional resource to facilitate knowledge sharing, while also highlighting the critical contingent roles of performance pressure and integration capabilities in this process.
