Exploring Influencing Factor of Green M&A: From Perspective of Innovation Information Disclosure

TIM
Qin Lu (Sichuan University); Helin Sun (Sichuan University); Liming Zhang (Sichuan University)
Innovation information disclosure is the influencing factor of promoting green M&A. This study explores how innovation information disclosure positively influences green M&A. The result shows that innovation information disclosure has a positive impact on improving green M&A. We use the alternative measurement, shorten and expand the time window, add the control variable, change sample construction, adopt alternative winsorizing proportion, to confirm this positive relationship. The Heckman test two-stage, 2SLS-IVs estimation methods and and variables lagged by one period are adopted to address the endogeneity issue. In the mechanism analysis, we explore the plausible underlying channels through which the innovation information disclosure affects green M&A. This study contributes to the research on green M&A, the effect of innovation information disclosure, and is useful for corporate green transformation and sustainable development.
