Session overview

Like every year, the OMT doctoral consortium aims at providing late-stage doctoral students with research interests in the areas of organization and management theory practical advice on finding jobs, managing their careers, and doing and publishing research. It will also provide an opportunity for students to form relationships with others at the same stage in their career and with more senior scholars who share their research interests. The event consists of a series of panel presentations, round-table conversations, and mentoring sessions.
