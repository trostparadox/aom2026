Expanding Collaborative Impact: The Role of Cross-Sector Networks in Addressing Societal Challenges

Authors: Maximilian Loeffel ETH Zürich; Felix Häusle ETH Zürich

Session overview

Cross-sector collaboration can achieve results beyond the reach of any single sector, as recently exemplified by COVID-19 vaccine development and NASA’s spaceflight partnerships. This project investigates how nonprofits use cross-sector networks to address complex societal challenges, moving beyond traditional partnerships. Further motivated by development policy goals and the social nature of stakeholder relationships, the project focuses on the broader network of actors and spillover effects that enhance nonprofit success. We conduct large-scale, longitudinal mapping and analysis of cross-sector networks using diverse data sources to understand how network embeddedness influences mission and financial outcomes. The research project aims to generate three key outputs: an empirical map of nonprofit participation in cross-sector networks, insights into the mechanisms driving collaboration benefits beyond dyadic ties, and practical recommendations for policymakers and practitioners. By revealing how nonprofits can leverage cross-sector synergies, the project will advance both academic knowledge and real-world impact.
