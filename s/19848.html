Session overview

This symposium aims to generate new insights into global mobility by exploring the well-being of international employees and its role in supporting successful career development.. Bringing together six presentations, the symposium examines diverse groups of internationally mobile employees - including migrants, expatriates, refugees, and digital nomads - and explores how organizational, institutional, and individual factors shape their well-being and career outcomes. Through the presentation and discussion of new research, the symposium seeks to foster scholarly dialogue and advance collective understanding of these critical issues.
