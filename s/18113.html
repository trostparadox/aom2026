Session overview

Building on eight years of the “Nevertheless, She Persisted” PDW and its recognition with the OB Division Award for Best Broad Relevance PDW in 2025, this session highlights the power of women and allies to advance success and collective change in academia. Over the past decade, this PDW has created a sustained space for community, reflection, and action in response to persistent challenges women academics face. Women scholars often navigate professional environments characterized by high expectations, structural inequities, and limited access to peers at the same career stage. These conditions can encourage individuals to internalize negative experiences while obscuring institutional dynamics shaping academic careers. At the same time, women and allies may underestimate their capacity to act as insider change agents who strengthen communities of practice and expand pathways for success. This PDW offers a three-pronged approach to broaden perspectives and build collective efficacy. First, we share narratives of persistence that normalize challenges while making visible patterns of structural constraints and supports. Second, we draw on scholarship and lived experience to examine allyship, mentorship, and sponsorship as mechanisms for navigating and reshaping academic environments. Participants collaboratively develop actionable recommendations for leaders and communities of practice committed to supporting women academics. Third, new this year, we will host a mini-writing retreat embedded in the session to provide peer support for scholarly work. The PDW continues to evolve as an ongoing community of practice that translates shared insight and support into scholarly momentum and sustained collegial ties.
