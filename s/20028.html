Session overview

Emotion often shapes how individuals and organizations think and act, influencing strategic decision making in both start-ups and incumbent firms. Strategic opportunities and challenges such as the threat of new entrants, emerging technologies, and regulatory change can arouse emotions that affect how organizations and their members interpret reality, respond to information, and ultimately perform. Yet for many scholars—especially newcomers—it remains unclear how to design, conduct, and publish high-quality research on emotion in strategy and entrepreneurship. Building on three prior iterations, this PDW convenes a panel of experts who have successfully published on this phenomenon in leading journals, to discuss their experiences and share their advice. In Part 1, open to all conference attendees, three panels will address qualitative, quantitative, and video-based approaches to studying emotion in strategy and entrepreneurship, with panelists sharing practical guidance on research design, execution, reporting, as well as how to navigate the peer review process. In Part 2, preregistered participants will discuss their emerging projects with panel members, other senior scholars in attendance, and peers to receive constructive, developmental feedback. As in previous years, the workshop will serve as a hub for connecting researchers with shared interests in studying emotion in strategy and entrepreneurship, enabling them to develop scholarly networks that extend beyond the immediate conference setting.
