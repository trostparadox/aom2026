Contextualization and Faking: Comparing Traditional and Neutralized Personality Measures

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 13
RM

People

Authors: Jeeho Sohn George Mason University; Mina Son George Mason University; Philseok Lee George Mason University

Session overview

Item contextualization, which provides respondents with a specific frame of reference, has been shown to improve the predictive validity of personality assessments; yet its effectiveness in high-stakes contexts where applicants may intentionally distort responses (i.e., faking) remains unclear. This issue is particularly relevant for neutralized personality measures, which are explicitly designed to reduce socially desirable responding by minimizing evaluative item content. The present study examined whether the effect of contextualization on score inflation differs between neutralized and regular personality items. Using a within-subjects design, 273 currently employed while job-seeking participants completed measures of conscientiousness across two sessions: a non-motivating condition and a motivating condition. Participants completed contextualized and non-contextualized versions of both International Personality Item Pool (IPIP) items and neutralized items from the Less Evaluative Five Factor Inventory (LEFFI). Results indicated that contextualized items showed lower score inflation than non-contextualized items, suggesting that contextualization attenuates faking susceptibility to improve selection accuracy. Importantly, this attenuation effect was comparable for both IPIP and LEFFI items, suggesting that contextualization remains effective even when items are already designed to reduce socially desirable responding. These findings provide evidence that item contextualization offers robust protection against score inflation in high-stakes settings and that its benefits extend beyond traditional personality measures. For practitioners, contextualization reduces faking susceptibility and should not be viewed as redundant with the design aims of neutralized personality items.
