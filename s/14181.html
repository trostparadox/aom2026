Session overview

The AOM Ethics Education Committee is hosting a PDW-style forum that discusses how to identify ethical dilemmas that are unfolding in real time and teaches participants how to bring them to the classroom. Our interactive forum introduces a useful framework from transforming current events into decision-based learning experiences. We will also cover best practices for publishing such pedagogical exercises should educators wish to broaden their reach.
