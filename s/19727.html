How Data Fuel Resilience: Data Orientation, AI Adoption, and Organizational Resilience

Session overview

In an increasingly data-driven economy, manufacturing firms face heightened environmental uncertainties alongside emerging strategic opportunities. Drawing on the knowledge-based view, we introduce the construct of “Big Data Orientation (BDO)”, defining it as a firm’s strategic propensity to prioritize and systematically leverage big data as a core asset for sustainable competitive advantage. We argue that BDO serves as a critical strategic orientation that transforms voluminous data into actionable knowledge, thereby enhancing organizational adaptability and recovery. Extending this argument, this study aims to examine how BDO influences organizational resilience. Specifically, we propose that AI technology adoption and absorptive capacity act as moderating variables that jointly amplify the positive influence of BDO on resilience. This study develops a theoretical framework and proposes the following hypotheses: (1) BDO is positively related to organizational resilience; (2) The BDO-Resilience relationship is positively moderated by AI adoption; and (3) also positively moderated by absorptive capacity. We will test these hypotheses using a panel dataset of Chinese A-share listed manufacturing firms. By developing this framework, we seek to advance strategic orientation theory in the era of big data, and are expected to offer theoretical and managerial implications for achieving resilience transformation through data-driven strategies.
