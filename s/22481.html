Session overview

Since its formation in 2014, the OB Division Global Committee has been dedicated to fostering a globally inclusive community by supporting and welcoming members from diverse regions, particularly underrepresented areas such as Central and South America, Eastern Europe, Africa, and Asia. In the 2026 meeting, we will review initiatives such as global co-writing sessions, global events, and micro-community programming, while also discussing succession planning and volunteer assignments. Join us to share ideas, connect with fellow members, and contribute to the strategic direction of the committee as we continue to enhance inclusivity and engagement across the OB Division.
