Exploring Employee Perceptions of Safety Interventions

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 8

People

Authors: Adelle Bish US Army War College; Frances Jorgensen Royal Roads University

Session overview

We explore employee perceptions of the effectiveness of safety interventions, in workplaces where violence is increasing. Adopting a safety climate approach, findings from 63 semi-structured interviews with front-facing hospital employees reveal that awareness of safety interventions is lacking and that the interventions often do not increase their sense of safety. In addition, the findings suggest that employees seek direct involvement in the design of interventions. We conclude with a summary of the contributions to theory and practice regarding the design and evaluation of safety interventions.
