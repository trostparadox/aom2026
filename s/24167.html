The Rise and Fall of the CSRD in Europe: Implications for CSR Consulting

MC
Javotte Gras-Laplante (iaelyon School of Management); Laura Khachan (Toulouse Business School)
Corporate Social Responsibility has become a central challenge to organizations operating in Europe today and reporting on sustainable actions following the CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) has been shown to be unstable. This paper explores the way the CSRD redefines consulting missions and the work of auditors in company X, a French-based company specializing in CSR-audit services. How does CSR reposition audit and transform consulting services in the context of sustainable development and regulatory uncertainty? This paper explores the shift in consulting services that followed the slowdown of the CSRD in Europe using qualitative methods, namely observations and interviews.
