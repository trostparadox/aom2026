The Role of Management Scholars in Influencing Corporate Action for the SDGs: What Will it Take?

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 2:00PM–3:30PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Regency C
SIM

People

Participants: Rumina Dhalla University of Guelph; Armand Bam Stellenbosch University

Session overview

We seek to examine how organizations use the SDGs framework to develop their corporate strategies to build and enhance their corporate reputation, as well as the extent to which corporate engagement is necessary for the global achievement of the SDGs. At the same time, from a SIM perspective, greater scholarly engagement with corporate sustainability raises an important ethical tension: as scholars increasingly act as advisors, intermediaries, and evaluators, there is a risk that engagement may legitimise symbolic compliance rather than disrupt it. This PDW therefore invites SIM scholars to explore: (1) what barriers limit substantive corporate engagement in sustainable development; (2) how, and under what conditions, responsible management scholars should influence and enhance corporate engagement; (3) how effective UN programs are in mobilizing meaningful business action; and (4) what gaps in scholarship must be addressed to advance credible, accountable SDG engagement.
