Session overview

Scholars studying AI's organizational consequences face a common set of research design challenges: measuring adoption beyond binary indicators, generating and evaluating candidate mechanisms in complex settings, theorizing evaluation when AI alters the informational basis of selection and feedback, and understanding how AI reshapes skill formation. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) addresses these challenges through a training-oriented format organized around three integrative themes: measurement and early-stage theory building, evaluation under AI, and skill formation and adaptation. Workshop leaders will briefly introduce research frameworks developed in their own work, with emphasis on how these approaches can be adapted to new contexts. Participants will then engage in facilitated roundtable discussions addressing five research-design challenges: measuring AI adoption and diffusion; systematizing hypothesis generation; studying AI-mediated evaluation and selection; understanding evaluation and feedback in collaborative work; and examining how digital tools reshape training and skill development. Participants will leave with sharper research questions, clearer mechanisms, and better-aligned empirical strategies.
