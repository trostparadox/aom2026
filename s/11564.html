Scientists, Visionaries, and Opportunists: The Role of Vision in Entrepreneurship

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 4
ENT

People

Authors: Joel Adams Washington and Lee University; Jeffery S. McMullen Imperial College London

Session overview

The pragmatist, theory-based view of entrepreneurs as scientists who develop theories and hypotheses to craft their ventures in alignment with the strongest market opportunities has emerged and evolved with the widely adopted lean startup method of entrepreneurial development through experimentation. These experimentalist views of entrepreneurship do not align with how many entrepreneurs approach their ventures. Across thirty interviews, we find that entrepreneurs generally fall into three camps: experimentalists/scientists, visionaries, and opportunists. We elaborate on this typology of entrepreneurial journeys and explore six dimensions that define and differentiate these types. In doing so, we reveal implications for entrepreneurship theory, practice, and education.
