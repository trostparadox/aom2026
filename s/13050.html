Session overview

The rapid advancement and widespread adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies are fundamentally transforming decision-making and creative work in organizations. This paradigm shift highlights the urgent need to explore the design of AI embedded in organizational processes using design science research (DSR), shifting the focus from understanding AI phenomena to prescribing innovative solutions for real-world problems using cutting-edge AI approaches and novel DSR methods. While a growing body of information systems (IS) research adopts a design perspective to develop complex AI and human-AI systems, researchers face significant challenges in conceptualizing, designing, and publishing impactful DSR on AI. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) provides a platform to tackle these challenges, foster meaningful dialogue, and illuminate pathways for advancing research in this critical domain. Grounded in recent DSR methodological advancements and major developments in the field, this session will focus on identifying barriers to impactful design research, proposing strategies to overcome them, and pushing the theoretical and practical boundaries of AI-driven design artifacts. Through keynote presentations, focused paper discussions, and hands-on paper development sessions, this PDW will bring together renowned scholars and early-career researchers to collaboratively explore the opportunities and challenges of DSR on AI. By bridging academic research and practical applications, this session aims to inspire novel contributions to the design and understanding of AI systems, making it a relevant and timely event for those driving the future of DSR and AI.
