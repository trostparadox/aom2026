When DEI Language Is Erased: How Policy, Law, and Organizational Silence Produce Material and Psychological Harm

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 9:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Salon C
DEI

People

Moderators: Jennifer Robin Bishop Case Western Reserve University; Darryl B. Rice Miami University

Session overview

This year’s opening session is intentionally designed as a field- setting conversation focused on the consequences of the erosion and removal of DEI language across institutions, particularly within academia. The session centers on how institutional silence, retreat, and reframing translate into lived consequences for scholars whose identities and experiences are increasingly rendered invisible.
