Active Learning as Pedagogical Design in Undergraduate Marketing Education

People

Authors: Yun-Sin Chuang National Yunlin University of Science and Technology; Shang-Ping Lin

Session overview

This poster examines active learning as a course-level pedagogical design in third-year undergraduate marketing courses at a university of technology in Taiwan. Drawing on course artifacts, instructor reflections, and descriptive instructional indicators, the study highlights how active learning shaped classroom practice, facilitation, and instructional design in a multicultural learning environment.
