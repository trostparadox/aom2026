When Does Human–GenAI Collaboration Foster Innovation? The Roles of Task Complexity in Public Sector

PNP
Li Jiangyu; Huan Huang (Sichuan University)
Against the backdrop of accelerating AI integration into public sector governance practices, how public sector employees can achieve AI-assisted innovation through human–GenAI collaboration remains to be systematically revealed. This study employs a mixed-methods approach grounded in Cognitive Load Theory and Conservation of Resources Theory to test hypotheses: Study 1 conducts empirical validation using two-stage questionnaire data (N = 718) from grassroots public sector employees across multiple regions; Study 2 further verifies causal relationships and model robustness through a 2×2 scenario experiment (N = 277). Findings indicate: Human–GenAI collaboration significantly reduces public sector employees’ cognitive load. Cognitive load mediates the relationship between human–GenAI collaboration and AI-assisted innovation behavior. Task complexity negatively moderates the relationship between human–GenAI collaboration and cognitive load. This study deepens the understanding of the relationship between human–GenAI collaboration and innovation behavior among public sector employees from a resource perspective. It reveals the cognitive mechanisms and situational dependence of AI-empowered innovation among public sector employees, providing theoretical foundations and practical insights for advancing human– GenAI collaboration and technology adoption in the public sector.
