Session overview

The purpose of this symposium is to present empirical research that uses a relational perspective to shed new light on organizing. Specifically, the focus of this symposium is on how a distinctly relational perspective can lead to new insights into the phenomena of trust and psychological safety in the workplace. Four papers that use diverse approaches and methods underscore the importance of a relational perspective. This symposium makes a two-fold contribution to management theory and research. First, this symposium showcases the unique insights that can be gained, in general, by adopting an expressly relational perspective. We believe this will enable audience members, regardless of their specific area of interest, to conceive of and envision new directions for studying other phenomena. Second, this symposium offers new insights specifically into trust and psychological safety, underscoring how a relational perspective can shed new light on these specific phenomena.
