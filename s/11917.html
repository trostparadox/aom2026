Session overview

Behavioral business ethics research focuses on understanding the causes and consequences of (un)ethical behavior within organizations. While much progress has been made with understanding (un-)ethical behavior at an individual level, research on higher-order effects (e.g., climate, groups, teams) remains sparse, pointing to a potentially fruitful area to advance the literature. With the goal of developing and generating future scholarship, this PDW showcases frontiers in behavioral ethics research. The theme this year is “Kicking it Up a Notch with Group-Level Behavioral Ethics.” This tenth rendition of the PDW will begin with eight Pecha Kuchas (5-minute timed presentations) by renowned scholars in the field, followed by a plenary discussion. This part of the PDW is open to all attendees of the Academy of Management. Presentations will focus on experts’ ideas about how to extend the ethics literature to a higher-level of analysis (e.g., climate, groups, teams). Around the 90-minute mark, we will transition to a 90-minute research development workshop where 30 pre-registered PhD students and (new) Assistant Professors (if space permits) are welcomed to bring their research models to discuss with and receive feedback from experts, followed by a one-hour networking reception where research development workshop attendees can network and foster connections with additional renowned faculty experts and each other.
