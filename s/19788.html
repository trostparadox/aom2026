Session overview

Scholarly research in neuroscience thrives on diversity, connectivity, and the bridge between theory and practice. However, early-career scholars often struggle to find "what resonates with both top-tier journals and their research passions. The NEUB “Voice” is an innovative, high-engagement PDW designed to amplify the research voices of doctoral students and junior faculty. As a parallel, think how when we work with entrepreneurs, there are pitch competitions, but the best models are far more developmental and collaborative. Why not the same for newer/younger NEUB scholars? Drawing inspiration from The Voice, this session shifts the focus from adversarial critique to collaborative "coaching." Before the conference, selected participants are matched with a Mentor: a world-class neuroscience scholar. This duo works together to refine the research’s "pitch" for both academic rigor and real-world relevance. Additional mentors can be added as desired. During the 120-minute session, five finalists present their work to an in-person panel of senior scholars. The audience acts as an extended ecosystem, providing live "peer support" through structured feedback and offers of tangible resources—such as access to datasets, field sites, and research tools. Unlike traditional "Shark Tank" formats, the goal here is not to eliminate, but to "turn the chairs" by demonstrating project viability. Judges identify the most promising projects based on their potential for impact and theoretical novelty. Participants walk away with a refined research plan, a strengthened network across the academic-practitioner divide, and actionable takeaways for navigating the complex research landscape that NEUB is blessed with.
