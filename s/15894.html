Session overview

Creative work is widely assumed to be deeply meaningful. People often report creating as a source of meaning, and creative occupations are frequently framed as quintessential examples of meaningful work. At the same time, research has found that work meaning shapes motivation, persistence, and openness to new ideas, which are key aspects of the creative process. Given the strong connection between work meaning and creativity, it is surprising that the two literatures have largely evolved independently, missing the opportunity for synergistic contributions at their intersection. This symposium brings these two conversations into closer dialogue and highlights new directions for research by examining how creativity and work meaningfulness are deeply intertwined.
