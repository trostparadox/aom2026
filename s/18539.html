Climate Policy Divergence and Recombinant Innovation in Multinational Firms (WITHDRAWN) WITHDRAWN

Author: Xueying Lin Fudan University

Session overview

Climate policy divergence across countries increases institutional complexity and poses significant challenges for multinational enterprises (MNEs), yet it also constitutes a source of policy-embedded knowledge and diverse inputs for recombination, creating unique opportunities for innovation. Drawing on the knowledge-based view, this study examines how climate policy distance influences MNEs’ climate-related recombinant innovation (CRI). We develop an issue-specific measure of CPD based on cross-national climate policy portfolio dissimilarity and decompose it into positive and negative directions. Using a panel of 9,127 firm-year observations from 2009–2023, we find asymmetric effects: positive CPD has an inverted U-shaped relationship with CRI, whereas negative CPD is negatively associated with CRI. Moreover, firms’ absorptive capacity attenuates both relationships.
