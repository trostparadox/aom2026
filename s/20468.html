Session overview

The aim of this workshop is to help instructors approach the use of cases in their classrooms in novel ways. Case studies are powerful tools for encouraging active learning, critical thinking, and practical problem-solving in students. Traditional case teaching has been impacted by the student use of AI and engagement is more difficult than ever, which means that teachers can no longer rely solely on tried-and-true traditional case teaching methods. In this session, we address these issues by helping participants learn about some new ways to incorporate cases in the classroom. We will focus on three novel approaches for case studies: 1) creating unique case studies for classes, 2) facilitating case sessions that create an immersive simulation-like experience, and 3) helping students craft cases themselves, applying class knowledge to frame a real-world situation. By the end of the session, participants will leave with tools and tips for creating new and engaging experiences with cases along with ideas for incorporating cases in new ways in their own classrooms.
