Session overview

AI has been described as facilitating literature reviews (Thau & Katila, 2025; Stokel-Walker & Van Noorden, 2023; Bail, 2024), systematizing expert-driven tasks such as scale item development (Hernandez & Nie, 2023), or aiding theory generation (Cornelissen et al., 2024), while there are few reviews of its role as an analytic methodology. This symposium contributes to organizational science by illustrating how generative AI can be used not only as a research aid, but also as a methodological component within empirical research. Across five papers, the presenters demonstrate how AI can be systematically integrated into measurement, inference, simulation, and qualitative analysis, along with the boundary conditions of using generative AI across those domains.
