CAU

People

Organizer: Natalie Schneider

Session overview

This caucus calls for a gathering of those interested in promoting research on workplace sexual harassment. This session will serve three purposes: 1) to preserve space for sexual harassment researchers to convene at AOM, 2) to network and build new research relationships, and 3) to shine light on attendee research.
