Session overview

Building on several years of momentum around health and well-being in entrepreneurship research and previous PDWs, this problem-solving lab format is designed to generate new research directions. The PDW brings together scholars interested in advancing theory, methods, and ethical approaches to studying entrepreneurial well-being across diverse contexts. Rather than relying on paper presentations or posters, participants will engage in facilitated small-group labs organized around shared research challenges. Senior scholars will serve as facilitators to guide collective sensemaking, identify theoretical tensions, and help participants move early-stage ideas forward. The emphasis is on developmental engagement, cross-disciplinary learning, and collaborative agenda-setting for the field. The workshop is designed to support scholars at multiple career stages who are interested in health, well-being, and entrepreneurship, while also strengthening connections across Entrepreneurship, Organizational Behavior, and Human Resource Management. By foregrounding participant-driven problem solving, this PDW highlights the next phase of scholarship on entrepreneurial well-being.
