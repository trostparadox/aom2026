Session overview

Creativity at work is a socially embedded process shaped by organizational structures, power relations, and interpersonal expectations. Individuals must navigate these complex social and structural dynamics not only to generate novel ideas but also to sustain creative efforts. Now in its sixth year since the 81st Academy of Management Annual Meeting in 2021, this symposium advances an ongoing conversation by examining how such social and structural factors shape creativity evaluation and creative processes in organizations.
