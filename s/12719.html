Session overview

This symposium offers a timely—and timeless—provocation: What if time itself is the next frontier of gender equality? Through a moderated and interactive discussion, the symposium explores how dominant linear assumptions about how careers should unfold produce privileged and exclusionary organizational norms, structures, and practices that shape women’s career trajectories. Panelists include leading academics whose research examines how sensemaking and identity unfold over time; how temporal dynamics shape the ways individuals and organizations evolve and change; and how gendered organizational contexts and cumulative gender bias influence women’s agency, career paths, and belonging over time.
