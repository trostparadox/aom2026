Session overview

Workplace gossip is prevalent in everyday work life and occupies a meaningful share of workplace communication. Given its ubiquity, it is important to deepen our understanding of this construct, such as its antecedents, consequences, and typology. This symposium contributes to the gossip literature from multiple perspectives. Across four papers, we examine both positive and negative workplace gossip, investigate gossip from the perspectives of both gossipers and recipients, explore the interpersonal consequences in teams and power asymmetric relationships, and also provide deeper understanding of the typology.
