Session overview

This professional development workshop is designed for Academy of Management (AOM) educators seeking practical, research-informed approaches to cultivating a global mindset among business students using the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). As business education grapples with climate risk, inequality, technological disruption, and geopolitical uncertainty, the SDGs offer a widely recognized, integrative framework for linking managerial decision-making to global social, environmental, and economic systems. The workshop introduces participants to pedagogical strategies that embed the SDGs across core business disciplines—strategy, finance, operations, marketing, and organizational behavior—without sacrificing analytical rigor. Drawing on examples from undergraduate, MBA, and executive education contexts, facilitators will demonstrate how the SDGs can be used to frame case discussions, experiential projects, simulations, and assessment tools that develop systems thinking, ethical reasoning, and cross-cultural awareness. All of these examples are based on practical classroom experiences by the presenters at an international business school. Participants will engage in hands-on activities to redesign an existing course module using selected SDGs, identify alignment with AACSB and AOM learning goals, and anticipate common student misconceptions or resistance. The workshop also addresses how to move beyond superficial “SDG mapping” toward deeper engagement with trade-offs, unintended consequences, and stakeholder conflicts. Attendees will leave with adaptable teaching resources, sample assignments, and a clearer understanding of how the SDGs can function not as advocacy tools, but as analytical lenses that prepare students for leadership in an interconnected global economy.
