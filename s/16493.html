Session overview

In recent years, questions of research impact have moved to the forefront of management and social science research. Yet, despite growing interest, impact remains unevenly defined, methodologically fragmented, and often siloed across disciplines and stakeholder domains. This PDW examines how scholars can intentionally design, enact, and evaluate impactful research that advances theory while engaging meaningfully with organizations, communities, policymakers, and industry actors. Drawing on diverse empirical and methodological perspectives, this PDW brings together panelists to address five interconnected dimensions of impact: (1) workplace impact; (2) community and societal impact; (3) policy and government impact; (4) industry perspectives; and (5) methodological approaches to impactful research. Through short panel inputs, a moderated fireside chat, and interactive roundtable discussions, participants will engage critically with the opportunities and tensions inherent in impact-oriented scholarship, including issues of power, ethics, and unintended consequences. The PDW concludes with a synthesis of key insights, concrete outcomes, and practical strategies to help scholars articulate, evaluate, and strengthen the impact pathways of their own research. Overall, this workshop seeks to foster cross-divisional dialogue and equip participants with specific tools for advancing rigorous, reflexive, and socially meaningful scholarship within and beyond the AOM community.
