Organizer: Susanne Beck Warwick Business School

Session overview

TIM invites you to join a lively and informative discussion about how to publish research on innovation and technology management. The Editors will share their perspectives on what constitutes a publishable advance in our field, important methodological and epistemological considerations for scholars seeking to publish in their journals, and important questions they believe the field of innovation and technology management must address over the next 10 years or so.
