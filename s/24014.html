Reimagining OBE: Advancing Management Education Through Career-Outcome-Based Personalized Education

MED
Vikas Rai Bhatnagar (Chandigarh University)
Outcome-based education (OBE) has long dominated educational systems, emphasizing standardized competencies and measurable outputs as the pathway to career readiness. Yet this paradigm encounters a complication because contemporary careers unfold in volatile, complex environments where adaptability, identity coherence, and ethical salience are as critical as technical skills. This complication is of concern because OBE’s flawed assumption that static course outcomes serve as proxies for career success produces graduates who may secure credentials but lack the reflexivity and resilience to recalibrate across transitions—leaving students disillusioned, employers frustrated by mismatched skills, and society deprived of responsible contributors. To address this concern, the paper problematizes OBE by challenging its assumptions, adopts abductive reasoning and integrative synthesis, and develops the theory of Career Outcome Based Personalized Education (COBPE). Six propositions converge in a conceptual process model that reconceptualizes assessment as career capacity constructs, positions personalization as mediator, and reflexive practice as moderator, while linking immediate interventions to protean career orientation as a longitudinal trajectory. This paper contributes to the literature by reframing education as a dynamic pathway rather than static outcomes, integrating strengths deployment, reflexivity, and career theory into a coherent architecture. COBPE not only advances theory but also offers a compelling framework that enhances learner wellbeing, improves employer productivity, and strengthens societal impact, thereby positioning personalized education as a cornerstone of sustainable careers and responsible citizenship.
