Session overview

This workshop brings together colleagues who will share their work in transforming business education into a powerful vehicle for meeting the sustainability challenges of the 21st Century. They are looking for ways to help all of us move existing and new initiatives forward. Six possible breakout group topics will be offered by the PDW presenters. These or other topics will be chosen based on audience interest – creating opportunities to honor participants’ interests and expertise by creating new topics if desired. One major topic (#1) will be the BE the Tipping Point initiative (BEtp) that was a major part of a related AOM PDW in Copenhagen last year. Members of the four organizations leading the BEtp initiative will be in this session and will build on the summer 2025 discussions, BEtp progress in the last year, and future plans. Other possible topics for breakout group discussion and/or post-conference actions include (#2) “swarming” – an approach for bottom-up organizational and societal transformation, (#3) a new initiative based on the 2009, 2016, and 2024 MacArthur Foundation $100 million and Change competitions that members of this workshop were involved in, (#4) opportunities for workshop members to join a podcast series based in part on two Routledge books of journey stories by business education pioneers, (#5) access and opportunities to contribute to classroom-ready materials for transforming business school courses, curricula, research, and positive activism, and (#6) ways to become involved in a series of scheduled conferences, workshops, and initiatives directly related to transforming business education. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
