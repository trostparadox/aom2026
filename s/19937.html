Session overview

The globalization of management scholarship has not been matched by equitable globalization of academic career opportunities. Evidence from recent reviews of the academic career literature shows pronounced fragmentation, heavy reliance on Global North samples, and limited theory addressing the structural inequalities that shape academic careers across underrepresented regions such as Asia, Eastern Europe, Latin America, the Middle East & North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa. These disparities restrict participation in global knowledge production and limit the Academy of Management’s ability to fulfill its strategic priority of strengthening engagement in underrepresented global regions. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) brings together scholars, editors, and AOM leaders to examine how academic careers can be made more inclusive and globally representative. Part 1 features a panel of prominent speakers offering regionally grounded insights from underrepresented regions such as the Middle East & North Africa, Latin America and Sub-Saharan Africa. The panel will also discuss career constraints and opportunities, highlighting gaps and future research pathways at the intersection of global inclusion, institutional conditions, and academic career development. Part 2 provides developmental roundtables where participants, especially early-career researchers from underrepresented regions, receive constructive feedback on research ideas, career strategies, and cross-regional collaboration. By integrating cross-regional perspectives with emerging career theory and structural analysis, this PDW enhances scholarly understanding and offers practical pathways for supporting inclusive global careers. Participants will gain region-specific insights, conceptual tools, and new international networks to advance more equitable participation in the global academic community.
