Unfolding the Generativity in Digital Industrial Platforms: A Platform Openness Perspective

CTO
Fuyicheng Lou (Zhejiang University)
Despite the proliferation of Digital Industrial Platforms (DIPs), realizing their generative potential remains a significant challenge due to the dual complexity of technology and organization. Challenging the assumption that generativity is an inherent attribute that emerges spontaneously, this study investigates how it is actively unfolded through elaborately formulating platform openness. Inspired by the generativity framework, we conduct a longitudinal case study of Tuya to examine the interplay between generative architecture and community. We delineate the evolutionary pathway of the Tuya DIP, manifesting as three distinct archetypes: the intelligence provisioning pipeline, the ecosystem-interconnecting hub, and the democratized innovation infrastructure. Underpinned by specific configurations of architecture and community openness, these archetypes co-evolve recursively with generative outcome. This study contributes to the literature by shifting the analytical focus from generative outcomes to antecedents, illuminating the dynamic orchestration of the DIP’ openness, and offering a process model for unfolding generativity within complex settings of DIPs.
