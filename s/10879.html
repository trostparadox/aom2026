Session overview

The purpose of this Presenter Symposium is to explore the lesser-known international knowledge on the governance of organizations for audience of the 2026 Annual Meeting of the Academy of Management. Through the presentation of six papers from eight authors, coming from four countries, we first create a general understanding of what constitutes corporate governance as it is presently accepting for managing for-profit and not-for-profit organizations, how we got to this state, and how much of it is influenced or guided by the knowledge that occurred long ago, maybe millennia ago. We also get into how it evolved and won the acceptance of corporations and the general population in which they operate. We take the function of corporate governance to create a framework to guide the board of directors to set how the company will direct and control its working of the corporation in the best interest of its owners—the stockholders and the general population. The symposium also covers how the governance sets the task of assigning responsibilities for the various corporate functions, sets accountability as to who will be held responsible if the targets are not achieved, how to reward or punish, and what to learn from the experience for the future planning and goal-setting. We also look at how nonbusiness social and cultural value systems have an effect on corporate governance.
