Session overview

Today’s global regulatory environment of the firm is not fixed but continually reconstructed as powerful actors redefine their spheres of influence. This new reality of a more fragmented and multipolar order contrasts with established scholarship on organizations’ regulatory environment that often treats a state’s regulatory territory-the spatial scope of a regulator’s legal authority-as fixed, mutually exclusive, and geographically defined by state borders. This symposium advances a novel territoriality lens that conceives of regulatory territory as a boundary that is claimed, contested and interpreted as the result of territoriality claims. It embeds this novel lens into IM, OT, and SIM research traditions, specifically, on the international and geopolitical environment of the firm, on digital platform and sustainability governance. The session features insights from different research fields and solicits the panel’s interactions with the audience to envision a future research agenda on regulatory territory as a global management and governance issue.
