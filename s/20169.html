Session overview

‘Intuitive intelligence’ is defined as the capacity to be aware of, understand, interpret and manage your intuitions and use this knowledge to make better judgements and take more effective decisions (Sadler-Smith, 2024). As things currently stand, intuitive intelligence is unique to humans and cannot be replicated by a machine (Kuzma, 2025) and hence adds value for humans in workplaces and labour markets where AI is becoming increasingly used to perform tasks that previously were the sole preserve of humans. However, intuition is best understood and used effectively in relation to its counterpart, i.e. ‘analysis’, and scholars and practitioners tend to agree that the best results come from combining intuition (System 1 processing) with analysis (System 2 processing). This PDW will use highly interactive format that will help participants to do two things: (1) Develop their own intuitive intelligence by learning how to combine intuition with analysis; (2) Learn how to develop their students’ intuitive intelligence by applying some of the methods learned in their own classrooms. Participants will leave the PDW with actionable insights, practical skills and tools that have been tried and tested by the facilitator. The PDW seeks to inspire and equip management educators and practitioners from across the globe with new skills and create a lasting impact in the MED community and beyond.
