Session overview

The Strategic Management Division offers again its annual Mid-Career Consortium. The Consortium aims to provide mid-career faculty members with the opportunity to interact with each other and with the panel of senior faculty from diverse kinds of business schools around the world. The interactive format aims to foster meaningful discussions about the opportunities and challenges faced by scholars making the transition from junior to senior roles in diverse kinds of business school. It will feature short senior faculty presentations, roundtables and Q&As. The Consortium welcomes STR faculty members who attained tenure on or before August 2025 and possess a minimum of five years of academic experience.
