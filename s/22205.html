CEO and AI Activism: Exploiting Gen AI Algorithms for Political Purposes

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 6
STR

People

Authors: Rosana Dangui UNIVALI; Rodrigo DeMello Bentley University

Session overview

We investigate the extent to which responses from major publicly available gen AI systems align politically with their respective CEOs' activism. Although CEO sociopolitical activism is a well-established research area, AI-related political activism remains a novel area of inquiry, with limited literature linking executives' political activism to algorithmic bias. We conducted a “field experiment” using the major commercially available GenAI algorithms. Leveraging models of political bias to measure CEO activism from their tweets and political bias from the gen AI models they manage, we test the hypothesis that CEOs' political leanings are associated with their models' political bias on issues important to the CEO. Our final sample comprises four Gen AI models that answered questions about 17 issues, 30 times each, for a total of 2040 answers. We found that, when left unattended, the sample models tend to provide left-leaning responses to both right-leaning and left-leaning prompts. However, this story changes when we model CEO Activism. We found that the more politically right-leaning the CEO is and the more relevant the issue is to the CEO, the more right-biased the AI's responses to left-leaning prompts will be. No effect was found for the right-leaning prompts. Further developments of this paper will test whether AI governance tools can mitigate the influence of CEO activism on algorithmic bias.
