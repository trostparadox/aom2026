Session overview

The HR function is undergoing profound change due to technological innovation, new forms of work, and evolving workforce expectations. These developments are reshaping HR’s identity, purpose, and capabilities, while elevating the strategic role of HR leaders. This session explores how advances such as AI, data-driven decision-making, and increased attention to wellbeing, inclusion, and sustainability are transforming HR leadership. Bringing together current academic insights, the session highlights key challenges and emerging research directions related to the future role of HR and next-generation HR leadership in a rapidly changing workplace.
