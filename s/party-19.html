STR Museum Visit: American Revolution at 250 (Registration Rqd)

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 2:00PM–4:00PM
Museum of the American Revolution, 101 S 3rd St, Philadelphia, PA 19106

Event details

Directions and Visitor Information

What better place to experience Philadelphia in 2026 — the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence — than the Museum of the American Revolution? Join the STR Membership Engagement Committee for an informal afternoon exploring the ideas, conflicts, and people that shaped the American Revolution and continue to influence political and social movements around the world. Through original artifacts, recreated historical settings, interactive exhibits, and the museum’s signature presentation of George Washington’s Revolutionary War tent, the museum brings the founding of the United States to life in ways that go well beyond the familiar textbook story.

We will meet in front of the museum at 2:00 PM and explore the galleries together. This will be a relaxed opportunity to experience one of Philadelphia’s most distinctive museums, connect with other STR members, andreflect on questions of leadership, collective action, institutional change, and the enduring meaning of revolution. Guests are welcome!

Admission: $27 per person if purchased at the museum, or $25 if purchased online in advance. Participants are welcome to purchase their tickets either at the museum upon arrival or online beforehand to receive the$2 discount.
