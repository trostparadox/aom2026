STR Museum Visit: American Revolution at 250

Event details

Directionsand Visitor Information

What better place to experience Philadelphia in 2026 — the250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence — than the Museum of theAmerican Revolution? Join the STR Membership Engagement Committee for aninformal afternoon exploring the ideas, conflicts, and people that shaped theAmerican Revolution and continue to influence political and social movementsaround the world. Through original artifacts, recreated historical settings,interactive exhibits, and the museum’s signature presentation of GeorgeWashington’s Revolutionary War tent, the museum brings the founding of theUnited States to life in ways that go well beyond the familiar textbook story.

We will meet in front of the museum at 2:00 PM and explorethe galleries together. This will be a relaxed opportunity to experience one ofPhiladelphia’s most distinctive museums, connect with other STR members, andreflect on questions of leadership, collective action, institutional change,and the enduring meaning of revolution. Guests are welcome!

Admission: $27 per person if purchased at the museum, or $25if purchased online in advance. Participants are welcome to purchase theirtickets either at the museum upon arrival or online beforehand to receive the$2 discount.
