Session overview

Antitrust scrutiny of acquisitions has intensified across major jurisdictions, reshaping the regulatory environment for corporate growth, especially in digital and technology markets. This panel brings together scholars and policy experts to examine how renewed enforcement changes who acquires whom, the timing and structure of deals, and the relative attractiveness of alternative growth modes. The discussion focuses on the micro-mechanisms of capability and knowledge reallocation: what assets are sought (data, IP, talent, distribution), how integration is managed, and when substitutions replicate (or alter) the innovation effects traditionally associated with acquisitions, including debates around “killer acquisitions.” Leveraging cross-jurisdiction variation (U.S./EU/UK and beyond) as a natural laboratory, the panel will develop testable mechanisms and research agendas on regulation, M&A adaptation, and downstream consequences for startups, venture capital, innovation ecosystems, and long-run competition.
