Session overview

The domains of strategic human resource management (HRM) and leadership research have traditionally evolved in parallel despite their shared influence on employee and organizational outcomes. Emerging studies increasingly recognize that leadership throughout the management hierarchy plays a central role in shaping how HR practices are designed, enacted, interpreted, and experienced. Yet, a comprehensive understanding of how leadership and HR practices interact to produce alignment remains underdeveloped. This professional development workshop (PDW) brings together scholars and practitioners to examine how different leadership styles, actors, attributes, and contextual factors shape the interplay between HR systems and leadership across organizational levels while taking into account the dynamic processes involved. Through interactive discussions and integrative perspectives, the PDW aims to advance theory and practice on how aligning strategic HRM and leadership can foster employee wellbeing and organizational performance.
