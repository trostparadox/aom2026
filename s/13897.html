Session overview

Failure is pervasive in organizational life, and in the process of creating new businesses, in particular. This panel symposium explores the lingering effects of failure – that is, how individuals connect and disconnect with dead organizations, and the possible role of identity, attachments, and memories in informing their thoughts, behaviors, and the trajectory of their careers after failure. It considers individuals’ emotional/intrapsychic relationships with their defunct businesses/es and the recognition that defunct organizations “live on,” in the hearts and minds of their ex-members. The discussion surfaces challenges and opportunities that lingering failures create for entrepreneurship and management researchers and scholarship today
