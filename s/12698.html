Session overview

This workshop advances careers scholarship by providing a developmental space for scholars from Low and Middle Income countries (LMICs) to present work, receive feedback from senior academics, and engage in conversations that strengthen individual research projects and the field's global representativeness. Building on the AOM Careers Division Research Incubator and Regional Ambassador Programs, the PDW aims to cultivate a sustainable community by providing a forum for scholars to exchange insights on research and academic career challenges specific to LMIC contexts, including how contextual realities influence how research is positioned, methodological choices, access to data and funding, publication opportunities, and international collaboration. Through presentations, a panel discussion, and roundtable conversations, the PDW seeks to promote learning, collective organizing, and international networking that nurtures both the growth of LMIC scholars and the development of career scholarship that is contextually grounded and globally representative while enriching the field theoretically and empirically.
