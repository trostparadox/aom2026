Session overview

Austrian economics has remained a relevant theoretical lens for understanding entrepreneurial action, organizational decision-making, and market dynamics in management and entrepreneurship research. Building on recent discussions, most notably the Research Handbook on Austrian Economics in Management and Entrepreneurship (Gupta & Bylund, 2025), this panel brings together leading scholars to explore where the Austrian tradition can take management and entrepreneurship research in the coming decades. The discussion examines how core Austrian concepts such as subjective value, entrepreneurial judgment, alertness, uncertainty, market processes, and praxeology can inform contemporary debates on entrepreneurial decision-making, firm strategy, organizational adaptability, and emerging technologies. Together, the panelists articulate emerging research opportunities, methodological implications, and theoretical extensions that can enrich mainstream scholarship. Overall, the panel symposium aims to foster a deep discussion of the relevance of the Austrian tradition and to outline forward-looking research avenues for scholars across management, entrepreneurship, and strategy.
