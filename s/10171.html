Session overview

This workshop explores the potential of artificial intelligence (AI) image generation as a tool for qualitative interviewing. Workshop participants will experience how AI-generated imagery can support interviewees in articulating complex experiences and reflect upon the methodological challenges and opportunities this technology introduces. We introduce and compare two approaches: participant-generated AI images versus researcher-curated AI-image collections for participant-led collage creation. Participant image generation maximizes individual expression and agency but encounters technological barriers and AI training biases that may produce unsatisfactory results. Researcher-prepared images reduce exposure to bias and technical disruptions while improving accessibility, yet risk silencing unique participant experiences by constraining the available visual material. Workshop participants will engage with both approaches, discussing strategies to maximize AI’s benefits while mitigating its challenges. This workshop contributes to growing scholarship on arts-based and reflective interviewing methods, illustrating how emerging technologies can advance interview-based research while requiring careful consideration of their limitations. Participants will leave with practical frameworks for incorporating AI image generation into their qualitative research practice. Please bring a device that you can use to engage in the workshop exercises.
