Session overview

Leveraging a unique opportunity, this panel symposium offers a structured conversation with Philadelphia-based CEOs about contemporary leadership challenges. Organizations today operate as complex systems characterized by interdependence, uncertainty, and persistent plurality in goals, stakeholders, and temporal horizons. Yet dominant leadership and senior team models continue to emphasize clarity, alignment, and control - implicitly assuming environments in which problems can be diagnosed, trade-offs optimized, and direction stabilized. This symposium challenges those assumptions. Prominent Philadelphia-based CEOs will engage in a fireside chat moderated by Wharton Professor Adam Grant to explore contemporary challenges of leadership under conditions of enduring complexity. By engaging with the challenges of senior leaders, this session seeks to test extant leadership research and inspire future theory generation.
