Dynamic Knowledge Management: Toward a Capability-Based Reframing of Knowledge for Strategic Renewal

STR
Bailu Li (Shandong Polytechnic)
In the digital economy, firms increasingly compete under conditions of rapid technological change and strategic uncertainty, where traditional knowledge management (KM) approaches often struggle to sustain competitive advantage. This paper advances Dynamic Knowledge Management (DKM) as a capability-based reconceptualization of how firms purposefully reconfigure knowledge for strategic renewal. By integrating the logic of dynamic capabilities (DCs) into KM, DKM reframes knowledge not as a supportive operational function but as a strategy-oriented capability embedded in sensing, seizing, and transforming processes. Conceptually, the paper clarifies the positioning of DKM and specifies the core activities through which it operates. An illustrative case of AirAsia is used to demonstrate how DKM activities support strategic renewal during digital transformation. By embedding KM within the DCs framework, this study contributes to strategy research by explaining how firms mobilize knowledge to renew competitive advantage in digitally intensive environments.
