Session overview

How does concealment impact creativity in teams? Concealment can pertain to protecting information and knowledge as well as strategically concealing or revealing certain aspects of one’s identity to be more effective. On one hand, concealment can negatively impact creativity by jeopardizing relationships with stakeholders and undermining sharing of non-redundant information and accessing of knowledge beyond the team’s boundaries. On the other hand, there are benefits to secrecy that aid in creativity and innovation through enhanced focus and strategic advantage over competitors. At the individual level, although authentic self-expression makes coordination easier, protecting oneself from scrutiny by others can also aid higher creativity by increasing positive affect. Building on these tensions within the research on concealment in creative contexts, the symposium brings togetehr five papers to answer three broad research questions. How do contextual and personal factors shape concealment in creative contexts? How does concealment evolve as a process during teams’ functioning? When is concealment effective for team and individual creativity? Professor Justin Berg, an expert scholar in creativity and innovation, will discuss theoretical and practical implications.
