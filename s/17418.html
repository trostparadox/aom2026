Session overview

This social event is for participants in the Health Care Management Division's Emerging Scholars Consortium (ESC). Both emerging scholars and faculty who participated as facilitators, mentors, and organizers are encouraged to attend. This is a time to socialize with those with whom you have spent the day and build your HCM community. This session will be held offsite. Please consult the HCM conference newsletters in July (emailed) or contact HCM Membership Engagement Chair, Geoffrey Silvera at gsilvera@uab.edu for more information.
