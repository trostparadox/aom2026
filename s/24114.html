Intersectional Perspective on Geographical Mobility Intentions of Entrepreneurs

IM
Liudmila Ivvonen (De Vinci Higher Education, De Vinci Research Center)
Location choice of entrepreneurs is an important question for both entrepreneurship scholars and policymakers. Existing entrepreneurship and migration studies on the determinants of why and where entrepreneurs move present contradictory evidence. We apply the intersectional perspective that allows us to simultaneously investigate the configurations of conditions that can lead to the geographical mobility intentions of entrepreneurs, rather than considering these conditions as independent, analytically separate variables. We draw on the embedded agency approach and consider entrepreneurs as structurally embedded agents whose choices are influenced by individual differences and the socio-political as well as economic attributes of locations. We find distinct configurations that lead to geographical mobility intentions of entrepreneurs as well as their absence.
