Session overview

Research on virtuous leadership behaviors and styles—including ethical, servant, authentic, humble, and spiritual—have garnered widespread attention over the past decade and remains a relevant topic for both researchers and practitioners alike. This paper development workshop (PDW) will bring together researchers across disciplines and paradigms to discuss ongoing trends, key challenges, and future directions on virtuous leadership, including areas for further development and integration between areas. The first part of the PDW, which will be open to all AOM participants, will feature a group of panel experts who will share their journey, vision, and perceived challenges regarding the study of virtuous leadership. In the second half of the PDW, participants who have submitted and been accepted to a paper development workshop will receive feedback from leading scholars in this space. Overall, this PDW will provide a much-needed venue where established scholars can share their vision and insights regarding the study of virtuous leadership. Likewise, this PDW will also serve to help foster a rising generation of scholars who are interested in adopting a virtuous, prosocial lens when studying leadership. We believe that attendees will gain valuable perspectives on the topic and that this PDW will serve as an important launchpad for future scholarly discovery.
