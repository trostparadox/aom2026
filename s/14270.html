Session overview

The pace of digital transformation currently surpasses that which we have experienced in the past. Headlines are filled with news of layoffs due to artificial intelligence (AI) replacements. Often, efficiency and profit margins are the focus rather than human connection, partnerships, equity, or embodiment. Those who remain in the workforce face tremendous changes, which contribute to burnout and uncertainty, while the focus on inclusion diminishes, and a sense of isolation grows. Yet these troubling trends can be offset by designing organizations, policies, and frameworks that ensure humans remain involved in the transformation process. Through conversations with an expert interdisciplinary panel and roundtable discussions, this PDW aims to stimulate research in this rapidly changing environment, fostering a community and network for collaboration. The goal is to ensure that humans remain central, not peripheral, to the digital transformation process. Through the four pillars of connection, partnerships, equity, and embodiment, we aim not only to critique the current state of digital transformation but also to develop solutions for preserving our humanity amid the inevitable changes ahead.
