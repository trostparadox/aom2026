Session overview

In this PDW, regional ambassadors of the AOM Careers Division (CAR) seek to initiate a conver-sation that begins to explore the most pressing topics from the Careers Division. The purpose of the AOM CAR Divisions Regional Ambassadors Initiative is to: (a) involve discussions about the most pressing CAR topics (i.e., career characteristics; digitalization and AI; well-being and mental health; career sustainability; career transitions; lifelong learning; careers and family; improving careers research); (b) connect perspectives and members from different regions of the CAR division; (c) integrate and engage our global membership and to generate and share knowledge across national borders and (d) start collaborations between CAR members around the globe. The main objectives are to develop projects across our community that involve, engage, and serve our global membership and leverage the knowledge within the division and develop initia-tives that contribute to the research, teaching, and service objectives of division members. By focusing on local and marginalized perspectives, we seek to broaden the theoretical scope of careers research and add to dominant frameworks that privilege linear, individualistic, and Western-centric models of career success. Understanding these diverse career contexts is not only critical for advancing career theory but also for addressing broader societal challenges, such as global inequali-ty and the exclusion of marginalized groups.
