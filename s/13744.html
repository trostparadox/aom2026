Advancing Ethical AI HR Scholarship: Developing Critical Questions for Research and Practice

Session overview

The rapid deployment of artificial intelligence in workplace settings has outpaced the development of robust ethical frameworks to govern its use. While organizations increasingly rely on AI for talent acquisition, performance management, and workforce decisions, fundamental questions about fairness, transparency, worker dignity, and organizational responsibility remain underexplored in HR scholarship. This interactive roundtable session convenes HR scholars to identify and prioritize research questions advancing ethical AI applications in the workplace. Through a modified World Café format, participants will rotate through facilitated discussions addressing seven critical domains: algorithmic management, human dignity in AI-augmented work, capability development, bias and justice, transparency and worker rights, evolving HR professional roles, and collective action in algorithmic workplaces. The session produces a tangible, actionable research agenda co-created by participants, identifying urgent research questions, articulating practitioners' "wicked problems," and proposing concrete collaboration opportunities, including special issues, multi-site studies, and research consortia.
