Session overview

This symposium collects four innovative research papers examining “a tale as old as time,” namely, workplace sexual harassment, to report new perspectives and make recommendations on tackling this challenge in the current socio-political environment. Despite the incredible progress this stream of research has made in outlining the causes and effects (Cortina & Areguin, 2021), workplace sexual harassment experiences still exist, and are even on the rise internationally. The purpose of this symposium is threefold. First, the symposium presents research using innovative perspectives to highlight uniqueness of sexual harassment experiences. Second, the symposium discussant and presenters aim their dialogue on how to create meaningful change through research so that the experiences of workplace sexual harassment dwindle, rather than grow in the current amplified and complex societal environment. Finally, the symposium will focus on building community among workplace sexual harassment researchers.
