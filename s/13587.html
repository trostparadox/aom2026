Session overview

The purpose of this Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is to create a space for discussing how Critical Management Studies (CMS) scholars can integrate critical pedagogy into their teaching and embody CMS values across different dimensions of academic life. Critical pedagogy frames management education as inherently political, ethical, and potentially radical. It invites students to engage critically and reflexively with the historical and ongoing patterns of exploitation, oppression, and inequalities reproduced through various political-economic systems and mainstream management education, including neoliberalism, white heteropatriarchy, colonialism, and environmental degradation. In this PDW, we aim to explore how critical management pedagogy can cultivate students’ critical and social consciousness, encouraging them to imagine and develop alternative possibilities for working, organizing and managing. Through dialogue and shared reflection, the workshop seeks to advance pedagogical practices that challenge dominant narratives and foster transformative learning experiences. This PDW is divided into 4 parts: (1) Thematic discussions led by the workshop organizers. (2) Small-group thematic tables, with organizers rotating among attendees to facilitate dialogue. (3) A critical pedagogy lab, where teaching and learning resources are shared. (4) A plenary debrief, synthesizing insights and reflections.
