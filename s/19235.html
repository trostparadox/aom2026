Session overview

This caucus explores Leadership Courage as an emerging, ethics-first concept responding to systemic risk, inequality, and institutional failure. Participants will co-develop directions for advancing Leadership Courage conceptualization, measurement, and education, fostering interdisciplinary dialogue on how leaders can balance ethical responsibility, power, and systemic constraints in high-stakes contexts.
