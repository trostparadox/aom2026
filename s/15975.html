Session overview

Corporate sociopolitical activism (CSA), where companies engage with contested social and political issues, is emerging as provocative force reshaping the role of business in society. This form of corporate engagement goes beyond traditional corporate social responsibility, as firms actively advocate for specific causes or policies, often in response to stakeholder expectations or moral imperatives. The goal of this session is to highlight key knowledge gaps and offer the audience the opportunity for a rich discussion around the tensions and opportunities of research and practice in this emerging area. Our panel of distinguished scholars will share their insights from their respective domains and suggest potential directions for future research.
