Session overview

Scholarship at the intersection of sociology and management has thrived, but discussions about the future of organization theory have mostly taken place in the discipline of sociology. Given the active presence of scholars of organization theory in management, as well as the evolution and expansion of the boundaries of organization theory, we started a discussion about the future of sociology in management scholarship at last year’s Annual Meeting. Continuing the discussion this year, we invite faculty mentors who are actively publishing in the intersection. The first part of this workshop, open to all, will be a panel discussion that addresses four key aspects in the intermingling of sociology and management: theoretical development, publishing strategies, networking opportunities, and future innovations. The second part of this workshop, based on applications to participate, will consist of roundtables in which participants engage with faculty mentors to gain individualized insights on how to develop their current research projects to maximize contribution to both disciplines. By fostering connections among the sociological community in management at large, reflecting on the present and future of organization theory, and providing opportunities for developmental discussions, the workshop will highlight organization theory’s richness and potential, extending its continued vibrancy within the OMT Division and management scholarship.
