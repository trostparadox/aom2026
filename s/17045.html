Leveraging HR Practices for Sustainable Employability: A Moderated Mediation Approach

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 1
CAR

People

Author: Eun-Jee Kim Kyonggi University

Session overview

In today’s rapidly changing work environments, sustaining employability has become a critical concern for both employees and organizations. Drawing on person-environment fit theory and career construction theory, this study examines how developmental HR practices influence employees’ sustainable employability through career adaptability, with job crafting acting as a boundary condition. Developmental HR practices, including training, career development initiatives, and performance appraisal, align organizational resources with employees’ growth needs, fostering career adaptability and enabling employees to accumulate the resources necessary to navigate dynamic career demands. Career adaptability further mediates this relationship by helping individuals respond effectively to challenges and maintain long-term employability. Additionally, employees’ engagement in job crafting strengthens the effects of HR practices on career adaptability and employability outcomes. Based on the extant literature review, the research hypotheses along with a research model were proposed. Results, discussions, implications for research and practice will be discussed in the poster presentation.
