Designing for Dignity: Inclusive Transport Systems for Commuters with Disability

DEI
SUDHANSHU BHATT; Emmanuel Mogaji (Keele University); Simeon Stevenson Turay (University of Sierra Leone); Hayatullah Taiwo Busari
Despite years of reform, equitable transport access for commuters with disabilities (CWD) remains limited. This study introduces the Integrated CWD Accessibility Enhancement Framework, mapping systems along Policy Support and Socio-Technical Inclusivity axes. It identifies four scenarios—Systemic Exclusion, Community-Driven Adaptation, Policy-Driven Inclusion, and Inclusive & Assistive Transport. The framework reveals that infrastructure alone does not ensure accessibility; cultural, institutional, and social dynamics matter. A stakeholder matrix outlines roles across sectors, urging alignment between policy and lived realities. Framing accessibility as a co-produced outcome, the paper offers a scalable tool for equity assessment and calls for further research on transitions and intersectionality.
