The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Industrial Chain Resilience: Evidence from Chinese Cities

OSCM
Yinan Shang (Beijing Information Science and Technology University)
Under the global wave of digital transformation, artificial intelligence is being widely introduced into industrial supply chain systems to enhance their ability to cope with risks. However, does the application of artificial intelligence truly help enhance the resilience of the industrial chain? Based on the panel data of 275 prefecture-level cities in China from 2012 to 2022, this paper conducts an empirical test on the relationship between artificial intelligence and the resilience of the industrial chain. Research has found that artificial intelligence can significantly enhance the resilience of the industrial chain, and this effect is mainly achieved by promoting technological innovation, strengthening risk management and optimizing information sharing. Further analysis shows that the enabling effect of artificial intelligence varies among different regions and resource-type cities, especially in enhancing the industrial chain elasticity of central and western regions and resource-exhausted cities. The conclusion of this article indicates that artificial intelligence plays a substantive "stabilizer" role in the construction of China's industrial chain resilience, providing empirical basis and policy reference for promoting the stable development of a modern industrial system.
