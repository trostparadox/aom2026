From Tool to Self-Expansion Partner: How Generative AI Usage Shapes Employee Adaptive Performance

OB
Xi Ran (Renmin University of China); Mengjie Feng (Renmin University of China)
Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is increasingly embedded in the workplace, reshaping employees’ psychological experiences and adaptive behaviors, yet little scholarly attention has been paid to how it influences employee adaptive performance through the lens of self-expansion. Drawing on self-expansion theory, this study proposes that GenAI usage enhances employees’ adaptive performance by fostering self-expansion and examines the moderating role of GenAI anthropomorphism. We conducted a three-wave longitudinal survey of 220 full-time employees in China. The results show that GenAI usage is positively associated with self-expansion, which in turn significantly enhances adaptive performance. Moreover, this indirect effect is stronger when employees perceive GenAI as more anthropomorphic. By conceptualizing GenAI as a self-expansion partner rather than a purely instrumental tool, this study extends research on how GenAI influences employees’ adaptive performance and provides insights for designing GenAI systems that better support employee adaptability.
