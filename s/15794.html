Session overview

This highly interactive workshop equips management and consulting educators to design and implement powerful experiential learning in their courses. Participants will examine evidence-based strategies for structuring simulations, cases, client projects, and team-based activities to maximize student engagement, collaboration, and learning. The session foregrounds critical design decisions (e.g. team composition, duration, preparatory work, and workload balance) to mitigate social loafing, maximize engagement, and mirror professional practice. Through live demonstrations, debriefs, and adaptable frameworks, attendees will leave with concrete tools for scaffolding team development, integrating pre-work, and aligning experiential components with course objectives across disciplines, levels, and class formats.
