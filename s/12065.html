Session overview

Research in organizational behavior has examined voice as a discretionary form of communication, focusing on how individuals’ voice and silence shape organizational culture, climate, and other workplace experiences. Similarly, research on strategic communication by top management teams and boards of directors demonstrates how organizations’ public discourse influences external evaluations of firms by investors, analysts, and other stakeholders. This symposium aims to bring the two complementary streams of research together by integrating micro-level perspectives on voice processes with research on communication dynamics in the upper echelons. By examining how interrelated processes of voice and communication translate into firm-level outcomes, the symposium advances a multi-level understanding of how communication connects individual and group-level voice behavior to organizational consequences. The symposium aims to generate new theoretical and empirical insights at the intersection of communication, voice, and upper echelon research.
