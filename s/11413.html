Session overview

The rapid integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in higher education is reshaping student learning, yet many students engage with AI tools superficially or unethically. This PDW equips instructors with evidence-based, practice-ready strategies to teach AI across degree levels. We present research on how students use AI in business courses and translate findings into workshop activities tailored to MBA, postgraduate, and undergraduate audiences. Through short demonstrations, small-group design tasks, and facilitated discussion, participants will learn how to frame AI as tutor, coach, teammate, and tool; design assignments that promote critical use; and implement classroom templates and assessment rubrics. The session foregrounds ethical considerations, equity of access, and curricular alignment. Attendees will leave with concrete resources: sample assignment prompts, in-class exercises, grading rubrics, and a roadmap to integrate AI literacy into existing courses. The PDW supports faculty at all career stages seeking to adapt teaching practices for an AI-rich learning environment. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
