Session overview

The goal of this symposium is to highlight ongoing research that adopts a multi-method, multi-level, and cross-cultural perspective on diversity in organizations. Specifically, the symposium examines (1) the effectiveness of diversity interventions, (2) how diversity information shapes individual perceptions and decisions, (3) how diversity processes generalize across national contexts, and (4) how diversity is constructed and represented at the cultural level. The papers in this symposium use a range of methodological approaches, including large-scale field studies using organizational reports and employee reviews, computational analyses of natural language corpora, preregistered field experiments, and laboratory experiments.
