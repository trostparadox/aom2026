Session overview

Organizations are facing high uncertainty, resulting in the intensification of calls for studying the future. Foresight, however, is often framed as prediction or anticipation, and yet the future cannot be treated as a set of predetermined exogenous events; rather, it emerges through collective action. This PDW advances future-making as a core concern for SAP research, with a research focus on how actors sense emerging possibilities, negotiate temporal interpretations, and mobilize resources toward enacting preferred futures. Building on Luhmann’s conception of time, the session engages emerging SAP debates on temporal work, collective agency, dynamic capabilities under uncertainty, and critiques of foresight through practical examples from industry on how firms perceive and act to impact the future. The session brings together scholars from foresight, sociology, and management in an interactive, co-creationary format to advance SAP research on shaping and making the future. This PDW workshop is open to all participants interested in future-making and the integration of practical strategy with emerging theory on futures. The session is highly interactive, with opportunities for audience engagement throughout. Attendees will receive a summary of the workshop and key insights after the session.
