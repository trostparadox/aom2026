Session overview

The Junior Faculty Consortium (JFC) is an annual event aimed at supporting early-career faculty members and postdoctoral scholars associated with the Social Issues in Management (SIM) and Organizations and the Natural Environment (ONE) divisions. It offers a structured environment for networking with senior scholars and helps participants develop their research agendas. Attendees receive personalized feedback on a working paper and participate in panel discussions and roundtables that focus on career paths, publication strategies, and non-research challenges faced by early-career academics. Bringing together scholars from various institutional and national backgrounds, the JFC creates a unique platform for building international connections and sharing best practices to advance professional growth and scholarly impact.
