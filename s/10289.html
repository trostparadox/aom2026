Author: Samantha G Deane Rutgers University-New Brunswick

Session overview

A comparative literature review exploring research around diversity practices and outcomes compared to research around disability diversity practices and outcomes. The review includes 36 articles from 1995-2022, including top management journals (e.g., The Academy of Management Journal), as well as disability journals (e.g., Disability and Health Journal). Disability diversity management is shown to lag behind that of general diversity management, with implications for organizational research and practice.
