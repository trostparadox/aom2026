Session overview

Every educator knows that teaching is hard. Not just intellectually demanding, but situationally demanding and always shaped by who is sitting in front of you, what they expect from education, what their context has taught them to value, and what the institution around you considers legitimate. The challenge of reaching students, building relevance, and making ideas land in a room is one we all share, wherever in the world we teach. And yet the global conversation about how we teach management continues to be dominated by a narrow set of North American and Western European perspectives. The innovations happening in classrooms across Latin America, Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and Indigenous educational contexts rarely make it to the main stage. That is a gap our community can no longer afford to ignore. The MED Welcome and Ambassador Programme addresses both of these things at once. This session opens with a warm welcome from the MED Executive team and PRME our sponsor, with an opportunity for every educators regardless of discipline or whether you know the division well or are walking in alone, to feel part of the MED community during the conference and beyond. It then moves into a series of short, high- energy spotlight presentations from MED Ambassadors representing underrepresented regions, each sharing a teaching practice, challenge, or innovation that the broader AOM management educator community will immediately find useful. The session closes with structured networking designed to connect attendees across regions and career stages, seeding conversations and collaborations that will continue. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
