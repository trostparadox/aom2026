Research on Female Entrepreneurial Behaviors: An Exploratory Analysis Based on Grounded Theory

ENT
Ping Li (Harbin Institute of Technology); Ye N/A He (Harbin Institute of Technology); Xuan Liu (Harbin Institute of Technology)
Taking female entrepreneurs as the research object, the influencing factors and mechanisms of women's entrepreneurial behavior were studied. Based on semi-structured in-depth interviews with 9 Chinese female entrepreneurs, the interview data were analyzed and sorted out by using straussian grounded theory, and the influencing factor model of women's entrepreneurial behavior was obtained by introducing the theory of planning behavior, and the influencing mechanism was analyzed. The results show that the influencing factors of women's entrepreneurial behavior mainly include entrepreneurial attitude, subjective norm and perceived behavior control, and the entrepreneurial intention is regulated by the situational factors composed of internal and external factors. Finally, from the perspectives of entrepreneurs themselves, the government and society, corresponding strategies and reference suggestions are provided for promoting women's entrepreneurship.
