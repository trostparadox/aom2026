How Does Digital Product Complexity Shape Iterative Innovation?——A Perspective Based on Absorptive

STR
Jian Guan (Central South University); Haitao Yang (Central South University); Luxuan Jiang (Central South University); linna li
In digital platform ecosystems, intense competition and platform governance push digital products to rely on continuous iterative innovation. While prior research focuses on external factors like user reviews or developer experience, how a product’s own characteristics affect innovation remains unclear. Drawing on absorptive capacity theory, this study examines how digital product complexity influences iterative novelty and agility, and the moderating role of developer product portfolio size. Analysis of 521 iOS apps shows that structural complexity enhances both novelty and agility, whereas operational complexity reduces them. Furthermore, a larger product portfolio weakens the positive effect of structural complexity and strengthens the negative effect of operational complexity on agility. These findings advance the understanding of product-driven iteration and offer practical insights for managing complexity to improve innovation efficiency.
