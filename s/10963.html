Session overview

Using the concept of a “sales pitch”, this Professional Development Workshop (PDW) presents management education researchers with an exclusive opportunity to receive immediate, constructive, and developmental feedback from leading journal editors in an engaging, interactive setting. Prior to the annual meeting, we called for 3-minute video paper submissions from which we shortlisted the top six (6) finalists to present live at AOM 2026. During the PDW, finalists present their latest management education research in a single-slide, three-minute format: (1) overview of the topic, (2) why it is interesting, (3) why it is important, and (4) what is the contribution? After all the paper pitches, authors engage in roundtable rotating table discussions with the audience to seek further feedback and then share their takeaways from this engagement before the panel of editorial judges provides detailed feedback. Our panelist editors come from prominent management education journals, including the Academy of Management Learning & Education, Journal of Management Education, Management Learning, and the British Journal of Management. This 120-minute PDW is designed to be dynamic, developmental, and highly interactive, enabling presenters to share their research with expert editors and to explore effective strategies for publishing scholarship that endures. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
