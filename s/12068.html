Session overview

Generative AI has drastically altered business education. Faculty are teaching about how it works, how to use it effectively, and how not to use it. Certainly, this is the current hill we all need to climb. However, there is a larger mountain beyond it: Agentic AI. These AI-powered bots can find information, make decisions, and take actions, limited only by the imaginations and constraints set by their human creators. This workshop has three goals. The first is to provide a method for helping teachers explain AI agents to their students, focusing on how agents can impact business operations. This includes exercises, materials, and references. The second goal is to introduce professors to existing tools to construct AI agents and agent-based simulations. These tools use agentic AI, so they can also show agents in action. Third, the workshop will share accumulated wisdom, including empirical assessments from students, on the effectiveness of these tools and techniques in achieving learning objectives. Participants will leave the session with lesson plans, hands-on experience with several tools that employ AI agents to create agents and simulations, a foundational understanding of how agents work, and predictions for how agents will impact business operations in the near future. The tools introduced in this session are also useful for professors who seek to deploy multi-role, multi-stage, auto-graded simulations to teach any subject, not just AI agents. Participant evaluations are invaluable.
