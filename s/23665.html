Session overview

You are invited to join us in downtown Philadelphia on Sunday, August 2, 2026, from 3:00 to 5:00pm for an official AOM ONE Social Event hosted with the Impact Scholar Community (ISC). In the spirit of connecting research with real-world change, we will be teaming up with PennFuture and Sustainable Business Network, two local impact-focused organization. The Impact Safari is all about getting out into the city, meeting local change makers, and reimagining how we build communities for impact. Expect open conversations, new connections, and fresh inspiration on how to make your research matter. Come walk, talk, and think impact where it actually happens. We will meet at 3:00 p.m. in the Broad Street Atrium of the Convention Center. Please arrive 10 minutes early because participation is limited to 40 people.
