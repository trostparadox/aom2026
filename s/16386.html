Session overview

Talent management (TM) has been a prominent research topic for more than three decades, yet scholars continue to struggle with explaining how TM is conceptualized, implemented, and made effective across different organizational contexts. As a result, TM research remains fragmented and dominated by insights derived from large organizations, raising concerns about the transferability and relevance of prevailing models across diverse organizational contexts. This PDW responds to these critiques by explicitly foregrounding organizational context as a core element in TM research. It brings together scholars examining TM across a range of organizational contexts, including large organizations, SMEs, family firms, public sector organizations, and firms at different growth stages. Through short presentations, interactive discussions, and small-group exchanges, the workshop seeks to advance theory development, methodological reflection, and future research on contextualized TM.
