Responsibility Under Algorithms: A Double-Edged Sword in Human–AI Collaboration

MOC
Xiaomeng Li (Liaoning Technical University); Lixia Niu (Liaoning Technical University)
Although the application of artificial intelligence has substantially enhanced the efficiency and sophistication of organizational decision-making, its deep integration has also reshaped the boundaries of human–AI labor division and responsibility, creating new tensions for highly responsible employees. As a result, responsibility may function as a double-edged sword in human–AI collaborative decision-making. Drawing on role theory, this study examines when and how employee responsibility enhances or undermines the quality of human–AI collaborative decision-making in AI-enabled work contexts.Using three-wave matched survey data from 512 employees who routinely work with artificial intelligence, we find that responsibility operates through two distinct role-based pathways. On the one hand, responsibility improves human–AI collaborative decision-making quality by enhancing role breadth self-efficacy, which facilitates boundary-spanning integration and careful scrutiny of AI outputs. On the other hand, responsibility can also increase role overload, leading to resource depletion and cognitive narrowing, thereby impairing collaborative decision-making quality.Moreover, organizational dehumanization climate serves as a critical boundary condition for these dual effects. When dehumanization climate is low, responsibility is more likely to be experienced as a meaningful resource for role expansion, strengthening its positive effects while attenuating its negative effects. Conversely, when dehumanization climate is high, responsibility is more likely to be experienced as externally imposed pressure, weakening its enabling effects and amplifying its depleting effects.By elucidating the role-based mechanisms and contextual boundaries through which responsibility shapes human–AI collaborative decision-making quality, this study advances research on human–AI collaboration and responsibility management and offers practical insights for organizations seeking to integrate artificial intelligence in ways that enhance decision quality without overburdening employees.
