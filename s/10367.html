Session overview

This hands-on Professional Development Workshop (PDW) investigates how artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) can extend the frontier of research in organizational theory, strategy, and international management. Building on our 2025 PDW—which attracted over 350 participants and featured 12 speakers in a four-hour, highly interactive format—we return with an updated program that showcases research-grade AI tools, including large language models (LLMs), multimodal models, and agentic systems tailored to scholarly work. The PDW starts from substantive questions central to OMT and related divisions (e.g., innovation, knowledge diffusion, discourse, labor markets), and then demonstrates how AI methods can help address these questions. Participants will explore supervised and unsupervised ML for document classification and pattern discovery; ML-enhanced causal inference (e.g., matching); deep learning for measuring complex constructs; and LLM-based coding, simulation, and research workflow support. Throughout, we emphasize evaluation, reproducibility, and ethical governance, including how to benchmark LLM-based coders against humans, log prompts and outputs, and document AI use transparently. Designed for scholars ranging from AI novices to experienced ML users, the PDW combines short conceptual talks, live demonstrations, and roundtable-based paper development. By the end of the workshop, participants will (1) have at least one AI-enabled research design or analysis plan tailored to their own project; (2) understand how to benchmark ML/LLM-based measures or coders against human coders and traditional models; and (3) be able to outline a transparent, reproducible AI pipeline that includes basic ethical safeguards.
