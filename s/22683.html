Session overview

This Paper Development Workshop, hosted by the Asia Pacific Journal of Management (APJM), supports scholars seeking to advance research on management and organizations in the Asia Pacific context. Participants will receive developmental feedback from experienced editors and scholars on theory building, methodological rigor, contribution positioning, and alignment with APJM’s scope. The workshop aims to strengthen manuscripts and enhance their ability to publish through constructive dialogue and mentorship. Scholars interested in participating should submit a full paper or extended abstract by June 15, 2026 to apjmpdw2026@gmail.com. Submissions should clearly articulate the study’s theoretical contribution and relevance to the Asia Pacific region.
