Session overview

Pre-registration required. The Operations and Supply Chain Management (OSCM) Division is providing an exclusive walking tour of the Philadelphia Navy Yard on Tuesday, August 4, 2026 at 10:30am. Discover one of Philadelphia’s most unique and historically rich locations with a guided tour of the Philadelphia Navy Yard. As one of the nation’s first U.S. Navy shipyards, it played a critical role in American naval history for nearly two centuries and served as a major hub of shipbuilding and innovation. Today, the Navy Yard blends historic architecture with cutting-edge industry and is home to more than 120 companies and thousands of employees across sectors such as technology, life sciences, and advanced manufacturing. The guided walking tour will offer a behind-the-scenes look at how this former industrial shipyard has evolved into a model for urban redevelopment and innovation. Meeting location: The Navy Yard - 4500 S Broad Street Philadelphia, PA 19112
