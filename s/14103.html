People

Participants: Cynthia S. Wang Northwestern University; Nir Halevy Stanford University; Dawei Wang The University of Hong Kong; Zikang Dai The University of Hong Kong

Session overview

This PDW introduces innovative classroom tools for teaching topics in organizational behavior, including navigating organizational hierarchy, power and status, team dynamics, and innovation. A moderator and two presenters with extensive expertise in developing and using experiential teaching tools will share cutting-edge approaches to OB teaching. Each presenter will introduce a classroom tool they have designed, provide an interactive demonstration of the tool, and explain how it is used in their courses. The workshop emphasizes research-grounded methods that help make complex organizational processes more tangible for students. Dr. Nir Halevy (The Jagdeep and Roshni Singh Professor of Organizational Behavior at Stanford University) will present Promotion!, a strategic card game that simulates the interactive dynamics by which individuals gain, maintain, and lose power and status within organizational hierarchies. Participants will learn how social influence shapes hierarchical outcomes, relationships, and reputations. Dr. Dawei (David) Wang (Assistant Professor of Innovation and Information Management and Management and Strategy, HKU Business School, The University of Hong Kong) will present the Beat the Bot simulation, an AI-enabled teaching tool designed to compare creative performance across individuals, teams, and AI systems. The simulation makes the differences in creative production between humans and AI directly observable. Dr. Cynthia Wang (Clinical Professor of Management & Organizations and Executive Director of the Dispute Resolution and Research Center and Kellogg Teaching Resources at the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University) will moderate the session and facilitate audience discussion and Q&A.
