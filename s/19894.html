Bringing Order to the Mess: The Role of Innovation Management Systems

Session overview

Firms increasingly rely on external knowledge sources to support innovation activities, yet the performance implications of such openness are neither uniform nor guaranteed. While engaging with external actors can enhance firms’ innovative potential, it also increases coordination and integration complexity, thereby making the performance implications of openness contingent on how innovation activities are organized and managed. This study examines the relationship between Openness to Innovation and firms’ Operational performance, and investigates how Innovation Management Systems, understood as structured and systematized approaches to managing innovation activities, shape firms’ ability to translate openness into operational performance implications.
