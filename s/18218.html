Session overview

Today’s political climate is reshaping work: it changes how coworkers interpret each other’s behavior, what information they share or avoid, and whether they trust and cooperate with one another. This symposium brings together five complementary studies that map these dynamics across relationships, attitudes, and everyday interaction: how people manage uncertainty with suspected partisans, how perceptions can harden into friction around organizational equity efforts, and how concerns about backlash can inhibit collaboration. The symposium also points to what organizations can do next, showcasing evidence on interventions that shift relationships over time and communication patterns that reveal how even well-meaning feedback can influence how people engage across partisan divides.
