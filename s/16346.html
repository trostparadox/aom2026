Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) provides a developmental and dialogical space for emerging scholars and early-career faculty seeking to publish research that engages in the intersections Management, Spirituality, Religion, and Social Issues in Management and Entrepreneurship. Bringing together editors and associate editors from leading journals in these fields and their intersections, the PDW explores how diverse ontological and epistemological orientations, particularly those grounded in spiritual, religious, indigenous, and contemplative traditions inviting the unknown or the beyond, can be articulated with rigor and scholarly credibility influencing social issues in management and entrepreneurship and business management. Through moderated exchange and open dialogue, participants gain insight into editorial expectations, peer-review processes, and publication pathways, while reflecting on how inner development goals, ethical orientation, entrepreneurial endeavours and scholarly identity shape research design and contribution. The PDW strengthens epistemic reflexivity, scholarly confidence, and inclusive participation in MSR and allied fields.
