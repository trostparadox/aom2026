Session overview

In recent years, algorithms have been used as directories that delegate work and maximize the value of labor and workers (Kellogg et al., 2020). Algorithms can also be used as a knowledge repository and help experts make decisions in various fields such as medicine, chemicals, and finance (Bussone et al., 2015; Chan & Zary, 2019; Henrique et al., 2019). Current research on AI decision support primarily examines the effectiveness of algorithm-assisted human decision making, often in contrast to decisions made by individuals without such assistance (Bansal et al., 2019; Hosny et al., 2018; Silver et al., 2017; Yang et al., 2019). Artificial intelligence (AI) can improve human efficiency and decision-making accuracy, reducing errors in specific tasks (Dwivedi et al., 2021; Miller, 2018). While research has documented AI's effects at the individual level, the mechanisms by which artificial intelligence powers team and organizational performance remain poorly understood. This session showcases four scholarly presentations exploring how AI integration reshapes organizational design and coordination structures, as well as the challenges and optimal strategies for incorporating AI into team environments.
