Session overview

This research-focussed PDW explores how academia can strengthen its role in social value creation within and across ecosystems. Together with the participants, this PDW aims to co-create a research agenda at the intersection of universities, entrepreneurship, and social value creation. The workshop is structured around five areas: “Resources, Actors, Resources, Ecosystem engagement, Outcomes, and Impact.” Designed for members of the SIM, ENT, and ONE divisions because the PDW focuses on entrepreneurial approaches to social and environmental issues, emphasizing stakeholder interactions and ecosystem perspectives. Expert panellists will synthesize existing research and practice in each of the six areas, highlighting what is currently known and where important gaps remain. Building on these inputs, participants will engage in moderated roundtable discussions to identify promising research questions. The PDW will conclude with a discussion to explain the main takeaways per table.
