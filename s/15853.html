Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces Learning by Teaching, an innovative pedagogical approach situated within the broader Guided-Flexible Education (GFE) framework. Learning by Teaching reimagines the role of students from passive recipients of content to active, accountable co-teachers who engage in deep self-study with the expectation that they may be required to teach all or part of a forthcoming session. Unlike conventional student presentations, Learning by Teaching removes pre-selection and predictability. All students are required to prepare the session content in advance, while the educator selects-immediately prior to the session-which students will teach specific components. This design promotes continuous engagement, equitable preparation, academic rigour, and authentic learning accountability. Teaching by students may also form part of summative assessment, with structured formative feedback provided by the educator, who remains responsible for academic coherence and correction. This PDW is designed as an interactive How-to Workshop that enables management educators to (1) understand the pedagogical logic, benefits, and challenges of Learning by Teaching; (2) experience the approach through live simulation; and (3) co-design an implementation plan tailored to their own institutional contexts. Participants will leave with practical tools, assessment strategies, facilitation techniques, and evaluation frameworks that can be immediately applied across undergraduate, postgraduate, and executive management education. The workshop directly addresses MED priorities around pedagogical innovation, learning design, faculty development, inclusivity, and impact-driven management education, while offering a scalable and research-informed model for transforming teaching and learning practices.
