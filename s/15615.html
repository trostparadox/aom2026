Session overview

Effective leadership is foundational to thriving healthcare organizations, yet many leaders struggle to translate evidence-based behaviors into daily practice. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces participants to three empirically supported behaviors from the Full Range of Leadership Model: Idealized Influence, Individualized Consideration, and Contingent Reward. These leadership behaviors are strongly associated with improved staff well-being, engagement, morale, and retention in healthcare environments, making them critical competencies for practitioners, educators, and researchers committed to advancing healthcare management. This PDW will guide participants in developing actionable best practices tailored to their own settings through small-group discussions, structured breakout activities, and participant teach-backs to foster meaningful engagement and peer learning. Participants collaboratively identify contextual factors, implementation challenges, and practical solutions for integrating transformational and transactional leadership behaviors into complex healthcare organizations. By combining evidence-based leadership insights with experiential learning, this PDW equips healthcare leaders, early-career scholars, and educators with concrete strategies to cultivate environments where professionals can thrive.
