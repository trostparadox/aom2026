Session overview

This workshop celebrates the 10th Anniversary of Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA). We begin with a short celebration and reflections on NCA from leading organizational research methods scholars who had an impact on its growth: Herman Aguinis, Peer Fiss, and James LeBreton. Afterwards, participants will learn: 1. What NCA is, and how and why it has evolved over the past 10 years, reaching its current status as a valued addition to the methodological toolbox. 2. The core concepts of NCA and how NCA can be applied as a standalone method or in combination with widely used approaches such as SEM and QCA. 3. Future directions for high-quality NCA, with a special focus on newly developed tools to be published in a forthcoming book (Dul, 2026), including a decision tree to support the development of necessity hypotheses, new model-fit metrics (multiple indicators), and practical guidance for interpreting and reporting NCA results (e.g., table templates and a reporting checklist). This interactive session familiarizes scholars with NCA and these new tools. Following the anniversary reflections, Part 1 provides an introduction/refresher on NCA. Part 2 presents the new tools designed to support high-quality application of NCA. It includes a small-group discussion about how to formulate necessity hypotheses using the new decision tool. In Part 3, participants are invited to discuss the application of the method to their own research questions. Celebration of 10 years of NCA at the start of the PDW with special guests Herman Aquinis, Peer Fiss, and James Lebreton and new book presentation.
