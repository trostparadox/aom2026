Session overview

This MED-sponsored Professional Development Workshop celebrates the inaugural Award for Courageous Experimentation and Innovation in Management Education by showcasing four finalists whose bold pedagogical experiments offer valuable insights regardless of outcome. This interactive how-to workshop equips participants with practical frameworks and actionable strategies for conducting and documenting their own pedagogical experiments. Participants will engage with finalists presenting with radical transparency, participate in facilitated roundtable discussions extracting cross-cutting insights and best practices, and develop personal experimentation plans using provided tools. The workshop addresses a critical field gap: management education reluctance to share what does not work, despite failed experiments containing invaluable pedagogical knowledge. Participants leave with skill-building frameworks for designing productive experiments, evidence-based tools for extracting insights from unexpected outcomes, practical strategies for building institutional support for risk-taking, and connections to a professional community committed to honest pedagogical dialogue. The session concludes by announcing the award winner and launching next year competition. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
