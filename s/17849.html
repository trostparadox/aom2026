Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop addresses a gap in management education: teaching students to navigate strategic partnerships with dominant big-tech AI firms. Seven firms now control AI infrastructure that organizations must access through partnerships, yet existing courses on alliances and platform strategy assume balanced power dynamics. Traditional frameworks inadequately prepare students for negotiations where one party controls essential infrastructure. The workshop equips educators with case materials, diagnostic frameworks, and experiential exercises grounded in recent research. Participants learn to apply polarity management theory as a pedagogical tool, helping students distinguish problems from polarities. The workshop also introduces the AI tech stack model for understanding how big-tech firms create value across seven layers of AI infrastructure. Through collaborative case development and teaching demonstration, participants create adaptable materials for MBA, executive education, and undergraduate courses. Participants leave with a polarity worksheet, case materials, and facilitation guides. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
