Session overview

A Google search of the phrase “workplace narcissism” yields over one million search results – from discussing how to deal with narcissists at work (Morin, 2022) to national organizations explaining the importance of managing narcissism at work (Palmieri, 2020). Clearly, there is researcher and practitioner interest in narcissism in the workplace. Despite the extensive focus on narcissism for the past several decades; the field is still fragmented. This literature review suggests that narcissism is a misunderstood construct in which researchers have only begun to scratch the surface. Additionally, studies are lop-sided and highly favor the study of leaders and CEOs, while largely ignoring followers, employees, or teams. Thus, the purpose of this critical review is to provide an overview of narcissism in organizations. Papers included in this review were found by searching various databases such as Google Scholar, EBSCO, and JSTOR for articles with keywords containing: “narcissism” and “narcissistic”. Papers were only included if they contained empirical analyses and tested a specific hypothesis that contained narcissism.
