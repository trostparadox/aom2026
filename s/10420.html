Session overview

Kickstart your day with a morning run! There’s no better way to refresh your senses and get the blood pumping than getting up early and running with like-minded scholars. The run is coordinated by the OSCM Division, but is open to all AOM members, regardless of your division. Depending on the desires of the group, we will either do one run together or lead a shorter and a longer run. We meet at: Convention Center, Arch Street Entrance Outside Hall F - 100 Level, Sunday, Aug 2 2026 at 7:00AM!
