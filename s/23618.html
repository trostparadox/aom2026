Session overview

This session focuses on how gender shapes advancement, leadership, and career development across organizational settings. The papers address female talent development, work-life programs, within-gender faultlines in executive advancement, servant leadership, and leadership schemas affecting women followers' fit. Collectively, they show that gender inequality in careers is sustained not only by formal barriers but also by subtle organizational structures, beliefs, and expectations that shape who advances and who remains on the margins.
