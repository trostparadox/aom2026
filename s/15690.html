Session overview

The book series Research in Organizational Change and Development (ROCD) has for nearly four decades provided a distinctive platform for advancing engaged scholarship at the intersection of theory, practice, and societal relevance. Building on the long-standing tradition of ROCD symposia at the AoM, this symposium brings together authors of eight ROCD volume 32 chapters to engage the community in reflective and forward-looking conversations around central themes including organizational health, viability and adaptation across levels, purpose, direction, and fragility, agency in interdependent systems, and the future trajectories of OD and change scholarship. An invitation will also be extended to the authors of earlier contributors of the ROCD book series.
