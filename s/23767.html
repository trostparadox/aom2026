Entrepreneurial Training Makes Incubated Startup Resilient! An Experimental Chain Mediation Insight

ENT
Tehreem Fatima; Ali Tariq (The University of Lahore); Ahmad Raza Bilal (University of Sargodha); Muhammad Kashif Imran (The Islamia University of Bahawalpur); Sobia Shabeer (Mukabbir University of Science & Technology, Gujrat, Pakistan)
One of the key services provided by Business Incubation Centres (BICs) is training and coaching their incubatees; however, there is a lack of research on the development and effectiveness of training in this sector. Apart from much researched impacts on intentions and early stage survival, this study found that individual entrepreneurial orientation (IEO) training delivers a psychological mindset for startup resilience among incubatees via entrepreneurial alertness and resource orchestration. The research used a randomised control field experiment of the business incubatees operating in universities in Lahore, Pakistan. The data were gathered from 131 incubatees (training group=65, control group=66) via multi-stage cluster sampling. The data were analysed via multivariate analysis of variance and chain mediation analysis. It was demonstrated in the results that IEO training improves the resilience of incubated startups via making them more alert and enabling them to orchestrate resources.
