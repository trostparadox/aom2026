CEO STEM Background and Corporate Intelligentization: An Empirical Study of New Ventures

TIM
zhaohua xiao (Central South University); Lu Zhang; Dianwen Tang
In the era of artificial intelligentization, enterprises urgently need to apply intelligent technologies and equipment. CEO's professional background can influence corporate strategic decisions and development direction. We explore the impact of CEO’s STEM background on the intelligentization of new startups listed on Chinese A-share from 2010 to 2020. The foundings releval that CEO’s STEM background has a positive impact on the intelligentization of new startups, and R&D investment positively moderate this focal relationship, and the CEO's R&D functional background positively moderate the moderating effect of R&D investment. This study deepens the upper echelons theory, and enriches the research on the influencing factors of new venture intelligentization.
