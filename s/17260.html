Session overview

The goal of this proposed symposium is to interrogate broad-based employee ownership from initiation to practice to policy implementation to worker financial outcomes. The presenters in this symposium intend to engage their audience in a learning arc that starts with two qualitative papers, which give insights into the motivation to sell a business to employees and the challenges of creating an organizational identity in an employee-owned company. The third paper makes a case for the inclusion of workers with disabilities in companies that are employee-owned, and the final paper reports on long-term financial outcomes for workers of color in employee-owned companies.
