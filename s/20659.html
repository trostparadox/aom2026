Session overview

The rapid diffusion of generative AI in management education has increased access to information and expert-like responses. However, many educational applications still emphasize answer genera-tion rather than fostering learning, judgment, and reflection. This Professional Development Work-shop (PDW) introduces a structured learning design that positions AI-supported expert interviews as a self-regulated learning strategy rather than a tool for solution provision. Grounded in self-regulated learning theory, the workshop presents a process-oriented framework that guides learners through goal setting, knowledge gap identification, engagement with AI-simulated expert input, critical evaluation, and structured reflection. Participants experience how AI-supported expert in-terviews can foster sensemaking, decision-making under uncertainty, and metacognitive awareness. The PDW is designed as a highly interactive, hands-on session combining short conceptual inputs, guided simulations, and reflection. Participants apply the framework to a concrete management challenge and reflect on both content-related insights and their learning processes. The workshop targets management educators, doctoral students, and education-focused researchers interested in pedagogical innovation and AI-enabled learning design. Participants leave with a transferable framework and practical tools for integrating AI responsibly to enhance learning quality rather than efficiency. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
