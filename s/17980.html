Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) brings together scholars and educators interested in developing original, classroom-tested teaching cases for inclusion in a double-blind, peer-reviewed edited casebook on Technology and Innovation Management, currently under contract with Edward Elgar Publishing. Designed as an active working session rather than a passive information briefing, the PDW is intended for faculty who teach or research technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and related domains, and who seek a rigorous, supported pathway to publishing cases with a leading academic press. Led by the editor, the workshop introduces the intellectual framing and pedagogical objectives of the casebook, clarifies editorial and review expectations for publishable cases and teaching notes, and demystifies the double-blind review process used for selection. Participants will engage directly in refining case ideas through structured peer roundtables organized by thematic fit, with guided feedback focused on narrative construction, pedagogical positioning, and instructor usability. Emphasis is placed on transforming authentic classroom material into cases that meet scholarly standards while remaining teachable across diverse institutional contexts. The PDW is particularly well-suited for faculty seeking to convert teaching or consulting innovation into peer-reviewed scholarly output, junior scholars looking to build a publication record through edited volumes, and experienced case writers interested in contributing to a contemporary, methodologically grounded collection focused on technology and innovation management.
