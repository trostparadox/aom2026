How AI-Powered Capabilities Enhance the Collaborative Innovation Performance of Manufacturing Firms?

TIM
Qing Fan (Nanjing University); Shuqi Li
Research on the factors influencing manufacturing firms’ collaborative innovation in digital business ecosystems (DBEs) remains limited. Based on profiting from innovation (PFI) theory, this study examines whether and how AI-powered capabilities enhance manufacturing firms’ collaborative innovation performance, focusing on the mediating role of digital complementary assets (breadth and depth) and the moderating role of AI governance between these assets and collaborative innovation performance. Using structural equation modeling on 326 questionnaires from Chinese manufacturing firms, the findings show AI-powered capabilities boost collaborative innovation performance. Digital complementary assets (breadth and depth) positively mediate this relationship, and AI governance positively moderates the link between these assets and collaborative innovation performance. This study provides a PFI-based theoretical framework for DBE firms, synthesizing complementary assets, appropriability mechanisms, and dynamic capabilities to explain value creation and capture via AI-powered capabilities, digital complementary assets, and AI governance. It also offers managerial insights for firms building AI capabilities and strengthening competitive advantages through collaborative innovation.
