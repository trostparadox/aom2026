Session overview

This year’s STR division’s teaching PDW series tackles the importance of having fun while teaching. When educators enjoy their teaching, they are more engaged, less burned out, and better able to create emotionally supportive learning environments, which in turn improves students’ academic and non-academic outcomes. In the first half of the PDW, the participants will learn from award-winning professors on four teaching skills essential to business school teaching: maintaining one’s enthusiasm while teaching the core (Tomasz Obloj); creating your own teaching materials (Metin Sengul), developing your own elective (Matthew Bidwell) and teaching using the case method (Mary Tripsas). In the second half of the PDW, the participants will join breakout tables to workshop and collaborate with the panellists and other attendees on the topics of interest.
