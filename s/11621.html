Session overview

The aim of the present PDW is to explore the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in entrepreneurship education in Africa, and particularly sub-Saharan Africa. Although Africa needs entrepreneurship education, there is a scarcity of well-trained entrepreneurship educators. Hence, turning to technology and particularly artificial intelligence could provide an opportunity to reduce this gap. Consequently, the present PDW explores the role of AI in entrepreneurship education. In doing so, it aims to foster a fruitful discussion and further empirical research on AI and entrepreneurship education in sub-Saharan Africa. The deliverables of this PDW are twofold: 1) contribute to the Entrepreneurship Education & Pedagogy Special Issue on Entrepreneurship Education and Generative Artificial Intelligence; 2) Organize an AFAM webinar on artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship education in Africa.
