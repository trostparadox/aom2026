When Goals Lead Them Astray: Institutional Logics Configurations and Clean-Tech Start-Up Resilience

Session overview

This study examines how configurations of institutional logics shape the resilience of clean-tech start-ups facing diverse adversities. Integrating institutional logics and organizational resilience literatures, we argue that resilience is not a uniform capability but depends on the fit between a venture’s dominant logics and the type of adversity encountered. Using a mixed-method design that combines process tracing with coincidence analysis, we analyze clean-tech start-ups characterized by varying combinations of market, sustainability, community, and profession logics. Preliminary findings show that specific logic configurations systematically enable or constrain resilient responses in sales- and operations-related crises. The study advances a contextualized view of start-up resilience with implications for founders, investors, and policy makers.
