Navigating the Aftermath: CEO Network Renewal and Firm Survival After Natural Disasters

ONE
Jiabin Zhang (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam)
Despite the asserted importance of a CEO’s external network ties for firm survival after natural disasters, the theoretical understanding of which kinds of social capital are more or less valuable remains limited. In this study, we examine the contrasting influences of a CEO’s (1) pre- versus post-disaster network ties and (2) intra- versus extra-industry network ties on the odds of firm survival after a natural disaster. Using Cox hazard models of firm survival after the 2008 Sichuan earthquake, we find that CEOs’ pre-disaster networks reduce a firm’s post-disaster survival likelihood. Conversely, CEOs’ post-disaster and intra-industry networks increase a firm’s survival prospects. Our study has important implications for theories of firm resilience and social capital in the context of natural disasters.
