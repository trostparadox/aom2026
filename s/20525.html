When the Watercooler Runs Dry: Communication Pathways Linking Remote Work to Team Effectiveness

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 13
ODC

People

Authors: Beatrice Schuldt University of Münster; Manuel J. Vaulont Northeastern University; Julia Backmann University of Münster

Session overview

While remote work benefits individual employees, its effects on team functioning remain underexplored. Drawing on Media Richness Theory, we examine how Team Remote Work Intensity (RWI) and within-team RWI Dispersion influence team effectiveness through face-to-face and digital communication. Analyzing 105 teams across 56 organizations, we find that both constructs positively influence team effectiveness directly yet negatively affect face-to-face communication frequency. Contrary to expectations, neither increases digital communication frequency. Face-to-face communication mediates the RWI– effectiveness relationship, whereas digital communication does not function as a compensatory mechanism. These findings challenge the assumption that individual-level benefits aggregate to teams, highlighting that digital communication cannot substitute for informal interactions essential for team alignment. We contribute by introducing RWI Dispersion as a boundary condition and demonstrating the indispensability of physical co-presence for team effectiveness.
