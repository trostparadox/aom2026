Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) provides a forum for scholars to engage in open discussion surrounding positionality & reflexivity in management research, with a focus on how researchers’ identities shape their research contexts and pursuits. The workshop will explore how lived experience influences the research process, from topic selection and methodological choice to data interpretation and publication. We examine how more explicit and intentional engagement with positionality can contribute to more open, responsible management scholarship by providing context for the voices amplified through research. While reflexivity is more commonly addressed in qualitative research, this PDW extends the conversation to mixed-methods and quantitative approaches, including issues of sampling, analysis, and interpretation. Participants will also have the opportunity to examine how the researcher's positionality influences how research is received by those who consume it. The session will begin with a panel of scholars who employ diverse methodologies and vary in their engagement with positionality. They will share insights and strategies for reflecting on and communicating how lived experience impacts our work. The panel will be followed by an interactive small-group activity in which participants identify and share how positionality & reflexivity influence their own research agenda. This workshop aims to illuminate how identity & experience add value to management research across methodological traditions and to encourage scholars to more fully examine and articulate how their worldviews shape their work.
