How AI and Big Data Create Innovation Outcomes: The Mediating Role of Knowledge Integration

Author: Keontaek Oh Keimyung University

Session overview

Artificial intelligence (AI) and big data have recently emerged as key digital transformation technologies that fundamentally transform firms’ innovation activities and operational decision-making processes. Firms are increasingly leveraging AI and big data to improve the accuracy of demand forecasting, to reduce uncertainty in new product development, and to enhance production and logistics efficiency. However, the adoption of digital technologies does not always lead to superior innovation performance, and even among firms that have adopted similar technologies, there are significant differences in innovation performance. This suggests that organizational and knowledge mechanisms play a crucial role in the process of converting digital transformation technologies into innovation performance. Prior research has focused on the direct effects of AI and big data adoption on innovation performance and has provided limited insights into the processes through which these technologies create value. In particular, empirical research on how firms integrate internal and external knowledge and transform it into practical insights that support innovation is not sufficient. To address this gap, this research investigates the mediating role of knowledge integration in the relationship between AI and big data utilization and innovation performance.
