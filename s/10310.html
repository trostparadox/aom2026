Session overview

The emergence of generative artificial intelligence (genAI) has the potential to transform organizations and society. A central conduit for these transformations is the way genAI alters and interacts with human, especially managerial cognition. However, ambiguities and tensions between different lines of reasoning arise at this nexus. Addressing them will be critical to harness the potentially complementary strengths of human and artificial intelligence. This PDW aims to explicate and discuss the status quo and unanswered questions on how genAI interacts with human thinking. The PDW introduces a framework that structures four core tensions in current reasoning about genAI and managerial cognition, related to humans’ bounded rationality, genAI’s agentic abilities, dynamics in human-genAI interaction, and the possibility of homogenous outcomes of genAI and managerial cognition. By outlining these competing perspectives and providing a forum for discussion with distinguished speakers, the PDW informs new research projects among the audience. The PDW combines a lightning talk, expert statements, an integrative panel discussion, and interactive breakout sessions to interrogate these tensions and identify their limits, and a collective synthesizing summary. Through this structured design, participants engage with divergent viewpoints and develop a shared agenda for future research on genAI and cognition.
