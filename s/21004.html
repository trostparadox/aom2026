From Green Performance Management to Engaged Employees: Understanding the Role of Commitment

Authors: Sajra Hodžic International University of Sarajevo; Amila Ahmethodžic International University of Sarajevo; Emil Knezovic International University of Sarajevo

Session overview

The purpose of this study is to examine whether green performance management is related to employee engagement and whether organizational commitment mediates this relationship. The study surveyed 199 employees from various industries in Bosnia and Herzegovina, using convenience sampling. Structural equation modeling was used to test the hypothesized direct and indirect relationships. The findings indicate a significant link between green performance management and employee engagement, with emotional attachment playing an important role, as affective commitment emerged as a strong mediator. With multiple organizational commitment dimensions as potential mediators, the study adopts a more complex approach to the green performance-engagement relationship while contributing to the limited literature in transitional economies such as Bosnia and Herzegovina.
