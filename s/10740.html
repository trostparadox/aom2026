Session overview

The purpose of this PDW is to bring together experts across social movements, technology emergence, and social evaluation scholarship to rethink hype’s key assumptions beyond the traditional boom-and-bust cycle and reconceptualize it as a socially constructed process that creates lasting narratives, expectations, and consequences even after the “peak of inflated expectations” has passed. We aim to spark dialogue among both panelists and audience members to understand it through a field-evolution perspective and to explore how organizations can strategically respond to the uncertainty and amplified attention. This PDW will consist of two parts, inclusive of a panel discussion and a roundtable paper session.
