Strategic Decision-Making for Internationalization: An AI-Augmented Predictor of Success

Session overview

Management requires strategy with a forward-looking, adaptable lens to effectively internationalize from conception, start-up, or transformation. With a forward-looking, adaptable lens to strategic development, ongoing strategic decision-making (SDM) also requires swift shifts, often with limited or even unknowable information. Traditional models of internationalization have relied on predictable outcomes (Chetty et al., 2024) and are thus ill-fitted to inform SDM in a rapidly evolving context. In this same context of disruption, the underlying technology that international management (IM) researchers experiment with today offers new insights, faster. This paper leverages artificial intelligence (AI) to address a critical gap in IM literature: How can AI-enabled Natural Language Processing (NLP) models support predicting successful internationalization during the strategic decision-making process?
