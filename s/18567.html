Session overview

This symposium examines how individuals with marginalized social identities navigate identity management across digital and leadership contexts, exploring responses to threats, praise, and fairness. As organizations grow more diverse and adopt new technologies, understanding how employees manage multiple social identities becomes increasingly critical. Drawing on social identity theories and intersectionality research, the three papers in this symposium illuminate the complex strategies employees use to construct positive identities while navigating organizational demands.
