Investigating Full-Time Employees’ Gig Work Preferences From An Identity Development Perspective

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 7
OB

People

Authors: Linwei Gan The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Xueqi Chen The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Huiyao Liao The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Kenneth S Law The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Session overview

Individuals navigate between gig work and full-time jobs, yet our understanding of their choices remains limited. Specifically, we lack clarity on when individuals focus exclusively on their full-time jobs, engage in side hustles, or transition entirely to gig work. Draw on the identity development perspective, we argue that employees’ varied work preferences and behaviors stem from different identity development statuses, influenced by how job meaningfulness impacts the identity commitment and identity exploration that constitute these statuses. Using latent profile analysis on data from 393 employees, we validated job meaningfulness as a predictor and identified four identity patterns that each forecast distinct gig career choice.
