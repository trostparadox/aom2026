Who Engages and Why? Developing Student Typologies in Entrepreneurship Education

Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 4
MED

People

Authors: Meg Marcozzi University of Delaware; Stephanie Elizabeth Raible University of Delaware

Session overview

This poster examines how entrepreneurship educators conceptualize different types of students engaging in entrepreneurship education. Drawing on qualitative interview data from 32 educators across U.S. higher education institutions, the study develops seven student typologies based on educators’ perceptions of student motivation, engagement, and entrepreneurial orientation. Using ideal-type analysis, the findings highlight how expectancy, value, and cost considerations shape educator interpretations of student behavior. The typologies contribute to research on entrepreneurship education by foregrounding educator perspectives and offer practical implications for curriculum design, co-curricular programming, and student support.
