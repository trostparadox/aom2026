Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) conceptualizes work and research productivity as a process that can consistently and continuously be improved through learning. Thus, the main goal of this PDW is to help develop an awareness of the processes used by prolific faculty. Although most PDWs utilize esteemed senior scholars as panelists, we propose a unique model that highlights the perspectives of peer junior faculty members with established track records of productivity. These junior faculty members will share their work habits and how they go about their work (e.g., time allocation, co-authorships, writing blocks, and revise-and-resubmit strategies) as well as other “life” factors that influence their productivity (e.g., exercise, work/family balance). After their individual presentations, we will hold group panel discussions where participants are able to have conversations with each of the panelists. The session will culminate in a keynote address from an esteemed senior scholar, offering broader reflections on the topic. We encourage Ph.D. students, as well as faculty across all stages of their careers—Assistant, Associate, and Full—to join this session, to collectively reflect on, learn from, and share productivity-related strategies. This PDW is organized by members of the OB Division’s Making Connections Committee (MCC).
