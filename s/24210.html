Session overview

The Department of Management, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) will host a reception at the 86th Annual Meeting of the Academy of Management on Sunday, August 2, 2026 from 6 to 8 p.m. at Philadelphia Marriott Downtown. The Department advances the vision of CUHK Business School, “To develop global business leaders for the Asian Century”, through transformative educational programmes, impactful research and knowledge, and our presence and contact with the community and society. For more information, please visit https://www.bschool.cuhk.edu.hk/departments/management/.
