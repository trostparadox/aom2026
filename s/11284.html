Session overview

Many management scholars are interested in taking their career to a new culture and context. In spite of increasing opportunities to teach and conduct organizational research at business schools around the globe, first-hand information on business schools outside of North America and Europe can be hard to come by. This PDW is for junior and senior OB scholars seeking more information regarding academic careers at reputable business schools in the Asia Pacific region. It brings together six panelists with experience at schools in Australia, mainland China, Hong Kong, Singapore, South Korea, and Japan, including faculty at different career stages, and ‘international hires” and “local” faculty with experience in international recruitment. The panel will provide information, highlight challenges, and share distinctive features for academic careers in each country/region, including tacit knowledge and unspoken rules that applicants should be aware of. Topics for discussion include: What does the hiring and job application process look like? How are faculty evaluated, and what are career stages and promotion procedures? What are key challenges that international faculty experience in adapting to the national culture and university system? What is often surprising for international faculty? What are norms of professional conduct (e.g., dos and don’ts in presenting oneself and one’s work)? The PDW includes individual presentations by panelists, country-specific roundtable discussions, and a closing Q&A and sharing of highlights.
