Session overview

Over the past decades, research on compensation has expanded our knowledge about how compensation impacts various stakeholders of organizations, including employees, managers, and society. A stakeholder perspective allows us to organize compensation research around how compensation contributes to value creation and how it enables different stakeholders to capture value, ultimately shaping organizations’ ability to build and sustain competitive advantage. In this Professional Development Workshop (PDW), we seek to take stock of the current state of compensation research and identify promising avenues for future research through the stakeholder lens. Our PDW is structured around three key themes: (1) the role of compensation for value creation; (2) the role of compensation for value capture by stakeholders; and (3) cross-level integration and the emergence of a human capital resource. Our PDW will feature panel discussions centered on these themes, followed by interactive roundtable sessions that offer attendees the opportunity to engage with panelists in more focused and personalized discussions.
