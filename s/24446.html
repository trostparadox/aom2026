Maturity Model for Remanufacturing Through the Lens of Paradox Theory

ONE
Piera Centobelli (University of Naples Federico II); Daniel Luiz De Mattos Nascimento (University of Barcelona); Giuditta Pagnotto Mogrovejo (University of Naples Federico II)
In an increasingly complex industrial context, paradox theory presents a key interpretative lens for understanding seemingly irreconcilable, yet highly interconnected dynamics of sustainable growth. These tensions are further intensified with the adoption of the circular economy (CE), especially in the remanufacturing sector, which entails organisational, logistical and technical challenges often overlooked by existing maturity models (MMs).This paper proposes a new MM articulated on five levels and structured along three main perspectives: People & Organisational Culture, Supply Chain Optimisation and Sustainable Production. To develop this model, a multi-method methodology was adopted, including a systematic literature review, semi-structured interviews, focus groups and a single case study. The results obtained from the evaluation of the maturity model for remanufacturing (MMR) within an Italian manufacturing company made it possible to introduce a MM capable of supporting companies in identifying paradoxical tensions arising from the implementation of sustainable practices such as remanufacturing, through the innovative lens of paradox theory.
