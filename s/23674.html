Organizer: Abdy Taminsyah MonsoonSIM

Session overview

As AI makes polished student work easier to generate, it becomes increasingly important to observe how learning actually occurs. This workshop explores how structured business simulation activities can make learning more visible through decisions, trade-offs, outcomes, and reflection. Participants will examine practical ways experiential learning can support classroom discussion, assessment, and evidence of learning without adding unnecessary burden to faculty. The session will also briefly consider how emerging Agentic AI may change the way students make, explain, and evaluate decisions. Participants are encouraged to bring a laptop for the hands-on portion of the session.
