Session overview

Business schools face urgent questions about artificial intelligence in teaching: Should we resist AI to preserve authentic learning? Embrace it to prepare students for AI-enabled workplaces? Navigate carefully between these extremes? This interactive workshop moves beyond binary debates to explore responsible, solution-oriented approaches to AI pedagogy. Rather than arguing whether AI should exist in education, we examine how to navigate AI's integration thoughtfully, what risks require mitigation, and how to lead institutional change effectively. This constitutes a fundamental question of responsible management education: What pedagogical choices honor our obligations to students, employers, and society? Three expert practitioners from leading business schools represent complementary positions. One has implemented full AI integration in a required MBA course, embracing AI use even on exams while maintaining educational rigor through transparency and Honor Code principles. Another applies critical research identifying specific risks, while offering strategies for cautious integration that mitigates these concerns. A third brings change management expertise for navigating institutional transformation when faculty hold divergent views. The innovative format combines brief presentations with World Café methodology, where participants rotate through expert-hosted stations. Each expert offers concrete solutions: integration strategies, risk mitigation approaches, and change management frameworks. Participants leave with decision frameworks, implementation tools, discipline- specific examples, and connection to a community navigating these challenges together. By examining multiple solution-oriented perspectives, the workshop models the critical thinking and ethical decision-making we aim to develop in students. Registration is required for this PDW.
