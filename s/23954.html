Industrial Metaverse: A Digital Twin Evolution Perspective and Ecosystem Building Blocks

TIM
Uliana Schmück (University of St.Gallen); Kilian Schmück (University of St.Gallen)
The industrial metaverse—an interoperable, persistent environment for coordinating high-fidelity digital twins—is only beginning to take shape in practice, yet it is rapidly gaining strategic importance for industrial firms. Because the phenomenon is novel and practitioner-led, we lack a coherent understanding of the ecosystem architecture required to scale it. Using an inductive, qualitative design following the Gioia methodology, we conducted 55 semi-structured interviews with software vendors, OEMs, platform architects, and simulation specialists in the automotive production context. Two contributions emerge. First, we theorize a five-stage evolution of digital twins showing how fidelity, behavioral transparency, updatability, interoperability, and composability increase nonlinearly. Second, we identify six foundational building blocks of the industrial metaverse and link them to the technological, trust-based, and economic requirements at each evolution stage. The results offer an actionable roadmap for practitioners and a conceptual foundation for researchers investigating this emerging socio-technical system.
