Partnering With Threat Sources: How GAI Work Identity Threats Promote Employee Innovation

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 11
OB

People

Author: Jie Huang Henan University

Session overview

Based on identity threat process theory literature, this study considers employee collaboration with threat sources, focusing on how to promote employees GAI-enabled innovation. Based on a scenario experimental design utilizing a sample of 202 workers from different companies, this paper find a positive relationship between GAI work identity threat and GAI-enabled innovation and collaborative crafting mediates this relationship. Moreover, this paper examines the moderating role of GAI literacy. GAI literacy positively moderates the correlation between GAI work identity threat and collaborative crafting and moderates the mediation effect of collaborative crafting on the correlation between GAI work identity threat and GAI-enabled innovation.
