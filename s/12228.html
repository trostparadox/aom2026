Session overview

As artificial intelligence capabilities accelerate, fundamental questions emerge about intelligence itself and its relationship to human flourishing (VanderWeele & Teubner, 2025). This symposium examines how human wisdom and AI capabilities can be integrated to support societal wellbeing. We explore intelligence beyond computational definitions to include embodied wisdom, moral imagination, and relational attunement—distinctly human capacities irreducible to algorithms (Mogi, 2024; Zepf & Colley, 2025). Mindfulness practices offer critical metacognitive capacity for discerning when to employ AI tools versus human judgment, potentially inoculating against cognitive biases that AI systems often amplify (Wamsler et al., 2025). Our panelists address whether AI augments human capabilities or creates learned helplessness, how contemplative practices scale from individual to collective intelligence, and what wisdom traditions teach about developing ethical AI that serves human and planetary flourishing. By examining consciousness, intelligence frameworks, and practical organizational applications, this symposium advances theoretical understanding while providing actionable insights for responsible AI integration grounded in mindfulness and human-centered values.
