A Progressive Intensification Model of Emotional Collectivism in AI-Driven Social Media

Session overview

Social media plays a central role in shaping collective emotions. However, the introduction of artificial intelligence (AI) has led to a marked increase in both emotional cohesion and polarization, making this phenomenon more pronounced, persistent, and extreme. Existing research has largely focused on individual level interactions on these social media, offering limited insight into understanding the dynamics of how AI role in the emergence of collective emotions over time. To address this limitation, we develop a progressive intensification model of emotional collectivism in AI-driven social environments. In our model, social media is theorized as an AI-driven informational environment where algorithmic curation and propaganda, through repetition and reinforcement, produce shared interpretations of events while intensifying evaluative distinctions among individuals and groups. As collective emotions emerge via such processes, emotional cohesion and polarization simultaneously take shape and mutually reinforce one another, further exacerbating both dynamics. Our framework redefines AI-driven social media as informational environments of progressive intensification rather than grounds for neutral communication, and advances theorizing on collective emotions by explaining that cohesion and polarization are not mutually exclusive outcomes but rather interconnected dynamics.
