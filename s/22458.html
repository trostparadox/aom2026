Session overview

This consortium is designed for pre-tenure and early post-tenure public management and nonprofit scholars who are preparing for or considering their first sabbatical. The workshop will address how to strategically plan and use sabbatical leave to advance your research agenda, develop new skills, and position yourself for the next stage of your career. Participants will hear from scholars at different career stages who have successfully navigated sabbaticals, as well as department chairs and senior faculty who review sabbatical proposals. We will discuss how to craft compelling sabbatical proposals, set realistic yet ambitious goals, manage the tension between rest and productivity, and return from sabbatical with sustained momentum. Part of the session will be spent in small group discussions addressing specific concerns and challenges related to sabbatical planning.
