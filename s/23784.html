Unlocking Sustainable Performance Through Green HRM: Mediating Roles of AI-Driven Knowledge Manageme

HR
Sonia Najam Shaikh (Jiangsu University); Li Zhen (Jiangsu University); Jan Muhammad Sohu (Sukkur IBA University)
This study examines how Green Human Resource Management Practices (GHRMP) contribute to sustainable performance in manufacturing firms, focusing on the mediating roles of AI-Driven Knowledge Management (AI-DKM) and Knowledge Sharing Culture (KSC). Integrating human, technological, and cultural enablers, the research explores how businesses can achieve eco-innovation and enhance both environmental and economic outcomes. A quantitative survey of 291 HR leaders and executives in Pakistan’s manufacturing sector was analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM). The results show that GHRMP significantly impact sustainable performance, both directly and through AI-DKM and KSC. The combined deployment of green HR practices, AI-enabled systems, and a knowledge-sharing culture enhances environmental efficiency and economic performance. Grounded in the Knowledge-Based View (KBV) and Nonaka’s SECI Model, this study demonstrates how technological capital and social capital mediate the relationship between GHRMP and sustainable performance. The research highlights the importance of integrating green HR practices with AI platforms and fostering a collaborative knowledge-sharing culture. For policymakers, investing in digital infrastructure and promoting industry-wide knowledge networks is essential for widespread adoption. This research contributes to the discourse on business strategies for environmental transformation in emerging markets.
