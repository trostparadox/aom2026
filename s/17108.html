Session overview

Cultural entrepreneurship research has gained significant traction in recent times by focusing on how novel ideas and organizations gain legitimacy through cultural processes. However, this literature has traditionally emphasized the intentional strategies and successes of the individual entrepreneur. The goal of this panel symposium is to shift the focus toward the “multifaceted nature” of cultural entrepreneurial action, specifically examining the unforeseen positive and negative consequences for broader constituencies. Panelists will (1) critically examine the multifaceted nature of cultural entrepreneurship by exploring its intended strategies alongside its unforeseen positive and negative consequences; (2) discuss the ethical implications and societal responsibilities associated with cultural entrepreneurial practices; and (3) encourage scholars to enrich our collective understanding of the complexities of cultural entrepreneurship, and equip both scholars and practitioners to anticipate, as well as mitigate or leverage, its unintended consequences more effectively.
