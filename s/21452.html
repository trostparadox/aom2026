Balancing Trust, Standards & Regulation: Key Determinants of an R&I-Led Industrial Strategy

Authors: Tim Vorley Oxford Brookes University; Francisco Javier Trincado-Munoz Oxford Brookes University

Session overview

The rapid emergence of frontier industries—ranging from green energy technologies to advanced AI—necessitates a robust framework within modern industrial strategies. This poster conceptualises the tripartite relationship between standards, regulation, and trust as fundamental to sector development. Rather than viewing regulation as a barrier to innovation, we argue that it serves as a ‘market maker’. By re-imagining the dynamics of trust, standards, and regulation reduces systemic risk and has the potential to attract long-term capital investment. This is particularly important in many of the sectors pioneered through industrial policy and industrial strategies, where governments are seeking to pursue growth in new sectors characterised by high uncertainty. The poster unpacks what we propose as a ‘pro-innovation’ approach to industrial strategy, which seeks to understand the dynamics of trust, standards, and regulation – as well as how they vary by sector. By understanding and aligning these elements, industrial strategies can serve to better create stable ecosystems and institutional environments that foster competitive advantages to aid the growth from experimental technologies to industrial innovations, which are both rapid and resilient
