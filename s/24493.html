How to Cross the Valley of Death:Government Attention and Firm’s Innovation Transformation Efficieny

TIM
Jiancong Tao (Renmin University of China); Yu Teng (Renmin University of China)
The Valley of Death in the innovation transformation process severely impacts firms’ innovation activities and even survival, yet an optimal solution for crossing it remains elusive. Therefore, in this study, adopting an innovation systems perspective and starting from governmental policy attention towards innovation transformation, we utilize a sample of Chinese listed firms in the energy sector from 2015 to 2022 to examine the effect of government attention on firm’s innovation transformation efficiency. Our results indicate that government attention to the innovation achievements transformation significantly enhances firms’ innovation transformation efficiency. Furthermore, this positive effect is stronger among firms receiving a lower proportion of government innovation subsidies and those with higher levels of industry-university-research collaboration. Additionally, using partially linear functional-coefficient models, we uncover the nonlinear moderating effects of these two factors. Our findings provide valuable reference for policymakers and firms’ managers facing the dilemma in contexts similar to China’s market.
