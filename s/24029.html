Doubling Down or Spreading Bets? Technology Selection Strategies Under Solution Uncertainty

ONE
Gaomin Liu (RMIT University); Glenn Hoetker (Melbourne Business School, University of Melbourne)
How do firms choose technologies during the incubation stage of an industry? While existing research focuses on post-commercialisation decisions, this study shifts attention to the incubation stage, when technologies remain uncertain and fragmented. Using a novel panel dataset of green steel projects (2015–2024), I examine how firms balance reinforcing mature, familiar technologies with diversifying into new ones. Findings show that firms typically favour mature and portfolio-aligned technologies, but when solution uncertainty increases, they shift toward diversification to preserve flexibility. This reveals a contingent logic: firms “double down” under normal uncertainty but “spread bets” when turbulence rises. The study contributes to strategic management by integrating capability, path dependence, and real options theories, offering practical insights for innovation strategy under uncertainty.
