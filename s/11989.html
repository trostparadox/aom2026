Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop explores often overlooked experiences of gendered and boundary-crossing interactions in classroom settings. While such behaviors are frequently directed at women faculty, the session adopts an inclusive approach, recognizing that instructors of all gender identities may face similar challenges depending on factors such as appearance, career stage, or teaching context. Flirtatious comments, excessive familiarity, and subtle challenges to authority can undermine professional presence and demand significant emotional labor. Early-career faculty, instructors with diverse gender expressions, and those teaching in cross-cultural environments may be particularly affected. Key themes include flirtatious remarks, “buddying” dynamics that blur professional boundaries, and disparities in how tone and authority are perceived across genders. Although these behaviors can affect all instructors, the challenges they pose may vary depending on one’s gender, role, or cultural context. The session also addresses boundary-pushing student behavior, the emotional toll of managing these dynamics, and strategies for maintaining both authority and rapport. Although countries such as Iceland and Finland are seen as leaders in gender equity, gender- based challenges persist even there—and are often more pronounced in societies with slower progress. Recognizing the relevance of these dynamics across cultures, this PDW features a multicultural panel of educators sharing personal experiences and strategies for navigating classroom boundaries, followed by interactive discussions. Ultimately, the workshop aims to validate participants’ experiences, provide tools for managing classroom dynamics, and foster a safe, supportive community—especially valuable for early-career faculty and those interested in inclusive pedagogy and professional boundary-setting. Many instructors have experienced moments in the classroom that feel difficult to name, such as subtle challenges to authority, blurred boundaries, or interactions shaped by gendered expectations. This PDW creates a space to bring these experiences into the open. Bringing together a diverse panel of faculty, the session offers an opportunity to explore these often overlooked yet impactful dynamics. Panelists will share real-world experiences alongside practical strategies for navigating boundary-crossing interactions in academic settings. Participants will have the opportunity to engage in dialogue, reflect on their own experiences, and gain insights into maintaining both professional authority and inclusive classroom environments. The session is open to all and requires neither prior preparation nor additional registration. While particularly valuable for early-career scholars, we welcome participants at all career stages who have experienced or are interested in these dynamics.
