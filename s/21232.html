Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) seeks to build and enliven an emerging scholarly community in strategic management and organizational theory that is leveraging newly accessible data on employee career trajectories. This fine-grained, large-scale career data, often sourced from Revelio Labs, captures the near-universe of LinkedIn profiles. Just in the last few years, hundreds of people in our field have started using this data, but we have only begun to scratch the surface of understanding its potential benefits – and potential deficiencies. This PDW brings together scholars interested in how this individual-level career history data can be used to inform research on com- petitive strategy, innovation and entrepreneurship, strategic human capital, nonmarket strategy, and organization theory. The focus will include both new opportunities for theory development and testing using this kind of data, and methodological issues that are unique to this data. The PDW for- mat will include a traditional panel with interactive Q&A, a small-group brainstorming session, and a set of paper development roundtables. We aim to stimulate cross-domain dialogue across strategy, innovation, strategic human capital, and nonmarket research communities.
