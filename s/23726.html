Advancing Inclusion: Exploring the Voices of Equity-Seeking Students

DEI
Tasnuva Chaudhury (University of Regina); Leighann Neilson (Carleton University)
The study takes place within the context of current heightened attention to longstanding problems of racialized inequality in academia. At the post-secondary level, students from equity-seeking groups in Canada have reported experiences of racialized stereotypes on campus, cultural isolation, and feeling invisible in the classroom. Through this research, we seek to investigate the dual experience of graduate students as both learners and teachers. Our findings suggest that students experience stereotypical biases, discrimination, and out-group effects. Using an inclusion and belongingness framework, we examine the voices of equity-seeking groups and provide insights about strategies and practices to address racialized and gender-based oppression in higher education.
