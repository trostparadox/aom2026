Configuring Responsible Management Learning: A Fuzzy-Set QCA of PRME Champion Schools

MED
Keyang Drake Zhou (The University of Manchester); Oliver Laasch (The University of Manchester); Hongwei He
Despite the global diffusion of the Principles for Responsible Management Education (PRME), business schools institutionalize responsible management learning and education (RMLE) unevenly. Drawing on eight RMLE conditions and analyzing 91 PRME Champion schools’ Sharing Information on Progress reports with fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA), we identify three configurations that consistently characterize strong RMLE embeddedness: (1) Governance–Pedagogy Institutionalizer, (2) Faculty–Community Engager, and (3) Partnership Broker. These archetypal pathways reflect distinct strategic emphases: internal governance and curriculum, faculty-led engagement with local stakeholders, and externally oriented global–local partnerships. Theoretically, we introduce a configurational typology of RMLE implementation logics and show how multiple enablers jointly underpin embedded RMLE. Practically, we offer business school leaders a diagnostic map to select and sequence bundles of governance, pedagogy, engagement, and partnerships when advancing responsible management education.
