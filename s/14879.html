Working Stigmatized Identities: Social Identity Deployment in Diversity-Backed Occupations

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 3
OMT

People

Author: Leticia Smith Boston College

Session overview

Abstract: While traditional professionalization emphasizes technical expertise, diversity-backed occupations - a subset of movement-backed occupations distinguished by their explicit focus on advancing social equity and diversity aims - leverage stigmatized social identities to negotiate occupational legitimacy. Using a qualitative, inductive approach grounded in interpretive paradigms, this research examines members of one such occupation – community health workers (CHWs) – and how they navigate professionalization tensions. By theorizing the double-edged nature of identity-based legitimacy, this paper aims to provide insights regarding the commodification of personal identities and how occupational members respond within evolving institutional contexts.
