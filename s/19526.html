Session overview

The rapid rise of generative artificial intelligence has challenged the integrity and relevance of traditional assessment formats, particularly take-home written tasks and unsupervised online tests. In response, educators are seeking assessment designs that remain authentic, equitable, and resistant to AI-assisted misconduct. This workshop introduces the QR Micro Viva, an innovative, technology-enabled assessment that uses unique QR code tokens to reward brief, in-class oral presentations, such as responding to instructor questions, offering novel perspectives, and linking concepts to real-life examples. The system promotes active participation, deeper engagement with course materials, and meaningful dialogue between students and instructors, while remaining scalable for large cohorts and adaptable across disciplines. Participants will explore the pedagogical rationale behind the QR Micro Viva, examine evidence from its implementation in university classrooms, and learn how to construct the system using freely available survey tools such as Qualtrics. The workshop combines demonstration, hands-on technical guidance, and collaborative design activities to support attendees in adapting the method to their own teaching contexts. By the end of the session, participants will be equipped with a practical, AI-resilient assessment strategy that enhances authenticity, fosters student engagement, and strengthens academic integrity in contemporary higher education.
