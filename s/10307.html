Session overview

In pursuit of strong theoretical contributions and impactful advances in management, scholars have emphasized the need for methodological rigor and the importance of robustly testing the claimed theoretical mechanisms. While experiments remain the “golden standard” of establishing causality and testing theorized mechanisms, they are rarely used in corporate governance and strategic leadership research, where access to corporate leaders is limited. Yet to advance theory and gain a better understanding of different phenomena, scholars need to go beyond the typically used regression analysis to provide a more complete picture of how decision-making occurs at the top of organizations. The purpose of this PDW is to equip scholars with some of the available non-traditional tools to shed light on the theoretical mechanisms by engaging a group of expert panelists in a moderated, interactive discussion using corporate governance as a setting to provide concrete examples. During this PDW, four panelists will share their thoughts on: (1) How different methodologies could be used to shed light on theoretical mechanisms; (2) The important steps to follow when designing a study (having in mind the review process); and (3) The major opportunity and challenge of using a specific method in the corporate governance and strategic leadership field. The major topics covered in this year’s PDW include: qualitative research with executives, survey methodology with international executives, machine learning and large language models to uncover new corporate governance constructs, and qualitative comparative analysis for configurational theorizing.
