Artificial Intelligence as a Driver of Disruptive Innovation: Mapping the Intellectual Structure of

TIM
Letizia Saviano (University of Naples "Parthenope"); Roberto Cirillo (University of Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli"); Karen Paul (Florida International University)
Artificial intelligence (AI) is progressively viewed as a technology that remolds competition and revitalizes organizations. However, management research on AI-powered disruptive innovation continues to be fragmented. To bring the field together, we conduct a bibliometric review with performance analysis, science mapping, and qualitative synthesis. Following a PRISMA protocol, we combine and duplicate Web of Science and Scopus records from 2000 to 2025. We examine the data through co-citation networks, collaboration structures, keyword co-occurrence, and themes over four periods. Our findings show a shift from a technology-centric start to a more management-focused field. AI is now viewed as infrastructure for digital transformation, renewal, and resilience. Co-citation patterns connect strategic management and technology-driven change. Thematic evolution shows increased specialization and growing attention to governance and social issues. Our study maps the field’s structure and sets directions for research on boundaries, details, and responsible AI disruption.
