From IT Mindfulness to GenAI Competence: A Capability-Based Explanation of GenAI Job Engagement

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 2

Authors: Dhruv Pratap SINGH NEOMA Business School; Helena Gonzalez-Gomez NEOMA Business School; Anuragini Shirish; Shiva Taghavi NEOMA Business School

Session overview

Generative AI (GenAI) is rapidly diffusing into knowledge work, yet organizations face uneven employee uptake and growing concerns about responsible use. We develop and test a resource-to-capability-to-outcome model explaining when GenAI use translates into GenAI job engagement. Drawing on job demands–resources theory and IT mindfulness (ITM), we argue that ITM operates upstream by fostering Responsible GenAI Competence (RGAIC), a multidimensional employee capability for informed, effective, ethical, adaptive, and socially appropriate GenAI use. RGAIC enables employees to realize GenAI-enabled productivity and innovation gains, which function as proximal performance experiences that boost GenAI job engagement. We test this model using a two-wave survey of working adults recruited via Prolific (N = 301). At Time 1, participants completed measures of ITM, RGAIC, and demographics; at Time 2, they completed GenAI productivity, innovation, and GenAI job engagement measures. Temporal separation served as a procedural remedy for common method bias and did not constitute an experimental manipulation. Results support the proposed mechanism. ITM predicts RGAIC, which predicts GenAI productivity and innovation; both outcomes predict GenAI job engagement. The direct ITM–engagement link is non-significant, whereas the total indirect effect is positive and statistically reliable. Findings remain robust when controlling for perceived organizational support, job complexity, age, and GenAI training-oriented HR practices. We contribute by positioning responsible GenAI competence as the key capability translating mindful technology orientations and HR investments into productive and innovative GenAI use that sustains engagement.
