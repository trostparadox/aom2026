Session overview

This Manuscript Development Workshop focuses on doctoral students’ perspectives on emerging and under explored questions at the intersection of business and society, with relevance to scholarship examining organizations embedded in broader social, institutional, and political contexts. The workshop provides a structured, developmental environment where doctoral researchers refine early-stage manuscripts addressing issues central to the Social Issues in Management Division, including ethics, governance, sustainability, inequality, stakeholder relations, social responsibility, and institutional change. Rather than emphasizing completed papers, the workshop supports early-stage manuscript development by helping participants clarify core research questions, strengthen theoretical framing, and position their work within business and society scholarship. Through facilitated roundtable discussions with senior scholars, participants receive focused feedback on conceptual clarity, theoretical grounding, and contribution logic, with attention to how doctoral research can engage and extend SIM-relevant conversations. The workshop is sponsored by Business and Society Review and informed by ongoing editorial conversations about doctoral scholarship in the field, including a forthcoming Special Issue titled “Future Scholars in Business and Society: Current Work and Emerging Directions”. The primary aim of the workshop, however, is to support the development of durable research agendas and well-positioned manuscripts that contribute to SIM’s long-term scholarly community.
