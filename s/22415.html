Session overview

Connecting is a social event that encourages interaction and discussion between scholars with similar interests. During this fun and interactive session, participants will rotate to two different tables to discuss topics of interest. We have an amazing group of scholars at each table that will facilitate discussion on each topic. We will have the following themes of tables this year: (a) Emotions at Work, (b) Meaningful Work (c) DEI, (d) Leadership/CEO/TMT, (e) Well-being, Mindfulness, Family-Work Conflict, (f) Identity and Cognition, and (g) Future of Work. Join us to connect with other people from our field!
