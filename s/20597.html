Indian MSMEs Encounter with Delayed Payments: A Resource Dependence Theory Perspective (RDT)

Author: Vishnu Saranya

Session overview

The development of the country is significantly influenced by the MSME sector. Understanding the numerous issues this sector faces becomes crucial given its significance to the nation's prosperity. Previous studies on MSMEs discuss the numerous issues that MSMEs face globally. The MSME sector, particularly in India, faces numerous challenges, including access to technology, issues with infrastructure, issues getting bank loans, and more. However, one issue that we focus on in this article is the issue of delayed payments experienced by the Indian MSME sector from its corporate clients. The government has put in place several policies and procedures to help with the resolution of this issue because it is one of the most important and frequently encountered issues, yet this problem persists. So, it is crucial for us to comprehend why MSMEs are still facing this issue and how they are responding to it. In this article, we have developed a conceptual framework to examine the relationship between MSME suppliers and their corporate customers by incorporating the theoretical lens of Resource Dependence Theory (RDT). We did this by utilizing the extensive literature on delayed payments and by using a grounded theory approach to analyze the interviews of 35 entrepreneurs from the MSME sector.
