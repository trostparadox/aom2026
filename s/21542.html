Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) invites participants to engage Organization Development and Change (ODC) as a living field whose history, identity, and future are actively being shaped. Rather than treating history as a retrospective account, the workshop uses large-scale, participatory timeline artifacts and dialogic methods to support collective sensemaking across generations of OD scholars and practitioners. Participants engage OD’s intellectual lineage alongside emerging practices—such as artificial intelligence, Agile–OD integration, and participatory inquiry—to explore how foundational ideas are being reinterpreted in contemporary organizational contexts. Through facilitated dialogue, real-time polling, and reflective inquiry, the PDW surfaces themes related to field legitimacy, stewardship, and emergence. The session is designed as an experiential learning space that bridges founders, working professionals, scholars, and doctoral students. Participants leave with a deeper understanding of OD’s historical foundations, insight into current field-level tensions, and greater clarity about their own role in shaping the future of ODC.
