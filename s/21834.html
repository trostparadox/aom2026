Green Consumption: AI’s Role in Attitude toward Advertising Theory on Social Media

Authors: Hamza Smajic International University of Sarajevo; Hanan Babic International University of Sarajevo; Šejma Aydin International University of Sarajevo

Session overview

Based on the Attitude Toward Advertising Theory, this study aims to investigate the mediating role of green product evaluation in the relationship between attitude toward the ad for green product and green purchase intention within the social media context. Additionally, considering recent developments, the study analyzes the moderating role of AI content visibility in this indirect relationship. The study is based on quantitative cross-sectional data gathered from 203 adults from Bosnia and Herzegovina. Hypotheses were tested using structural equation modeling. Results show that the relationship between the attitude toward the social media ad for a green product and green purchase intention is partially mediated by green product evaluation. AI content visibility in the ad does not moderate this indirect relationship. This study enriches the attitude toward the ad theory by testing it in a social media context based on a sample from a developing country. By offering evidence that AI content visibility in ads does not moderate the Attitude toward Adverting Theory predictions, it offers practical suggestions for companies advertising green products through social media by showing that using AI-generated ads will not endanger the ad and product evaluations and purchase intentions of their consumers, despite AI technology’s negative environmental impact.
