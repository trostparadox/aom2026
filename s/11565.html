Session overview

This symposium brings together complementary perspectives that advance our understanding of how organizations can sustain support for diversity efforts in the face of resistance. The presenters conceptualize resistance as contingent on how diversity efforts are communicated, structured, and experienced. The panel, therefore, examines how strategic choices in narratives, framing, policy design, and organizational climate can redirect resistance. Drawing on perspectives from multiple stakeholders, including minority groups, high performers, and decision-makers, the papers illuminate distinct pathways through which resistance can be mitigated, from shaping how inequality is addressed and policies are designed, to challenging diversity-meritocracy tradeoff narratives and disrupting prejudice through reframing. Collectively, this symposium demonstrates that effective approaches vary across different audiences, contexts, and forms of resistance, offering actionable pathways for advancing diversity in practice.
