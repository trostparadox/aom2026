How Information Quality in Career Services Shapes Graduate Employability

CAR
Bilal Ahmad (Superior University); Syed Yasir Abbas Zaidi (Riphah International University); Shahid Latif (Curtin University Malaysia); Saba Bilal (University of the Punjab)
Drawing on human capital theory (HCT), this study investigates the role of the quality of information resources (QIR) at institutional employability centers in fostering students’ perceived employability by improving their career clarity and job market knowledge (JMK). Time-lagged data were collected in three waves from university students enrolled in diverse faculties. Covariance-based structural equation modeling (CB-SEM) was employed to test the proposed hypotheses. The results suggest that the quality of information resources available at institutional employability centers supports students in building their career clarity, which subsequently enhances their understanding of job market knowledge, thereby promoting their perceived employability. In addition to its theoretical contributions, the study offers valuable, actionable strategies for academicians, policymakers, and career counselors.
