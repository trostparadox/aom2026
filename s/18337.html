How Does Moral Intelligence Influence Employee Trust in AI?

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 10
OB

People

Author: Md Al Amin The University of Texas at El Paso

Session overview

Organizations face continuous changes in operational activities due to artificial intelligence. Employees use AI in different organizational activities, such as in human resources and decision-making. The study examines how personal moral traits affect employee trust in artificial intelligence systems. The study found that employees with strong moral intelligence perceive that AI systems lead to elevated AI moral risk concerns and diminished trust in AI technology. The study contributes operational tools to managers who want to build AI systems with transparency while keeping workers both trusting and actively involved in ethical practices. Keywords: Artificial intelligence, trust, moral intelligence, risk
