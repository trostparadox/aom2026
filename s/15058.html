Critical Thinking in the Age of AI: Navigating Strategic Boundaries in Higher Education

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 8:00AM–9:30AM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Salon 9
CAU

People

Presenters: Katarzyna Bachnik Hult International Business School; Imen Ameur Hult International Business School

Session overview

This caucus examines how AI transforms strategic decision-making in higher education, balancing automation with academic values and human agency. Through guided discussions, participants will explore AI's role in teaching, research, and governance—identifying where it excels, where it falls short, and how institutions can integrate it effectively.
