Session overview

This inclusive Paper Development Workshop (PDW) provides students and junior faculty from diverse backgrounds with a valuable forum to discuss leadership research in a collegial environment alongside distinguished international leadership scholars. Prior to the conference, junior scholars are required to submit concise research proposals and will be matched with senior scholars who have similar research interests (excluding those from their own institutions). During the PDW, participants engage in focused, theme-based table discussions, receiving tailored feedback, insights, and recommendations from senior scholars aimed at advancing their research. The event also features a networking session with current and former editors of leading leadership journals, who offer guidance on converting research proposals into publishable journal articles. The workshop prioritizes developing robust theoretical frameworks and designing high-quality empirical studies. Held annually since 2008, this PDW has consistently fostered mentoring relationships and collaborations among scholars worldwide. For junior scholars, it presents an opportunity not only to refine their research but also to build meaningful professional connections that may lead to future academic partnerships.
