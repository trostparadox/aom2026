Contributions of COIL to the Development of Intercultural Competencies in Latin America

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 5
MED

People

Authors: Carlos Gabriel Colin Flores Tecnologico de Monterrey; Santiago Colin Nuñez Tecnologico de Monterrey

Session overview

This study measured the effect of developing a COIL process on the development of intercultural competencies using a quasi-experimental design. Our sample consisted of 105 undergraduate students from two private universities, one located in Mexico and the other in Colombia. The students' intercultural competence was measured using a self-report survey in two “pre-post” waves that included the Cultural Intelligence Scale (CQS) and the Multicultural Personality Questionnaire (MPQ). The data showed a significantly greater increase in intercultural competence for the experimental groups in both Mexico and Colombia, supporting our hypothesis that COIL develops intercultural competence. Likewise, multicultural personality was found to be higher for the experimental groups for students in both countries.
