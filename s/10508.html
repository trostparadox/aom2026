Session overview

Online panels have become increasingly prevalent in organizational research. Researchers typically design surveys using platforms like Qualtrics, then recruit participants through services such as Amazon Mechanical Turk, Prolific, and CloudResearch. However, best practice guidance and formal training for this form of data collection remain limited. This is especially relevant given the current rapid AI developments that further accelerate the opportunities and threats associated with online survey and panel research. This professional development workshop offers a comprehensive approach to these challenges. First, expert presentations address survey design best practices, platform selection, and strategies for detecting problematic responses. Second, a panel spanning North America and Europe discusses how regulatory frameworks and the recent advances of AI shape online research practices. Third, representatives from CloudResearch, Prolific, and Qualtrics present their approaches to ensuring response integrity and demonstrate recent platform developments in the age of generative AI. The workshop concludes with interactive roundtables designed to provide participants with personalized feedback on their online studies. Participants are invited to submit a project abstracts (about 500 words) one week in advance, outlining their current status and specific challenges. This preparation enables facilitators to provide tailored guidance on survey design and platform setup. Attendees will thus leave with actionable both methodological insights and concrete progress on their own online studies.
