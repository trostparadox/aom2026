Session overview

In this PDW proposal, we focus on addressing the challenges experienced by business schools and researchers when seeking to capture and communicate the societal impact of their research. The goal of this PDW is to deepen business school researchers’, deans’, and managers’ understanding of societal impact by presenting a range of approaches and frameworks to capture and communicate the societal impact of business research. This will be achieved by engaging multiple presenters who represent a range of perspectives and utilise a range of frameworks for capturing societal impact. These presenters will span different global regions—Australia, North America, and Asia-Pacific and Europe—and bring insights into business research evaluation at individual, national, and international levels. They will share insights of their research and/or work on societal impact, and demonstrate how they currently capture, evaluate, and communicate their societal impact to different stakeholders. From this PDW, participants will be able to expand their toolkit with approaches for capturing and communicating societal impact. This PDW is co-organised by representatives from the Australian Business Deans Council (ABDC) and the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB). They will share their respective approaches and frameworks for societal impact conceptualisation and evaluation. Workshop participants will include researchers who are seeking to effectively capture their societal impact, as well as business school managers and deans who struggle to manage societal impact in their business schools and are seeking effective ways to capture and communicate their societal impact.
