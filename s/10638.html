Hope as Practice: Collective Sensemaking Formats Shaping Organizational Responses to Climate Change

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 3
OMT

People

Authors: Clara Scheve Hamburg University of Technology; Thomas Wrona Hamburg University of Technology

Session overview

This paper examines how hope can sustain organizational action without retreat into denial or despair. Empirically, we study Climate Fresk, a science-based collective sensemaking format (CSF) in large industrial corporations. We conceptualize CSFs as socio-material, performative devices that organize interaction, emotion, and attention around climate futures. Based on an ethnographic, collaborative phenomenon-driven research design in several industrial firms, we examine how the Climate Fresk organizes key tensions—cognitive vs. affective, reflection vs. action, descriptive vs. normative, and individual vs. collective— and how keeping these tensions in play can generate energy, critical reflection, and agency. We then trace how emotions, narratives, commitments, and “hope cultures” travel beyond the format and are enabled or constrained by organizational temporalities, structures, and power dynamics. The paper advances a practice-based view of hope in organizations, theorizes CSFs as devices of organizational future-making, and identifies conditions under which participatory climate formats in carbon-intensive firms can shift engagement from symbolic participation toward more disruptive, hopeful trajectories of action.
