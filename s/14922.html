Session overview

This panel symposium examines how hosts can pursue mega-events while avoiding harmful escalation of commitment and governance drift. Using Los Angeles 2028 (LA28) as a timely focal case, we treat hosting as a phase-sensitive governance challenge: decision rights, oversight capacity, narrative control, and stakeholder voice evolve across the multi-year cycle, often under tightening deadlines and rising reputational stakes. Our goal is to bring organization theory, consulting, and social impact perspectives into direct dialogue to generate (a) integrative insight that is researchable and (b) practical governance 'brakes' that can be implemented before outcomes are locked in.
