How the Use of Generative AI Shapes Opportunity Discovery and Creation in Entrepreneurial Action

ENT
Fabio Emanuel Farago (LINK SCHOOL OF BUSINESS); Felippe Cippiciani (LINK SCHOOL OF BUSINESS); Felipe Mendes Borini (University of Sao Paulo); Leandro Marcilio Schunk (LINK SCHOOL OF BUSINESS); Francisco Isidro Massetto (LINK SCHOOL OF BUSINESS)
This paper explains how entrepreneurs integrate generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) into opportunity discovery and creation through entrepreneurial action. Drawing on an inductive multiple-case qualitative study of 12 startups, we developed a process model that identifies three mutually reinforcing mechanisms through which GenAI becomes embedded in opportunity discovery and creation. First, GenAI-enabled sensemaking couples market scanning with cognitive structuring and cumulative memory, helping entrepreneurs turn dispersed cues into actionable direction. Second, GenAI-enabled iterative refinement forms a technology-assisted learning system through which entrepreneurs revise stakeholder engagement, iterate experiments, and adapt the business model as signals evolve. Third, GenAI-enabled dual evaluation allows entrepreneurs to pressure-test opportunity beliefs while simultaneously calibrating and triangulating the reliability of GenAI outputs. We contribute to discovery and creation theories in three ways. We shift the locus of opportunity search and development from cognition alone to a GenAI action assemblage, clarifying how discovery-like and creation-like logics can be enacted within the same unfolding process, conditional on disciplined feedback and judgment. We theorize dual evaluation as a reflexive safeguard that helps explain how GenAI can amplify both finding and forming opportunities without collapsing the process into either pure discovery or pure creation. Finally, we show how GenAI helps entrepreneurial action under uncertainty by transforming ambiguous information into workable interpretations and next steps.
