Session overview

Graduating college students last this and this year feel they largely missed the AI change to higher education, while incoming freshman often know as much or more than their faculty. Like most new breakthrough innovations, artificial intelligence has both acolytes and luddites, with a varied mix in between. The truth is it is a tool, a sharp tool. Like sharp tools it can either make work easier or cause major injuries to your work. AI is a predictive tool; based on what you ask it its response is a prediction of what it thinks you want. In this PDW we walk you through a brief introduction as to what we mean by AI, some commonly used AI tools (e.g. Gemini, ChatGPT, etc.) and how they operate. We walk through prompts work, illustrating how changing prompt parameters can greatly change output. We engage in a mini-case study, deconstructing a journal article submission, and how AI helped improve the overall article. The case study involves a Management History article, involving both archival data, quantitative methods, and a historical narrative. Generative AI was employed n two roles: verifying pattern matching in the data, and as a personal journal editor in revising the rough draft. These are different roles, both are valuable. We wish to be very clear: never cut and paste AI output! Rather, use it as a coach to help you improve your work.
