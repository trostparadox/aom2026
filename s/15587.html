Session overview

Virtual work, which involves job-related interactions between individuals using technology rather than face-to-face, has become a worldwide phenomenon. Despite growing interest in understanding how to implement virtual work effectively within and across different countries and cultural contexts, gaining a comprehensive understanding of research in this area is challenging for several reasons. First, research on different types of virtual work arrangements (e.g., virtual teams, telecommuting, hybrid work, gig work) varies in its degree of global focus. Second, this research is fragmented across multiple disciplines, emphasizing different levels (individual, group/team, and organization). Finally, research needs are constantly evolving to address new virtual work forms and emerging technologies that facilitate virtual interactions in a global context. Through presentations from an expert interdisciplinary panel, roundtable discussions, and an integrative plenary session, this PDW seeks to advance a more global perspective in virtual work research by integrating existing findings and developing novel future research directions.
