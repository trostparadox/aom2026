Neuro Work Environment: Rethinking Work Design through a Brain-Friendly Lens

NEUB
Sebin Joy (St Joseph's College of Engineering and Technology Palai, Autonomous); Hanfia Rahman (Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology Allahabad)
In the dynamic and ever-evolving landscape of today’s work environment, organizations are confronted with an array of complex challenges that can impact employee performance and overall organizational health. To address these challenges, the integration of neuroscience presents groundbreaking solutions designed to enhance both employee well-being and productivity levels. One powerful concept that arises from this intersection is the creation of a “neuro-work environment.” This innovative approach focuses on applying insights from neuroscience to develop workplaces that are intricately aligned with the innate needs and preferences of the human brain. This conceptual paper aims to lay the groundwork for future investigations into this promising area, advocating for empirical and experimental research that can substantiate these concepts across various sectors and industries. By integrating the principles of neuroscience and organisational behaviour, this approach not only enhances our theoretical understanding of employee dynamics but also provides actionable strategies that can be applied in real-world settings. Ultimately, it outlines a transformative roadmap that aspires to optimize workplace environments, resulting in improved employee satisfaction and enhanced organizational performance.
