Session overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has advanced rapidly in the last year. From its predictive and generative capabilities to acting as agents for management. This rapid surge poses various governance, trust and policy challenges for management who are looking for guidance on implementing AI in enhancing managerial capabilities. In this panel symposium, we seek to examine the potential of AI, especially generative and agentic AI, in critically questioning current leadership and managerial practices as well as improving decision-making and other organizational processes. We are bringing together leading scholars and practitioners to provide insights into how generative and agentic AI may shape these practices, what changes may occur in the organization due to the widespread use of AI. Also, we look to guide future researchers into the right directions so that their research can inform practitioners.
