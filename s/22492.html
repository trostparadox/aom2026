Session overview

Orneita was not only a foundational leader within MSR, but also a deeply grounding presence—someone who embodied faith, care, service, and quiet moral courage in how she showed up for this community. For many of us, she was MSR in its most human form. Your presence at this session is requested. The 90- minute session is structured around: Opening and framing (5 min) — Lee Robbins, MSR Flame Keeper Curated video remembrances (15 min) Facilitated panel dialogue (30 min) Emerging scholar voices (2–3 contributors, 30 min) Audience reflection and closing ritual (10 min)
