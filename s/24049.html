Circular Business Models and Entrepreneurial Orientation: Creating Value with and for Stakeholders

SIM
Leila Ahmadpour (LUISS Guido Carli University); Laura Toschi (University of Bologna); Francesco Rosati (Technical University of Denmark); Riccardo Fini (University of Bologna)
The transition toward circular business models (CBMs) has become central to achieving the goals of the circular economy (CE), yet empirical evidence on the organizational drivers of this transition remains limited. Drawing on the resource-based view and stakeholder theory, this study examines how entrepreneurial orientation (EO) influences the adoption and implementation of CBMs through social engagement (SE). Using survey data collected in two waves from 296 Italian firms, we empirically test the relationships between EO, SE, and CBMs implementation. The findings show that EO does not directly lead to CBMs implementation; rather, its effect operates through firms’ social engagement with stakeholders. This study contributes to the CBM literature by providing empirical evidence on the EO-CBM link and by highlighting SE as a critical mediating mechanism. Additionally, we introduce a firm-level scale to measure the implementation of key CE strategies.
