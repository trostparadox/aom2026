Session overview

The panel symposium examines artificial intelligence and robotics as an emergent organizational field rather than as monolithic technologies or external shocks to organizations. Bringing together empirical studies, it explores how firms, research communities, users, and institutional actors jointly produce, shape, and legitimize AI and robotics across various contexts. Drawing on technology lifecycle, ecosystem, organization theory and institutional field perspectives, the symposium highlights the heterogeneous trajectories, shifting bottlenecks, and uneven diffusion that characterize AI’s evolution. By unpacking how technical paradigms, platforms, governance arrangements, and social ideals co-evolve, the symposium advances a more nuanced understanding of how general-purpose technologies develop and reshape organizations, industries, and management theory.
