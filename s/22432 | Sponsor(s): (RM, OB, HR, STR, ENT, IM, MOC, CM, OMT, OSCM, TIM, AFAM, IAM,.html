Session overview

This PDW has a long-standing tradition as a crowd favorite. It is a great opportunity to ask experts any questions you might have about qualitative research methods, such as ethnography, grounded theory, discourse analysis, interpretative phenomenological analysis, participant observations, case studies, interviews, focus groups, conversation analysis, narrative inquiries, coding, content analysis, and computer-assisted qualitative data analysis. No preregistration is required and the format is very laid back. Attendees are encouraged to come and go as they please to have their questions answered by qualitative method experts in the field.
