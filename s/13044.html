Saving Face: When and Why Daily Abusive Supervision Leads to Impression Management

Authors: Wei Pan Harbin Engineering University; Yang Liu Harbin Engineering University; Vivien Lim National University of Singapore; Yi SU Harbin Engineering University; Thompson Teo National University of Singapore

Session overview

While research on abusive supervision typically emphasizes destructive employee reactions, constructive response strategies remain under-explored. Drawing on face theory, we posit that daily abusive supervision triggers a loss of face in employees. To restore their standing, targets engage in impression management tactics to project a positive image to their supervisors. We further propose that individual differences—specifically self-face concern and sensitivity to interpersonal mistreatment—moderate this process by amplifying self-image expectations and awareness of hostile behaviors. Results from an experience sampling study support these predictions. Our findings contribute to a nuanced understanding of when and why abusive supervision can lead to constructive behavioral responses.
