AI-Driven Export Automation: Smart Contracts and Digital Infrastructure in Chinese SMEs

TIM
MUHAMMAD NAFEES KHAN (Harbin Institute of Technology); Zhen Shao (Harbin Institute of Technology); Pornthip Panthong (Harbin Engineering University); Ali Raza Akhter (Harbin Institute of Technology)
Small and medium-sized manufacturing firms (SMEs) continue to face challenges in international marketing due to resource limitations and the complexity of global commerce. This article examines how AI-based smart contracts affect the export-oriented international marketing performance of manufacturing SMEs when enabled by digital infrastructure. Drawing from the resource-based view and dynamic capabilities theory, we consider these technologies as strategic resources and capabilities that SMEs employ to overcome operational-level logjams and promote competitive advantage. The empirical evidence, based on a survey of 250 Chinese manufacturing SMEs, underscores the positive effects of AI smart contracts on the automation of export processes and manufacturing operational efficiency, as well as on international marketing outcomes. In addition, digital infrastructure plays a crucial moderating role in amplifying the effects of AI-driven innovations and ensuring scalable, integrated world operations. This research extends contemporary theories by providing a fresh perspective on the dynamic relationships between digital assets and ecosystems within SME internationalization. The implications for policymakers and SME managers are that investment in AI technology and digital infrastructure can be a strategic response to the need for greater export performance and resilience to the increasing dynamism of global markets.
