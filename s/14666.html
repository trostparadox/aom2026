Session overview

This panel symposium marks over a decade of X-Culture as a pioneering force in experiential learning, global collaboration, and management research. What began in 2010 as a classroom-based innovation connecting students across cultures has evolved into a worldwide ecosystem that integrates teaching, research, and professional development. Over time, X-Culture has demonstrated its adaptability to profound global disruptions and contextual challenges. Today, X-Culture functions not only as a pedagogical model, but also as a worldwide research infrastructure supporting multi-level, multi-source data collection and large-scale international collaboration. This symposium offers participants a comprehensive view of X-Culture’s evolution and current capabilities. Attendees will gain insight into how the platform supports instructors, empowers students, and enables impactful research across Organizational Behavior, International Management, and Management Education. Whether new to X-Culture or long-standing collaborators, participants will leave with actionable ideas, practical resources, and a deeper understanding of how X-Culture can support their teaching, research, and professional networks.
