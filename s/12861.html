Session overview

The Academy of Management (AOM) Annual Meeting continues to serve as a crucial venue for developmental opportunities across career stages. While existing PDWs provide robust guidance for doctoral students and early-career faculty, far fewer resources are directed toward scholars who have reached the midpoint of their tenure clocks. The purpose of the “Halfway to Tenure” PDW is to address this gap by offering practical insights, mentorship, and community for junior faculty who have recently completed their mid-tenure review or are approaching the midpoint milestone. Building on the success of prior Making Connections Committee programming, this PDW introduces a refreshed and holistic format designed to support participants as they evaluate progress towards career goals, including achieving tenure, navigate new service and mentoring expectations, and chart a sustainable path toward tenure. A number of experienced faculty members across diverse institutions have agreed to share guidance on topics such as preparing the tenure packet, managing expanded responsibilities, assessing one’s progress, and evaluating opportunities on the advanced-assistant job market. Registration is required to attend. A link to the registration form is distributed on listservs before the conference and can be obtained by contacting the organizers.
