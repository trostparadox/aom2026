Connection as Currency?: The Value of LGBTQ+ Networking

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 2
DEI

People

Author: Giavanna Chavez-Ruscitto Michigan State University

Session overview

The present study examines the role of networking ability in shaping perceptions of emotional and instrumental support among LGBTQ+ and non-LGBTQ+ employees. Cross-sectional data analysis from a sample of 194 employed individuals indicated that higher networking ability is positively related to emotional and instrumental support, and total support (average of emotional and instrumental support), with an unexpected finding of the positive relationship of social astuteness to total support. Implications for the use of networking ability in fostering supportive work relationships are discussed.
