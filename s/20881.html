Executive Role Crafting Under Disruption: How Senior HR Executives Navigate and Shape Organizations

Authors: Jamaal Sebastian-Barnes Drexel University; Rajiv Nag Drexel University

Session overview

This study examines how senior HR executives navigate and craft their roles amid technological disruption, economic volatility, and policy uncertainty. Using constructivist grounded theory, the research investigates the lived experiences of senior HR executives managing role transformation. Rather than passive role incumbents, these executives are positioned as active architects who shape both their roles and organizational systems. The study addresses a critical gap in job crafting literature, which has focused predominantly on non-executive workers despite industry evidence of dramatic executive role transformation. Skills requirements for senior HR executives have increased by 23% over the past five years with minimal legacy responsibility reduction (Deloitte, 2025). Through semi-structured interviews with 20-30 senior HR executives in large organizations, this research applies constructivist grounded theory to build inductive theory from executive narratives. Theoretical contributions will advance understanding of executive agency, organizational change, and strategic leadership under conditions of disruption.
