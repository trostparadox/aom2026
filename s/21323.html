Session overview

Human Resource Management systems have overlooked the integration of disability and technology frameworks with organizational behavior theories, creating a critical gap in equitable performance assessment for remote and hybrid work. Current metrics treat capacity variation as individual limitation rather than system design opportunity, magnifying exclusion in AI-driven digital environments where disabled workers' access has expanded yet organizational visibility into their needs remains limited. Addressing this we propose the Ability-Adaptive Digital Performance Tracking (AADPT) architecture. Integrated with the Job Demands-Resources (JD-R) theory, Human Activity Assistive Technology (HAAT) principles, and jagged cognitive profile research, AADPT reconfigures traditional job design via feedback-responsive algorithmic systems that model dynamic individual capacity alongside organizational demands. The model comprises three layers: (1) an Antecedent Layer to capture dynamic individual ability profiles capturing real-time capacity variation (pain, cognitive load, burnout) (2) an Algorithmic Core utilizing multimodal intent classifiers to build standardized work units and select adaptive performance modes, specifically Sprinter for variable output with time and Pacer for chronic fatigue while replacing surveillance with artifact-based validation (3) an Output Layer for sustainable productivity for disabled individuals, synchronized diverse working styles in teams, and productivity data for organizations. By incorporating feedback mechanisms for system adaptability for real-time homeostatic correction, algorithmic contextual calibration, and performance reinforcement connecting successful outcomes back to individual capacity perception and the HRM system. This framework repositions disability accommodation as catalyst for designing cognitively sound, adaptive work systems benefiting all employees experiencing capacity variation, generating measurable business value through talent retention and equitable performance outcomes.
