Session overview

As artificial intelligence (AI) rapidly transforms the academic landscape, the urgency to rethink career possibilities and pathways has never been greater. This is not another AI workshop—it is an immersive, future-focused experience designed to shift mindsets and empower academics from all disciplines to shape their own career success and leadership in academia. Through innovative activities—including g scenario sprints, immersive “Futures Wheel” career mapping, and real-time collaborative challenges—participants will navigate the complex disruptions and unprecedented opportunities redefining academic work in the age of AI. The workshop focuses on equipping participants with the essential skills, ethical insights, and creative strategies needed for success in an evolving academic world. Attendees will learn to identify and develop future-ready competencies, build strategic networks, and adopt adaptive approaches to career development. With a strong emphasis on peer learning, cross-cultural perspectives, and expert facilitation, the session fosters resilient and ethical approaches to career planning. Participants will leave with a concrete toolkit for reimagining and advancing their career trajectories—whether pursuing traditional academic paths, interdisciplinary roles, or alternative opportunities. The experience will broaden perspectives, spark innovative thinking, and instil a readiness to harness AI for meaningful and sustainable career growth. By the conclusion of the workshop, attendees will be fully prepared to embrace uncertainty, capitalise on emerging opportunities, and carve out fulfilling academic futures in a technology-driven, continuously changing environment.
