Session overview

HR has been affected by revolutionary technological advancements such as Artificial Intelligence (AI). Automated systems are frequently used in sourcing, screening, and selecting, even interacting with job applicants to optimize and support the hiring process. However, few studies focus on whether AI in recruitment and selection can be trusted. We use a qualitative research approach with 21 semi-structured interviews with HR professionals in large firms. Thus, this paper comprehensively examines how AI impacts the adoption by HR professionals and analyses the perceived benefits and challenges. We contribute to the algorithmic accountability theory and the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM).
