Session overview

Publishing in leading internationally renowned applied psychology and career journals is a primary means by which our research work in the field of careers is disseminated as new knowledge to scholarly ‘conversations’. Publishing in these leading journals is also critically important to career success in academia. Yet, career scholars may still find it challenging to decide which journal to target to best place their career-focused papers. Awareness of how these journals vary in their positioning, review processes, and appetite for career research is an important subject for career scholars making choices about where to submit their work. The goal of this PDW is therefore (a) for the panelists to share information regarding the publishing of career research in leading internationally renowned journals from applied psychology and career research (here: Career Development International, European Journal of Work and Organizational Psychology, Applied Psychology: An International Review, Journal of Vocational Behavior, and Journal of Applied Psychology); and (b) for participants to have their burning questions answered. The panel will consist of editors (i.e., [co-]editors-in-chief, and [senior] associate editors). Each editor will provide a short overview of their journal (e.g., characteristics of an ‘ideal’ paper, review process). This format will allow participants to ask the editors more targeted questions during a Q&A.
