Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to support doctoral students and junior faculty in developing one of the most important yet under-taught academic skills: conducting high-quality peer reviews. Along these lines, the session will consist of two parts. The first portion, open to all attendees, will feature a panel discussion on reviewing best practices. A distinguished group of scholars who have served as Associate Editors and Editorial Board Members at leading journals will share their insights on how to craft constructive, developmental, and high-impact reviews. The second portion, open to the first 30 participants who pre-register and submit a review of the initial draft of the now-published Administrative Science Quarterly paper, “Recognition killed the radio star? Recognition orientations and sustained creativity after the Best New Artist Grammy nomination” (Harrison, Askin, & Hagtvegt, 2023), will involve small-group sessions facilitated by experienced faculty members, during which participants will discuss and receive individualized feedback on their reviews. Practice reviews will be due in ~mid-June.
