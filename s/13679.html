Session overview

Lessons from the Field: Publishing Management Research in a Burgeoning Psychedelics Ecosystem marks the fourth annual Academy of Management symposium focused on psychedelics as a consequential and maturing site for organization and management scholarship. Building on growing evidence that psychedelic-assisted therapy (PAT) represents a promising alternative within the global mental health crisis, the symposium situates this domain squarely within mainstream management inquiry as it moves from the margins toward increasing scientific, regulatory, and organizational legitimacy. Where earlier symposia emphasized conceptual framing and field emergence—including the 2025 Copenhagen symposium highlighting European regulatory and cultural variation—this year’s program turns to the practical realities of sustained empirical engagement. As scholars have progressed into deeper phases of data collection, analysis, and publication, new insights have emerged around access, positionality, ethics, and theory-building in stigmatized and legally complex settings. The symposium features five empirical research projects examining leadership, professionalization, institutional logics, expertise construction, and historical consciousness, alongside candid reflections on methodological and ethical challenges encountered in the field. Together, the presentations offer both theoretical advances and practical guidance for navigating real-time, morally complex research contexts. Designed to be both grounded and generative, the session provides an energizing space for established scholars and newer researchers alike who are interested in producing rigorous, impactful management research in emerging and contested domains.
