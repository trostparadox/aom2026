Against the Algorithm: Decoding "Anti-Work" Sentiment Among Gig Workers

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 8
OB

People

Author: Yanli Chen American Public University System

Session overview

The gig economy is growing fast. At the same time, many workers are posting "anti-work" messages on social media. We want to know why. We use "Self-Determination Theory" to study this. This theory looks at human needs. We studied 150,000 posts from Reddit communities like r/UberDrivers and r/Doordash. We collected these posts from 2023 to 2025. We used computer programs to analyze the topics and emotions in these texts. Our findings show something important. "Anti-work" feelings are not just about being lazy. They are a reaction to "algorithmic dehumanization." Workers feel the algorithm treats them like robots. We found three main complaints: (1) Fear of being fired by a bot for no reason, (2) Tiredness from the app's "games," and (3) Loneliness. Our analysis shows that hidden rules make workers the angriest. We also found that posting online helps workers feel better. It helps them rebuild their identity. This study helps us understand how algorithms hurt workers' basic needs. It explains why workers fight back online.
