Session overview

Storytelling, as a tool in workplace coaching, supports employees in reflecting on personal issues within professional contexts by promoting self-expression and enabling the reappraisal of constraining narratives, thereby fostering conditions for positive organizational change. This 90-minute PDW will deliver research insights on the use of storytelling in workplace coaching across different sectors, while demonstrating hands-on how storytelling enables transformation in coaching. By providing frameworks for implementing storytelling as a coaching tool and practical tips from expert panelists, this PDW will support participants in exploring the role of storytelling in developing language awareness, managing private communication, and supporting others in professional settings.
