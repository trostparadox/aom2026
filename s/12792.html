Session overview

Recent advances in AI code generation are reshaping software development work, yet their productivity effects vary widely across organizational contexts. This Professional Development Workshop examines how AI-augmented development practices alter productivity and why similar tools yield divergent outcomes across teams. Through an interactive demonstration of AI-enabled application development, participants observe core developer–AI interaction patterns, including prompt-driven generation, iterative refinement, and human oversight. These practices are connected to productivity frameworks such as the SPACE Framework and research on developer experience to support analytical interpretation rather than prescriptive use. The workshop highlights organizational culture and governance as moderating conditions shaping productivity, satisfaction, and learning, and surfaces open questions for future research on AI-enabled organizations. Keywords: AI Code-Generation, AI-Driven Development, Developer Productivity, SPACE Framework, Developer Experience, Context Switching, Job Satisfaction
