Session overview

This PDW explores how theater can enrich organizational scholarship and teaching education by activating forms of embodied, relational, and imaginative knowing often overlooked in business research and education. Panelists will demonstrate how dramaturgical tools such as performance, improvisation, and dramatic analysis can deepen learning, strengthen managerial capabilities, and illuminate complex organizational phenomena. The session combines presentations, interactive discussion, and a roundtable addressing both pedagogical practices and research opportunities. Participants will gain practical strategies for integrating dramaturgy into teaching, methodological insights for using theater in scholarly work, and a broader appreciation of how the performing arts can advance management knowledge.
