Harnessing AI to Support Team Performance, Assessment, and Intervention in Management Education

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 5
MED

People

Authors: Nachiket Bhawe University of North Carolina at Pembroke; Yuanyuan Lin

Session overview

Effective teamwork is a critical learning outcome in management education, yet instructors often lack visibility into how student teams make decisions. In experiential learning settings such as capstone simulations or business projects, teams engage in complex, collaborative decision-making, but the interactions driving those decisions typically remain hidden. Instructors may see only the outcomes, such as simulation scores or final reports, without access to the discussions and reasoning that produced them. This limits the ability to assess team dynamics, provide formative feedback, or evaluate individual learning, especially in virtual environments where informal observation is not possible. Traditional tools like peer evaluations and reflective essays offer only partial and often biased insights. These limitations also present challenges for Assurance of Learning (AOL), as business schools are increasingly required to demonstrate direct evidence of teamwork proficiency. We propose that recent advances in artificial intelligence, such as real-time transcription, speech recognition, and natural language processing (NLP), can make team interactions more transparent. AI offers a scalable and unobtrusive way to capture and analyze how students communicate, coordinate, and make decisions together. Drawing from a pilot case in an online MBA capstone simulation, we illustrate how AI-enabled analytics can reveal participation patterns and team processes, offering new opportunities for assessment and instructional intervention. This paper explores how such tools can enhance pedagogical practice, support equitable team development, and strengthen learning assurance in management education. Keywords Capstone Project, AI, Teamwork, Assessment, Assurance of Learning (AOL)
