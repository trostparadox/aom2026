Session overview

Leadership transitions often fail not due to lack of technical competence, but due to underdeveloped leadership mindsets. This interactive Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces a holistic, research-informed approach to cultivating leadership mindsets that align with complex, global, and strategic demands. Drawing on theoretical frameworks, as well as introducing the Integral Leadership Mindset and the Integrated Framework developed by two of our scholar-practitioners. Overall, this session blends theory with practice through immersive experiential activities. Participants will engage in a dynamic sequence of reflection, scenario-based team challenges, and guided co-creation to explore the essential elements of leadership mindset, including systems awareness, empathy, long-term thinking, and strategic communication. A special focus is placed on the transition from subject-matter expert to an Adaptive Transformational Leadership style. This workshop is especially timely in an economy shaped by global uncertainty, technological disruption, and a workforce that prioritizes purpose and human-centered leadership. The session connects leadership mindsets to contemporary models of leadership succession and developmental pathways needed in today's global economy. If not, what happens? Attendees will leave with actionable tools, peer-informed insights, and a practical toolkit for applying these concepts across academic, consulting, and organizational leadership development contexts. Keywords: Leadership development, mindset, emotional intelligence, systems thinking, succession planning
