Artificial Intelligence as an Organizational Capability and Cross-Border Knowledge Spillovers in EVs

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 3
IM

People

Author: Xiayu Zhai Peking University

Session overview

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly embedded in firms’ global strategies, yet international business research has limited evidence on whether AI adoption enhances firms’ ability to generate and diffuse knowledge across national boundaries. We examine how AI functions as an organizational capability shaping cross-border knowledge creation and transfer in the global electric-vehicle (EV) industry from 2015--2025. We conceptualize digital absorptive capacity as an AI-enabled organizational learning capability that strengthens firms’ ability to sense, recombine, and redeploy externally sourced digital knowledge, and argue that it mediates the relationship between AI adoption and cross-border technology spillovers, measured by foreign-origin patent citations. We further theorize that organizational transformation and embeddedness in global innovation networks condition the extent to which AI translates into cross-border spillovers. Using firm-level patent, disclosure, and network data for EV challengers from China and incumbent automakers in advanced economies, we estimate fixed-effects count models and leverage exogenous variation in AI talent supply and policy shocks. The study contributes to Track 4 by clarifying when AI becomes a source of cross-border knowledge diffusion rather than an inward-facing efficiency tool, and by identifying digital absorptive capacity as a transferable organizational mechanism linking AI to global innovation outcomes.
