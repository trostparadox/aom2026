Session overview

This PDW is designed to help scholars critically rethink the methods, measures, and theories that underpin research in international corporate finance, a domain increasingly central to global strategy and organizational outcomes. As firms operate across borders, financial decisions are deeply embedded in diverse institutional contexts, regulatory frameworks, and technological disruptions, creating unique challenges for IB research. Understanding these dynamics is essential for explaining variations in firm behavior across countries. The PDW addresses key themes such as comparative approaches to corporate governance, the influence of sociopolitical factors on investment flows, and the evolving role of financial technology in reshaping global markets. It also explores valuation practices that account for lifecycle stages, managerial judgment, and behavioral biases, as well as dividend behavior and credit allocation in emerging economies. This PDW provides a collaborative platform for generating innovative ideas and advancing scholarship at the intersection of finance and international business.
