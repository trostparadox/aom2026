Session overview

Management consulting remains a highly sought-after career path for doctoral students, early-career scholars, and practitioners interested in applying evidence-based management knowledge to real-world organizational problems. However, many prospective consultants lack clarity about how to enter the field, the skills required for success, and the multiple career pathways available within and beyond traditional consulting firms. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed to demystify the management consulting profession and provide participants with practical, actionable guidance on how to become a management consultant. The workshop introduces participants to the consulting landscape, including strategy, operations, human capital, and internal consulting roles. It addresses key questions such as: What do management consultants do? How do academic skills translate into consulting value? What are the differences between large consulting firms, boutique firms, independent consulting, and internal consulting roles? Participants will gain insights into required competencies such as: problem structuring, client communication, data-driven storytelling. recruiting and business development strategies, and ethical considerations in consulting practice. Through a highly interactive format that includes brief presentations, guided discussions, and hands-on exercises, participants will leave with a clearer understanding of consulting career options and a personalized action plan for pursuing consulting opportunities aligned with their professional goals. This PDW is particularly relevant for doctoral students, early-career academics, and practitioners seeking applied impact through management consulting. By the conclusion of the workshop, participants will: • Have a clearer understanding of management consulting roles and career pathways • Be able to articulate how their skills and experiences translate into consulting value • Understand multiple strategies for entering the consulting profession • Develop a personalized action plan for pursuing consulting opportunities • Gain awareness of ethical and professional standards in management consulting
