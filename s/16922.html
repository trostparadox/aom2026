Two Faces of Entrepreneurial Optimism: Dispositional and Unrealistic Optimism in Venture Performance

Author: Prabhat Kumar Indian Institute of Management Lucknow

Session overview

Entrepreneurship provides an opportunity for individuals as well as nations to grow and thrive by exploiting opportunities. This study explores how the cognitive orientation of an entrepreneur affects the performance of their venture. Drawing from cognitive theory, we analyse how entrepreneurs with different cognitive orientations, such as Dispositional optimism and unrealistic optimism, affect firm performance. We also argue that there is a mediating role of opportunity exploitation that will mediate the relationship. We examine the moderating role of two cognitive resources: knowledge and experience of an entrepreneur, which can significantly moderate the relationship and enhance the interplay between different facets of optimism and venture performance. This research aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the relationship between optimism and venture performance.
