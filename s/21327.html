Session overview

The rapid emergence of generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools like ChatGPT has sparked excitement and concern in management education. While these technologies offer unprecedented opportunities to enhance learning experiences and prepare students for an AI-driven business landscape, they also raise critical questions about academic integrity, skill development, and the future role of educators. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to explore the implications of generative AI in education and provide participants with practical strategies for its effective integration. Drawing on recent empirical studies and experiences from diverse academic settings, this PDW will address three key areas: 1) Student perceptions and adoption; 2) Organizational readiness; and 3) Pedagogical innovations. The workshop emphasizes the importance of balancing technological innovation with core educational values. We will address concerns about academic integrity and the potential for AI to replace critical thinking skills, while highlighting how AI can enhance personalized learning, foster creativity, and prepare students for AI-augmented workplaces.
