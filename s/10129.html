Session overview

Identifying a research gap is a critical element of scholarly inquiry and a prerequisite for meaningful contributions to academic discourse. However, this process is frequently misunderstood and inconsistently executed by doctoral students. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) addresses this challenge by presenting a structured framework for identifying and articulating substantive research gaps. Grounded in scholarly peer-reviewed literature and informed by pedagogical experience, this PDW introduces a typology comprising empirical, theoretical, methodological, and contextual gaps. Participants get equipped with tools to move beyond superficial “gap-spotting” toward a rigorous approach rooted in problematization and contribution logic. Through expert-led discussions, interactive exercises, and peer feedback, participants will leave with a draft gap statement tailored to their research projects, enhancing their capacity to engage in doctoral-level inquiry with impact toward positive social change.
