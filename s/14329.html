Session overview

Management learning requires more than just reading about and listening to lectures to make management concepts come to life. Experiencing the concept is also critical to the learning process (Kolb and Kolb, 2018). Often, the experience involves the emotional impact of the concept. In many cases, this is accomplished through storytelling. Broadway musical numbers provide an innovative teaching tool to incorporate both storytelling and emotional affect. This session will introduce the participants to the use of Broadway musical numbers to experience different management topics. Participants will also explore a curated list of songs they might use in their courses. Participants will receive two takeaway handouts: 1. A handout with one Broadway musical number for each management discipline. A second handout of the last 15 years of at least two nominated Tony scores to be used in selecting their own musical number. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
