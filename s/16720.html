Session overview

This PDW introduces AI resilience as an innovative and largely uncharted educational concept that has not yet been systematically explored in management education. AI resilience is defined as the ability to engage with AI tools and systems in a critical, confident, reflective, responsible, and self-efficacious manner. Developing this capability is essential for maintaining agency, professional judgment, and self-efficacy in AI-rich educational and organizational contexts. The PDW is designed as a peer-based exploratory space in which participants collectively examine, refine, and operationalize the concept of AI resilience based on shared experiences and educational practice. Participants are provided with a structured conceptual framework and will discuss teaching and training scenarios, and concrete didactic approaches for systematically developing AI resilience in management education and professional training contexts. By the end of the session, participants will leave with a shared conceptual language, practical reflection guides, and transferable teaching methods that can be directly applied to strengthen learners’ and educators’ capacity to engage with AI in a resilient, agentic, and self- efficacious way across diverse educational settings.
