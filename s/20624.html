Organizers: Bill Foster Not Associated; Daniel Muzio University of York

Session overview

This caucus brings together a growing community of current and former editors of leading management and business studies journals to discuss issues affecting the academic publishing field. In particular, we will focus on the increasingly challenging issue of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the challenges and possible opportunities this has for journal editors. Our goal is to engage in a dialogue with a view of developing some industry wide best practices to better manage this substantial change in the field.
