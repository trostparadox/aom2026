Entrepreneurial Network Evolution: An Integrative Framework Across Firms and Ecosystems

ENT
Aziza Sellah (Cadi Ayyad University); Said Ouhadi (Cadi Ayyad University)
Despite the progress made in recognizing the role of networks in ventures and ecosystems, literature has not explored the integrative evolutionary perspective across venture development stages and entrepreneurial ecosystems. To address this gap, this review aims to investigate the evolutionary dynamics of networks from serving to venture development to serving to a broader ecosystem orchestration. First, through a bibliometric process, we selected 1458 papers identified from Scopus database and perform a bibliographic coupling analysis and co-citation analysis that determines historical foundational papers and recent relevant literature to the objective of the review. Second, the results of both analyses enable us to select 65 relevant papers for a deep literature review that investigates the integrative perspective across firms and ecosystems which results in four comprehensive categories: (1) network formation, (2) network evolution, (3) drivers of network evolution, and (4) network orchestration. The elaboration of integrative research frameworks emerged from each category helps us to identify the integrative research framework illustrating the mechanism of network evolution from micro level perspective of firms to macro level perspective of entrepreneurial ecosystems and identify overlooked areas in network research which necessitates further investigation in this promising field.
