Session overview

The rapid evolution of AI is fundamentally transforming how organizations operate, innovate, and succeed, placing unprecedented pressure on the role of managers. AI systems with agentic properties such as autonomy, opaqueness, and learning abilities are not merely the next stage of digital technologies but constitute a fluid and evolving set of socio-technical systems that reshape organizational logics. To thrive in this environment, managers must transition from optimizers of predefined processes to designers of human-AI collaboration. Therefore, management education must evolve from reactive adaptation to proactive problem-based, experiential learning grounded in real-world AI challenges. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) in the format of a practical masterclass presents a holistic framework that applies Design Thinking as experiential educational mechanism to (1) build creative confidence, (2) develop learner agency though reflective practices, and (3) equip managers and educators to actively design and govern emerging AI-enabled organizational systems. The 90-minute PDW combines an expert panel showcasing best practices in experiential AI education with interactive breakout sessions that allow participants to translate insights into their own teaching and institutional contexts. The session positions AI simultaneously as a design object, a managerial decision-making tool, and a transformative force reshaping organizational and entrepreneurial processes.
