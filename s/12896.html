Session overview

As digital ecosystems evolve, the traditional focus on singular platforms is becoming insufficient to explain modern market dynamics. A new architectural phenomenon, nested platforms, is emerging as a dominant force. Nesting occurs when one platform embeds its user experience within another, creating interdependent, asymmetrical relationships between a host and a nester. This integration fundamentally alters the strategic landscape by shifting where value is created, who captures critical data, and where the primary locus of customer interaction resides. This workshop explores the tensions inherent in these structures. For sponsors, nesting offers the potential to accelerate growth and amplify network effects. However, these benefits come with significant risks, including brand dilution, data invisibility, and the threat of disintermediation. We will examine the spectrum of nesting intensity—from light-touch integrations to deeply embedded experiences—and how these choices impact governance. Participants will engage with leading scholars to address how nesting challenges theories of orchestration and curation. We will specifically investigate how data-sharing in nested arrangements influences competitive advantage, particularly as AI becomes central to platform value. This session aims to set a robust research agenda for the next frontier of platform strategy.
