Unveiling the Impact of Innovation Technology: How It Shapes the Connection Between CRM and CB

TIM
FAHIMEH KHATAMI (Bocconi University); Francesca Romana Rinaldi
The main aim of the current paper is to investigate the relationship between customer relationship management and consumer behaviour, with a particular focus on the moderating role of innovative technology in European countries. This paper adopted a quantitative methodology using linear regression analysis to evaluate the relationships among hypotheses involving interaction terms, ANOVA tests, and regression coefficients. Results indicate that communication activity and broadband connectivity significantly impact consumer prices, and their effects are further enhanced when combined with high levels of innovation adoptions. The significant interaction term indicates that the relationship between broadband infrastructure and consumer prices is strongly moderated by the extent of digital technology payments, underscoring the critical role of technological integration in shaping economic outcomes. This study contributes to the theoretical understanding of the moderating role of innovation technology in affecting the relationship between customer relationship management and consumer behaviour. Moreover, this study's main limitation lies in the data collection and selected time frame; however, further research could investigate more by adopting qualitative methods and focusing on one special industry. Despite the existing literature on customer relationship management, the moderator role of innovation technology has not been investigated appropriately in cross-countries studies.
