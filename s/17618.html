Session overview

As educators, managers and leaders for keeping pace with the demands of organizational life, one gets often accustomed to analyze, judge and act quickly. Can we then pause, observe and look inwardly to ensure our decision making is determined from an equanimous standpoint? This workshop invites participants into a contemplative/ reflective learning space where observation becomes a way of knowing. Drawing from Indian contemplative traditions, the workshop engages participants with the concepts of Panchamaya Kosh (five layered human human existence) and Sakshi Bhava (witness consciousness) along with Somatic Awareness, and enable participants to experience how decisions could be the resultant game play of bodily sensations, emotions, habits and deeper values. Through a guided contemplative inquiry exercise on ‘Witnessing Inner Alignment’ participants reflect on moments of alignment and compromise around ‘integrity’ and witness their experiences without judgement through image/ metaphor based individual journaling, pair and group sharing. For reduced automatic cognitive reactivity (Creswell, 2017; Hölzel et al., 2011) and reaching an attention span without thought loops a short guided mediation precedes the inquiry exercise. Designed for researchers, educators and management practitioners, the workshop brings forth a structured approach for generating clarity and intuitive insight through inner awareness and reflective capacity. We hope this shared inquiry offers participants a meaningful pause to reconnect with their whole self and continue exploring how embodied practices can be integrated into management education. By infusing these into our daily setting, we foster learning environments that are inclusive and responsive to the complexities of contemporary organizational life.
