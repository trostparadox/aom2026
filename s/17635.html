Measuring Expected Characteristics for Followers Across Three Dimensions

Session overview

This study integrated follower categorization theory with follower role theory to develop a measurement structure for a scale, based on the typology of follower characteristics, developed by Coyle, Foti, & Goswami (2023). First, a measure of expected follower characteristics, using a semantic differential measurement scale across 8 contexts was proposed and confirmed using exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis. Following, associations with relevant invariant measures (IFT’s, ILT’s and IPT’s) and outcomes (OCB’s, CWB’s, personal initiative, passivity, job dedication, and contextual performance) were examined. The theoretical structure proposed by Coyle et al. (2023) was supported, using this 21-item measure.
