Session overview

During the Journal Editors panel, the editors-in-chief of the leading journals in Operations & Supply Chain Management will share the latest developments in their respective journals. The session will be interactive and allows for Q&A with the audience, so join us to get to know these top journals and ask your questions to the editors.
