Session overview

While research has suggested that some forms of artificial intelligence (AI), such as generative AI, may have negative or mixed effects on student learning (Larson et al., 2024), other research suggests that AI may have the potential to enhance higher-order learning (HOL) (Csaszar et al., 2025; Razmerita et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2025). This may include using AI to generate personalized and individualized content during the learning process (Yang et al., 2025). However, one challenge in achieving HOL outcomes through recent technological advancements is the lack of instructors' familiarity with and skill in using these capabilities, even though they could improve management education outcomes (Richter et al., 2025). Therefore, this poster session will demonstrate effective approaches to integrating AI into management education to enhance HOL (Bazouche, 2025; Orynbassarova & Porta, 2024). We will present findings from recent literature and provide practical guidance on how college and university instructors can use AI.
