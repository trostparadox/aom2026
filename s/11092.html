Session overview

Technological change, alongside geopolitical and geoeconomic realignment, are reshaping how the organization of global work. Digital collaboration infrastructures and AI-enabled tools have widened the scope for virtual cross-border work, making it easier to collaborate through technology-mediated communication while posing new challenges pertaining to job design, coordination, and differences in time zones, languages, and institutional contexts. At the same time, shifting national and industrial policies and intensifying competition for high-skilled talent have imposed new constraints regarding the regulatory terrain for cross-border work and mobility, prompting organizations to rethink staffing models, leadership pipelines, and compliance capabilities. This panel examines how global work is evolving at the intersection of technological advances and regulatory complexity in a multipolar world.
