Session overview

The rapid diffusion of artificial intelligence (AI)—particularly generative AI—has accelerated organizational experimentation while also intensifying resistance, ethical concern, and uncertainty around productivity outcomes. Although early evidence highlights AI’s transformative potential, significant questions remain regarding how organizations move from resistance to sustained adoption, how productivity effects unfold across contexts and populations, and how trust, governance, and human well-being shape AI’s long-term impact. This Professional Development Workshop brings together leading scholars and practitioners to examine AI adoption and productivity through four interrelated lenses: 1. AI adoption dynamics 2. Productivity and economic impacts 3. Trustworthy AI and data privacy governance 4. Human and organizational consequences of AI integration. Through a combination of expert insights, interactive discussions, and participant engagement, the workshop provides a structured forum for synthesizing emerging research, identifying unresolved theoretical tensions, and developing actionable research agendas. Designed for scholars, doctoral students, and practitioners, this PDW emphasizes learning across disciplinary boundaries, connecting innovation economics, organizational change, human–AI collaboration, and AI governance. Participants will leave with clearer theoretical frameworks, practical research questions, and a shared language for studying AI adoption as a socio-technical and human-centered transformation rather than a purely technological shift.
