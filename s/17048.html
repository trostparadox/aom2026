Session overview

Now in its 15th year, the STR Junior Faculty Paper Development Workshop aims to provide junior scholars with the opportunity to receive developmental feedback on their current research projects from a panel of distinguished scholars. With expertise in a diverse range of topics and methods, the panelists will discuss junior scholars’ papers and give advice on how to improve. The workshop is particularly suitable for junior faculty who are struggling with their papers, e.g., a paper that has been rejected or received contradictory reviews. In the process of paper discussions, senior scholars will also have an opportunity to share their best practices in research.
