Session overview

This symposium examines how commercialization strategy shapes innovation by bringing together new research on the determinants and consequences of “choosing the path to market.” The papers investigate why firms follow different commercialization pathways and how these choices influence follow-on innovation. Across diverse empirical settings, the contributions highlight how incentives, information frictions, and governance structures shape firms’ decisions to develop technologies internally or engage external markets. Together, they demonstrate that commercialization choices play a central role in shaping firms’ innovation trajectories.
