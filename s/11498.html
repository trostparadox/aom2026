Session overview

The panel symposium aims to discuss review articles as a key form of scientific knowledge production in the field of management. In this symposium, a panel comprised of editors of some of the leading journals that publish review articles will discuss their roles in the production of scientific knowledge, what forms they could take, various review methods and knowledge contributions and what makes an excellent review article that could be published in top journals. In light of the rapid proliferation of AI tools, the panel will specifically focus on the potentials and limitations of AI in producing reviews that advance scientific knowledge. They will provide junior and senior scholars across multiple AOM divisions with advice and guidance for producing and publishing trustworthy and impactful review articles.
