Session overview

“Collaboration allows us to know more than we are capable of knowing by ourselves.” --Paul Solarz This interactive PDW provides participants with a structured activity to foster team collaboration and build team trust through the creation of a written quick team charter. The activity helps teams define their mission, goals, and processes while enabling open dialogue about strengths, weaknesses, and strategies for success. By fully immersing themselves in this activity, teams develop familiarity, trust, and collaborative team norms that improve both team dynamics and learning outcomes. Participants will learn how to implement this quick team charter activity in their own courses by participating in a guided team activity through three key phases of the charter: 1) Who “We” are as a Team, 2) What each member can contribute to the Team, and 3) How we will work together as a Team. Minimal individual-based pre-work, including a personality assessment and toolkit (e.g., Quick Team Charter Template, etc.; link to be provided), will prepare participants to maximize learning during the session. A mini team activity after the team charter activity will allow participants to put their charter to the test. This symposium equips participants with practical tools to enhance student collaboration, whether in virtual, hybrid, or in-person settings.
