Embeddedness and Tie Formation in Private Equity Deal Networks

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 4
OMT

People

Author: Kaitlyn Cunningham University of California Los Angeles

Session overview

Between 2007 and 2023, the private equity industry recorded a cumulative deal value of over $9 trillion on nearly 86,000 deals. Opaque ownership structures, fewer regulations and disclosures, and complex financial transactions have made this industry difficult to assess. Socioeconomic research on private equity remains fragmented, often focusing on sector-specific or firm-level case studies rather than the industry as an interorganizational system. Adopting a network approach, this study will examine private equity relationality. Drawing on embeddedness theory, the study will conceptualize private equity acquisitions as relational actions shaped by prior relationships and shared partners. I will examine which actors participate in acquisitions, with whom, and how network structures organize these relationships. I expect firms to be more likely to co-invest with prior partners or with actors connected through trusted third parties, and for similarity across organizational characteristics (i.e., sector, geography, and institutional maturity) to increase the likelihood of tie formation. I will construct an event-based network of private equity transactions using 17 years of PitchBook data (2007–2023). From this, I will project a deal-participation network connecting actors based on joint participation in acquisitions. I model dyadic tie formation using Exponential Random Graph Models (ERGMs), estimating the influence of relational embeddedness, homophily, and transitivity. This study will contribute to the socioeconomic study of private equity by treating the industry as a relational network rather than a collection of isolated transactions and will advance embeddedness theory by demonstrating how actor attributes and endogenous network structures shape participation in private equity acquisitions.
