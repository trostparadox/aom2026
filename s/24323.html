AI, Blockchain, and IoT for Sustainable Logistics Services: a PRISMA-Guided Bibliometric Review

TIM
Matteo Tavano (University of Naples "Parthenope"); Robeto Cerchione; Renato Passaro (University of Naples "Parthenope")
This paper examines the scholarly landscape on the use of artificial intelligence (AI), blockchain, and the Internet of Things (IoT) to support sustainable development in freight transport and logistics services, with particular attention to logistics service providers (LSPs). Using a dataset of 444 peer-reviewed publications from 2015 to 2025, identified through a PRISMA-compliant selection process, the study applies performance indicators alongside co-citation analysis and keyword co-occurrence mapping. The results point to an intellectual domain that remains dispersed but shows signs of alignment: blockchain research primarily addresses inter-organizational transparency and coordination, whereas AI and IoT are more frequently associated with intra-organizational automation and operational optimization. Four thematic clusters are delineated, and a multi-level framework is advanced to connect specific digital technologies to corresponding supply chain and logistics processes. The study delivers three core contributions: (1) it extends theorizing by emphasizing systemic patterns in technology adoption; (2) it proposes a triangulated bibliometric design for rigorous knowledge synthesis; and (3) it provides actionable implications for managers and policymakers pursuing sustainability in freight transport and logistics. The paper also outlines future research opportunities concerning ESG, digital maturity, twin transition, and the governance of integrated technology portfolios in relation to supply chain sustainability objectives.
