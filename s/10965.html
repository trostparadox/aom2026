Session overview

An academic career is often bounded by what scholars do within their institutions – research, teaching, and service. Yet, given that management is an applied science, our expertise is much desired by those outside of our institutions (i.e., those in the work world). To bridge this gap, many AOM sessions help scholars understand how to translate their research to a broader audience (e.g., the Speaking Science PDW). But these sessions often assume that the sole goal of translation is to expand the reach of our research. While certainly important, there exists an additional truth: our time and expertise have both societal and financial value. As a result, for some, an equally significant consideration is the ability to monetize our expertise in a way that both broadens the impact of our research and is financially beneficial. Through the theoretical lens of “side- hustles” (Sessions, Nahrgang, Valuont, Williams, & Bartels, 2021), the proposed PDW aims to provide members of the Academy with insight into the broad range of monetized activities in which scholars engage beyond their academic positions. The session will equip attendees with practical tools and tactics to help them successfully navigate their own potential side-hustles through stories and advice from those who are both successful scholars and successful side-hustlers.
