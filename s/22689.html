Session overview

All attendees are invited to kick off the 86th Annual Meeting of the Academy of Management at the Opening Reception. The evening will feature presidential remarks from Tammy Madsen, followed by a two-hour networking event in the open Exhibit Hall where attendees can connect in a casual, welcoming atmosphere. Light food and refreshments will be provided as you explore exhibits, reconnect with colleagues, and build new professional connections.
