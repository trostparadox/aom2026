Imagination Augmented Search

People

Author: Enlan Wang University of Missouri-Columbia

Session overview

This paper develops an Imagination-Augmented Search (IAS) model within an NK landscape framework, formalizing imagination as an often-biased cognitive representation that enables hypothetical exploration prior to experiential learning. Simulation results show that, despite its biases, imagination enhances adaptive performance when combined with experiential search. IAS outperforms alternative strategies, particularly in complex and dynamic environments. The findings demonstrate despite of the irrational features of imagination—optimism, error, and simplification, imagination may still enhance organizational adaptability and long-term performance under certain conditions.
