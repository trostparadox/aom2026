Session overview

The Public and Nonprofit Division’s Editor Panel offers an opportunity for members and other conference attendees to learn about potential publication outlets for their work. The Editor Panel offers journal editors the opportunity to provide an overview of their journals to the audience. This year, we focus on how to strategically focus submission to journals and build a timeline for consistent, impactful publishing. We will also consider current trends, and hear from the editors about their vision of their respective journals. This panel provides conference attendees with the opportunity to learn about public and nonprofit management journals and ask questions about the peer review and publication process. Participation in this PDW is open to all conference attendees.
