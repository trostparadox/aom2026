Session overview

Since its establishment in February 2023, the Turkish Academy of Management (TAOM) has developed into a notable platform for advancing management and organization scholarship in Türkiye, while simultaneously building bridges with the global academic community. Alongside scholars based in Türkiye, TAOM has drawn members from the Balkans, the Middle East, Asia, and Europe, steadily extending its regional and international presence. Today, TAOM brings together 661 members from 30 countries and 220 universities, pointing to its expanding global character. A significant step in this trajectory was TAOM’s entry into the Academy of Management Community Accelerator Program (AOM-CAP) in May 2025. This collaboration has deepened TAOM’s engagement with the wider management research community and opened pathways for closer ties with the Academy of Management. The partnership was celebrated with a reception at the AOM2025 in Copenhagen, marking the strengthening relationship between the two organizations. Through AOM-CAP, TAOM members have gained improved access to AOM membership, contributing to a measurable rise in TAOM affiliation and increased participation of regional scholars in AOM activities. As a result, scholars connected with TAOM now benefit from enhanced access to international networks and academic communities. In support of this partnership, the AOM-CAP program has contributed to several TAOM-led events, including TAOM2025 in Istanbul, TAOM2025 in Budapest, and the 2026 Winter Workshop in Ankara. The collaboration will extend to TAOM2026 in Izmir, continuing to reinforce the connection between AOM and TAOM and supporting the advancement of management and organization scholarship in the region. In 2026, this ongoing journey will be commemorated once more with TAOM members and colleagues at a reception during the AOM Annual Meeting in Philadelphia.
