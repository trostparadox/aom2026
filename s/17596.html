Session overview

Management educators face a critical challenge preparing students for organizational problems that don't yet exist. This professional development workshop (PD workshop) introduces challenge-based learning (CBL) - a pedagogical framework moving beyond case studies to engage learners with authentic, stakeholder-driven challenges requiring adaptive, collaborative challenge-solving. Through immersive experience, participants design, facilitate, and assess CBL implementations appropriate for their teaching contexts. The workshop guides participants through CBL three phases (engage, investigate, act) while addressing challenge identification, stakeholder engagement, team facilitation, and assessment strategies capturing both process learning and solution quality. Participants leave with concrete implementation tools: CBL Espresso guidebook, CBL design templates, assessment rubrics, stakeholder engagement protocols, and connections to an international community of CBL practitioners. Based on previous practices, approximately 70% of participants implement CBL practices within one academic year, transforming student engagement and learning outcomes across undergraduate, postgraduate, MBA, and executive education contexts. Participant evaluations are invaluable.
