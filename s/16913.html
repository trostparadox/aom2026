Session overview

As organization and management theory research on data-driven technology, especially artificial intelligence (AI), has expanded rapidly in recent years, now is a timely moment to reflect on how we theorize data-driven technologies and why. The impacts of AI unfold across multiple levels—individual, organizational, and societal—yet the technology itself remains widely described as opaque, evolving, and only partially knowable, rendering both its functioning and consequences fundamentally uncertain. Against this backdrop, this symposium brings together an experienced panel of scholars to share in-depth reflections on how they make conscious theoretical and analytical choices when studying AI in organizations. The discussion then opens to critically examine what these academic insights mean for translating research into strategic understanding, managerial sensemaking, and the future of organizing.
