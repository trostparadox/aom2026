Session overview

Managers and leaders who construct and consistently re-evaluate narratives that critically assess their present and envision their futures are likely to be more intentional about their career development choices, stay more focused on their management and leadership goals, reach their goal more quickly, and are better able to develop other leaders in their career trajectories too. There are many different ways that coaches can help individuals to construct and reevaluate narratives, as well as many tools that individuals can use to do this work for themselves. This professional development workshop will begin with the workshop leaders sharing their experiences of successfully (or unsuccessfully) constructing and reevaluating narratives, particularly focusing on the use of mindfulness activities and other spiritual practices to reveal their deepest motivation and clearest sense of the “why” for their goals. Session facilitators will then introduce specific techniques and take participants through the process of constructing a present, forward-looking narrative; recognizing the degree to which this may have changed in the past year; and planning for a reevaluation and revision of the narrative on a regular basis. The workshop will conclude with participant volunteers sharing their narratives with the full group, ending with a discussion of how to keep this as a critical and regular activity for their future development
