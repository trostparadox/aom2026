Session overview

Employment with justice is a foundational aspect of comparative management studies, supporting the Management, Spirituality, and Religion Division 2026 theme of “Integrating Wisdom and Purpose in Management” for the 2026 Academy of Management conference. This Professional Development Workshop continues the multi-AOM Division supported 2025 offering, this year featuring presentations by scholars all proactively involved with aspects of government, employers, and employees in light of justice obligations for workplace authenticity. We take up the need for appropriate participation in public policy crafting about AI in sociotechnical systems deployment. Notions of justness in corporate identity are explored and evaluated across the largest firms in three national settings: Denmark, Japan, and the U.S. Social costs associated with U.S. “employment at will” are assessed for employees and employers with a legally actionable Appendix proposal. The countervailing role for hope in U.S. executive leadership is introduced. And demonstrable union success tactics dealing with automation are reported from the largest EU maritime port of Rotterdam. Our goal is to improve knowledge and awareness of employment and management themes related to ultimate human values throughout the world, recognizing the role of the state as indirect employer, and through this Workshop to focus and localize Academy support for scholars with allied interests.
