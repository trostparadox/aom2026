Digital Balance: A Framework for Well-being in the Era of Digital Coworkers

Authors: Shalini Sabharwal Gopalkrishnan San Francisco Bay University; Nicole C. Jackson Golden Gate University

Session overview

The increasing integration of digital coworkers—AI agents, bots, and collaborative platforms—reshapes human work experiences . This research presents a framework for "Digital Balance," examining how these technologies both alleviate and amplify stress . It proposes organizational practices that foster well-being, digital literacy, and adaptive human-AI collaboration for sustained workplace health
