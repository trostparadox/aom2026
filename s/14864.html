Session overview

Organizational environments are increasingly characterized by competing demands and growing complexity. Rather than viewing contradictions as problems to be resolved, recent research highlights them as enduring tensions that shape cognition, emotion, behavior, and organizational processes. This symposium advances this perspective by examining how organizational actors navigate complexity through ambivalence, paradox mindset and emotional complexity. Bringing together theoretical and empirical work across multiple levels of analysis and organizational contexts, the session offers an integrative, actor-centered view of duality and paradoxes in the workplace, fostering cross-fertilization across research streams and contributing to more cumulative theory building.
