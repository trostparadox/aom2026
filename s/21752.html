Session overview

Organisations that will be able to use emerging technologies (AI, Digital Twins and Metaverse) in a correct business context will be a step ahead. The challenge organisations face today is that they do not have a mature data strategy, and some do not have one at all. This hinders the adoption of emerging technologies across businesses. The 2nd Watch company surveyed in 2021 had 26% of total respondents had a data strategy. This paper sets out a practical framework for how the Chief Data Officer and leadership teams can shape an effective data strategy. It focuses on the skills a leadership role demands, not just the technical expertise, but the ability to connect strategy, technology, governance, and organisational change. By strengthening this skillset, organisations can begin to close the gap between where they are today and what is required to use data and emerging technologies with real purpose.
