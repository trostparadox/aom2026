Session overview

Early career scholars see impact as an important personal goal. They produce high-quality research and aim to make a difference with it. However, many are unaware that achieving impact requires more than strong scholarship, it requires actively presenting one’s work and engaging confidently with the academic community and beyond. This Professional Development Workshop addresses these challenges by positioning academic speaking skills as a core component of scholarly identity, visibility, and career progression. The session equips participants with practical communication strategies to strengthen their academic voice and enhance their credibility within and outside of academia. Through expert inputs, reflective and interactive self-assessment exercises, and facilitated discussion rounds, participants will learn why effective academic speaking is essential for academic success and how to apply actionable techniques immediately in conferences, networking, and public engagement.
