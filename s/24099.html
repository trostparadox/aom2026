How Much Is Enough? The Inverted-U between Digital Cross-Border M&A and Digital Innovation

IM
Liu Xuan (Central South University)
To better understand how digital cross-border mergers and acquisitions (DCMAs) shape firms’ digital innovation, drawing on the knowledge-based view, we use an unbalanced panel of Chinese listed firms that conducted DCMAs from 2012 to 2023 and identify an inverted U-shaped relationship between DCMAs and digital innovation. We also examine boundary conditions by analyzing the moderating roles of digital executive positions and internationalization experience. The results show that both digital executive positions and internationalization experience steepen the inverted U-shaped relationship. Our findings contribute to research on M&As and innovation in the dual-complexity context of digital cross-border integration.
