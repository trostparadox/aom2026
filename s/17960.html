Session overview

This symposium addresses a critical gap in negotiation scholarship by centering communication as fundamental to negotiation effectiveness. Five presentations examine how communicators exchange information, manage emotions, and create shared meaning across diverse contexts, from environmental stakeholder negotiations to international business dealings. The research reveals communication's multidimensional nature, encompassing verbal, nonverbal, cognitive, and emotional elements that operate differently across cultural and power contexts. Collectively, these studies advance context-sensitive negotiation theories by examining communication at individual, dyadic, group, organizational, and societal levels through experimental, field-based, archival, and cross-cultural methodologies. The symposium offers actionable frameworks for developing communication competencies while identifying future research directions.
