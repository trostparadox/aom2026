Career Advancement in Academia: Insights from Women Scholars at AMJ

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 9:00AM–11:00AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — 107A
PUBS

People

Organizers: Quinetta M. Roberson Michigan State University; Laura McAndrews Little University of Georgia; Susan Zaid Academy of Management; Michael Malgrande Academy of Management

Session overview

Current and former women editors of the Academy of Management Journal (AMJ) invite women junior faculty, as well as scholars who identify as women or as non-cisgender, to participate in an advice and networking session. The program will include brief reflections from current and former editors on their pathways to leadership, along with guidance on high-quality reviewing and pathways to editorial board service. The session will conclude with small-group breakout discussions to facilitate networking.
