Session overview

Generative AI is reshaping management theories across disciplines. This symposium bridges Information Systems, Strategy, and Entrepreneurship to explore how AI challenges foundational assumptions. We ask: Which theories need adjustment or reinvention? How do assumptions shift when non-human agents participate? Join us to redefine management theory for an AI-driven era featuring: Jay Barney, Anandhi Bharadwaj, Ann-Katrin Eicke, Satish Nambisan, Judy Rady, and Monideepa Tarafdar.
