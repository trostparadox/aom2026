Innovation Meets Inequality: Female Entrepreneurs’ Wellbeing in the Age of AI

People

Authors: Cheng Wang California State University-Sacramento; Laura Riolli

Session overview

Based on semi-structured interviews with female entrepreneurs in the technology industry, this study aims to explore how artificial intelligence (AI) facilitate female entrepreneurs navigate social barriers and institutional constraints. This study propose that AI is more than a neutral technological tool, but a socio-institutional tool that reshapes entrepreneurial dynamic for female entrepreneurs. By centering female entrepreneurs’ startup experiences, this study illustrates the specific mechanisms through which AI enables female entrepreneurs to establish professional identity, enhance self-efficacy, address legitimacy deficit, and access critical resources. This paper suggest that effective use of AI can improve female entrepreneurs’ economic and mental wellbeing.
