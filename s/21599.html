Session overview

AI is now embedded in student teamwork and professional collaboration, especially in remote and hybrid settings, yet it is often treated as a productivity shortcut rather than as a tool that reshapes how collaboration actually works. This PDW is a practical workshop for management educators who want to teach and assess collaboration through a relational lens: how trust forms, how voice is distributed, how accountability is negotiated, how coordination is sustained, and how conflict is mitigated and managed under digitally mediated conditions. In this PDW, relational dynamics are defined as how individuals and organizations co-create meaning and connection within relational contexts. This practical workshop equips management educators with a teachable framework and classroom-ready routines to develop (and assess) key relational conditions that determine collaboration quality in digitally mediated work: trust, voice, accountability, coordination, and conflict management. Participants engage with teaching routines that make these conditions visible and discussable, while distinguishing AI as both a facilitator (e.g., bridging cultural/linguistic gaps, improving workflow, supporting inclusivity) and as a disruptor (e.g., Western-centric biases, lack of global representation, misinterpretation, hegemonic classification, ethical concerns) of collaboration. Grounded in an AI literacy and relational capabilities approach, the workshop provides: (1) a learning outcomes insert; (2) facilitator debrief architecture; (3) a student-facing team agreement section on AI use (transparency, ethics, safeguards); (4) an assessment guideline; and (5) an evaluation plan. Virtual Exchange is used as an illustrative lens of practice-based team collaboration, providing a revealing example of digitally mediated remote intercultural teamwork. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
