Session overview

This PDW offers an immersive, field-based exploration of social enterprise, experiential learning, and purpose-driven organizational design through a site visit and facilitated dialogue with Saxbys—a Philadelphia-based education company known for its Experiential Learning Platform (E.L.P.®) and pioneering “Student CEO” model. In this model, undergraduate students hold paid, full-time operational leadership roles with responsibility for people, performance, and day- to-day decision-making, integrating management education and leadership development directly into business operations. Co-developed with Saxbys’ leadership and learning teams, the PDW centers on a structured, dialogue-based session at a Drexel University café location. Participants will engage directly with Saxbys’ approach to experiential learning, examining how responsibility, accountability, and development are embedded in everyday organizational practice. Discussion will explore how organizations design and govern experiential roles, navigate mission–market tensions, and align educational objectives with commercial performance. A facilitated debrief will connect insights from the site visit to research in responsible management education, leadership development, hybrid organizations, and organizational design. The session equips participants with both conceptual and pedagogical tools for studying and teaching experiential learning and purpose- driven organizing, along with concrete examples for integrating practice-based cases into research and classroom settings. 1.
