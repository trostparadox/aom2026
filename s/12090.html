Session overview

Generative AI systematically deliver inaccurate, unhelpful, uninnovative responses. Nonetheless, too many decision-makers trust AI (literally) without question. This symposium asks several experts on decision-making to describe their current research on how professionals apply disciplined rational reasoning and healthy skepticism to improve AI-powered decision-making. More importantly, the panelists will engage with each other to derive cross-disciplinary insights and agendas for future study.
