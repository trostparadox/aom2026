Session overview

AOM members seek to identify and solve management and organizational problems. AOM Today (aom.org/today) gives AOM members a platform to help narrow the research-practice gap and reach a global, non-academic audience interested in business news and workplace trends. This session will cover these topics: • Enough with the elites—what about regular folks? Thinking about employees’ perspectives, not just managers’ views. • Stick to the usual research suspects or search for hidden gems? Balancing demand for researching hot topics with breaking new ground. • When is it smart to be a little dumb? Thinking and talking about research in simple terms. • Questions from AOM members.
