Business Intelligence Influence on Banks’ Operational Performance - The Balance Scorecard Approach

Session overview

The research contributes to the ongoing discussions on the impact of business intelligence (BI) adoption within the banking industry by providing an in-depth analysis on how business intelligence influences organisational performance. It evaluates the effect of business intelligence adoption on the organisational performance of commercial banks in Nigeria across the four dimensions of the Balanced Scorecard: financial outcomes, customer satisfaction, internal processes, and learning and growth. A quantitative approach is deployed, using combining survey questionnaires on five strategic departments in the commercial banks in Nigeria. Descriptive analyses are conducted using SPSS, while inferential analyses are carried out through Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modelling (PLS-SEM) using SmartPLS. The findings enrich existing literature on BI adoption and provide practical guidance for managers and policymakers seeking to enhance organisational performance through data-driven decision-making. Furthermore, it offers a clearer understanding on BI potentials to support competitive advantage in a developing economy
