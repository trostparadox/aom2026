Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) offers an immersive exploration of leadership development, behavioural dynamics, and cultural influences through an African lens. Through presentations, interactive sessions, and facilitated discussions, participants will examine leadership philosophies, cultural influences, and identities across diverse African contexts. The workshop addresses how these dynamics create unique leadership challenges and opportunities in contemporary African contexts as participants navigate globalisation, technological transformation, and generational shifts. The PDW emphasises practical application, equipping participants with frameworks for culturally responsive leadership development programmes, cross-cultural management strategies, and contextually appropriate assessment tools. Scholars, practitioners, and organisational leaders working within or with African contexts will gain a nuanced understanding of regional variations across the continent while identifying common threads that distinguish African leadership paradigms. Ultimately, this workshop advances decolonised approaches to management theories and practice that centre African experiences and contributions to global leadership discourse.
