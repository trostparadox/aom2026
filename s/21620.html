Session overview

The primary objective of this PDW is to provide Academy Members with the opportunity to work together to address the challenge of designing a new, impact-focused MBA program that breaks free of current approaches to MBA program design. The MBA, as a degree program, is over one hundred years old, and current design approaches may not be adequate to address the challenges facing businesses and business schools today. By addressing the challenge from multiple perspectives and knowledge traditions, this PDW hopes to broaden our understanding of the challenges facing graduate management education, explore innovative and paradigm-shifting models of program design, and better inform future research and practice of management education. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
