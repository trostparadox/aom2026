Commercializing Emerging Technologies in Mature Fields: A Strategic Action Field Perspective

TIM
Shiwen Liu (Zhejiang University); Jia Wei (Zhejiang University)
Emerging technology commercialization faces challenges such as crossing the "valley of death" from technology to industrialization, acquiring cognitive legitimacy, and constructing industrial ecosystems. To reveal its underlying commercialization mechanisms, drawing on Strategic Action Field (SAF) theory, this study conducts an embedded case study to explore how challengers drive emerging technology commercialization and shape new sub-fields. Findings indicate that this commercialization follows a three-stage evolutionary process, synergistically driven by dual-dimensional strategic actions: cognitive-oriented sensemaking and resource-oriented actions. Concurrently, challengers undergo a dynamic role evolution from cross-domain pioneers to resource integrators and finally to ecosystem stewards, enabling new sub-field construction. This study enriches the literature on technology innovation and SAF by revealing the phased commercialization mechanism and highlighting micro-actor value.
