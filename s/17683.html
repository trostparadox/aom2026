Session overview

This symposium examines how AI, increasingly perceived as a socially embedded actor rather than a neutral tool, interacts with processes of social stratification in organizations and society. We explore two core questions (1) how do individuals infer socially consequential categories such as race, class, and status from AI and (2) does AI use alleviate or exacerbate existing inequalities? Across four papers using experimental, survey, and field methods, the symposium shows how social categorizations of AI shape trust, persuasion, and evaluations of humans, and how AI can both reinforce stratification and, under certain conditions, support equity-oriented outcomes.
