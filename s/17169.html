Session overview

Peer reviewing is the bedrock of the publication process and a critical component of academic service expected at research institutions. However, junior faculty often begin reviewing for journals with no formal training, learning through trial and error. This is costly for everyone involved: authors receive worse feedback, editors waste time sifting through disorganized reviews, and reviewers rarely receive feedback on mistakes to avoid or ways to improve. This OB Division spotlight PDW tackles these problems by systematizing the hidden curriculum of writing good peer reviews, with the goal of making the review process ultimately more efficient, effective, and rewarding. Designed primarily for micro-level OB junior faculty, this session will include presentations and interactive roundtable sessions from editors-in-chief and associate editors at top-tier OB-focused journals (i.e., Academy of Management Journal, Academy of Management Review, Journal of Applied Psychology, Personnel Psychology, Journal of Management, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, Organization Science). By the end of the workshop, participants will have actionable, editor-informed guidelines to improve their reviewing skills. As this session aims to facilitate meaningful conversations with editors, participation is limited to 75 people, and we ask that those interested in attending pre-register.
