Session overview

With fewer than five years remaining to achieve the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, this symposium explores how business education can be transformed to accelerate progress toward the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) while laying the foundations for a net-zero 2050.The session will feature award-winning educators, including recipients of the ONE Distinguished Educator Award and the ONE Early Career Teaching Award, and provide educators with innovative and impactful teaching practices that contribute to the achievement of SDGs. Panelists will draw on empirical evidence and reflective teaching experiences to illustrate how business education can be strategically aligned with the SDGs through curriculum design and pedagogical innovation. Particular attention is given to the interconnectedness among goals, highlighting synergies and trade-offs across the SDGs and their implications for business education. Participants will engage with systems-oriented and transition-focused educational approaches, including experiential learning, strategy role-play, and collaborative pedagogies that connect business education to real-world sustainability challenges such as climate change, biodiversity loss, and energy transitions. The discussion also addresses institutional, cultural, and contextual challenges in scaling these approaches across diverse educational settings. Overall, the symposium creates a collaborative platform for exchanging best practices and strengthening business education’s role in preparing learners to address both the near-term SDG agenda to 2030 and the longer-term net-zero transition toward 2050.
