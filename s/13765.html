Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) will focus on emerging research opportunities at the intersection of healthcare and entrepreneurship, a rapidly evolving domain shaped by technological innovation, shifting demographics, and new models of care delivery. Scholars play a critical role in examining how entrepreneurial activity can address pressing challenges in access, equity, innovation, and workforce transformation within the healthcare sector. This PDW offers participants insights into cutting-edge trends, under-explored research questions, and conceptual approaches that can inform impactful scholarship in this expanding field. By identifying new directions and deepening understanding of entrepreneurial phenomena in healthcare, researchers can contribute to more innovative, equitable, and sustainable health systems. A panel of experts representing diverse perspectives will discuss emerging trends, theoretical gaps, and areas of opportunity for scholars, guided by questions that highlight innovation, equity, data, and the intersection of practitioner and academic perspectives. Following the panel, participants will engage in an audience Q&A and brief “lightning reflections” designed to surface overlooked research opportunities and novel contexts for inquiry. The session will then move into panelist-led breakout circles, where participants rotate among small groups to explore research challenges, generate project ideas, identify relevant theories, and discuss feasible data sources. The workshop will conclude with a collective debrief that synthesizes the top emerging research topics and a brief collaboration-building segment to support continued idea development, while fostering networking opportunities.
