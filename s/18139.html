Interpreting Artificial Intelligence Signals: A Theoretical Framework on Stock Market Reactions

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 6
TIM

People

Authors: Oludare Oladimeji Fadare The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley; Wanrong Hou The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

Session overview

We draw on two complementary theoretical perspectives: signaling theory and sensemaking theory, to explain the market reactions to the announcements of further AI investment. Specifically, a theoretical framework was developed to explain the mechanisms and boundary conditions that shape stock market reactions to further AI investment announcements. We propose that the perceived strategic value of AI and analyst sentiment mediate the relationship between further AI investment announcements and investor responses. Furthermore, we argue that shareholder trust and the firm’s technological innovation capacity moderate these mediating effects. The theoretical and practical implications of our framework for research on strategic technology investments and investors behavior are discussed.
