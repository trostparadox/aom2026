Session overview

It has been over two decades since Bennis and O’Toole published their seminal work questioning how business schools lost their way by adopting a non-business facing scientific model. Today, we are here because that gap between 'rigorous' academic theory and 'raw' business reality has only grown more critical. We are seeing a lack of 'workplace readiness' in graduates and research that is often too slow to address disruptive shifts like AI or geopolitical instability. This PDW will explore the 'New Professional Business School'—a model where we treat industry as co-educators and knowledge partners. This session is designed to be a global exchange of 'strategic doing', with a primary focus on: 1. The Academic as the Driver: Assessing the specific skills you need to proactively develop external relationships. 2. Overcoming Barriers: Identifying proven strategies to dismantle the 'Ivory Tower' and balance research excellence with practical relevance. 3. Faculty Evolution: Discussing how we can redefine success and incentives to reward engaged scholarship and action-oriented research
