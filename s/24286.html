Strategic Focus in Decarbonization: Low-Carbon Specialization and Corporate Carbon Performance

ONE
Xueying Lin (Fudan University); Qinqin Zheng
How firms structure their low-carbon actions portfolios is increasingly critical for effective decarbonization. This study introduces the concept of low-carbon specialization, defined as the degree to which firms concentrate their low-carbon actions across multiple domains. Drawing on the attention-based view, we propose that low-carbon specialization exhibits an inverted U-shaped relationship with carbon performance, driven by opposing forces of efficiency gains from focused attention and opportunity costs arising from constrained coordination and learning. This relationship is intensified by TMT green experience and attenuated by firms’ absorptive capacity. By shifting attention from intensity to structure, this study advances climate strategy research.
