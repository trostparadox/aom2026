Appreciating Diversity in Entrepreneurial Ecosystems and Within Entrepreneurial Ecosystems

Tuesday, Aug 4 2026 · 8:00AM–9:30AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Franklin 6
ENTDEI

People

Participants: Laurel Steinfield; Wendy Cukier; Tania Saba University of Montreal; B Pick; Mohamed Elmi Toronto Metropolitan University; Azure Cook Ivey School of Business

Session overview

This panel explores how entrepreneurial ecosystem's (EEs) theories and practices can become more inclusive by examining two dimensions of diversity: diversity of EEs themselves and diversity and inclusion within them. We illuminate diversity of EEs by discussing how Global North enclaves and Global South informal economy EEs work similarly and differently than mainstream, geographically defined EEs. At the same time, we consider how existing EE structures may unintentionally reproduce intersectional inequities, even when aiming for inclusion. Through this integrative discussion, panelists will share insights on what works and what does not for equity-deserving entrepreneurs, uncover unintended consequences and identify wise and practical strategies for designing and theorizing about EEs that truly support diverse entrepreneurial participation.
