Too Many Cooks Spoil the Broth? The Impact of TMT Digi-al Expertise on Firms' Internationalization

IM
Tianyue Li (Central South University)
To better understand how top management teams’ (TMTs) digital expertise influ-ences firms’ internationalization in emerging economies, drawing on cognition theory, we use a panel dataset of listed firms in China from 2011 to 2023 and find an inverted U-shaped relationship between TMT digital expertise and internationalization. We fur-ther examine the boundary conditions that moderate this relationship by considering the unique context of emerging economies. The results show that regional digital economy development flattens the inverted U-shaped relationship, and integrative CEO leadership also weakens the inverted U-shaped relationship. Our findings contribute to the manage-rial cognition perspective on firms’ internationalization in emerging economies.
