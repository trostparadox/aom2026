Session overview

The Organizational Neuroscience and Biology (NeuB) interest group of the Academy of Management will be hosting a doctoral/early career consortium. It will take place on Friday, July 31, 2026, 8:30 am – 4:30 pm. The overall goal of the consortium is to provide attendees with guidance on how to incorporate neuroscience and biological concepts/methods into their own research. To accomplish this goal, the consortium will be highly interactive among presenters, facilitators, and attendees. The morning session will be open to the public, and we have a few showcases of the technology. Another session will feature an editor panel, during which we will invite journal editors to discuss the publication process. The afternoon portion of the consortium will feature senior scholar panlists and roundtable discussions. At each table, there will be a discussion of how neuroscience or biology concepts/methods might apply to the specific research interests or programs of individual attendees. Subject matter experts will be assigned as facilitators at respective tables. Attendance for the roundtable discussion is by invitation only. To be accepted for the consortium, individuals must: 1. Be a member of the NeuB Interest Group 2. Be either a doctoral student or a non-tenured faculty member 3. Submit an approximately 500-word summary of how the individual envisions neuroscience or biology concepts/methods as being specifically applicable to that person’s research interests or program (e.g., management topics that are of specific interest to the attendee, and how the attendee currently envisions the applicability of neuroscience or biology to those topics). Note that lunch will be provided. We encourage applications across a spectrum of interests, including attendees who identify as either “micro” or “macro”. If you have any questions, feel free to contact any of the 4 organizers. Please send your application to Danni Wang (dwang@business.rutgers.edu). In addition to the summary mentioned above, be sure to indicate whether you are a member of the NeuB Interest Group, your university, and your current position at that university.
