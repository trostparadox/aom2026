Session overview

As AI tools rapidly transform educational practices, grading and assessment represent both the greatest opportunity and the most significant challenge for instructors. Recent research shows AI usage for assessments surged from 53% to 88% in just one year (HEPI, 2025), while faculty integration remains minimal despite potential benefits. This interactive workshop provides hands-on experience with AI-assisted grading approaches that balance efficiency with pedagogical integrity. Drawing on practical implementation across diverse course formats, current research on AI grading effectiveness and limitations (Wetzler et al., 2024; Flodén, 2025; Ilieva et al., 2025), and evidence-based calibration strategies, participants will explore how to leverage custom AI models (such as GPT) for generating, consolidating, and critiquing feedback on student assignments, including essays and videos. The session addresses critical quality control needs, academic integrity concerns with AI-related misconduct increasing 400% (Goodier, 2025), and the ongoing tension between automation and personalized assessment. Through live demonstrations, case analyses, and collaborative problem-solving informed by a systematic review of 77 studies on AI grading (Discover Artificial Intelligence, 2025), educators will discuss strategies to integrate AI grading tools while maintaining academic standards, detecting AI-generated student work, and preserving the essential human elements of meaningful feedback. Participants will leave with an actionable framework, templates, and decision-making criteria for implementing AI-assisted grading in their own courses. Participant evaluations are invaluable.
