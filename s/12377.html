Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) equips participants with theoretically grounded, field-tested tools to transform performance management systems (PMS) from compliance rituals into engines of motivation, well-being, and sustainable excellence. Drawing on Goal Setting Theory (GST) and the systemic conceptualization of human beings, the workshop demonstrates how to redesign PMS so that it meaningfully engages employees’ psychological, cognitive, social, and spiritual dimensions for improved motivation and performance. Anchored in a live consulting case with a group of educational institutions in India, the PDW showcases a revamped PMS architecture and the Performance Enabler and Gratitude Journal as concrete exemplars of how robust theory can be translated into actionable tools that enhance focused effort, perseverance, and commitment through ongoing feedback and feedforward processes. Participants will experience a carefully sequenced set of experiential activities, reflective writing, peer sharing, and integrative discussions on diverse philosophical and religious views of human nature, which collectively challenge dominant assumptions underlying conventional PMS design. The session explicitly advances the Management Consulting Division’s mission by illustrating how consulting interventions can simultaneously generate client impact and theoretical refinement, while also speaking to OB, MED, SIM, CAR, CMS, and CM audiences interested in motivation, dignity, psychological safety, and conflict reduction in performance conversations. Attendees will leave with ready-to-use tools, design principles, and facilitation scripts that can be immediately adapted in their own institutional contexts and consulting to achieve breakthrough performance outcomes.
