Homo Sapiens Is Dead: Now What? Cultivating Homo Sentiens in Business Education (WITHDRAWN)

MED
Luc Glasbeek (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam); Ernst Graamans (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam)
Drawing on Michelangelo’s “Il Pensieroso” and Rodin’s “The Thinker” as symbols of embodied intellectual effort, this essay positions business education within a tradition that regards disciplined thinking as a defining human capability. The public release of ChatGPT in 2022 disrupted this foundation. Artificial intelligence (AI) systems now perform tasks that previously required sustained human intellectual effort, thereby shifting learning and thinking from individuals to large-scale socio-technical systems. However, this widespread delegation of intellectual effort to AI presents an existential risk for business schools and their students. In response, this essay links the concept of Homo sentiens with scholarly efforts to integrate secularized ancient philosophical principles into business education, providing a structured framework for navigating a world increasingly dominated by artificial intelligence. Thus, we reorient business education from knowing to being, from cognition to consciousness, and from optimization to relational and ethical engagement. This approach is cost-effective, scalable, sustainable, and culturally adaptable. Ultimately, what is at stake is not only the future of business education but also the future of human intellectual and moral agency.
