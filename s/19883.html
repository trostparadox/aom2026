Organizers: Jun Li University of New Hampshire; Liang (Arthur) Li Toronto Metropolitan University

Session overview

This caucus examines how CEOs’ political ideologies shape firm strategy amid geopolitical tensions. It integrates research from strategy, political science, and international business, synthesizing key mechanisms, boundary conditions, and measurement challenges in this emerging area. This caucus convenes an interdisciplinary community to share data and methods and catalyze collaborative research.
