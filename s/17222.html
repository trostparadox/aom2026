Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) builds on the growing movement of research at the intersection of religion and entrepreneurship [hereafter religious entrepreneurship]. Similar to other specialty areas (e.g., social entrepreneurship), there is limited focus on comparing and coordinating research agendas. Therefore, the PDW has three goals: 1.) to provide a seminar to educate early-career faculty about the opportunities and challenges of the growing field of religious entrepreneurship; 2.) to develop a network of scholarly peers interested in research on religious entrepreneurship; and 3.) to further develop research ideas on religious entrepreneurship towards potential publication. Our hope is to provide a pathway for publication for scholars interested in the growing subfield of religious entrepreneurship.
