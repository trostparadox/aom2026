Session overview

Are you looking for fresh, engaging activities to integrate into your human resources, management, leadership, communication or conflict management course? This workshop introduces two hands-on, interactive activities designed to help students actively practice navigating conflict. The first activity, Master Builder, emphasizes communication and leadership as participants collaborate to solve a puzzle. The second activity, Five Tricks, explores reactions to disagreement through a dynamic card game. Both activities have been student and instructor favorites, sparking meaningful discussions on conflict management strategies while fostering engagement, collaboration, and critical thinking.
