Session overview

Drawing on a case involving a controversial advertising campaign, the study explores how symbolic messaging in marketing campaigning can intersect with sensitive social issues related to race, inclusion, and historical power structures. Using the lens of social legitimacy, the study highlights how organizations navigate through the tensions between moral and pragmatic legitimacy. Additionally, the study examines how organizations employ legitimacy defense through symbolic clarification and reframing, aiming to distance themselves from negative discourse. This study contributes to discussions on corporate responsibility, ethical sensitivity, and the societal consequences of managerial decision-making.
