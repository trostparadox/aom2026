Digintelligence Leadership Scale Development: Focus on “Managing Employees” and “Managing AI”

OB
Wenyan Duan (Harbin Institute of Technology); Zhuojie Yang (Harbin Institute of Technology); Tung-Ju Wu (Harbin Institute of Technology)
Technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) are upgrading the “digital” to “digintelligence,” prompting leaders to reevaluate their approach and implement measures. In the conventional model, leaders primarily oversee employees; however, the pervasive implementation of AI necessitates leaders’ ability to manage both employees and AI. The advent of AI and other digintelligence technologies has given rise to novel demands and challenges for traditional leadership. However, prevailing conceptualizations and measurements of leadership have proven inadequate to address these practical demands. To address this issue, an exploration of digintelligence leadership in the AI era is necessary. In this multi-study investigation, we define digintelligence leadership as a multidimensional structure, emphasizing the multiple competencies leaders need in their work. Through a qualitative study (a semi-structured interview) and five quantitative studies, we found that digintelligence leadership comprises four dimensions: AI mastery competency, human-AI coordination competency, team catalyzing competency, ethical governance competency. We tested the reliability, convergent, discriminant, and predictive validity of the digintelligence leadership scale. This research develops a leadership measurement that is well-suited for the current era of AI. It assists managers in comprehending the novel capabilities afforded by AI, thereby enhancing management models and fostering effective collaboration between employees and AI.
