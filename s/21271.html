Session overview

This interactive PDW is designed for scholars aiming to publish in Organization Science. To ensure broad engagement across the Academy, we would like to adopt a strategy of rotating our primary sponsoring divisions. Following last year’s partnership with the Organizational Behavior (OB) division, this year’s workshop would ideally be held in collaboration with the Strategic Management (STR) division. While this year’s session highlights the STR community, the PDW remains committed to its identity as a premier outlet for the full spectrum of organizational research, welcoming diverse perspectives from across all management disciplines. The workshop consists of two key components: (1) a Roundtable Paper Development segment where editors and editorial board members provide direct, small-group feedback on research summaries; and (2) an Interactive Editor Panel addressing best practices, editorial standards, and the journal’s stance on data transparency and theoretical innovation. By engaging directly with the editorial team, participants—particularly doctoral students and junior scholars—will gain critical insights toward positioning their work for success. Our goal is to provide transparent guidance and foster an inclusive, supportive community for all Academy members seeking to contribute to Organization Science.
