Session overview

This session brings together Chinese management scholars and scholars interested in China-related research for mentoring, scholarly exchange, and professional development. The program features a keynote conversation and interactive roundtable discussions on dissertation development, publishing, job search, tenure and promotion, and career advancement across different career stages. Open to all doctoral students, junior faculty, and senior scholars.
