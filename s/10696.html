Session overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to machines carrying out cognitive tasks usually associated with human intelligence, such as problem solving, learning, and interacting. Recent advances in AI and machine learning technologies have led to an increased use of algorithmic tools by knowledge workers in organizations to augment their work. Not surprisingly, this also applies to management educators in business schools who are reshaping what and how they teach. In this PDW, we will explore implications of AI for strategic management teaching, from the strategic implications of AI for organizations to innovative teaching methods that make use of AI. This workshop brings together experts who are at the forefront of integrating AI into strategy content and strategy pedagogy, offering participants a deep and practical understanding of how to adapt their teaching to the age of AI.
