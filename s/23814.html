Perceived Organizational Support and Team Member Exchange in Multicultural Teams

OB
Chang-Lung Hsieh (Ming Chuan University); Chu-Chun Hsu (Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology); Tsai-Hsun Cheng (National Cheng Kung University)
Drawing on the social exchange theory, we propose that multicultural team workers who perceive a high level of organizational support will help teammates at work, thus increasing the individual’s experienced team member exchange. Integrating the culture literature, we propose that the indirect relationship between perceived organizational support and team member exchange via helping will be stronger when the individual possesses a low level of collectivistic cultural value. This indirect relationship will also be stronger when the individual perceives a low level of task interdependence. By analyzing matched data collected with a time lag from employees and their corresponding supervisors in multicultural teams among cross-national enterprises in Taiwan, we find support for our hypothesis in two studies. Our study complements the team member exchange literature by verifying perceived organizational support as its antecedent and helping behavior as the mediating mechanism. We also identify boundary conditions in the above relationship.
