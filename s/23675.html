Session overview

Artificial intelligence is transforming academic research, reshaping how studies are designed, data are collected, and knowledge is produced. While AI creates new opportunities for scale and efficiency, it also raises critical challenges for reproducibility, transparency, and data quality—key foundations of top-tier journal publishing. This session introduces OPENS (Open Panel Ecology Norming Studies), an open science infrastructure integrating large-scale online panels, longitudinal tracking, behavioral experiments, AI-assisted analytics, and multimodal methods. OPENS supports more rigorous, transparent, and scalable research, helping scholars produce high-quality evidence that meets the standards of leading journals in the AI era.
