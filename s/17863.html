Session overview

Heightened scrutiny of diversity initiatives in workplaces and universities, along with growing global inequality, raises critical questions about when and how inequality becomes institutionalized or contested. The presentations in this symposium expand the empirical and theoretical scope of how structures of inequality are created and maintained by foregrounding inequality as a dynamic process shaped by institutional logics, interpretations, resource dependencies, and power asymmetries. We intentionally bring together scholars with diverse methodological and theoretical perspectives that span individual, organizational, and institutional levels of analysis.
