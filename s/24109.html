Driving the AI Tide? Artificial Intelligence Adoption and MNEs’ International Expansion

IM
Ya Wang (Tongji University); siyao chen (Tongji University); Shouming Chen (Tongji University); Wenjia Du (Tongji University)
Although artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly adopted by firms, we know little about how AI adoption affects multinational enterprises’ (MNEs’) international expansion. Drawing on the dynamic capabilities view, we theorize that AI adoption enhances firms’ sensing, seizing, and reconfiguring capabilities, thereby enabling MNEs to manage the complexity and coordination challenges associated with cross-border expansion. We further argue that this relationship is contingent on managerial characteristics: international experience strengthens, whereas political connections weaken, the internationalization benefits of AI adoption. Using the staggered implementation of China’s New Generation Artificial Intelligence Pilot Zone policy as a quasi-natural experiment, we apply a difference-in-differences design to panel data on Chinese listed firms from 2016 to 2023. The findings support our theoretical arguments and contribute to research on AI and internationalization.
