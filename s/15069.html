Session overview

Despite growing calls to bring Africa in, African contexts and Africa-based scholars remain underrepresented in mainstream sustainability and management research, even as the continent is central to urgent grand challenges and rapidly evolving sustainability practices. This ONE panel symposium aims to connect and support scholars from Africa and those conducting sustainable development research about Africa. Panelists will address three themes: (1) the practical realities of conducting sustainability research in and about Africa, including data access, collaboration, methods, and ethics; (2) how structural, institutional, and linguistic barriers shape the visibility and impact of African sustainability scholarship and how scholars navigate them; and (3) how African research can advance management theory by enabling “theorizing from Africa,” beyond treating Africa as a field site for data extraction. Through panel discussions and dialogue with the audience, the symposium seeks to advance more inclusive and theoretically generative sustainability research.
