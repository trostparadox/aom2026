Session overview

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, including machine learning (ML), large language models (LLMs), and generative AI, is reshaping the methodological landscape of international management research. These technologies provide unprecedented opportunities to analyze complex phenomena, extract structured insights from vast corpora of unstructured global data, and generate new theoretical mechanisms. As AI becomes more accessible, scholars across subfields of management increasingly seek guidance on how to use AI rigorously, responsibly, and creatively as a research method. This symposium brings together four scholars at the frontier of methodological innovation in strategy, international management, information systems, entrepreneurship, and organizational research. The panelists will highlight concrete applications of AI, from ML-based matching to automated data extraction, literature analysis, text classification, and hypothesis discovery, and discuss the implications of these methods for causal inference, theory building, measurement, and research design. The discussion will emphasize methodological transparency, reproducibility, and the ethical considerations of AI-enabled research workflows.
