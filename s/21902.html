Session overview

This workshop will explore teaching practices that support free-market thinking and market-based management. Functional markets supported by private ownership of productive enterprises and limited government intervention promote innovation, competition, and social well-being. Each presenter in this workshop will share an exercise, case vignette, or another classroom approach grounded in a market orientation, emphasizing class interactions and discussions. Pre-registered participants will receive an electronic packet of readings in the months leading up to AOM to aid in their preparation for the event. Participants who do not register are also welcome.
