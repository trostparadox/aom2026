Session overview

The symposium will share lessons from a new $15 million initiative connecting 20 of the top US business schools' Nonprofit Board Fellows programs (NBFPs). The heads of three NBFPs, an educational nonprofit partner, and the new network connecting them will discuss the factors needed to build successful partnerships among schools and with nonprofit organizations. NBFPs match MBAs with local nonprofits where they serve as nonvoting members of the Board and deliver a pro bono strategic consulting project.
