Are Supportive Families Entrepreneurs’ Secret Weapons? Linking Family Support to Proactive Behavior

ENT
Tingting Niu (University of Science and Technology of China); Song Wu
This article investigates the influence of emotional/instrumental family-to-business support on proactive behavior via entrepreneurial passion and the moderating effect of promotion/prevention focus. Findings from a 320-entrepreneurs survey in China indicate that (1) emotional and instrumental family-to-business support is positively associated with proactive behavior; (2) entrepreneurial passion mediates the relationship between emotional/instrumental family-to-business support and proactive behavior; and (3) promotion focus strengthens the relationship between emotional family-to-business support and entrepreneurial passion. These findings provide novel insights into why and for whom family emotional/instrumental support is (not) beneficial. Implications and directions for future research are also discussed.
