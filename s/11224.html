Presenter: Shannon Caldwell

Session overview

In the practical reality of organizational transformation, a critical gap exists between the C-suite's strategic "why" and the front-line's operational "how." This workshop moves beyond abstract theory to introduce a framework for managing this tension by view change through the lens of a power grid. Just as high-voltage transmission lines cannot connect directly to household appliances without blowing the circuits, raw strategic urgency often overwhelms the workforce. Successful change requires two distinct, intentional forces. Senior leaders must act as the generators, creating dissonance (high voltage) to disrupt the status quo and signal the need for change. In tandem, front-line leaders must serve as the "step-down transformers." They create resonance by converting that high-voltage message into usable energy that powers day-to-day operations. This session focuses on the applied mechanics of this vertical alignment. Participants will learn to design strategic communications that generate the necessary urgency without triggering fear, and how to equip front-line leaders with the "translation protocols" needed to synchronize that message with local realities. Through role-specific simulations, scholars and practitioners will examine how to balance these opposing forces, moving the conversation from managing change to facilitating it at scale.
