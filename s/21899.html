Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) explores how faculty with deep practitioner backgrounds can meaningfully integrate industry experience into the classroom through the systematic use of the case writing and case teaching method. Drawing on the organizer’s professional career in global marketing leadership roles and her experience designing an entire semester of strategy around faculty-authored cases, this workshop demonstrates how a practitioner lens can transform marketing education into an immersive, decision-centered learning experience. While academics are rigorously trained in research and incentivized to publish, many lack formal training or experience in crafting case studies. The PDW highlights how cases can serve as a bridge between theory and lived managerial reality, enabling students to engage with ambiguity, trade- offs, stakeholder tensions and incomplete data, conditions that mirror real-world decision making. Special attention will be given to international and emerging market context through the featured case, Barista Expresso: Preserving Premium Branding or Seizing a Mass-market Opportunity. This case illustrates how geopolitics, currency instability, family business dynamics and brand positioning intersect in strategic marketing decisions. Participants will leave with a concrete pedagogical framework for designing a full-semester course around the case method, tools for sourcing and writing cases from practioner networks, and guidance on coaching students as case writers. This PDW invites academics at all experience levels to reimagine the role of case writing in their scholarly and pedagogical endeavors. This PDW is especially relevant for scholars seeking to increase the practical impact of their teaching, align with AACSB and AOM impact expectations, and deepen student engagement.
