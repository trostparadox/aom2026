Contextual Strategy and Workplace Mediation Effectiveness Across Seven Countries

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 2
CM

People

Author: Jorge Cea Austral University

Session overview

This study examines the effectiveness and strategies of mediation in different countries within the context of human resource management, focusing on conflicts based on interests and rights. A 2x2 experimental design manipulated the type of conflict and third-party affiliation (ingroup vs. outgroup). Factor analysis supported a three-dimensional model of mediation strategies. The results reveal cross-cultural differences in strategy use and perceived effectiveness, with Chilean and Spanish mediators reporting greater use of contextual strategies and higher effectiveness than mediators from other countries. Mediation analyses confirmed that contextual strategies mediate the relationship between the type of mediation and its effectiveness.
