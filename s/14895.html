Which Leaders, Which Followers? A Social Cognitive Model of Coaching Leadership and Job Crafting

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 7
OB

People

Author: Dahyun Lee Sungkyunkwan University

Session overview

Coaching leadership is widely associated with positive employee outcomes, yet the cognitive mechanisms through which it fosters proactive work behavior remain unclear. Drawing on Social Cognitive Theory, we develop a process model in which coaching leadership reduces role ambiguity by clarifying role expectations, thereby enabling proactive job crafting. We further propose that Positive Psychological Capital strengthens the cognitive processing of coaching cues, whereas work autonomy enables the enactment of clarity-driven intentions. Using time-lagged survey data from full-time employees across diverse organizations (N = 611), we test a dual-stage moderated mediation model. Our findings advance the coaching leadership literature by introducing cognitive micro-foundations and clarifying when and why coaching leadership translates into job crafting.
