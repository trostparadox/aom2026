Session overview

International experiences, in the forms of visiting positions, international research fellowships, sabbaticals, among other types, are commonly pursued by scholars across all career stages. These teaching-, research-, or mixed-focused short-term visits advance scholars’ career development and personal well-being through building international collaborations, developing teaching and research expertise, and shaping their global mindsets. Meanwhile, the academic environment has been evolving rapidly, posing challenges to plan for an international experience. To provide practical advice to scholars in various regions globally, this PDW is designed to offer tips on when to plan for an international experience, where to go, which type of international experience should we consider, and for how long, along with other region-specific tips and suggestions. Through a panel discussion with invited scholars from different parts of the world and with international experience in various locations, followed by a few roundtables targeting different regions, this PDW aims to provide actionable tips and resources for participants interested in planning an international experience.
