Session overview

Trust repair has become critical to maintaining coordination for individuals, teams, and organizations. The nature of trust violations threatens interpersonal processes and can undermine relationships and work. Drawing on interdisciplinary research from psychology, management, and engineering scholars, this panel explores the core mechanisms of trust and trust repair and how these processes vary by context. It demonstrates that the effectiveness of trust repair strategies is contingent on the nature of the violation, actors, and technological environment. Overall, this panel seeks to advance a more integrative understanding of trust repair by identifying common and context specific constraints and strategies that facilitate the restoration of trust, while also offering practical implications and directions for future research.
