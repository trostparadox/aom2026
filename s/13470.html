Session overview

Biodiversity underpins environmental, social, and economic well-being and is increasingly central to organizations (Panwar et al., 2024; Unter et al., 2023) and supply chains (Gualandris et al., 2024; Salmi et al., 2023). Addressing biodiversity loss requires people’s engagement to integrate biodiversity knowledge and ecological, cultural, and social values into corporate biodiversity management (Azizi et al., 2025). This workshop explores the interdependence among people, places, and nature in achieving effective and enduring corporate biodiversity outcomes. Building on the premise that people protect what they value, it examines how place-based values and people’s involvement in organizational decision-making contribute to corporate biodiversity management and advance socio-ecological systems. Participants engage with this question through four interrelated themes: (a) people’s engagement as a continuous socio-ecological process that lead to efficient corporate management; (b) strengthening environmental stewardship of the corporates; (c) operationalizing social learning and capacity building for proactive corporate conservation and empowerment; and (d) linking place-based values with biodiversity outcomes to ensure long-term corporate persistence. Using a World Café format, expert-led discussions and collaborative reflections enable participants to share experiences, co-develop research pathways, and identify opportunities for future research collaboration.
