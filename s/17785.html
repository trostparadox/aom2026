Session overview

Artificial intelligence is reshaping how consultants work, yet most researchers studying consulting have never experienced these emerging workflows firsthand. This workshop bridges that gap by immersing participants in AI-augmented consulting, demonstrating what becomes possible when consultants leverage AI tools for research synthesis, structuring, and communication. The session introduces The Consultant’s OS, a framework mapping eight consulting competencies across four imperatives: Think Clearly, Get to the Right Answer, Move Work Forward, and Create Impact with People. Participants will experience how AI amplifies what a consultant is able to do. Through hands-on exercises, attendees will work a condensed case using AI-assisted workflows experiencing both the acceleration AI enables and the human judgment it cannot replace. AI handles 80% quickly, but the last 20% (judgment, client context, stakeholder trust) remains the consultant’s distinctive value-add. This workshop serves researchers by providing experiential understanding of an emerging work paradigm. Rather than reading about AI transformation, participants will do the work themselves, generating perspectives on questions like: How might AI reshape expertise hierarchies? What happens to apprenticeship models? How do clients perceive AI-assisted recommendations? Participants leave with practical workflow exposure, prompt templates, and research questions worth pursuing.
