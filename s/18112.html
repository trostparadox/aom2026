Session overview

This syposium presents findings from four working manuscripts, which collectively provide varying approaches to exploring the fluctuating mental health of employees in relation to different facets of the workplace. The methods used for this body of new research include ESM, multi-source and multi-wave survey, as well as theory development and meta-synthesis. The papers focus on a variety of subtopics, including CEO mental health, uncertainty and anxiety, autonomy and psychological well-being, and the interventions and social support sources that can be leveraged to address employees with diagnosable mental health conditions. This symposium seeks to contribute to the study of employee mental health as a continuum, and it aims to provide novel insights as they pertain to both the average employee as well as in employees with common mental health conditions.
