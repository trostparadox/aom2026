Session overview

Publishing in peer-reviewed journals is a central component of academic development and scholarly identity. For non-U.S. scholars in particular, publishing in top-tier U.S.-based journals presents both substantial career opportunities and distinct challenges, including differences in scholarly conventions, review expectations, and editorial norms. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is explicitly designed as a developmental and mentoring-oriented forum for non-U.S. scholars seeking to strengthen their capacity to publish in top-tier U.S. journals in the Human Resources field. The workshop combines (1) experiential insights from senior scholars with extensive editorial and publishing experience; and (2) individualized, in-depth developmental feedback on participants’ manuscripts. The PDW emphasizes learning, skill-building, and confidence development rather than evaluation, positioning reviewers as mentors and coaches who work collaboratively with authors to enhance the quality and publishability of their work.
