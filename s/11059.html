Session overview

This symposium shifts our focus to less-studied and more diverse entrepreneurial phenomenon by bringing together four studies that examine how entrepreneurs create and sustain value in challenging contexts, highlighting a variety of institutional influences and entrepreneurial strategies. The studies provide a range of theoretical frameworks, methodologies, levels of analysis, and geographical contexts spanning Jordan, Brazil, Canada, and Guatemala. Together, they address an interesting set of questions and advance our knowledge about how macro factors such as institutional policies and practices as well as micro factors such as entrepreneurs’ capabilities and community connections affect venture outcomes with implications for entrepreneurial actions, firms’ choices, and policy decisions. The symposium should be of interest to scholars of entrepreneurship, strategy, and social issues in management.
