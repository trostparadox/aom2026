OB & IM Division Social: Sunset Riverboat Tour

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 5:30PM–8:00PM
Offsite — Penn's Landing
OB

People

Organizers: Robert Litchfield Washington and Jefferson College; Minna Marinova Paunova ESCP Business School

Session overview

This joint social event, sponsored by the Organizational Behavior (OB) Division Global Committee and the International Management (IM) Division, invites scholars to connect aboard a sunset river tour on the Ben Franklin Yacht. Designed as an inclusive and informal networking space, the event creates opportunities for international scholars, newcomers, and globally minded colleagues to build meaningful professional relationships beyond formal sessions. The gathering encourages cross-regional exchange and community-building within the broader AOM network. Boarding begins at 5:30pm. The boat departs promptly at 6:00pm. This event requires separate registration and ticket purchase. Participants must register in advance and pay the event fee (55.00 USD)
