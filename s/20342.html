Session overview

This symposium will feature a series of presentations on the cutting edge of entrepreneurial communication, with a specific focus on its interactional nature. This symposium builds on insights from a well-attended panel symposium at the 2025 AOM annual meeting (titled “Trends and Tensions in Entrepreneurial Communication Research”). In the spirit of that panel, we bring together four presenters whose work will move scholarship past unit theories of a set of variables towards a holistic understanding of a complex, multilateral, audience-sensitive process of entrepreneurial communication. The presentations in this symposium will thus bridge between entrepreneur and investor vantage points (Kalvapalle, Phillips, and Cornelissen, 2024), leaving behind unitary approaches to the study of entrepreneurial communication to pursue instead the dynamic, relational nature of entrepreneur-investor interactions. This interactional focus will address limitations to existing theorizing in entrepreneurial communication, the most notable ones being audience heterogeneity, temporality, and the contingent, dynamic nature of communication for resource acquisition. Short presentations of each paper, followed by lively discussion moderated by a senior expert on the topic and Q&A from the audience will reflect on topics of theoretical trends and tensions, evidence, methodology, and societal consequences of this interactional focus, specifically what it might mean for interactionally level the playing field.
