Session overview

There is a growing interest in research on entrepreneurial ecosystems among scholars of entrepreneurship. The scholarly contributions in the field have helped clarify the concept of entrepreneurial ecosystems, their definition, and the factors that lead to their emergence. Research on entrepreneurial ecosystems in the context of developed economies has grown significantly over the years. However, many critical gaps exist in the research on such ecosystems in the context of emerging markets. Our Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to provide impetus to this stream of research, with a specific focus on India, one of the leading emerging economies. The entrepreneurial community in India faces numerous challenges; therefore, it is essential to strengthen the country's entrepreneurial ecosystems through robust empirical research and targeted policy interventions. This PDW delves deep into the critical aspects of this stream of research. Leading scholars in entrepreneurship will share their insights on research into entrepreneurial ecosystems in the Indian context. We aim to provide a forum for constructive discussions that may lead to valuable outcomes. This is a great opportunity to delve deep into the entrepreneurial ecosystems with a special focus on India. This PDF presents an opportunity to discuss different research areas relating to entrepreneurial ecosystems with the panelists in small groups. It also offers an opportunity to gain valuable insights into entrepreneurial ecosystems and to share your thoughts.
