Leveraging GINs for Technological Catch-up of CoPs: Evidence from Chinese CNC Machine Tool Industry

TIM
Dong Guo (Communication University of China); yifan Mo (Communication University of China); Yangyang Deng (Communication University of China)
In the era of innovation globalization, global innovation networks (GINs) are having an increasingly important impact on innovation activities. As a growing number of enterprises in latecomer countries use the talents, scientific and technological resources brought by the GIN to promote the catch-up of complex product systems (CoPs), this study investigates the characteristics and mechanisms of GIN embeddedness that facilitate the technological catch-up of CoPs. Taking the high-grade Computer Numerical Control Machine Tool (CNCMT) industry in China as a case, we extract the characteristics, mechanisms and countermeasures of effectively using GIN to realize technological catch-up of CoPs. Furthermore, we analyse the embeddedness into the GIN in different historical stages of technological catch-up, considering factors of R&D, transformation and commercialization. We find that continuously strengthening the embeddedness of GIN for R&D, pilot and demonstration plants, and commercialization is an important external factor in promoting the catch-up of CoPs in emerging economies, and effective guidance of innovation policies is a prerequisite for promoting the catch-up of CoPs with embeddedness in GIN.
