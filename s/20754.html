Smart Cities and the Expansion of Innovation Collaboration Networks

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 4:00PM–5:00PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 4
TIM

People

Author: Yidi WANG Communication University of China

Session overview

Smart city initiatives are widely promoted as engines of urban innovation, yet little is known about how they reshape firms’ innovation collaboration networks. Drawing on panel data from Chinese cities between 2016 and 2022, this study examines how smart city construction affects the expansion of innovation collaboration networks along two dimensions: actor expansion and geographic expansion. We argue that smart city construction enhances digital infrastructure and information connectivity, thereby reducing coordination costs and search frictions in innovation collaboration. We construct a novel city-level smart city construction measure based on government procurement award data, capturing the actual implementation intensity of smart city initiatives. Innovation collaboration networks are identified using jointly applied patents. Empirical results show that smart city construction significantly increases firms’ likelihood of forming collaborations with new partners and expands the geographic scope of innovation collaboration networks. This study contributes to the literature by conceptualizing smart cities as digital and institutional platforms that reconfigure the structure and geography of innovation networks. Methodologically, it introduces a behavior-based measure of smart city construction grounded in public procurement data.
