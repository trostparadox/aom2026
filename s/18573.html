Session overview

Belonging has long been understood as a fundamental human need, most often met through personal and social relationships outside of work. Over time, however, changes in how work is organized and experienced have shifted the role that work plays in people’s relational lives. Hybrid, virtual, and flexible work arrangements have altered how employees interact with one another, reducing many informal connections that once supported day-to-day relationships. At the same time, organizations have increasingly turned toward inclusion and belonging efforts as ways to foster respect, trust, and connection across diverse workforces. This symposium advances an integrative exploration of employee belonging in the workplace, focusing on the relational foundations of human motivation and invites more rigorous scholarly consideration of belonging as an important consideration in leader–employee relationships, leadership behavior, and team relational systems.
