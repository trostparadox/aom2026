Session overview

The world and the lives of faculty are rapidly changing. There is political and social division in many parts of the world, and the use of AI has become widespread. Faculty today need to navigate these turbulent times and be able to deal with the increasing demands in providing a quality education that prepares students to function at a high level in organizations, all while resources become increasingly scarce. This 8th Annual Teaching Bootcamp at AOM addresses these challenges and provides tools and resources to help you effectively and efficiently meet and manage these demands while maintaining your own well-being. This highly interactive and engaging session features a variety of topics related to teaching across all management disciplines, presented by experienced educators in a roundtable format. We focus on innovative course management practices, policies, and techniques to help you navigate today’s challenges and ensure your classes are purposeful and provide the best classroom experience possible to equip students as current/future workers to meet workplace and societal challenges today and beyond. As usual, we expect this to be an interactive, informative, energizing session, so come join the fun as we explore ways to address how management education can harness AI, enhance LMS use, engage students, and effectively manage classroom dynamics. You will leave the session with new teaching resources to assist you in effectively and efficiently designing and delivering your classes in today’s environment.
