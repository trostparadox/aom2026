Session overview

Visibility is central to academic success, influencing credibility, collaboration, and career progression. Yet, how visibility is achieved and perceived varies greatly across gender, career stages, and communication channels. While many discussions have focused mainly on women scholars, this Professional Development Workshop (PDW) broadens the lens to include all genders, highlighting how norms around visibility differ across genders. Building on the success of last year’s PDW, which showed strong participant turnout and engagement as well as recurring questions about inclusivity and gendered visibility, this year’s session introduces a more interactive and gender-balanced format. Short opening keynotes by scholars from different genders and career stages set the stage for a dynamic panel discussion, followed by an interactive Q&A that encourages participants to co-create insights and strategies. This PDW provides a reflective discussion and practical tools to help participants navigate visibility challenges across: • Gender perspectives: exploring how different genders experience, enact, and support visibility differently • Career stages: understanding changing expectations and opportunities from PhD to full professor levels • Media and channels: comparing traditional academic visibility (publications, conferences, service) with social media and various opportunities of self-promotion. Ultimately, the PDW seeks to foster a more inclusive and reflective conversation on visibility, one that benefits all scholars, establishes easy ways to increase visibility, and reduces stereotype-driven barriers. By engaging with these themes, participants will gain insights into navigating the complexities of visibility and leveraging it as a tool for career growth and leadership development.
