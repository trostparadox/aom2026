Session overview

This symposium explores how multiteam systems (MTSs) adapt and sustain performance under conditions of disruption, ranging from technological change and organizational restructuring to crises and environmental shocks. As work increasingly occurs in dynamic, interdependent systems of teams, understanding how MTSs maintain alignment and resilience amid disruption is becoming a pressing theoretical and practical challenge. The papers in this symposium collectively seek to advance our understanding of the mechanisms, boundary conditions, and temporal dynamics that shape MTS effectiveness under disruptive events. Together, they offer fresh insights into how adaptive processes, leadership structures, and goal configurations unfold when the stability of the broader system is threatened, illuminating new directions for theory and research on collective performance in turbulent contexts.
