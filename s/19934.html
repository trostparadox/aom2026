Patent Litigation and Firm Innovation: A Configurational Perspective of Dynamic Competition

Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 5
TIM

People

Authors: Yougen Cao Anhui University; Shengce Ren Tongji University; Lanxuanjie Zhou Tongji University

Session overview

Patent litigation, as a critical component of the innovation protection system, continues to provoke unresolved theoretical debates and mixed empirical findings regarding its impact on firm innovation. Most existing studies are grounded in static and single-factor perspectives, lacking a dynamic and integrative analysis. Drawing on the dynamic competition perspective, this study develops an analytical framework centered on the Awareness-Motivation-Capability (AMC) model. It examines five key antecedent conditions: technological similarity, market commonality, litigation losses, slack resources, and technological capabilities. Employing fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA), this study uncovers the multiple configurational paths through which defendant firms adjust their innovation investment strategies in response to patent litigation. The findings demonstrate that it is not patent litigation per se that drives innovation responses, but rather the structural configurations and interrelationships among antecedent conditions. Specifically, three distinct strategic paths emerge among high-innovation defendant firms: a technology-driven proactive path, a market-driven defensive path, and a dual-driven path that combines technological and market stimuli. In contrast, low-innovation firms are typically hindered by awareness deficiency, motivation insufficiency, resource constraints, or capability bottlenecks. By advancing a configurational understanding of how firms navigate innovation decisions under litigation pressure, this study contributes to the literature on innovation strategy and intellectual property management. It also offers practical implications for corporate litigation strategies and provides theoretical insights for policymakers seeking to foster a more supportive innovation ecosystem.
