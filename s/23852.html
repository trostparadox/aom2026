Performance Management Behavior and Feedback Seeking: The Roles of LMX and Feedback Environment

OB
Yu Wei (Tianjin University); Xiyang Zhang (Tianjin University); Hui Liu (Macau University of Science and Technology)
Building on the trend toward the routinization of performance management (PM) and the research gap concerning the effectiveness of the modern form of PM, this study conducted a questionnaire survey and collected data from 217 employees to empirically examine the underlying mechanism and boundary conditions through which performance management behavior affects employees’ feedback-seeking behavior. The results indicate that performance management behavior has a significant positive effect on employees’ feedback-seeking behavior; leader -member exchange mediates this relationship; and feedback environment positively moderates the link between performance management behavior and feedback-seeking behavior, such that the direct effect becomes stronger when feedback environment is more supportive. By highlighting the interplay between managerial behavior and contextual cues, this study advances understanding of how employees’ feedback-seeking behavior is shaped and provides theoretical grounding and practical implications for organizations aiming to en-courage proactive feedback seeking in daily management.
