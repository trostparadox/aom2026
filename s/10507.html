Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) explores Entrepreneurship of the Soul as a spiritually grounded, relational, and action-oriented approach to management, leadership, and governance. Anchored in Spiritual Politics and the 4P4R Creative Governance Framework (Person, Place, Policy, Partnership; Rescue, Revive, Revise, Rebuild), the workshop addresses a central challenge in contemporary organizations: how to move from fear to trust while navigating complexity, conflict, and creative tensions. Drawing on interdisciplinary research and field-based praxis from the Spiritual Politics Lab, the PDW integrates ancestral and indigenous wisdom and embodied knowing into management theory and practice, with special attention to fear and emotions as core governance variables shaping leadership, power, and organizational culture. Participants engage as entrepreneurs, co-researchers, and knowledge holders designing new forms of organizational and societal value. Through Creative Aerobics and contemplative practices, participants learn to regulate fear, activate trust, and generate innovative responses to governance challenges. The workshop culminates in a 4P4R Co-Creation Lab linking inner transformation to outcomes such as peacebuilding, trustbuilding, and democratic vitality.
