Communicating Effectively with AI in Healthcare: The Role of Construal Level and Message Framing

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 4:00PM–5:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 1

Authors: Fiona Kubowitsch University of Regensburg; Eva Lermer Technical University of Applied Sciences Augsburg

Session overview

Individuals are increasingly using artificial intelligence (AI) systems to seek medical advice. However, it has not yet been systematically studied which combinations of design factors and psychological mechanisms increase the persuasiveness of AI-generated medical advice compared to human advice. Drawing on Construal Level Theory and Prospect Theory, this study addressed this gap by examining how the interactions of the source of advice (AI vs. human), construal level (low vs. high), and message framing (loss vs. gain) affect individuals’ behavioral intentions to follow AI-generated medical advice. Furthermore, the roles of perceived message fit and perceived psychological distance as additional explanatory mechanisms were analyzed. The study employed a 2 × 2 × 2 between-subjects design using an online vignette-based survey (N=767). Our results showed that behavioral intentions were higher when AI-generated advice emphasized low construal features or was framed as a loss. Additionally, perceived psychological distance emerged as a significant moderator, whereas perceived message fit served as a significant mediator. When perceived psychological distance was high, participants reported stronger behavioral intentions when the advice was generated by an AI system. This result implies that a strong sense of personal closeness may not be required for AI-based persuasion. Furthermore, participants who perceived the message as more fitting reported stronger intentions, and overall, perceived message fit accounted for approximately two-thirds of the variance in behavioral intentions. These findings advance our understanding of how AI-based health advice systems can be effectively implemented by identifying factors that promote successful communication of AI-generated medical advice.
