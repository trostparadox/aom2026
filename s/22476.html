MED Keynote Panel Discussion: Teaching, Publishing, and Impact: What Comes Next for Management Education?

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 3:00PM–4:00PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — 105B
MED

People

Organizer: Mairead Brady Trinity College Dublin, The University of Dublin
Speakers: Andrew Jack Financial Times; Lucy Swedberg Harvard Business School; Amon Barros FGV EAESP; Diego Coraiola University of Victoria

Session overview

Where is management education heading, what are we choosing to value, and what might we be getting wrong? This keynote brings together some of the most influential voices shaping the future of the field, spanning global business media, editorial leadership, cutting-edge research, and the classroom, for a frank, forward-looking conversation that the field needs to have. Management education is at an inflection point. Artificial intelligence is reshaping what we teach and how we teach it. Questions about relevance, rigour, and reach have never been louder. And the gap between what happens in our classrooms and what appears in our journals has never been harder to ignore. The panel includes Andrew Jack, Global Education Editor of the Financial Times, one of the most authoritative voices on where business education is heading and what the world outside the academy expects of it; Lucy Swedberg, Executive Editor and Senior Editorial Director at Harvard Business Publishing, who brings an editorial perspective on the ideas, formats, and forms of scholarship that reach and resonate with wider audiences; Professor Amon Narciso Barros of FGV EAESP, one of Latin America's leading business schools, who offers a global perspective on how management knowledge is shaped, challenged, and renewed in an increasingly complex and unequal world; and Professor Diego Coraiola of the University of Victoria, Editor-in- Chief of the Academy of Management Learning and Education, whose work spans the field's leading journals and who brings a scholarly publishing perspective to questions of rigour, relevance, and impact. The panel is chaired by Professor Mairead Brady, Trinity College Dublin and Chair of the MED Division. This is not a session about celebrating the field. It is a session about challenging it. Come ready to think, to disagree, and to leave with something that changes how you approach your work. Together, these four perspectives will open a conversation the field needs to have.
