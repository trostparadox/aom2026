OSCM

People

Coordinators: Anand Nair Northeastern University; Katie S. Wowak University of Notre Dame; Annachiara Longoni ESADE Business School; Desirée Van Dun Politecnico di Milano; Veronica Haydee Villena Arizona State University

Session overview

All OSCM Division members are invited to join this meeting during which the OSCM Division executive committee will share the latest information about our division's (current and future) strategic developments. The annual business meeting serves as a great platform to get up to speed with our division and explore opportunities to serve our division. At the end of the session there will be space for schools to announce calls for applicants and job- market candidates to present themselves. After the meeting, we will have our major social event. We look forward to seeing you there!
