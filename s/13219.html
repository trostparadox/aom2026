Session overview

Academic performance is judged in part on one’s level of linguistic competence in English. As a consequence, scholars who wish to succeed in the academic field must be able to write in English not only fluently but also eloquently. This places non-native English scholars at a disadvantage. The goals of this professional development workshop (PDW) are to discuss the challenges non-native English scholars face when writing and publishing in English and to share concrete tips for improving academic writing in English. We bring together a panel of experienced, well-published management scholars with non-native English backgrounds who approach the theme from diverse perspectives. In the first segment of the PDW, our six panelists will share their experiences and insights into the art of writing, publishing, and collaborating as non-native English scholars. In the second part of the PDW, we will hold a practice-oriented roundtable discussion to delve into the issues presented in the first segment of the workshop. We will conclude the PDW with a session-wide Q&A and a summary of lessons learned. No registration is required. Anyone interested in learning from discussions on strategies for overcoming the challenges faced by non-native English scholars in writing and publishing is welcome.
