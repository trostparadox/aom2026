Session overview

This workshop addresses how artificial intelligence transforms not merely work processes but the criteria by which we evaluate valuable knowledge and effective intervention. We introduce axiological displacement to characterize how AI systems recursively reshape value frameworks within which consulting operates. The workshop examines four features—recursivity, opacity, speed, and meta-automation—and their implications for practice. Participants engage with a central paradox: while AI enables unprecedented abundance in analytical outputs, it creates scarcity in direct observation. When consultants rely on AI-generated insights and clients experience organizations through algorithmic dashboards, what becomes of the observational foundations giving advice its validity? The workshop combines theoretical exposition with interactive exercises helping participants recognize axiological displacement, develop strategies for preserving observational capacity, and articulate value propositions acknowledging AI’s effects. Through dialogue between scholars and practitioners, we explore how consulting can navigate these challenges while maintaining commitment to actionable, grounded advice. Participants leave with practical frameworks including axiological audit checklists and value transparency protocols.
