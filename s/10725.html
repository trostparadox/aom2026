Session overview

This symposium brings together four papers synthesizing four distinct perspectives: individual motives, organizational learning, and ecosystem orchestration and dynamics, to explore the specific boundary conditions where the interests of users and producers diverge, align, or co-evolve. By examining these boundaries, we offer a more nuanced theory of innovation that delineates when firms should integrate user input and when they should maintain distance for the best interest of the whole ecosystem.
