Session overview

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into human resource management (HRM) is revving and is likely to become inevitable in the coming years. AI has already begun transforming traditional HRM processes and continues to evolve (Raisch & Krakowski, 2021). Human Resource Management contributes to superior organizational performance by integrating employee capabilities with strategic goals through systematic recruitment, training, performance management, and cultural development, resulting in increased organizational efficiency and a sustained competitive advantage (Tong et al., 2021). In emerging economies such as India, organizations adopting AI-enabled HR practices face new opportunities and challenges related to efficiency, fairness, transparency, and human–AI collaboration in managerial and HR decision-making (Leavitt et al., 2024). Despite growing practitioner adoption, scholarly understanding of how AI reshapes HRM practices in the Indian context remains fragmented. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to bridge this gap by bringing together academics, practitioners, and doctoral scholars to critically examine AI-driven HRM practices in India. In the context of India, the PDW will explore applications of AI across key HR functions, examine the strategic and behavioral implications of human–AI collaboration and address the organizational and ethical challenges associated with AI adoption. Thus, this PDW aims to advance context-sensitive theorizing and promote future research on AI and HRM in emerging economies.
