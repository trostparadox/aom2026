Session overview

This workshop aims to explore research frontiers in the increasingly active area of nonmarket strategy. Through a combination of panel presentations by distinguished scholars and interactive roundtable discussions with workshop participants, we seek to identify how to advance research and how to address some shortcomings in existing approaches – both empirical and theoretical – in nonmarket strategy research. Roundtables will provide a venue for participants to receive feedback on work in progress from leading scholars in the field. The proposed workshop is likely to be of interest to researchers working on topics such as corporate social responsibility, political strategy, (social) innovation, stakeholder governance, and sustainability, providing an informed perspective on cutting-edge research on these topics. This is a continuation of the AOM 2016, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 PDWs under the same name, sponsored by STR, IM, ONE, PNP, and SIM divisions. This PDW consists of two parts: a panel discussion and a roundtable session. Participation in the roundtable session requires pre-approval. An online application form will be circulated via AOM listservs and other channels. Accepted participants will be notified in advance and matched with discussants for their roundtables. Pre-registration is not required for those attending only the panel portion of the PDW.
