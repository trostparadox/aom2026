Pathway to Scaling: Unpacking How Digital Industrial Platforms Activate Data Network Effects

TIM
Yinan Zhang (Zhejiang University); Yang Liu (Zhejiang University); Jiaming Yang (Zhejiang University of Finance and Economics); zhenzhou ying (Zhejiang University)
Despite the increasing recognition of data driven innovation in digital industrial platforms (DIPs) literature, there is more pressing need for a deep understanding of how to activate data network effects in DIPs. We propose that the scaling up of DIPs critically depends on aggregating and leveraging data generated by heterogeneous physical assets owned by multiple customer firms within a focal industry. Drawing on the data network effects perspective, we theorize how two nascent DIPs strategically activate data network effects to support their scaling trajectories. By distilling key platform characteristics and illuminating heterogeneity among platform architects, we identify two distinct pathways through which DIPs activate data network effects. We further show how mechanisms associated with platforms’ different organizational origins shape their scaling trajectories. In doing so, we shift analytical attention from platform launch to platform scaling and demonstrate how activating data network effects underpins the sustained growth of DIPs beyond their initial launch. Our findings contribute to research on DIPs and advance understanding of the mechanisms underlying data network effects.
