Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) examines how professors can collaborate with practitioners to address AI-related workplace skills gaps in Asia through team-based learning (TBL) in both academic and organizational settings. Employers across the Asia-Pacific region report strong demand for AI-related capabilities alongside persistent challenges in finding and developing employees who can critically interpret AI outputs, exercise ethical judgment, and collaborate effectively in cross-functional teams. At the same time, universities and professional schools in Asia have accumulated substantial experience with structured, collaborative pedagogies, including TBL, that are well suited to complex, practice-oriented learning outcomes. The PDW is organized around AI-intensive workplace scenarios drawn from Asian contexts (e.g., AI-supported hiring, algorithmic performance feedback, and AI-generated marketing content). Participants, seated in teams that mix academics and practitioners where possible, interrogate flawed AI recommendations, develop and justify action plans, and reflect on how team processes shape the quality of reasoning and decision making. A facilitated debrief then highlights design principles for using TBL to support AI-related skill development in ways that are sensitive to regional workplace norms, institutional conditions, and organizational constraints. In the final segment, teams co-design TBL-based mini-modules that can be adapted for courses, executive programs, or corporate training in Asia.
