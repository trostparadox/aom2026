The Backlash Against DEI: Implications for Neurodiverse Individuals

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 4:00PM–5:00PM

People

Authors: Amy E. Hurley-Hanson Chapman University; Cristina Marie Giannantonio Chapman University; Amy Jane Griffiths Chapman University; Rachel Torres Chapman University; Karen S. Markel University of Colorado-Colorado Springs; Yeeun Jeon; Ashley Nicole Scott Chapman University

Session overview

This paper examines the recent backlash against DEI programs with a focus on its implications for neurodiverse individuals. Several high-profile companies have publicly stated that they are curtailing, downsizing, or renaming their DEI programs in response to Executive Orders issued by the Trump administration in the first quarter of 2025. The Department of Education’s “Dear Colleague Letter” informed educational institutions that they will be examined for the use of DEI programs and practices, especially if they receive federal funding.The elimination of such programs in the corporate world and in institutions of higher education ignores the contributions that neurodivergent individuals can bring to the workplace and the classroom. DEI programs focused on neurodivergent individuals have the potential to lead to more productive, innovative, and collaborative work environments, as well as to provide neurodiverse employees with positive career outcomes and life experiences.
