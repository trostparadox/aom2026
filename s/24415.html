From Disruption to Enhancement: Understanding the U-shape Impact of AI on New Venture Innovation

STR
Can Liu (Central South University); Zhuang Pan (Central South University)
Despite growing scholarly attention to artificial intelligence (AI) as an innovation driver, existing literature presents contradictory findings regarding its impact on organizational innovation performance. This study examines the nonlinear relationship between AI adoption and innovation performance in new ventures, exploring how internal knowledge and external knowledge moderate this relationship. Analyzing 2,556 samples of Chinese listed new ventures from 2010 to 2023, our findings reveal a U-shaped relationship between AI adoption and innovation performance. Early-stage AI implementation negatively affects innovation due to resource misallocation, organizational disruption, and insufficient knowledge integration capabilities in short term. As firms accumulate experience and develop complementary capabilities, AI significantly enhances innovation outcomes. Top management team functional background diversity moderates the U shape steeper and AI board interlocks moderate this relationship more flatten. This study reconciles conflicting perspectives by demonstrating AI's nonlinear innovation impact, extends the knowledge-based view to the AI era by elucidating how machine-generated knowledge reshapes absorptive capacity, and integrates internal and external contingencies for comprehensive understanding.
