Session overview

New digital technologies (e.g., GenAI, digital platforms, and social media) have fundamentally changed how firms organize and compete, challenging long-standing assumptions in traditional management theories about firm boundaries, governance, and managerial control. This panel symposium explores the implications of these changes by bringing together five experts. Each panelist will discuss how emerging digital technologies transform the internal organization of work, diversification strategy, shareholder activism, AI adoption patterns by ownership structure, and stakeholder management. By integrating these insights, the symposium aims to advance shared knowledge on future directions of research on strategy and organizational studies.
