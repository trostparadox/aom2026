Session overview

This dynamic plenary session brings together recognized scholars and leaders in the field of human resources. The panel includes researchers who have successfully translated their scholarship into practical applications and a senior HR executive who has worked to bring evidence- based practices into organizational decision-making. Together, they will discuss the challenges of crossing the research-practice divide, the strategies that have worked for them, and the opportunities that remain.
