Integrating Nascent and Mature Ecosystems: Technology Organizations as Ecosystems Integrators

Session overview

Technology-driven industries increasingly feature interaction between nascent and mature ecosystems. Nascent ecosystems—often enabled by emerging technologies such as blockchain, artificial intelligence, and digital platforms—are characterized by experimentation, fluid actor roles, weak standards, and limited institutionalization (Aldrich & Fiol, 1994; Adner, 2017). In contrast, mature ecosystems are stabilized by established actors, standardized technologies, routinized practices, and dense regulatory, normative, and cognitive frameworks (Scott, 2014). While prior research has examined ecosystem emergence and evolution, less is known about how organizations actively integrate ecosystems that differ fundamentally in maturity, institutional embeddedness, and governance. This gap is increasingly consequential as technology firms enter highly regulated and infrastructurally complex domains, where innovation depends on alignment, translation, and coordination between emerging technologies and established institutional systems. Understanding these processes is therefore critical for advancing ecosystem theory, institutional theory, and research on technological infrastructure. Financial services provide a salient empirical setting. Long dominated by banks, central banks, and regulated intermediaries, the sector constitutes a highly institutionalized field with tightly governed infrastructures for payments, settlement, and liquidity. In recent years, financial technology firms have begun operating at the infrastructural level, developing technologies that interact directly with core financial functions such as cross-border payments, settlement, and value transfer. These firms often occupy the intersection of a nascent blockchain-based digital asset ecosystem and the mature ecosystem of traditional finance. Rather than positioning themselves as disruptive challengers, some pursue strategies aimed at connecting and coordinating across ecosystems. This raises our central research question: How do technology organizations integrate nascent and mature ecosystems? We address this question through an in-depth qualitative case study of Company Alpha, a blockchain infrastructure provider positioned as a bridge between digital asset ecosystems and global banking.
