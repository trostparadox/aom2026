Session overview

Management researchers are increasingly studying contexts considered ‘extreme’, ‘unconventional’, or ‘atypical’. By venturing where others dare not, scholars can capture phenomena or relationships that may be too weak to notice (or have been overlooked) in traditional settings. While, in principle, unconventional contexts are now accepted as promising sites for generating theory, several challenges make it difficult to convince reviewers and editors of their generalizable theoretical contributions. This symposium brings together researchers who successfully published unconventional context research in top management journals with the editors who handled these manuscripts. By unpacking the journey of these papers through the review process, we aim to identify transferable advice on how one can bridge the gap between 'cool phenomenon' and 'breakthrough contribution'.
