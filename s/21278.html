Session overview

This timely PDW addresses how artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping international business (IB) and what these changes imply for what and how we teach about IB. Indeed, AI is transforming core IB activities. Further, the advent of AI challenges traditional teaching and examination approaches which rely heavily on static cases and retrospective analysis, but AI also opens the possibility of using new teaching methods which this PDW will demonstrate. The workshop has three objectives. First, it provides an integrated overview of how AI is changing how multinational enterprises build and capture advantages. Second, it examines how the advent of AI necessitates adjustments to established IB activities and curricula. For example, we will demonstrate how teaching about global supply chains should be adjusted to consider the role AI plays in global supply chain design, analysis, and monitoring. We will explain how we have adjusted our teaching about global supply chains to take this into account using a live case and having students use AI to analyze global supply chains. Third, it introduces and demonstrates an innovative teaching method which uses AI to support scalable, practice-based learning, including a simulation that allows students to gain experience making decisions in complex international settings. The session combines presentations, demonstrations, small-group discussions, and a panel discussion. It is designed to help international business faculty update course content, rethink pedagogical choices, and better align teaching with the realities students will face as managers in AI-enabled global organizations.
