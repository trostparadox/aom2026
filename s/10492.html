Session overview

The 2026 Academy of Management Meeting marks the 11th year of the Professional Development Workshop (PDW) on Organizational Stigma and Stigmatization. Over the last decade, the stigma research community has grown tremendously—stigma has, somewhat ironically, become a popular topic. With scholars from multiple traditions applying the term to an ever-widening range of phenomena, it is time to pause and ask: What is stigma, really? What is it not? In this PDW, we will confront this conceptual expansion directly through a constructive and collegial debate between two distinguished scholars, followed by an expert discussion and research development roundtables. Together, these components will encourage attendees to reflect on the theoretical boundaries, empirical challenges, and practical implications of studying stigma in organizational contexts. The PDW will comprise three key components: (1) a keynote-style debate between two scholars offering contrasting perspectives on the nature of stigma; (2) an open discussion among experts and participants; and (3) a series of facilitated research development roundtables organized around four themes—Theory, Methods, Data, and Contributions. By participating, attendees will deepen their understanding of the evolving conceptual landscape of stigma, develop strategies for rigorous empirical research, and engage in clarifying discussions about what stigma is—and what it is not—in today’s organizational scholarship. The keynote and discussion are open to all attendees, but roundtables will only be open to those that pre-register and submit a 1-2 page abstract and research challenge statement to aomstigma@gmail.com by July 1st, 2026.
