Session overview

In this symposium, we aim to advance the nascent organizational literature on political polarization. The presentations adopt both insider and outsider perspectives to provide greater insight into how political polarization might intersect with organizational life. The first two presentations explore polarization from insider perspectives, examining differences between partisans in their perceptions of political conversations (or lack thereof) at work, as well as their willingness to engage in behaviors that appear outwardly political. The final two presentations adopt outsider perspectives, examining differences between partisan group members in their reactions to organizations and occupations whose actions appear to be associated with politically polarized issues.
