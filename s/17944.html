Session overview

This symposium focuses on the context of community foundations (CFs), place-based grantmaking public charities that collect donations and distribute funds to address problems and priorities within a defined geographic area, to examine theoretical and empirical questions about how justice, power, and equity are enacted within philanthropic systems. Specifically, papers in this panel explore participatory processes and power dynamics, measure the institutional logics shaping justice philanthropy, trace the rise and dormancy of a racial justice network, and investigate how foundation staff engage with donors about organizational priorities related to racial equity.
