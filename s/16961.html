Session overview

The Innovations in Teaching Teamwork PDW is a practical, highly interactive workshop designed for faculty and doctoral students interested in enhancing the teaching of teamwork across various educational contexts. The 2026 session represents the seventh iteration of this popular workshop and focuses on the challenges of incorporating teamwork in classroom settings and the strategies instructors use to address them. Leading scholars and experienced instructors will share candid reflections on the pedagogical, logistical, and interpersonal difficulties they have encountered when teaching teamwork, along with evidence-based and experiential approaches for navigating these challenges. The session will consist of three parts. Part one will consist of presentations of novel approaches instructors can use to successfully implement team-related initiatives into the classroom, including team assessment innovations, accelerating psychological safety, gamified approaches to teamwork development, and AI-based micro-simulations for team communication. Part two will include an expert panel that will share broader personal experiences (shortcomings and successes) in implementing team-centric learning, as well as answer questions from the audience. Part three will consist of roundtable discussions, where participants will have the opportunity to discuss concerns and opportunities in a smaller setting. The session is designed as a collective learning experience wherein participants will leave with adaptable strategies and concrete ideas for strengthening teamwork instruction in diverse learning environments.
