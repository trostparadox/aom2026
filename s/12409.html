Session overview

Historically, the purpose of management education has been to equip students with knowledge and skills to find employment and contribute to society. Yet the necessary competencies have shifted due to a new reality: unemployment, Gen Z’s expectations, sensitivity to unsolved social issues, AI. Additionally, environmental and climate-related problems are impacting life ‘as usual’ literally everywhere, and accreditation bodies are requesting higher-ed institutions to focus and demonstrate the positive impact they are having on the community. What are the skills and knowledge that will prepare the students to make a difference? In this session we will posit that management education may have focused exclusively on the knowing and doing, avoiding the ‘being dimension’. What if addressing the underlying mindset, particularly the spiritual dimension of that mindset, would allow for a different pedagogical intervention: a conversation about who we are, who we want to be, in what ways we could be actors, shapers, influencers, inspirers, visionaries of the world we want to live in? How are our accounting, leadership, administrative, purchasing, HR, even technical IT roles the best context to express our highest version of ourselves? In this session, we will share what we have found to be the highway to sustainability. We will invite you to pause and ponder the underrated power of addressing the mindset, particularly the spiritual qualities: Mindful- and self-awareness values, sense of connectedness, purpose, oneness with nature and how these are formed or developed as key aspects influencing how the new generation will analyze information and make decisions.
