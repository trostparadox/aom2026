Session overview

Entrepreneurial judgment under Knightian uncertainty is central to strategic decision making (Foss & Klein, 2012; Foss, Klein, & Bjørnskov, 2019), yet research has largely emphasized individual decision making and single firm contexts. Modern entrepreneurship unfolds in entrepreneurial ecosystems— interfirm collaborations, alliances, and digital ecosystems, where judgment must be communicated, coordinated, and governed among multiple stakeholders and no longer by an individual entrepreneur or single firm. This PDW aims to advance a focused agenda on how judgment is mobilized, organized, and exploited in entrepreneurial ecosystems, highlighting areas such as ecosystem governance mechanisms, that support critical cooperation, coopetition, resource allocation and innovation between entrepreneurial and innovation ecosystem agents. please email your full papers to m.belitski@reading.ac.uk
