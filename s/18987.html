Session overview

Generative AI (GenAI) is rapidly reshaping entrepreneurial work, including how founders and executives design and redesign business models. Yet emerging evidence suggests GenAI can activate paradoxical tensions rather than delivering a linear ‘value add’. Business model innovation (BMI) is a particularly fertile setting because entrepreneurs must align interdependent elements of value creation, delivery, and capture while translating novel ideas into feasible, efficient activity systems. Building on the novelty–efficiency paradox in GenAI-enabled BMI, this PDW is designed exclusively as a research paper development workshop. Participants will bring papers-in-progress on GenAI and entrepreneurial BMI. Before the meeting, organizers will cluster submissions by theme and assign each to a mentor-led small group. During the session, participants will complete two rounds of structured paper clinics that provide targeted feedback on research questions, theoretical contribution (including paradox perspectives where appropriate), construct clarity (especially novelty versus efficiency in BMD), research design choices, and positioning for high- quality outlets. Participants will leave with a sharpened contribution statement and a concrete, time-bounded revision plan, while also building community among scholars studying GenAI and entrepreneurship.
