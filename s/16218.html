Session overview

In a world increasingly threatened by socio-ecological crises, attributed amongst others to socio-economic systems considered unsustainable in terms of protecting and regenerating available resources, the concept of the circular economy (CE) has emerged as an alternative paradigm (Branzei et al., 2026). After an initial phase in which management and organization scholars engaged with the CE enthusiastically and optimistically (Geissdoerfer et al., 2017), the CE paradigm has increasingly begun to attract critical scrutiny (Kirchherr & Hartley, 2025). This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to embrace such critical perspectives on the CE without turning them into outright rejection. Instead, the PDW treats critique as a fertile ground from which a renewed research landscape can emerge, invigorated by alternative assumptions (Dzhengiz et al., 2023), new logics of intervention that reorient how circularity is enacted in practice (Figge et al., 2018), and realistic projections (Bocken et al., 2025).
