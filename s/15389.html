Session overview

Organizations' environmental and social strategies increasingly unfold in an environment marked by uncertainty, polarization, and contested expectations. Policy frameworks are unpredictable and politically charged; stakeholder coalitions hold divergent views on corporate purpose; anti-ESG countermovements have gained political traction; and rating systems apply inconsistent or conflicting criteria. This symposium explores how rising uncertainty and polarization influence firms’ environmental and social strategies. Across four presentations, the symposium highlights how firms adapt their framing strategies, organizational culture, investment timing, and governance choices to navigate contested meanings, politicized policy environments, and divergent stakeholder demands. Collectively, the symposium advances conversations in management research by showing how organizations actively interpret, respond to, and sometimes reshape the uncertain and polarized ESG landscape.
