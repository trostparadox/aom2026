Session overview

Geopolitical tensions have become a defining feature of the contemporary business environment, yet how firms systematically anticipate, evaluate, and respond to geopolitics remains largely underexplored. Drawing on diverse theoretical perspectives and data, the panel examines how geopolitical risk is shaped by home–host government relations, political power and ideology, and policy-driven constraints, as well as how firms’ responses face trade-offs. Collectively, this panel aims to foster a coherent and cumulative conversation that advances our understanding of geopolitical strategy by linking structured geopolitical forces, firm-level ex ante and ex post strategic responses, and their often-overlooked unintended consequences across regions, governance systems, and political regimes.
