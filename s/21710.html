Session overview

The purpose of this professional development workshop is to provide a platform for Careers scholars to (1) connect with other scholars at various stages of their careers with similar research interests and (2) exchange and develop research ideas, stimulating scholarship that addresses important and emerging issues in Careers. We encourage attendees to come with research ideas, as they will have the opportunity to participate in two 40-minute roundtable discussions moderated by 1-2 accomplished or emerging Careers scholars. These careers scholars are thought leaders in emerging careers topic areas who will help answer questions and facilitate discussion of research ideas. We have chosen nine themes that reflect contemporary topics in careers research: (1) Future of Careers, (2) Calling, (3) Career Interventions & Developmental Relationships, (4) Career Transitions, (5) Career Choice, (6) Career Shocks, (7) Diversity Issues in Careers, (8) Culture and International Careers, and (9) Career Success. This PDW serves as a platform for careers scholars to build connections with each other, which may also lead to future research.
