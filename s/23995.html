Research on the Impact of AI Investment on the Organizational Resilience of Startups

STR
Jiamin Dong (Central South University); Yuxuan Ma (Central South University)
Artificial intelligence (AI), as a key force driving the development of new quality productivity, is deeply empowering corporate innovation and transformation. Startups, due to their characteristics of limited resources and nascent fragility, face severe survival challenges in the VUCA environment, where organizational resilience becomes a critical safeguard for their sustained growth. This study integrates dynamic capability theory to construct a "technology-capability-outcome" theoretical framework. Using China's STAR Market enterprises from 2019 to 2023 as samples, we empirically examine the impact mechanism of AI investment on the organizational resilience of startups and the moderating role of CEO's time focus. The findings reveal that AI investment significantly enhances the organizational resilience of startups; dynamic capability partially mediates the relationship between AI investment and organizational resilience; CEO's past time focus weakens the positive relationship between AI investment and organizational resilience. This study provides important insights for startups to enhance resilience through strategic AI investment, capability building, and cognitive management.
