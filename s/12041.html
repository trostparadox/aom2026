Session overview

Our field of management and organization studies has struggled to reorient our scholarship to match the urgency and enormity of the intertwined grand challenges that constitute the current period of polycrisis. This panel explores how management scholarship might respond through a renewed program of research on comparative societal governance. The panelists offer several, largely complementary points of view on how broadening our focus from firms to societies and by adopting an internationally comparative perspective could help us contribute scientifically to efforts to confront the current polycrisis.
