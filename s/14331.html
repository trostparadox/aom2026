Session overview

Digital platforms have fundamentally reshaped the economic landscape, but they now face a pivotal shift characterized by increasing regulatory scrutiny and evolving governance challenges. This symposium bridges the fields of Strategy and Information Systems to investigate the critical friction at the intersection of technological architecture, market governance, and public policy. Through a multi-level lens—spanning macro-structural theory, ecosystem dynamics, and micro-level user behavior—we examine how regulatory interventions alter power asymmetries and value creation. By integrating these diverse perspectives, this session aims to develop a shared understanding of how platforms can navigate complex institutional environments to foster responsible innovation while preserving the network effects that drive their value.
