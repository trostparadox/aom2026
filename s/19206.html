Session overview

In today’s interconnected world, organizations face an increasingly complex media environment. The interplay between social and traditional media channels continues to jointly shape how firms manage their legitimacy, influence stakeholder perceptions, and even impact internal strategic decision-making processes. Building on the success of our inaugural session at AOM 2025 in Copenhagen, this second annual symposium continues to explore these evolving dynamics, with a particular focus on the “double-edged sword” of media. We have aggregated four working papers that explore the interplay between organizations and media from diverse perspectives. These include examining the antecedents of media attention, such as how tokenism and categorical distinctiveness drive “hype”, and the strategic implications of such attention, specifically investigating how excessive coverage can inadvertently foster strategic inertia rather than flexibility and variation in how organizations benefit from piggybacking on broader narratives and events. Furthermore, we introduce a novel, transcript-based measurement for firm-level social media risk and opportunity exposure, offering a robust tool for researchers in an era of restricted data access. We have invited two distinguished scholars, James Westphal and Ann Mooney Murphy, to provide commentary and discuss future research directions. Overall, this theme is especially timely given a turbulent media landscape where firms must navigate biased information from myriad and tangled sources.
