Session overview

When AI enables anyone to build functional software, what happens to the boundaries between technical and non-technical work? This workshop examines that transformation through the lens of the product manager role, where a new archetype is emerging: the full-stack product builder, someone competent across strategy, design, engineering, and go-to-market. The session introduces a framework mapping competency across four domains: Strategy, Design & Engineering, Go-to-Market & Growth, and Data Science. Historically, organizations distributed these responsibilities across specialized roles. AI tools now enable individuals to operate across all four, compressing skill acquisition from months to days. Through hands-on exercises, participants will experience AI-assisted development: using natural language to generate code, building functional prototypes, and iterating rapidly. They will feel both the acceleration AI enables and the judgment that remains human. AI handles 80% of execution while the human's 20% (including strategic framing, user insight, and prioritization), is where value concentrates. This workshop serves researchers studying technology professionals, innovation processes, and organizational design. Rather than simply reading about AI's impact, participants will get to experience it, helping inform research questions like: How do skill boundaries shift when anyone can build? What happens to specialized roles? How do organizations capture value from AI-augmented professionals? Participants leave with practical workflow exposure, prompt templates, and research questions worth pursuing.
