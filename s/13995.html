Session overview

We present a curated collection of three conceptual and two empirical papers on how wearables can be used in HR and organizational research. These papers trace a progression from theoretical arguments about the value of wearable data to empirical evidence drawn from field settings. The symposium is organized to highlight different levels of workplace behavior that are difficult to observe using traditional methods, showing how wearables can render these processes visible.
