Limits of Perseverance at Work: Employees with Disabilities Navigating Unmet Adjustment Needs

Authors: Rasa Rudaitiene Mykolas Romeris University; Agota Giedre Raišiene Mykolas Romeris University

Session overview

Amid increasing social, economic, and geopolitical instability and demographic change, the career development of people with disabilities has become a matter of societal resilience. Despite growing scholarly and policy attention to inclusive work and careers, academic understanding of how people with disabilities experience career development over time, which coping and breakthrough strategies they employ, and what long-term consequences result from persistent structural constraints remains fragmented. This study addresses this gap by combining a scoping review with a qualitative research design grounded in lived career experiences. In the first stage, a PRISMA-guided scoping review of 45 peer-reviewed journal articles was conducted using the Scopus and Web of Science databases. The review systematically maps methodological, thematic, and geographical patterns in research on the work and career experiences of people with disabilities. Building on the review findings, the second stage develops a qualitative research based on interviews with individuals with disabilities. This stage is designed to examine how career trajectories are shaped under organizational and social constraints, which coping and breakthrough strategies are enacted, and how unresolved barriers translate into long-term career consequences. By integrating micro-, meso-, and macro-level perspectives, the study contributes to career theory by foregrounding the dynamic interaction between individual agency, organizational practices, and broader socio-political conditions. Practically, the findings offer insights for organizations seeking to design career systems that are not only formally inclusive but also responsive to lived experiences. More broadly, the study positions inclusive career development as a strategic priority for societal resilience in times of uncertainty.
