Session overview

The Entrepreneurship Division’s Global Scholar Development Committee (GSDC) is dedicated to supporting the diverse needs of entrepreneurship scholars worldwide by helping them navigate, connect, and succeed within the global academic community. This session is designed to combine scholarly dialogue with hands-on research development for scholars interested in entrepreneurship research using Non-WEIRD contexts. The first half of the session will feature a panel conversation with leading scholars who have extensive experience publishing entrepreneurship research grounded in Non-WEIRD contexts. Panelists will draw on their own publication experiences to discuss key challenges and opportunities associated with Non-WEIRD research, including strategies for positioning such work in top-tier journals, addressing reviewer concerns, and leveraging Non-WEIRD contexts to generate strong, novel, and impactful theoretical contributions. The second half of the session will be devoted to a paper development workshop focused on entrepreneurship research in Non-WEIRD contexts. Participants will actively engage with panel experts to refine research ideas and manuscripts, strengthen theoretical framing and positioning, and clarify contributions.
