Research on the Mechanism of the Impact of Work Intrusions on Creativity in Scientific Researchers

OB
Naizheng Liang (Huazhong University of Science and Technology); Zhiqiang Liu (Huazhong University of Science and Technology); Boheng zhao (Huazhong University of Science and Technology); Xin Jiang (Huazhong University of Science and Technology); Ziang Chen (Huazhong University of Science and Technology)
Based on enrichment theory and self-control theory, this research constructs an analysis framework of “work event-enrichment process-work outcome”, and explores the core issue of “when scientific researchers cannot change the passive and uncontrollable established fact of work intrusions, under the intervention of what factors, work intrusions will instead have a posi-tive effect on the generation of creativity”. This research carried out both qualitative and quanti-tative studies. Through semi-structured interviews with 15 researchers and a questionnaire sur-vey with 236 researchers, the findings indicate that the three-way interaction between work in-trusion, organizational competitive climate, and future focus affects cognitive developmental resources. When the organizational competitive climate is low and the individual’s future focus is high, the positive effect of work intrusions on cognitive developmental resources is strongest, and cognitive developmental resources positively affect researchers’ creativity. In addition, after controlling the influence of affective resources, cognitive developmental resources play a medi-ating role in the influence of the three-way interaction (work intrusion, organizational competi-tive climate, and future focus) on creativity. The conclusions deepen the understanding of the complex relationship between scientific researchers’ work intrusions and creativity, and provide insights for optimizing research environments.
