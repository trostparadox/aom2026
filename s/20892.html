Session overview

This professional development workshop supports researchers and instructors who are interested in using the results from the GLOBE 2020 study as a platform for their future research and teaching projects. This current phase of the GLOBE project offers updated, empirically robust measures of 10 cultural norms including Assertiveness, Future Orientation, Gender Egalitarianism, Humane Orientation, In-Group Collectivism, Institutional Collectivism, Performance Orientation, Power Distance, Uncertainty Avoidance, and a new measure for Religiosity. The study encompasses 144 countries/societies, and features data from over 52,000 managers across diverse organizational contexts. The session begins with sharing the rigorous methodology used for instrument design, translation, and measure validation. The empirical verification of 17 culture clusters facilitates both within- and across-cluster comparisons. These clusters provide a practical framework for managers to streamline global operations and for researchers to employ purposeful sampling and comparative analysis. Then, designed as an interactive session, it includes brief expert presentations on the methodology, findings, and implications for future research, followed by roundtable discussions, as described ahead. Participants will explore future research directions, the utility of cultural similarities and differences, and the development of new pedagogies for teaching about global societal cultures. Ultimately, the session aims to foster collaboration among AOM scholars who share an interest in conducting research that draws on the GLOBE project findings. Ultimately, this research could support new theories about antecedents and outcomes of cultural differences, while also equipping practitioners with actionable tools to navigate the complexities of an increasingly interconnected global landscape.
