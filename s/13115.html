Session overview

Territories frequently pursue “ecosystem” strategies to strengthen resilience and prosperity, yet value often leaks out through fragmented coordination, duplicated efforts, supplier displacement, under-developed competencies, and weak cross-actor governance. This PDW introduces participants to socio-economic intervention methodology. This intervention-research approach combines qualitative diagnosis with qualimetric estimation of “hidden costs” and “hidden performance. The purpose of this PDW is to demonstrate how it can be mobilized to activate circulating territorial value. Participants will learn who the intervention-research methodology’s core sequence (diagnosis, mirror effect, co-constructed action architecture, and evaluation loop) and apply it to a territorial ecosystem case of their choice (city, region, cluster, district).
