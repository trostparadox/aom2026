Session overview

In this symposium, we offer a timely update to the affect literature, focusing on affective reactions to positive and negative work events and stimuli, their work implications, as well as ways to mitigate undesirable affective reactions. The collection of research we present provides new theoretical and practical insights into how individuals react to affective events at work.
