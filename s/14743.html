Session overview

Management consultants are often asked to design leadership and development initiatives that produce durable impact beyond the life of the engagement. Yet many talent-development efforts underperform because developmental ownership is implicitly outsourced – to programs, experts, or external advisors – rather than retained by the client. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces an evidence-informed, consulting-relevant architecture for embedding client-led developmental agency into consulting engagements. Drawing on well-established research in adult learning, process consultation, organizational learning, and relational development, the workshop translates a relational learning framework—originally developed in the mentoring literature —into system-level design principles that enable client organizations to sustain learning beyond the consulting engagement. Participants will work through realistic organizational transition scenarios commonly encountered in consulting practice: founder or leadership succession, global leadership pipeline development, and post-merger integration. Through facilitated diagnostic and design exercises, participants will apply a cyclical developmental timing framework (Align–Collaborate–Evolve), a competency model for relational learning, and a set of consulting-ready tools to build a scalable development architecture deployable across engagements. Participants leave with practical templates, facilitation strategies, and a clear logic for transferring developmental agency from consultant to client, strengthening learning capacity long after the consultant exits.
