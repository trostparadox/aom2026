Session overview

As generative AI adoption continues to expand, there remain many uncertainties about how it affects interpersonal interactions within an organization. This symposium brings together scholars from organizational behavior to deepen understanding of how AI is affecting interpersonal perceptions and interactions at work. Specifically, the symposium will cover three main topics: how AI use is perceived among coworkers, predicting when and to whom people disclose their AI use, and the challenges and opportunities of using AI for emotional labor. Together, these topics cover multiple interrelated aspects of AI use and interpersonal relationships.
