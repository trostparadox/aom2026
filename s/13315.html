Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) explores breath-based contemplative practices as pathways for engaging ways of knowing beyond purely rational cognition in management scholarship and practice. Responding to calls within the Management, Spirituality, and Religion (MSR) community to broaden epistemological horizons, the workshop introduces multiple forms of breathwork—including Bhramari, Bhastrika, and Panchkosha guided meditation —as experiential entry points for cultivating inner development capacities such as self-awareness, ethical clarity, emotional regulation, and presence. The PDW also draws on peer-reviewed research and other breath-based practices to situate these experiential approaches within a growing empirical literature, without positioning the workshop as technique instruction or certification. Participants will gain (a) a conceptual framework linking breath, inner goals, and management, and (b) a plan for ethically and inclusively integrating contemplative approaches into their research, teaching, or professional contexts aligned with MSR’s aims.
