Session overview

Four talks highlight important new directions in research on punishment and leniency in organizations. They emphasize the roles of organizational change, power, norms, and desistance, and combine field studies, experiments, and interviews to highlight where research is going on the topic, challenge prior research findings, and stimulate new intellectual debate on an important organizational topic.
