Global Talent Management Revisited: An Integrative Review and Multi-Level Framework

Session overview

This paper develops a nested multi-level framework for global talent management (GTM). We review 101 GTM studies and use a micro-meso-macro scaffold to integrate fragmented evidence across individuals, work systems, organizations, and national and global conditions. The framework treats context as causal: macro institutions and talent systems bound viable GTM options, organizations assemble talent architectures within those bounds, and work systems shape how employees experience value, fairness, and growth. The framework aims to guide future multi-level GTM studies and offer policy makers and firms a more context-sensitive way to think about talent.
