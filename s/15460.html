The Invisible Work of Hybrid Work: How Line Managers Navigate Dynamic Configurations

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 2
HR

People

Author: Katy Liu ESADE Business School

Session overview

Hybrid work has become a dominant organizational arrangement, yet many organizations struggle with making it function effectively. This study shifts attention from employees to line managers—the actors responsible for implementing hybrid work in everyday settings. Building on recent scholarship that conceptualizes hybrid work as a dynamic system of shifting configurations of time, location, and presence, we argue that such configurations create distinctive demands for managerial work that remain poorly understood. Drawing on HRM implementation literature and sensemaking theory, this qualitative interview-based study explores how line managers experience and make sense of hybrid complexity, what practices they develop to navigate it, and how this ongoing engagement shapes their understanding of their managerial role. By foregrounding the often-invisible work involved in making hybrid arrangements function, this research aims to contribute to HRM implementation literature by illuminating the continuous sensemaking and coordination work that line managers perform in dynamic work contexts.
