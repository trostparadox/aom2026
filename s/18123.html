Session overview

Future-Ready Leadership is a high-impact, research-informed graduate-level workshop designed to deepen participants’ understanding of three essential leadership capabilities: resilience, adaptability, and lifelong learning. As global systems are rapidly transformed by technology, shifting workforce expectations, and new educational and economic realities, leaders must cultivate the clarity, agility, and learning orientation necessary for sustained success. This 2-hour session is ideal for emerging and experienced leaders seeking evidence-based strategies to enhance their leadership effectiveness, strengthen their decision-making, and build environments where individuals and organizations can thrive. This session is highly interactive, with participants bringing their experiences to enrich the discussion. For this reason, we request that anyone participating have 5+ years of leadership experience. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
