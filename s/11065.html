Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop responds to the growing prominence of family business research in top-tier outlets and the persistent related challenges of publishing such work in these journals. Family firms offer a rich yet demanding context for theory development and empirical investigation. Bringing together an interdisciplinary panel of editors from leading journals in entrepreneurship, strategy, and organization studies, this two-hour, highly interactive session is designed to help scholars navigate the path to publishing rigorous and impactful family business research. The PDW combines editorial reflections, open Q&A, and small-group roundtable discussions focused on issues such as framing contributions beyond a niche audience, methodological rigor, and mastering the review and revision process. By fostering dialogue across the Entrepreneurship, Strategic Management, and Organizational Behavior communities, the session aims to equip participants with concrete strategies to elevate the quality, relevance, and visibility of their family business research in top-tier journals.
