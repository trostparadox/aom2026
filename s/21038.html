5G Ecosystem Bottlenecks: Identification and Current Solutions

Authors: Mattia Magnaghi Politecnico di Milano; Massimiliano Dell'Orto Politecnico di Milano - School of Management; Antonio Ghezzi Politecnico di Milano; Andrea Rangone

Session overview

While the existing literature on Enabling Technologies (ETs) has mostly emphasized their successful broad applicability and economic impact, firm-level dynamics and early-stage commercialization issues are often overlooked. The current investigation into ETs is primarily linked to market-based and hierarchy-based approaches, neglecting the potential insights offered by an ecosystemic perspective. The latter could shed light on the coordination and cooperation challenges likely to emerge in the early stages of ET commercialization, particularly when investments in technological generality are influenced or even "forced" by regulators. This is evident in the case of 5G, which, despite being recognized as an ET by both practitioners and academics, has seen significant investment by Italian Mobile Network Operators (MNOs) through exclusive access auctions for 5G frequencies. Yet, seven years later, the Italian 5G B2B market remains valued at only a few million euros. Through an exploratory multiple-case study, the research aims to narrow the distance between the ownership of an ET and its commercialization, providing new insights from the complementors' perspective that is often neglected. Findings show that for an ET to become pervasive in use, there are bottlenecks and challenges that are difficult to understand within the current market-based representation found in ETs literature. Therefore, this study argues for a shift from firm-centric to ecosystem-wide strategies, emphasizing the role of downstream actors in 5G commercialization.
