Lead First, Ask Later: Leader-Driven Credibility in Temporary Teams

OB
Anshul Kapoor (Anshul Kapoor)
Leader credibility is essential for follower and team states and performance. This work explores how leaders quickly establish and inspire credibility in temporary, swift starting teams, where team members must begin performing before credibility perceptions may emerge organically through routine interactions. A multi-method qualitative study reveals several measures that leaders undertake to inspire credibility - developing a shared vision, engaging in extensive LMX, demonstrating competence, and inspiring efficacy. This work highlights the active role of leader in credibility establishment and counteracting the constraints inherent in temporary teams.
