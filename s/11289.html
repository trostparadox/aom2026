Session overview

A key inspiration for this workshop is President Abraham Lincoln’s Second Inaugural Address, in which he urged, “With malice toward none, with charity for all.” In this speech, Lincoln called for national reconciliation and compassion following the U.S. Civil War. Jon Meacham, a renowned presidential historian and Pulitzer Prize-winning author sums up these lines as one that “ illuminate... the capacity of conscience to help shape events.” Forgiveness is a profound inner practice with far-reaching outer consequences. In this workshop, we examine forgiveness from an inner perspective and consider how it exerts multiple positive influences on many levels that are central to MSR. Specifically, we investigate how forgiveness relates to human flourishing, the development of core values through moral conduct, spiritual leadership, global consciousness, and higher consciousness. In this deeply transformative workshop, we explore seven themes through exercises and practices that echo the presenters’ experiential journeys of inner forgiveness, shaped by their meditations, practices, and an in-depth reflective literature review examining forgiveness across managerial, spiritual, and religious literature.
