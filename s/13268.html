Author: Iman Ghalehkhondabi Howard University

Session overview

This paper looks at how to decide the best order quantities in an inventory system. The model focuses on a manufacturing stage of a supply chain that manages inventory for several products. Because customer demand is uncertain, the model is designed to control inventory of semi-finished items. These semi-finished products are kept in stock and then finished only when specific customer orders arrive. The model finds the best order quantity for each product, how much of each product should be completed in advance, and the reorder point for each product type.
