Geopolitical Risk and Heterogeneous Pathways of Entrepreneurial Exit

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 4
ENT

People

Authors: Nianyu ZHOU Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University; Patrick Peng Wang Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University; Chenguang Hu Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University

Session overview

This study investigates the impact of geopolitical risk (GPR) on entrepreneurial exit in the domestic market. Drawing on the conservation of resources perspective, we argue that GPR can generate resource loss threats, trigger entrepreneurs’ motivation to adapt and safeguard their resources, and thus lead them to prioritize exit as a coping strategy. We further explore the heterogeneous exit strategies: entrepreneurial exit with the firm remaining operational (exit through selloff), and entrepreneurial exit with the firm discontinuing operations (exit through liquidation). Our empirical analysis utilizes a dataset integrating individual- and country-level attributes from 2019 to 2021, covering over 42,374 entrepreneurs across 30 countries. This research finds that GPR exerts a positive impact on entrepreneurial exit. For distinct exit forms, when GPR is higher, the likelihood of exit through liquidation is lower relative to exit through selloff.
