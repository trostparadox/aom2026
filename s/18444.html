Session overview

Geopolitical tensions are fundamentally reshaping how innovation is organized, governed, and advanced across borders. For decades, international business scholarship and practice have been broadly optimistic about technological diffusion and advancement driven by cross-border collaboration and joint global efforts. Escalating geopolitical rivalry — particularly in high-technology industries — now challenges this optimism and signals a paradigm shift that calls for renewed theoretical and strategic attention. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) focuses on geopolitics and global innovation strategy, offering a structured dialogue on how innovation unfolds under heightened geopolitical tension. Through short thematic presentations, an interactive panel discussion, and audience engagement, the PDW examines national innovation systems, ecosystem-level coordination, and firm- and entrepreneur-level strategic adaptation. The workshop brings together leading scholars to advance research on innovation strategy in politically fragmented environments and to identify new theoretical and empirical directions for international business scholarship.
