Measuring Climate Patents with LLMs: Evidence from Patent Texts

ONE
YUKUN SHI (University of Glasgow); Yupu Zhang; Junyao wang (University of Glasgow)
This study develops a new firm-level measure of climate innovation using large language models applied to U.S. patent abstracts. By relying on the full patent text rather than fixed technology classes, the measure captures climate-related innovation that is missed by standard taxonomies. We show that the LLM-based measure is closely linked to patent value, market reactions to climate policy events, regulatory pressure, and real emissions outcomes. Using this measure, we find that climate innovation is negatively related to Tobin’s ??, with the effect driven by mitigation technologies, while adaptation-related innovation shows no systematic valuation effect. The results suggest that financial markets mainly price climate innovation through expected cash-flow costs and investment risk rather than uniform long-term value creation.
