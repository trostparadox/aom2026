Charisma Excites More Than It Pleases: A Valence-Arousal Perspective

Authors: Haimanti Ghosh Texas Tech University; William L. Gardner Texas Tech University

Session overview

We examine the effect of charismatic leadership tactics (CLTs) on followers' moment-to-moment emotional states in a within-subjects study by capturing dynamic changes in valence and arousal. Adopting an affective and follower-centric perspective, we unpack the temporal emotional processes elicited by CLTs. Using facial expression recognition software, we captured second-by-second fluctuations in followers’ emotional states as participants viewed a charismatic speech. Results indicate substantial variation in emotional activation across CLTs. Specifically, when the leader used “creating confidence” tactics, followers experienced heightened arousal, whereas “moral conviction” dampened arousal and elicited negative valence. By revealing the temporal dynamics of followers’ affective states, we provide a nuanced understanding of the affective processes underlying charismatic leadership. Keywords: Charismatic leadership, arousal, valence, face reader
