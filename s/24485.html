ESG Integration in Supply Chain Management: An AHP-FST Approach

OSCM
Roberto Cerchione; Viviana Sicardi (University of Naples "Parthenope")
This study proposes a decision-support tool for assessing the integration of Environmental, Social, and Governance criteria within corporate structures and across supply chains. The framework combines Analytic Hierarchy Process and Fuzzy Set Theory to operationalize the European Sustainability Reporting Standards into a structured evaluation model. The objective is to support governance in identifying sustainability gaps and benchmarking ESG performance among supply chain actors. The tool is empirically validated through a case study, demonstrating its capability to enhance transparency and strategic coordination. Results show that the framework enables firms to achieve an ESG score and competitive positioning within interconnected supply networks.
