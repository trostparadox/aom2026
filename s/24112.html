Driving Enterprises Green Transitions: The Role of Inward FDI in China’s Sustainability Landscape

IM
Hongyi Yang (London South Bank University); Jingnan Li (London South Bank University); Qicheng Lu (Yunnan University of Finance and Economics); Xian Wang (Yunnan University of Finance and Economics)
This study examines the impact of inward foreign direct investment (IFDI) on the green transition of Chinese firms. We propose a green innovation ecosystem that integrates firm, institutional, and entrepreneurial factors with consideration of locational characteristics. We adopt a panel dataset comprising all A-share listed Chinese manufacturing firms between 2010 and 2023. Our results show that IFDI positively influences the green innovation of Chinese manufacturers. This relationship is enhanced by state ownership, environmental regulation, and perceived climate risk, yet hindered by firm age. We elucidate the mechanisms through which transitional economies can advance their sustainable development by leveraging IFDI.
