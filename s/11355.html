Built to Help, Built to Hinder: The Double-Edged Team for New Hires' Knowledge Activities

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 7
OB

People

Author: Joanne Cao Cornell University

Session overview

Knowledge-intensive teams often inject new blood to stimulate knowledge creation, yet we still know little about how attributes of the hiring team shape new hires’ knowledge activities during transition. I propose a conceptual framework that integrates how new hires simultaneously engage in knowledge sharing within the team and knowledge acquisition beyond it. Drawing on a bounded rationality perspective, the framework argues that the very team attributes that facilitate new hires’ internal knowledge sharing can also constrain their external knowledge acquisition, which is critical for knowledge transfer and recombination. I further specify how managerial support must align with team attributes to mitigate these constraints and shape new hires’ knowledge activities.
