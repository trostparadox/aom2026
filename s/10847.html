Session overview

The goal of the symposium is to highlight recent research aimed at understanding the role of parenthood status and gendered outcomes in modern organizations. To do so, we bring together research on motherhood beliefs, experiences, and outcomes in the modern era—from transformative technologies currently reshaping the working world, identity transformations and disclosure, reentry decisions, and positive beliefs that underlie contemporary forms of discrimination against mothers. Collectively, the set of papers describes how modern workplaces generate disparate gendered beliefs for parents, and how their experiences and outcomes are shaped not only through prejudice, but through design, norms, and structures that constrain women’s careers. The symposium highlights new mechanisms through which inequality persists, and points to interventions for creating a more equitable future.
