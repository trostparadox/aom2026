Session overview

In this symposium, we will examine a wide array of questions and hypotheses that focus on an over-arching goal to improve organizations of science and the translation of scientific ideas into the workforce. To do so, we blend research focusing on how to strengthen and support scientific teams (e.g., Uhlbach; Fry) and individual scientists (e.g., Flake; Masters-Waage; Rizzuto). To do so, we start with two papers focusing on the factors that determine scientific team success, the first considers this in the context of science-based ventures and technology transfer (Uhlbach) and the second international collaborations in team science (Fry). We then transition to focus more on the individual scientists, providing a more granular perspective. The first reprises the theme of innovation by examining institutional leaders can champion technological transfer (Flake). This is followed by two paper focusing more leadership on academia, with a paper on the biases that Asian faculty face in rising to leadership roles (Masters-Waage), and another on how faculty leadership is rewarded in promotion and tenure (Rizzuto). Discussant Bruce Weinberg will provide reactions to the presentations through the interdisciplinary lenses that he has developed in decades of work on the relationships between innovation and careers.
