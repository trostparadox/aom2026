Diverse or Divergent? Investor Reactions to Nascent Ventures’ Formal Diversity Initiatives

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 6
ENT

People

Authors: Jasmine Nicole Kelley University of Tennessee at Chattanooga; Rajdeep Kaur Auburn University; Brandon Antoine Griffin Troy University

Session overview

With a focus on achieving firm profitability and increasing shareholder value, investors are increasingly pressuring nascent ventures to engage in socially responsible initiatives. However, certain initiatives, such as diversity initiatives, have become increasingly contentious, leading corporations to scale back such efforts. Given the liabilities of new ventures and growing activist pressures, we explore investor willingness in this evolving investment landscape. Specifically, we examine investors’ willingness to provide financial and social resources and how venture type shapes these relationships. Drawing on expectancy violations theory and organization stakeholder fit theory, we outline an experimental design to further explore these dynamics. We also examine how these initiatives may lead to role conflict between investor expectations and venture purpose, the primacy of venture identity in shaping responses to venture role, and how diversity initiatives influence venture resource allocation and attractiveness.
