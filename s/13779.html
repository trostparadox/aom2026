Session overview

As political polarization has grown around the world, macro- and micro-organizational scholars have become increasingly interested in understanding its consequences for businesses. In the fourth iteration of this symposium, our goal is to continue to bring together scholars across these camps to facilitate conversation, integration, and collaboration. Topics that will be covered include growing corporate backtracking from prior political stances, ambient political conversations in organizations, heightened salience of political affiliation congruence for employees during national political elections, and new approaches to understanding ideology and party affiliation from the field of political psychology.
