Session overview

AOM 2026 marks the thirteenth consecutive year of this Professional Development Workshop. Since its inception, the PDW has been guided by the recognition that the dissertation stage is a pivotal moment in the development of qualitative scholars’ research capabilities and scholarly identities. This is particularly salient because qualitative research lacks a standardized “boilerplate” approach (Pratt, 2009), requiring sustained reflexivity, methodological judgment, and tailored guidance. Doctoral students who employ qualitative methods therefore benefit from developmentally targeted support. The PDW is open to students at all stages of the dissertation process—proposal development, data collection, analysis, and defense. Participants will receive both general guidance and individualized feedback while engaging directly with senior qualitative scholars. Our objective is to connect doctoral students with experienced faculty facilitators who can provide substantive feedback on research design and execution, as well as mentorship on career trajectories as qualitative researchers. Facilitators are drawn from a diverse community of leading qualitative management scholars with publications in top-tier journals and extensive experience navigating academic careers. Students will be matched with facilitators based on methodological approach, research topic, and/or level of analysis. Beyond methodological guidance, the PDW also provides structured opportunities for networking, fostering durable professional relationships within the qualitative research community.
