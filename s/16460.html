The Influence Mechanism of Corporate Hypocrisy on Restaurant Employees’ PEVB

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 1
ONE

Authors: Li Lin Nanjing Agricultural University; Danmei Zhan Nanjing Agricultural University; disen Xiong Nanjing Agricultural University

Session overview

To reveal the influence mechanism of organizational contextual factors on restaurant employees' Pro-Environmental Consumption Voice Behavior (PEVB), this study constructed and tested a dual-path model (affective path and rational path) of "corporate hypocrisy -> PEVB" based on cognitive dissonance theory and attribution theory, using four-wave longitudinal data from 228 frontline employees in the restaurant industry as samples, while examining the moderating role of Pro-environment value. Specifically, the affective path is "corporate hypocrisy -> Emotional exhaustion -> PEVB", and the rational path is "corporate hypocrisy -> Symbolic attribute -> PEVB". The results show that: corporate hypocrisy is significantly negatively correlated with employees' Remedial voice and Routine voice, but has no significant correlation with Informative voice; the affective path is entirely significant, that is, corporate hypocrisy can inhibit employees' PEVB by triggering Emotional exhaustion; the rational path is not verified, and the effect of corporate hypocrisy on PEVB through Symbolic attribute is not significant; Pro-environment value can negatively moderate the relationship between Emotional exhaustion and PEVB, and also negatively moderate the mediating effect of the affective path, meaning that high Pro-environment value can weaken the inhibitory effect of Emotional exhaustion on employees' PEVB. This study refines the internal logic of how corporate hypocrisy affects employees' pro-environmental behaviors through the dual-path model, clarifies the core role of the affective path, and provides theoretical basis and practical enlightenment for restaurant enterprises to optimize green human resource management practices, stimulate employees' PEVB, and promote the green transformation of the industry.
