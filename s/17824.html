Session overview

This PDW provides information about the “Network for Practice and Process Management Studies in African Empirical Settings” (www.netprosafrica.org) and the opportunity to be involved in this network. Facilitators will share the ongoing and planned activities of NETPROS, which is focused on fostering qualitative research in Africa and collaborative relationships between those based in Africa and those away. Participants will briefly share ongoing qualitative research based in Africa. Round tables will allow participants to share details on their own qualitative research based in Africa and identify opportunities for collaboration. The product of the PDW will be a table (shared electronically) describing the research and contact information identified in the roundtables in order to grow the NETPROS community.
