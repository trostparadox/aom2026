Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) brings together scholars in social networks and other research areas and creates an inclusive space for opportunities to discuss their interdisciplinary research ideas, applying social network perspectives and techniques. Although social network scholarship is often associated with a set of advanced quantitative techniques, its true value lies in the relational perspective: understanding patterns of interaction, interdependence, coordination, influence, and opportunities embedded in relationships. This workshop invites participants to explore those relational foundations together and to connect with others who bring complementary conceptual, methodological, or domain expertise. The workshop features an expert panel that will explore how interdisciplinary collaborations take shape, how scholars can avoid technique-driven misunderstandings, and how relational thinking can enrich research in domains where network perspectives are still emerging. After the panel, participants will join topic-focused roundtables designed to spark new research ideas, surface areas of shared curiosity, and identify potential collaboration pathways. A defining feature of this PDW is its explicit collaboration goal: participants will be encouraged to meet at least one potential research partner during the session, exchange contact information, and set a concrete follow-up plan before leaving the room. By anchoring the workshop in this shared commitment, we aim to ensure that the conversations initiated here grow into meaningful, cross-boundary research partnerships. This PDW advances AOM’s professional development mission by: (1) lowering entry barriers for scholars who are new to network concepts and uncertain where to begin; (2) showcasing cross-domain applications where relational insights add value; and (3) cultivating a community that supports idea generation and fosters research partnerships across divisions. By the end of the session, participants will leave with new conceptual entry points, potential collaborators, and concrete next steps for integrating relational perspectives into their work. No advance registration is required to attend this PDW; all AOM attendees are welcome.
