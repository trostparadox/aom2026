Session overview

Organizations and regulators adopt AI and digital monitoring technologies with a coherent theory of change: technological capacity will translate into organizational capacity. This symposium challenges that assumption. Four papers examine the technology-to-outcomes pipeline across policing, healthcare, and hiring, identifying distinct breakpoints where intended effects diverge from organizational realities. Accountability structures hollow out human oversight even as formal mandates remain. Monitoring systems generate data exceeding organizational capacity to process. AI tools expand surveillance beyond their stated purpose of reducing burden. And interventions designed to re-engage human judgment may or may not succeed. Discussants Lindsey Cameron and Hatim Rahman reflect on implications for theory and practice.
