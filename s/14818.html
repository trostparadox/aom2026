When Does AI Support Enhance Learning? The Role of Epistemic Motivation and Information Processing

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 5
MED

People

Author: Hua Jin University of Bristol Business School

Session overview

As artificial intelligence (AI) in computer programs becomes increasingly common in schools and training programs, in the field of management education one issue that arises is when and how AI assisted learning could aid students to learn better. This study develops a moderated mediation framework linking AI support, systematic information processing, and perceived learning outcomes, drawing on research on epistemic motivation and heuristic–systematic information processing model. We propose that AI assistance can lead to deeper and more structured processing of information, especially for students with high epistemic motivation. When learners are actively motivated to learn, AI-supported learning environments can contribute to improved learning outcomes. Such confidence can be fostered through systematic information processing. We conduct an empirical study of this model in online learning data based on survey. By combining motivational and cognitive frameworks around AI-supported learning settings, this research adds to the management education and learning literature. The recommendation shows that AI tools are not equally effective for all learners. Rather, their impact depends on the epistemic motivation of the learners. These findings can aid educators and organisations in creating AI-enhanced learning environments for deep engagement and enhanced learning.
