Session overview

As the management consulting industry continues to evolve, the gap between academic theory and the practical competencies required by top-tier firms remains a challenge for business schools. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) presents a proven framework for bridging this gap, drawing on the successful implementation of the “Road to Consulting” (RTC) program at Rutgers Business School. RTC is a two-year exclusive pipeline program designed to equip sophomores and juniors with the technical and behavioral skills necessary for consulting careers. Through a rigorous, hands-on curriculum that includes case interview preparation, consulting-focused workshops, speaker events, mentorship, experiential learning, and exclusive networking opportunities with consulting firms, RTC has supported multiple cohorts of students in securing internships and full-time roles at leading organizations, including Boston Consulting Group, Deloitte, Ernst & Young, PricewaterhouseCoopers, Crowe, Johnson & Johnson, and boutique consulting firms. In this interactive session, the facilitators will utilize a “Blueprint and Build” format to dissect the genesis, curriculum design, and stakeholder management of the RTC initiative. Participants will move beyond passive listening to engage in a “Pipeline Design” exercise, identifying constraints at their own institutions and developing actionable strategies to foster consulting talent. This workshop is intended for faculty, program directors, and administrators committed to enhancing student employability and building lasting partnerships with the consulting industry.
