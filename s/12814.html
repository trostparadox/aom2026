Session overview

This symposium aims to showcase current research on the negative externalities of information diffusion on online platforms. The objective is to identify key empirical findings and theoretical insights that support information systems design, platform strategy, and public policy. Throughout the symposium, presenters will showcase research that informs our understanding of the origins and consequences of information pollutants on online platforms, as well as potential solutions to regulate their rise.
