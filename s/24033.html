Double Hazard, Single Shield: How Supervisors Support Protects Employee Engagement (WITHDRAWN)

PNP
Stephen Teo (University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City); Diep Nguyen (The University of Newcastle)
Through the integrated lenses of the Job Demands-Resources model and Ethical Impact Theory, we conceptualize a resource-loss sequence framework within public bureaucracies to examine how psychosocial hazards at the workplace impact organizational support for employee well-being (OSWB) and thereby, reduce their agency engagement. The eight waves of the Australian Public Service Employee Census (2018-2025; total N=965,495 observations) and a strata-based fixed-effects pseudo-panel (434 units) provide supported empirical evidence. More importantly, longitudinal and cross-sectional data analyses show that supervisor support amplifies the negative effects of workplace hazards on employees’ engagement and strengthens the positive effect of OSWB. These findings offer meaningful theoretical and managerial implications for complex bureaucratic settings.
