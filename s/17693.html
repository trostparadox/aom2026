Session overview

The collection of work in this symposium illustrates the importance of studying social class from a multidimensional perspective. Indeed, it reveals how social class–jointly considering current and prior—shapes career outcomes, workplace experiences, and one’s identity. Additionally, the type of objective dimension and consideration of both objective and subjective components matter for predicting outcomes and capturing class-based experiences. While thematically similar, these papers use diverse approaches to studying social class–inductive and deductive reasoning, multi methods that include text analysis and natural language processing, and from samples in the U.S. and elsewhere. In this symposium, some work integrates different objective dimensions of social class. Multiple papers consider the imprint of social class early in life while other work explores the dynamic nature of social class throughout one’s career. This diversity enables a rich discussion of different multidimensional approaches to provide a more complete understanding of social class in organizations.
