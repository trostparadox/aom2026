Session overview

Navigating a hybrid professional identity of “scholar-practitioner” or “practitioner-scholar” comes with both challenges and opportunities to enhance career agility and relevance. This career development intensive and hands-on professional development workshop is designed to provide specific tools and frameworks needed to transition between academia and professional practice. Participants will hear inspiring bridging stories, envision their current situation in the scholar-practitioner continuum, work on their personalised Career Bridge Map, engage in group discussions, and leave with an action checklist to guide their career journey. This PDW is open to faculty, researchers, industry professionals and executives, doctoral student/early career academics, mid-career academics and administrators, and is a continuation of past MED practitioner professional development workshops. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
