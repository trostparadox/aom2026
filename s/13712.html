Session overview

Mental health challenges have long shaped academic life, yet they remain hidden in silence, shame, and structural neglect. This PDW invites participants to imagine mental health otherwise by reflecting on their own experiences of stress, burnout, anxiety, and isolation through a collective process. Rather than speaking of resilience, this workshop seeks to focus on community, relationality, and the shared conditions that allow us to thrive in academia. By creating an environment where participants can share, listen, speak, and reflect without fear of judgment, this session aims to explore how care can function as an everyday practice of resistance that disrupts the expectations and rhythms of the neoliberal university.
