How Organizational Purpose Establishes Trust, Strategic Alignment, External Credibility & Engagement

ODC
Johanna Anzengruber (University of AS Upper Austria)
While the majority of the research focuses on the definition and characteristics of corporate purpose, up to now scholars have paid far less attention to how purpose is realized, diffused and shared within firms (Tafuro & Piccaluga, 2025). Moreover, prior research tends to describe organizational purpose as a uniform concept rather than a set of dynamic activities shaped by all actors involved. As the need to tackle global challenges (Waddock & McIntosh, 2017) grows, effective pathways to achieve and sustain shared organizational purpose within and beyond firms become more relevant for fostering a regenerative economy. Through 37 qualitative interviews with purpose-driven organizations, we explore organizational purpose diffusion patterns – differentiated by processes and activities. These diffusion patterns we call symbolic integration, organized transformation, attractive differentiation and agile co-creation. For each of them, we pinpoint (1) the starting point (2) the pathway (3) the obstacles to reach a shared purpose and (4) the outcome achieved. Overall, we offer a framework for identifying integrated mechanisms through which shared organizational purpose is created, diffused and maintained within different kinds of organizations. With this research we shed light on how to more systematically approach the diffusion of shared organizational purpose.
