Session overview

Management educators play an important role in sharing evidence-based content (theoretical approaches and research evidence related to specific management topics) with students (future and current managers) and helping them develop the skills to incorporate this content into their practice. This PDW addresses how management educators who teach HR and HR-adjacent topics use teaching tools to help their students learn and transfer their learning to other contexts (ultimately, to their jobs), linking research to practice. Panelists will (1) introduce a tool they have used, (2) discuss what they do to ensure that the tool has the intended impact on student learning outcomes (e.g., affective reactions, knowledge, behavior), and (3) explain how they assess the impact of their teaching tool over the short and longer term. This last step will highlight how instructors can make their teaching more evidence-based. We use a “tell and show” format: The panelists will deliver a series of brief presentations “telling” the audience about their tools. Then the panelists will host tables “showing” small groups how they use their tools and assess impact (during and immediately after using the tool, at the end of the course, and down the road). Audience members will have opportunities to ask questions and rotate across tables during the session. Organizers will facilitate a full group discussion at the end of the session summarizing how participants can use training transfer and training evaluation principles to ensure that student learning transfers within and beyond their classrooms and their teaching is more evidence-based.
