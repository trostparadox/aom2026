Navigating Automation and AI-as-a-Service Product Performance: The Role of Institutional Logic

TIM
Xi-Jie Guo (Sun Yat-Sen University); Hui Fu (Sun Yat-Sen University); Shao-shuai Zhang (Sun Yat-Sen University); Xiaowei Zhu (Sun Yat-Sen University)
While automation in AI technology has been extensively studied from the application-side of AI, its impact on the growth of AI-as-a-Service (AIaaS) providers remains largely underexplored. Using a unique dataset from AIaaS complementors on Alibaba Cloud, we address this gap by empirically examining the inverted U-shaped relationship between automation framing and AIaaS product performance, with automation initially enhancing product performance but ultimately undermining it as automation framing intensifies. Meanwhile, market logic moderates this relationship: portfolio breadth steepens the curve, whereas commercial framing flattens it. In addition, the inverted U-shaped relationship is more pronounced for products leveraging strategies embedded in professional logic, characterized by broader module scope, data-driven capabilities, and lower technological complexity. By integrating AIaaS product growth with institutional logics in a platform context, this research offers critical insights into the institutional dynamics of AI automation and provides practical guidance for optimizing product design strategies in platform-dependent AI start-ups.
