GenAI, Circular Supply Chains, and Green IS in Dry Ports: Insights from China and Germany

OSCM
Zulqurnain Ali (Zhejiang University of Science and Technology)
In today’s complex business settings, digital technologies like generative AI (GenAI) hold significant potential for transforming organizational excellence. Despite its growing relevance, the influence of GenAI on dry port performance remains unexplored. Further, how can dry port companies revive port sustainability while implementing circular supply chain practices (CSCP)? To bridge these gaps, we aim to investigate the impact of GenAI on dry port performance (DPP) through the CSCP framework based on resource orchestration theory (ROT). Further, how green IS for pollution prevention (GIS) moderates these connections. Empirical data from 236 Chinese and 195 German dry port companies allow us to predict the DPP using structural equation modeling. This research demonstrates that the adoption of GenAI in port operations serves as a catalyst for CSCP, which consequently enhances DPP. The findings validate the moderating role of GIS across the examined relationships and yield strategic insights for dry port companies in both national contexts.
