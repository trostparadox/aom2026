Session overview

While classroom-as-organization (CAO) is a well-established semester-long course design within which students build, run and manage real organizational dynamics, CAO sessions in executive education are much less established. Participants will learn how to implement a compressed, high-impact CAO experience specifically designed for executive learners, who demand immediate value, practical application, and transformative learning outcomes within significantly constrained timeframes. As a train-the-trainer workshop, experienced executive education faculty will run participants through a short CAO ‘chunk’ sharing key adaptations, educator preparation, and in-the-moment pivots essential for the unique characteristics and expectations of executive learners. Facilitators will share debrief methods, suggested discussion questions, and resources as well. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
