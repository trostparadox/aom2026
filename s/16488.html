The Responsible AI Paradox: How Social Norms Influence the Intention-Behaviour Gap in AI Development

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 2
CTO

People

Authors: Öykü Isik IMD - International Institute for Management Development; Ankita Goswami IMD - International Institute for Management Development
Presenter: Patrick Reinmoeller

Session overview

Responsible artificial intelligence (RAI) development requires navigating ethical trade-offs due to competing organizational interests. Drawing from theories of planned behaviour (TPB) and regret, we propose a conceptual model to explain the gap between developers’ ethical intentions formed at the onset of AI development and the subsequent failure to implement RAI values in practice. We suggest tone at the top fosters ethical intentions through the mechanism of regret, especially of violating RAI principles. While TPB asserts the translation of intentions to behaviour, we highlight incongruence between tone at the top and tone at the bottom as a key barrier to RAI operationalisation. We further argue that personal factors, such as moral attentiveness and psychological safety, help create a positive RAI feedback loop in organisations. Our research promises significant theoretical and actionable insights for improving RAI operationalization.
