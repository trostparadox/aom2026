From Breach to Abolition: Moral Trauma as the Darkest Side of Psychological Contracts Violation

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 12
ODC

People

Authors: Sebastien Point EM Strasbourg Business School; Yochanan H. Altman; Johannes Marcelus Kraak KEDGE Business School

Session overview

In the context of the Division’s focus this year on exploring the implications of disruptive times for organizational development and change, we propose a novel angle to reflect on current and ongoing cardinal institutional developments that go beyond the ‘normal’ breach or violation of psychological contracts (PCs) - that essential aspect of unwritten, often unspoken, but ever present understandings of the mutual obligations and commitments between a workplace institution and the individuals they engage. Building on prior work on PCs ‘breach and violation’, we propose that ‘abolition’ better captures the severity of certain cosmological episodes (CEs) that produce moral trauma —ranging from moral distress to moral injury disorder. We present two CEs and their ramifications: 1) the conduct of personnel in US university campuses in the aftermath of October 7th 2023 vis-à-vis their Jewish staff and students on, and 2) the radical organizational changes in France Telecom. We argue that these instances which transcend normative PCs violation, have prompted a moral trauma, by threatening an individual’s core values, causing profound identity erosion.
