Session overview

An increasing share of work and value is delivered by actors the organisation does not employ through alternative work arrangements. Encompassing independent professionals, gig workers, freelancers, and the self-employed who fall outside of traditional employment relationships with organizations (Spreitzer et al., 2018; Cappelli & Keller, 2013) challenge and unsettle long-standing assumptions practices about how work is organized, who is included as part of the organizational workforce, and where organizational boundaries lie (Kinnie & Swart, 2019; Kniffin et al., 2021). Importantly, these work arrangements are not temporary disruptions but reflect enduring shifts in worker preferences, technological development, and organizational design (Ulrich & Yeung, 2019; Snell et al., 2023). As more work is accomplished through alternative workers, organisations face a practical organizing challenge: how to align cross-boundary contributors, orchestrate their interdependencies, and realise and retain the value created when vital work is performed beyond employment relationships. The purpose of this symposium is to develop a clearer theoretical and empirical understanding of how workforce ecosystems are aligned and orchestrated when alternative workers are central to the creation of value.
