Session overview

This PDW brings together the leading scholars and practitioners championing, documenting, and mainstreaming regenerative business principles and practices across different disciplines on six continents (Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Europe, and Australia). It includes an expert panel on ideologies, epistemologies and ontologies of regeneration, expert-led roundtables exploring the latest advances and special issues in four disciplines (management, entrepreneurship, supply chains, and marketing), followed by six continental conversations on regenerative innovations. Our aim is to build an AOM manifesto and co-create multi-generational, multi-knowledge outputs to implement the 2024 World Economic Forum agenda on regenerative economies and regenerative futures.
