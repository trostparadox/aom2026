Session overview

To understand the interplay between culture, contestation, and emotions in the formation of moral markets, we bring together a group of scholars offering diverse perspectives on the emerging alternative protein landscape. The four presentations in this symposium advance theories of market formation by showing how contested categories are actively shaped through cultural meanings, emotional investments, and ongoing struggles over legitimacy.
