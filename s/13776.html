Session overview

The future of effective management hinges on human-centred connections. Gallup’s 2025 findings reveal a stark reality: global employee engagement is declining, costing the world economy US$438 billion in lost productivity—a crisis rooted in declining manager effectiveness. Yet, within this lies extraordinary opportunity. If engagement rises, trillions in productivity gains and long-term organisational success are within reach. The linchpin is human-centred management as a strategic imperative. This innovative masterclass, grounded in international trends, invites management educators to reimagine their approach. Through interactive, scenario-driven activities—including the ROTE (Relational, Organisational, Technical, Environmental) and Communication frameworks—participants will explore human-centred values in management education. The workshop prepares future leaders for complex, AI-driven workplaces, unpacking practical methods to nurture relational intelligence, adaptability, ethical reasoning, and global competence. They will focus on fostering manager engagement for powerful ripple effects across organisations. Building on breakthrough research and global trends, this PDW shows how repivoting priorities toward human-centred work yields measurable gains in creativity, inclusion, and performance. Attendees will develop actionable strategies to teach, assess, and model human-centred skills, supported by an engaged peer network. Participants will leave not only with practical tools but also with a peer network for ongoing growth in human-centred, AI-ready education. This session offers a transformative blueprint—empowering educators to move from insight to action and shape graduates who are not only human-centred but truly prepared to lead and thrive in the AI-driven workplace.
