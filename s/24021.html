Orchestrating Sustainability Knowledge Through an Industry Association to Address Grand Challenges

ONE
Guillaume Charles Frederic Pain (Laval University); Raymond L. Paquin (Concordia University); Catherine Geneviève Morin (Laval University)
Addressing grand challenges requires companies to orchestrate knowledge to generate the innovation capabilities needed for social and environmental value creation. We study an industry association and four members firms over an 11-year period to explore how firms engage in knowledge orchestration for value creation. Applying multiple qualitative methods, we track firms’ knowledge orchestration processes through their engagement with the industry association, identify a hierarchy of concepts within firms’ shared knowledge bases that may affect subsequent knowledge orchestration, and uncover three knowledge orchestration profiles – generator, adopter, and divestor - that differ by company size, explorative orientation, orchestration processes, and performance outcomes. We contribute to a better understanding of the links between knowledge orchestration, innovation, and performance in the context of grand challenges.
