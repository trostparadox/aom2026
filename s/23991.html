AI-Augmented ESSCA: Reinventing Environmentally Sustainable Strategy for Competitive Advantage

STR
Pradeesh Kumar
In an era fueled by rapidly growing digital transformation and intensifying environmental pressures, organizations face increasing challenges to align sustainability with long-term competitive advantage. Traditional sustainability strategies focus on compliance and incremental improvement, lacking the dynamic capabilities required to respond to complex, data-intensive, and rapidly evolving market environments. To address this gap, the study introduces the Environmentally Sustainable Strategy for Competitive Advantage (ESSCA) framework and advances it by integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) as a strategic, analytical, and governance enabler, resulting in the development of a novel ESSCA-AI model. The ESSCA-AI framework conceptualizes AI as a prime foundational intelligence layer embedded within the organizational architecture. This layer helps to enhance external environmental analysis, internal capability assessment, strategic moves, and green governance, thereby expanding organizational adaptability. Specifically, the model demonstrates how AI augments environmental scanning through predictive analytics and stakeholder sentiment analysis to sharpen external sensing, utilizes intelligent resource orchestration to optimize internal value chains, and supports strategic decision-making through algorithmic timing, market positioning, and innovation management. Furthermore, AI-enabled green governance facilitates real-time monitoring of the Triple bottom line (TBL) that integrates profit, planet, and people, ensuring transparency, accountability, and measurable sustainability outcomes. Importantly, the ESSCA-AI framework does not position AI as inherently sustainable; rather, its impact is contingent on responsible governance, ethical design, and organizational intent. By incorporating AI within the ESSCA architecture, the proposed model reframes sustainability from a static compliance-oriented function into a dynamic source of sustained competitive advantage. This research contributes to the intersection of sustainability and strategic management literature by offering a future oriented framework that combines digital intelligence with environmental responsibility. ESSCA-AI model provides both scholars and practitioners with a comprehensive analytical lens to design, implement, and evaluate AI-enabled sustainable strategies, particularly within sectors that are environmentally sensitive and technologically intensive.
