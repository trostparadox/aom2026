Session overview

Globally, we are facing multiple, interacting social and ecological crises, most of which have been exacerbated, if not caused, by untrammeled, extractive economic activity in line with shareholder capitalism. As business academics, we share responsibility for reproducing this system. Yet business also has the power and potential to deliver better outcomes – innovating, investing in and organizing positive solutions that can regenerate ecological systems and lead to human thriving. Can we imagine a society where business simultaneously works to create regenerative economic, social and ecological value rather than prioritizing only shareholder value? In this workshop, we’ll outline the problem and some current solutions, then work with participants to map the system and identify new leverage points for change. We’ll invite participants to imagine their own contributions to catalyze system transformation through their research, teaching and engagement.
