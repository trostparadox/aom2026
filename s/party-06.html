STR Happy Hour

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 4:30PM–6:30PM
Independence Beer Garden, 100 S Independence Mall W, Philadelphia, PA 19106, USA

Event details

A short walk from the Pennsylvania Convention Center and directly across from the Liberty Bell, Independence Beer Garden is a sprawling 20,000-square-foot outdoor beer garden overlooking Independence National Historical Park. Kick off the conference by meeting fellow STR members over a rotating lineup of regional and domestic craft beers alongside cocktails, wine, and zero-proof options, with a casual menu of American bites (yes, including a cheesesteak). Our gathering falls entirely within the venue’s daily happy hour, so drinks are budget-friendly.

This is an informal, pay-your-own-way meet-up: no program, no reservations — just conversation. Everyone is welcome, and doctoral students and early-career faculty are especially encouraged to drop by, whether for ten minutes or the full two hours. Look for the STR sign to find the group.

No registration required.
