Session overview

Organizations increasingly learn under conditions of ambiguity, volatility, and complexity, where clear goals, stable causality, and reliable feedback can no longer be assumed. This symposium shows that learning in such environments is not automatic, but shaped by how experience is interpreted, socially situated, and scaffolded through relational and cultural infrastructures. This symposium aims to reconceptualize learning as a fragile, contested, and temporally unstable process rather than a cumulative or mechanical one.
