All Sizzle and No Steak:The Predicament of AI Strategies Under Central Bank Digital Currency Policy

TIM
Jinlei Yu (Nankai University); Rui Chen (Nankai University)
This study examines the effect of central bank digital currency policies on the decoupling of enterprise AI strategies, using China’s digital currency (e-CNY) pilot as a policy shock. Based on data on A-share listed firms from 2014 to 2024 and a multi-period difference-in-differences model, we find that the introduction of the e-CNY significantly increases firms’ AI-related statements without raising actual technology investment, leading to a gap between stated strategy and real action. Mechanism tests suggest that this effect operates through higher currency liquidity, stronger transaction traceability and regulatory oversight, as well as greater payment efficiency. Further analysis shows that AI decoupling lowers firm market value and suppresses venture capital investment. This study adds to the literature on the microeconomic effects of digital currency and enterprise AI strategy decoupling.
