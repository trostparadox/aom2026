From Harnessing to Co-Evolving: How Human–Machine Collaboration Dynamically Shapes Innovation

ODC
Zhidan Tan (China University of Mining and Technology (Xuzhou)); Yujie Sun (Tongji University)
With the deepening of digital transformation in the healthcare sector, human–machine collaboration has become a key pathway to addressing the uneven distribution of high-quality medical resources and improving diagnostic efficiency and accuracy. However, existing research largely focuses on the static models and performance outcomes of human–machine collaboration, while lacking systematic exploration of its dynamic evolutionary mechanisms. To fill this gap, this study selects KingMed Diagnostics as a typical case and adopts a longitudinal case study approach to construct a logical analysis framework of “collaborative drivers—collaborative mechanisms—innovation outcomes.” It analyzes the intrinsic mechanisms and evolutionary logic of human– machine collaborative innovation. The findings reveal that: (1) the evolution of human–machine collaborative innovation follows a stepwise progressive path of “linear assistance—complementary empowerment—closed-loop co-nurturing”; (2) the collaborative mechanism is embodied in a dynamic adaptation system of “technology–human–scenario,” evolving from “tool-based collaboration” to “integrated collaboration” and then to “ecosystem collaboration”; (3) the outcomes of human–machine collaborative innovation manifest as progressive breakthroughs of “cloud-based services—intelligent diagnostics— value co-creation,” driving organizations from localized efficiency improvements toward multi-stakeholder value co-prosperity. Building on these insights, this study uncovers the evolutionary patterns of human–machine collaborative innovation, expands the theoretical boundaries of human–machine collaboration research in the context of the digital economy, and provides practical implications for enterprises in various industries to identify collaboration stages and optimize collaboration models during intelligent transformation.
