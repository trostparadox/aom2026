Sustainable Entrepreneurship: Securing Funding and Navigating Legitimacy

ONE
Sonal Nayak (Indian Institute of Management Bangalore)
The literature on sustainable entrepreneurship has expanded significantly, yet there remains limited understanding of how sustainable entrepreneurs secure funding. Funding is crucial for any organization, whether an incumbent or a startup, and this challenge is especially pronounced for startups in their early stages. While the literature has explored various strategies that sustainable startups use to navigate these challenges, much of the research at the intersection of sustainable entrepreneurship, funding efforts, and legitimacy seeking behaviors remains fragmented and there has yet to be a comprehensive effort to provide a unified overview of this literature. This paper investigates how sustainable startups secure funding from various sources, and what obstacles they encounter in this process. It undertakes an extensive analysis of 558 articles across 32 leading journals on the topic. It integrates extant literature around two broad themes namely securing funding and navigating legitimacy as well as identifies promising avenues for future research.
