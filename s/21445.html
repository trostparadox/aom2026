Session overview

Business schools increasingly operate as complex academic enterprises in volatile financial environments shaped by shifting enrollments, constrained funding, rising costs, and growing stakeholder expectations. Yet many management faculty who move into leadership roles lack formal preparation in academic financial management. This Professional Development Workshop, Financial Management in Academic Enterprises, addresses this gap by introducing frameworks and practical insights into the economics of courses, programs, and schools. The workshop examines how revenue models, cost structures, cross-subsidies, and investment decisions vary across institutional and regulatory contexts. Through interactive discussion and peer exchange, the PDW aims to build financial literacy and confidence among current and aspiring academic leaders, strengthening their ability to align educational missions with sustainable resource decisions.
