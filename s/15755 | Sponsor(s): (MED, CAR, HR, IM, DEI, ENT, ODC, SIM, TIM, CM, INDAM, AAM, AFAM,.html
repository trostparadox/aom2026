Session overview

This PDW has been designed by the MED’s (Management Education and Development) doctoral students and early career team to promote retention, professional development, success, and most importantly, thriving and sustained well-being of doctoral students and early career academics in management as they navigate their academic and professional journeys and the key transitions associated with them. A primary target audience for this PDW is doctoral students who are in the process of acclimating to their programs. The workshop seeks to support this transition by enhancing participants’ understanding of the roles and responsibilities associated with PhD training and by increasing clarity regarding expectations and milestones across different stages of doctoral education. In addition, the PDW supports early career faculty by offering developmental guidance for navigating career expectations, professional demands, tenure-process, and role transitions within academic careers. Our goal is to do so with careful attention to the diverse backgrounds of our participants, recognizing cross-cultural challenges experienced by international students and scholars, variations in doctoral program structures, and the perspectives of both traditional and non-traditional academics. We aim to equips doctoral students and early career faculty with practical tools to support successful and sustainable careers in management. In addition, this PDW offers participants opportunities to network, build relationships, and foster a strong sense of belonging to a supportive professional community. Finally, our PDW provides an avenue for participants to learn more about the MED Division’s mission, values, and activities, and to explore opportunities for ongoing engagement within the division. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
