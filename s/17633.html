Why Employees Adopt GenAI and When It Pays off: A Job Demands-Resources Perspective

Authors: siyu zhou Shantou University; Jiarui Yin Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya - BarcelonaTech; Katy Liu ESADE Business School

Session overview

Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is rapidly transforming the workplace, yet we lack systematic understanding of what drives employees to adopt these tools and whether such adoption improves performance. Drawing on the job demands-resources (JD-R) model, we propose a dual-pathway framework wherein both job demands (work overload, time pressure) and job resources (autonomy, organizational support) predict GenAI use at work, which in turn enhances task and creative performance. We further theorize that AI literacy serves as a critical boundary condition determining whether GenAI adoption translates into performance gains. Unlike prior research that examines consequences of GenAI collaboration or treats AI characteristics as demands/resources, our model positions GenAI adoption as a behavioral mediator linking job characteristics to performance outcomes. A three-wave time-lagged survey will be administered to validate the model. This research contributes to JD-R theory by extending mediating mechanisms from psychological states to behavioral responses, and provides practical guidance for organizations seeking to understand when and why GenAI adoption benefits employee performance.
