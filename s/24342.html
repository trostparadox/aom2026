Does Artificial Intelligence Amplify the Green Innovation Dividend of Green Bonds?

TIM
Junjun Zhou (Central South University); Kuo-Jui Wu (Hainan University); Shoujuan Zang (Harbin Institute of Technology); zihui xu (Jinan University)
Green finance is playing an important role in promoting green and low-carbon development. Although the economic outcomes of green bonds have been widely examined, far less attention has been paid to heterogeneity in firms’ internal technological foundations, particularly the role of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI), in shaping the green innovation of green bond issuance. We fill this gap by quantitatively assessing how AI influences the impact of green bond issuance on firms’ green innovation. Based on firm-level data from 698 Chinese listed bond-issuing firms over 2010 to 2023, we use a difference-in-differences approach to investigate whether AI amplifies the green innovation benefits of green bond issuance. The results show that firms with AI capabilities experience significantly greater marginal gains in green innovation after green bond issuance than other firms, indicating that AI strengthens the positive effect of green bonds on green innovation. Mechanism analyses suggest that the effect operates through AI-driven improvements in firms’ green productivity. Further analyses reveal a noteworthy finding that firms with AI capabilities are less likely to engage in greenwashing, suggesting that AI facilitates more substantive and genuine green innovation. Our findings offer new managerial insights into the synergistic application of AI and green finance.
