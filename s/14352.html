Session overview

This symposium examines how everyday workplace communication shapes social judgment and interpersonal outcomes. Across diverse organizational contexts, the papers show that the timing and form of communication influence how others evaluate people, attribute credit and responsibility, and form judgments about trust and support. Drawing on theories of impression management and social evaluation, the session highlights ways in which well-intended communication can backfire and advances understanding of how communication affects relationships at work.
