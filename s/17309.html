From Inspiration to Innovation

Authors: Dahyun Lee Sungkyunkwan University; Jiseon Shin Sungkyunkwan University

Session overview

Creativity is critical in knowledge-intensive work, yet little is known about how individual psychological resources and organizational contexts jointly shape the translation of leadership into creative behavior. Drawing on Social Cognitive Theory and a large-scale, nationally administered dataset of 2,000 employees, this study conceptualizes creativity as a psychologically contingent and contextually embedded outcome of leadership. We show that psychological capital strengthens the relationship between transformational leadership and psychological safety, and that organizational flexibility culture amplifies the effect of psychological safety on creative behavior. The indirect effect of transformational leadership on creativity via psychological safety is strongest when both psychological capital and organizational flexibility culture are high. These findings highlight the importance of cognitive–contextual alignment in enabling creativity.
