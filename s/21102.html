Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) offers an interactive and reflective exploration of the relationship between generative artificial intelligence (AI) and critical thinking in management education. Drawing on a PechaKucha-style presentation previously delivered by the facilitators, the workshop uses visual storytelling, rapid provocations, and experiential dialogue to engage participants in one of the most pressing debates in contemporary education: whether AI is a threat to, or an enabler of, critical thinking and creativity. The workshop accommodates educators who are new to generative AI as well as those already experimenting with AI in teaching. Participants engage with short, provocative prompts followed by structured reflection and collaborative sense-making. The session moves beyond binary debates and supports participants in reframing AI as a potential co-pilot in developing future-fit graduates. Participants leave with practical strategies for designing learning activities that foster critical thinking, ethical awareness, and creativity in AI-augmented classrooms, alongside a reflective framework for making pedagogically informed decisions about AI use in management education.
