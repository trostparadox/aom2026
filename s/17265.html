Session overview

The research on effectuation during the last 25 years has introduced a novel way of viewing entrepreneurial journeys. Yet, the effectuation process remains abstract in many of its principles especially the cocreation and lemonade principles (Arends. et al., 2015; Mckelvie et al., 2019). The study of the entrepreneurial journey requires to move from mindset and reasoning to real action. This study aims therefore to establish an operational link between reasoning and entrepreneurial action via strategy. Qualitative research has been adopted to study the entrepreneurial journeys of 15 expert entrepreneurs using in-depth interviews and contextualized think aloud protocols. This research focuses on how expert entrepreneurs cocreate their business model in facing uncertain environments. Empirical findings support literature but also reveals how these entrepreneurs utilize both effectual and strategic reasoning based on simplified business plan formats. It highlights the role of the cocreation process and the experimentation phase as central mechanisms adopted by entrepreneurs. Therefore, it contributes by introducing a new effectual and strategic tool based on the GRP model (Verstraete, Jouison-Lafitte, 2012) that can be mobilized in other settings to help entrepreneurs create and pilot their ventures.
