Session overview

This PDW introduces the Spinning Top Practice, a contemplative, embodied, and epistemic tool emerging from the Book, Rethinking the University. The spinning top functions throughout this book as a metaphor and reflective method to explore attunement, complexity, epistemic humility, and the unknown, supporting an institutional transformation informed by spirituality, systems thinking, and integrating simplification. The method helps participants access deep inner awareness while simultaneously engaging in collective sensemaking under conditions of polarization, epistemic fragmentation, and polycrisis, issues increasingly shaping universities and organizations. Through experiential spinning-top exercises, structured reflection, small-group systems mapping, and pedagogical design activities, participants learn how the practice develops Epistemic Courage, Epistemic Dharma, and mindfulness as epistemic infrastructure. The PDW provides a developmental, interactive forum to apply this contemplative-systems approach across research, teaching, leadership, and organizational practice, supporting institutions and educators seeking to strengthen epistemic resilience, reflective capacity, and adaptive coherence. Participants will leave the workshop with a transferable embodied pedagogy, a classroom-ready sensemaking exercise, and a design template for integrating contemplative and systems-based practices into management education and faculty development.
