Session overview

This quiet, low-stimulation room is designed to support individuals who are neurodivergent, sensory-sensitive, or simply in need of a calm space to rest and recharge. The environment is intentionally free from bright lights, loud sounds, and overwhelming activity. Comfortable seating and soft lighting will be available to help regulate and restore focus. All attendees are welcome to use this space as needed—no explanation required. Please help maintain a peaceful atmosphere by keeping voices low, silencing devices, and avoiding strong scents or disruptive behavior.
