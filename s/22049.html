Houston, We Have a Prompt: AI's Impact on Managerial Cognition in Open Innovation

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 5
TIM

People

Author: Basudeo Dubey XLRI Xavier School of Management

Session overview

Introduction: Open innovation (OI) platforms promise to unlock value, yet managers often struggle to identify the "best" submissions. This study explores how AI prompts can reshape managerial decision-making in OI, potentially leading to more effective selection processes. Theory/Hypotheses: Drawing on cognitive psychology and behavioral economics, we hypothesize that AI prompts will influence managers' attention, framing, and ultimately, their selection of OI submissions, potentially leading to a more data-driven approach to innovation. Methods: We conducted an experimental study with 100 managers. Participants evaluated a set of OI submissions. Participants were randomly assigned to either receive AI-generated prompts or no prompts before making their selection decisions. Results: We find that managers receiving AI prompts selected submissions with higher novelty scores and lower risk profiles. These effects were mediated by changes in perceived value. Discussion/Implications: Our findings suggest that AI can act as a "Moneyball" tool, helping managers make more informed OI decisions. This has implications for platform design, the strategic management of innovation, and the potential for AI to democratize access to innovation insights.
