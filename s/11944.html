Session overview

The contemporary polycrisis, characterized by complex, interconnected global risks, stems from systemic failures in human behavior and organizational approaches rather than historical accident (Mark et al., 2025; Purdey, 2025). Critical scholars argue these crises represent interconnected manifestations of deeper structural contradictions within global capitalism (Albert, 2025; Holgersen, 2024). This symposium proposes "leadershift" (Maxwell, 2019) as a transformative solution: an internally driven transition from ego-centered ambition to collective consciousness through mindfulness practices (Marques, 2024; Waddock, 2024b; Stenfors et al., 2025). By cultivating altruistic leadership that prioritizes collective wellbeing over personal aggrandizement, organizations can reduce inequality and stress while fostering engagement and advancement, ultimately addressing root causes of the polycrisis through evolved consciousness.
