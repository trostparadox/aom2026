Session overview

The purpose of the workshop is to equip health leaders and health educators with the knowledge and skills needed to strengthen workforce sustainability while improving organizational performance in healthcare environments and workplaces. This professional development workshop (PDW) will build conceptual, analytical, and leadership capacity among health leaders and health educators. These groups need to understand how evolving digital technologies, while often organizationally positive, can dramatically influence work environments and subsequently foster moral distress that leads to complex relationships and retention challenges. Solutions to these challenges focus on identifying evidence-informed strategies to enhance technologically competent, ethical, and supportive work environments. In the opening PDW statements, panelists from a broad range of backgrounds, empirical contexts, and methodological approaches will provide an overview of this triad: technological changes, moral distress, and workforce retention; followed by leadership strategies for distress mitigation. In the overview, we will: 1. Identify technology as an enabler of compassionate and effective care, and incorporate a forward-thinking perspective on how new technologies and organizational strategies can both exacerbate and potentially alleviate workplace/workforce pressures, 2. Identify moral distress as an organizational issue in the resource-constrained environments of healthcare and education, 3. Link technological advances and moral distress in the workplace to health provider and educator retention challenges, and 4. Identify leadership strategies to mitigate moral distress for health providers and health educators. Following the overview, panelists will facilitate discussions as part of break-out sessions with participants to explore actionable procedures within healthcare organizations and academic environments in which healthcare leadership is taught. The goal of these procedures will be to focus on organizational and system-level strategies that integrate leadership practices to sustain the healthcare workforce and mitigate moral distress emerging from technological advancements in the workplace.
