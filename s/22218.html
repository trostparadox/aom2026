Session overview

In this proposed symposium, we seek to advance two arguments in response to dominant theoretical perspectives on diversity and marginality in entrepreneurship: first, that entrepreneurship among marginalized groups can be understood through an emancipatory and value-driven lens; and second, that technology, public policy, and institutions shape the emancipatory and value-driven entrepreneurial possibilities of marginalized entrepreneurs.
