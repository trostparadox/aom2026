Disclosure and Investment in Corporate Digital Transformation: An Attention-Based View

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 6
TIM

People

Authors: Yueqi Wang Dalian University of Technology; Wenqian Chen

Session overview

What determines corporate digital transformation (DT) strategies? A nascent group of literature has well investigated this question. However, they largely adopt the notion of DT to encompass various corporate DT-related activities, making the process of DT still unclear. To this end, we differentiate DT strategy into disclosure and investment. Drawing on the attention-based view (ABV), we establish the nexus between DT disclosure and investment and contend that a firm’s DT disclosure, conceptualized as managerial attention (in terms of breadth and depth), positively shapes its DT investment amount. Furthermore, we introduce government digital policy intensity and corporate digital leadership as two distinct moderators and develop a three-way interaction framework. Using archival data of 3,037 listed manufacturing firms in China during 2014-2022, we find that both digital attention breadth and depth facilitate corporate DT investment, especially under high policy intensity. However, digital leadership exhibits divergent influence on the moderating role of policy intensity: for firms with high digital leadership, the positive effects of policy intensity on the attention breadth (or depth)-investment relationship will be attenuated (or amplified). Overall, our work contributes to corporate DT literature from the perspective of ABV, and delivers critical implications for practitioners in both management and policy making.
