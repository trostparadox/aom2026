Session overview

The word information comes from the Latin informare, to take form or structure. The strategic governance of information increasingly determines how firms build and sustain competitive advantage in intangible-asset-driven economies. While strategy research has generated foundational insights on knowledge transfer, spillovers, and imitation barriers, it lacks an integrative framework explaining how firms proactively manage information as a strategic asset and source of risk—through selective disclosure, secrecy, IP portfolio design, security investments, and legal devices—especially in the age of AI- enabled information generation, inference, and expanding digital leakage opportunities. This panel symposium introduces the Strategic Management of Information (SMI) framework, which positions information governance as a core pillar of firm strategy shaping both innovation incentives and value capture.
