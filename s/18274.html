Bolstering Creativity and Innovation in Remote Work Environments

Authors: Payton Stewart Louisiana State University; Grace Mahfouz Louisiana State University; Michelle Todd Louisiana State University

Session overview

This study investigated the interaction between feedback environment and remote work frequency on creativity, as well as the moderation of interpersonal variables on this relationship. We found that interpersonal variables, including trust, psychological safety, and communication, moderated the effects of feedback on creativity in remote work contexts. Our findings offer a deeper understanding of how to foster creativity within remote work environments.
