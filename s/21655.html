Session overview

The Consortium is designed for TIM faculty members. The overall objective is to support TIM scholars who have recently achieved tenure or a similarly significant career advancement to develop a strategy for the next stages of their careers. Towards this objective, senior TIM scholars will reflect on their experience and how they think about the mid-career phase of their career. Panel discussions, as well as break-out and/or Q&A sessions, will provide mid-career faculty members with the opportunity to interact with senior faculty for practical advice and mentorship on various topics including research, teaching, outreach, networks, administrative functions and more.
