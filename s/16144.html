The Role of Humanistic Leadership in Cultivating Followers' Sustainable Lifestyle Habits

Authors: Boxiang Lin Nottingham University Business School China; Qing Qu Tsinghua University; Ping Ping Fu Nottingham University Business School China

Session overview

Studies on organizational sustainable topic have long focused on corporate level issues, such as corporate social responsibility (CSR) and environmental, social and governance (ESG) criteria. The important role of individual behaviors in achieving these goals has been increasingly recognized. Humanistic leadership, characterized by regarding followers as holistic human beings, providing humanistic education and working harmoniously with all parties, has been found to play a positive role in the implementation of UNSDGs in organizations by engaging subordinates. However, the specific mechanisms for this have not been quantitatively tested. This study explores the mechanism of humanistic leaders cultivating followers’ sustainable lifestyle habits. Based on the theory of habit formation, values are introduced as a mediating variable. Building on the feature that humanistic leaders act as “influencers” rather than “commanders”, we specify the mediators as a chain from followers’ prosocial values to person-organization values congruence. This study developed a scale for measuring humanistic leadership and tested the above mechanism. The results show that there is a significant indirect positive effect of humanistic leadership on the sustainable lifestyle habits of subordinates, specifically through the chain mediating effect of prosocial values and person-organization values congruence.
