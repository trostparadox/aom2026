Author: Cédric Poivret Gustave Eiffel University

Session overview

The text presents the evolution over the past decade of research on the history of managerial thought in France, a field that has historically been underdeveloped compared to the United States and the Anglo-Saxon world. Since 2017, thanks to initiatives such as the publication of programmatic articles and the organization of research workshops, in institutions that are emblematic of the history of management in France this field has developped. They brought together around one hundred researchers. The project aims to highlight the history of managerial thought as a field distinct from business history and to promote a more nuanced approach than that offered by organizational theory.
