Session overview

The rigor, transparency, and relevance of research methods are central to the credibility and impact of scholarly work. This symposium brings together current and former editors of Organizational Research Methods to discuss how methodological research can constitute meaningful contributions, be evaluated as credible, and influence both scholarship and practice. The focus of the symposium is to help aspiring authors create papers that contribute to methodological knowledge that researchers find credible, and that positively impact empirical research practices. Emphasis is placed on crafting accessible, transparent, and well-justified methodological contributions that extend beyond existing approaches and enable cumulative knowledge building. Drawing on their editorial experience, the panelists will offer practical guidance and resources for scholars seeking to enhance the contribution, credibility, and impact of methodological work.
