Session overview

The integration of AI into R&D processes is reshaping how organizations produce, evaluate, and manage scientific knowledge and innovation. This interactive PDW convenes leading scholars to examine a set of core managerial questions that arise as AI becomes embedded in scientific and innovative activity. How has the organization of R&D evolved as AI has become an increasingly important input into the innovation process? How does the use of AI in the evaluation of scientific outputs change our ability to assess the reliability and quality of research? How might the growing reliance on AI-based systems to evaluate scientific talent reshape firms’ hiring decisions? Finally, how can AI be used to understand the structure of the relationship between science and technology? The workshop brings together frontier empirical research that speaks to these questions from complementary perspectives. Through short paper presentations, discussions by senior scholars, and interactive breakout sessions, the PDW aims to synthesize emerging evidence on AI’s implications for the management of science and innovation, surface shared conceptual and methodological challenges, foster a coherent research agenda within the TIM and STR communities, and support PhD students and early-career scholars interested in studying AI and innovation.
