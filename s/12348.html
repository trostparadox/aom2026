Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW), organized as part of the AOM HR Division Ambassador Program, provides a unique opportunity for scholars to engage directly with editors serving as HR Ambassadors from leading journals in the field. The session is designed for both early-career and experienced researchers seeking to understand the publication and review process, gain insights on positioning manuscripts, and develop skills as effective reviewers. It speaks to two key goals of the Ambassador Program: to enhance the opportunities for international members to achieve representation at the annual conference, and encourage dialogue and mutual learning across and beyond Ambassador membership. There will be two parts to the PDW. A first part will be devoted to the theme highlighted above: ‘Insights from HR Ambassador Journal Editors’ (2 hours) while the second part of the meeting will be the Ambassador Program Annual Meeting (30 minutes). The HR Executive Global Committee will highlight progress throughout the year, discuss opportunities for further development of the Program and encourage Ambassadors who are present at AOM to share their thoughts and ideas as to next steps. During the first part of the session, editors from journals including Journal of Business Research, Human Resource Management, European Management Journal, International Journal of Human Resource Management, Human Resource Management Journal, Group & Organization Management, Management and Organization Review, Human Resource Development International and Human Resource Development Quarterly will provide brief introductions to their journals’ scope, focus, and audience, followed by an interactive Q&A session. The workshop concludes with small round-table discussions, allowing participants to interact directly with editors and peers according to research interests. By fostering dialogue and mentorship, this PDW supports career development, strengthens scholarly identity, and promotes international and inclusive engagement in the HR scholarly community.
