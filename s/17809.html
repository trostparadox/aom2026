Session overview

Areas of Organizational Behavior, Human Resource Management, Employment Law and International Management have been broadly impacted by anti-DEI legislation. This PDW convenes experts in the field to examine (1) the wide range of topics encompassed by DEI, (2) Anti- DEI legislation shaping our academic work and practice, (3) the impacts of DEI backlash on research and teaching, and (4) the consequences for students on our campuses. This session moves beyond solely identifying the challenges we currently face to co-create tools and strategies for navigating the academic environment amidst DEI backlash.
