Session overview

The primary objective of this Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is to formally initiate planning for the Fourth Edition of The Global Human Resource Management Casebook by convening current and prospective contributors. The Casebook’s First Edition, published in 2011, was the inaugural initiative of the HR Division’s Ambassadors Program. Since the program’s establishment in 2008, it has made a significant impact not only within the HR Division but across the Academy of Management, inspiring several other divisions to establish their own ambassador programs. The Second and Third Editions of the Casebook were published in 2018 and 2023, respectively. While the First Edition focused on cases and discussion questions, subsequent editions expanded the pedagogical value of the volume by including detailed teaching notes, and, in the Third Edition, lesson plans for two- to three-hour HR classes accompanying each case in the online version. All three editions have been warmly received internationally and have demonstrated strong global sales performance. Taylor & Francis has confirmed its commitment to publishing the Fourth Edition, with the HR Division continuing as the beneficiary of the Casebook’s royalties. The PDW will serve as a platform to engage HR Ambassadors, align editorial objectives, and develop a structured roadmap to ensure the successful launch of the Fourth Edition planned for the 2028 Academy of Management Annual Meeting.
