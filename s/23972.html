Dynamic Capabilities as Sociotechnical Mechanisms for Digital Transformation and Innovation

STR
ALESSANDRA YULA Yula TUTIDA; Carlos Ricardo Rossetto; Ivanete Schneider Hahn (Universidade do Extremo Sul de Santa Catarina (Unesc)); Luísa Carvalho (Instituto Politécnico de Setúbal); Janaina Maria Bueno (Federal University of Uberlândia)
This study examines how dynamic capabilities operate as sociotechnical mechanisms enabling digital transformation and innovation in traditional industries. Drawing on a qualitative multiple case study of five family-owned wineries in Brazil and Portugal, the analysis reveals that digital transformation unfolds through the recursive interaction between social and technical subsystems rather than through technology adoption alone. The findings show that sensing, seizing, and transforming emerge as interdependent sociotechnical processes integrating leadership, organizational cognition, and historically embedded practices with digital technologies and data infrastructures. Rather than disrupting tradition-based identities, digital transformation enables strategic renewal through the recombination of traditional and digital resources. By reconceptualizing dynamic capabilities as sociotechnical mechanisms, this study advances dynamic capability theory, challenges techno-deterministic assumptions in digital transformation research, and extends strategic management insights into innovation in traditional industries.
