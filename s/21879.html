Session overview

This symposium integrates four papers documenting novel systematic inaccuracies in organizational forecasts of positive subjective experiences. The first part of our symposium investigates within-organization processes of misperceptions, highlighting that misperceptions are embedded throughout the structure of the organization. The second part of our symposium investigates between-organization misperceptions, highlighting that observers’ perceptions of different types of occupations are often biased. Together, they show that perceptual gaps concerning authenticity, meaning, and enjoyment are shaped by structural cues—job status, incentives, occupational categories, and norms—and that these inaccuracies have consequences for hiring, career choices, compensation, and performance.
