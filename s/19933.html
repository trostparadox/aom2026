Session overview

Employee volunteering is on the rise as organizations are increasingly expected to tackle societal challenges. Despite growth in employee volunteering scholarship, research remains fragmented. Recent reviews point to inconsistent findings in the relationships between employee volunteering and its antecedents and outcomes, and we also lack understanding of the processes and mechanisms that drive volunteer engagement, especially in large multinational organizations. Drawing on quantitative and qualitative methods, this symposium addresses these gaps, and provides a holistic picture of the antecedents, outcomes, mechanisms, and processes that underscore employee volunteer participation and program success. The symposium will shed new light on why employee volunteering matters, the impact this has on key stakeholders, the mechanisms through which these impacts emerge, and the contextual and situational factors that shape its uptake. The symposium will close with an audience discussion led by our discussants on the implications of the four papers, with the aim of positively shaping future research directions.
