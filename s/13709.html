Session overview

Developmental relationships in the workplace are critical to long-term career success and satisfaction. In this symposium, we advance research on developmental relationships by focusing on development that occurs within horizontal workplace relationships, with particular attention to development provided by peers and colleagues. Collectively, the contributions highlight peers as central actors in employees’ developmental experiences, advancing theory on workplace development. Specifically, the symposium addresses three core questions: (1) how do peers support one another’s development; (2) how does peer-based development compare with development provided by leaders or pursued individually; and (3) what benefits does peer-based development offer for employees and organizations. Drawing on a range of methodological approaches, including surveys, field experiments, qualitative studies, and longitudinal program evaluations, the symposium offers a multifaceted examination of horizontal developmental relationships.
