Session overview

This PDW aims to explore new trends and research opportunities in entrepreneurial finance research and provides a broad and recurring platform for discussing entrepreneurial finance topics at the AOM Annual Meetings. Building on successful editions in 2023 (Boston), 2024 (Chicago), and 2025 (Copenhagen), the 2026 edition brings together renowned scholars from diverse research fields, topics, and methods. Through a panel debate and subsequent interactive roundtable workshops, the PDW will invigorate entrepreneurial finance research by addressing its most pressing challenges and exciting opportunities.
