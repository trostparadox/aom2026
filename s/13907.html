Session overview

AI agents are becoming increasingly important in OSCM, as they have the potential to take on important tasks that were previously performed by humans and thereby change the way people work. They offer particularly high potential for application in OSCM, as AI agents are especially suited to context-specific, data-intensive, and rapid decision-making in day-to-day business. Surprisingly, the role of AI agents as a ground-breaking digital technology has so far received only limited attention in OSCM research. This PDW aims to bring researchers together to jointly develop key challenges, opportunities, and further research fields. The workshop is designed as an interactive, participant-driven format and will consist of the following sections. Participants will collaboratively identify and discuss critical success factors associated with the launch and use of AI agents in OSCM, with particular attention to human-AI collaboration, governance, and responsibility. The second part focuses on the opportunities of a deeper integration of AI agents for operational decision-making processes, human-machine collaboration, performance dimensions, and cost structures. Building on these discussions, the final section introduces a conceptual framework for assessing the maturity of AI agents across different OSCM processes, which is further refined and extended through an interactive World Café format. The PDW provides a collaborative forum for researchers to advance emerging research. Participants are encouraged to bring in their own ideas, collectively develop promising research projects, and explore potential collaboration constellations. The workshop aims to sharpen a shared research agenda and to outline concrete next steps for future empirical and conceptual work.
