Session overview

This is the 12th year when the IM Division has been organizing the Paper Development Consortium (PDC) at the AOM Annual Meeting. As one of the three consortia organized by the IM Division, this PDC aims to provide junior scholars with developmental and constructive feedback from a panel of seasoned and current editors at top tier management and international management journals on crafting and publishing international management research. This PDC starts with a panel discussion on how researchers can successfully build theory in International Management. The panel will be followed by a roundtable session in which each participant will receive feedback on a manuscript from designated panel members and other participants.
