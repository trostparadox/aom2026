Build or Buy? CEO Regulatory Focus and International Entry Mode Choice

IM
Weicheng Chiu (Tongji University); Xuzhe Liu (Tongji University); Peien Chen (Tongji University); Weiyi Zhou (Sichuan University)
This study integrates Upper Echelons Theory and Regulatory Focus Theory to examine how CEO regulatory focus influences firms’ international establish mode choices, specifically the preference between greenfield investments and cross-border mergers and acquisitions (M&As). We argue that CEO regulatory focus systematically shapes firms’ strategic decision-making by affecting risk perception and opportunity evaluation in the context of international expansion. Using a sample of Chinese listed manufacturing firms from 2013 to 2023, we investigate the effects of CEO promotion and prevention focus on establish mode choices, as well as the moderating role of top management team (TMT) overseas experience in this relationship. Our findings indicate that promotion-focused CEOs are more inclined to choose greenfield investments, whereas prevention-focused CEOs prefer cross-border M&As. Moreover, TMT overseas experience attenuates the positive effect of promotion focus and mitigates the negative effect of prevention focus on greenfield entry. This study contributes to the growing literature on behavioral strategy in international business by elucidating the psychological mechanisms underlying internationalization decisions and highlighting the importance of TMT cognitive resources.
