Session overview

Corporate political activity (CPA)— the diverse strategies firms use to influence government, such as lobbying, political campaign contributions and revolving doors—is, by its very nature, interdisciplinary. While political science, economics, sociology, and other disciplines provided the field’s foundations, scholars have begun to expand these boundaries by intersecting CPA with fields like corporate social responsibility (CSR), ethics, organizational design, entrepreneurship, political psychology, and international business. This PDW highlights this nascent intersectional research, with the aim of drawing attention to new directions in the field and encouraging further cross-pollination of CPA. The workshop will feature presentations of exciting new work by emerging CPA scholars, alongside contributions from established scholars who will help pull insights together and situate these projects within the broader literature. In addition, the PDW will provide feedback to session participants on their own boundary-crossing CPA research projects. By highlighting nascent hybrid work in CPA, as well as identifying underexplored opportunities for hybridization, this PDW aims to inspire fruitful growth in the field. Ultimately, this workshop seeks to foster mentorship and develop a collaborative community dedicated to the future of CPA.
