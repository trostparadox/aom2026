Session overview

With rapid recent progress in the development of powerful technologies like artificial intelligence and robotics, some have begun to speculate that startups may soon have radically different organizational designs. However, amidst talk of one-person unicorns, AI cofounders, and non-technical founders developing full web stacks alone, decades of research on startup organization design can inform what the future holds for practitioners and academics. In this symposium, leading researchers of startups and organizational design discuss recent advancements in startup organizational design research and what they can tell us about the future.
