Gender Gaps and Cross-Border Acquisition Performance Abstract Gender Gaps and Cross-Border Acquisition

IM
Xiujuan Jin (Department of Management and Marketing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University); Hong Zhu (Peking University)
Prior research on cross-border acquisitions (CBAs) has largely overlooked gender, even though it plays a critical role in shaping employee interactions, cooperation, and CBA outcomes. This study examines two key dimensions of gender—gender equality and gender orientation (masculinity). Using data from 957 CBAs conducted by U.S.-listed firms between 2000 and 2021, the results support that asymmetric gender distance—capturing both magnitude and direction—significantly shapes acquisition performance.
