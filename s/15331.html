Session overview

As organizations face new challenges, they articulate renewed needs. Traditional consulting postures are being reexamined. This workshop invites participants to reflect how current contexts shape organizational needs and how intervention postures respond to them. Building on insights from previous PDWs (RISE in Europe 2025; AOM 2025), participants will collectively examine on current organizational needs and the relational dynamics generated by intervention practices. This PDW is part of a series, exploring interventions supporting organizational transformation and confronting theoretical assumptions with field-based experiences. Join us to engage in a reflective discussion on how organizations are supported in practice and what this implies for researchers and intervention practitioners.
