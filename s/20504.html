Session overview

Firms attempt to predict stakeholder reactions based on prior patterns and experiences. However, stakeholder complexity is increasingly unpredictable. Stakeholder groups are heterogenous in their perceptions, societal ideologies are dynamic, and society has greater expectations of firm engagement in social issues. This symposium examines unexpected stakeholder reactions across four contexts: government retaliation to corporate activism, CEO activism's cascading effects, ideological competition as strategic positioning, and cross-cultural variation in crisis management. These papers illuminate how cultural context, ideological frames, and identity-based cognition shape stakeholder expectations and reactions, creating challenges and opportunities for firms.
