Session overview

Envy is a prevalent but often overlooked emotion in workplace settings, triggered by upward social comparisons and associated with adverse outcomes, such as cognitive strain and diminished relational energy. This study explores how envy depletes relational energy via affective rumination, highlighting the moderating role of organizational identification (OI). While OI is traditionally viewed as a positive force, fostering alignment with organizational goals, this research reveals its paradoxical effect: high OI can exacerbate envy’s negative consequences by intensifying the significance of social comparisons. These findings offer novel insights into the complexity of OI’s role in workplace dynamics and its implications for managing envy.
