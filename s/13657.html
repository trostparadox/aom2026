Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is part of a multi-year research initiative (3–5 years) examining the professional foundations of fields dedicated to human-centered change. The intent of this work is to explore foundations for professionalizing the knowledge base of Organization Development and Change (ODC) at the intersection of research and practice. The initiative began in 2024 with a qualitative study exploring how scholars and practitioners understand, research, and enact human-centered change across contexts. Initial data were drawn from a series of public interviews conducted through the ODC Scholarly Foundations Webinar series, followed by a collaboration with Bowling Green State University’s ODC Doctoral Program. The project has expanded this year to include another round of interviews with senior thought leaders and a review of ODC literature. Building on this work, this PDW will provide an overview of project findings to date. Participants will be introduced to key findings and invited to engage critically with the identified themes, particularly as they relate to the field’s professional identity, knowledge base, and future direction. The workshop will serve as a forum to surface additional perspectives and explore implications for research, education, and practice in Organization Development and Change. The PDW is structured around five phases: (1) an overview of the research initiative, (2) presentation of research findings, (3) facilitated dialogue around these themes, (4) exploration of future directions for the field, and (5) collaborative identification of actionable next steps.
