Session overview

As diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives in higher education and organizations continue to evolve in response to legislative action, legal challenges, and political scrutiny, this Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to facilitate critical thinking on how scholars navigate the evolving DEI research stream. The increasing uncertainty and contested nature of DEI efforts have become a central concern for both management scholars and organizational leaders, raising urgent questions about the sustainability, scope, and impact of DEI research and practice. This PDW seeks to deepen understanding of contemporary DEI discourse by moving beyond critique toward more expansive, resilient, and evidence-based approaches to DEI scholarship. We advance this goal by facilitating a structured and interactive dialogue focused on four interrelated areas: (1) discuss and analyze current DEI research used to preserve and strengthen DEI work within the academy; (2)identify theoretical frameworks and methodological approaches that have demonstrated meaningful impact in the DEI research space and offer recommendations for future studies; (3) examine strategies for advancing DEI scholarship in ways that can withstand political, legal, and institutional pressures; and (4) articulate a forward-looking research agenda that supports the sustainable evolution of DEI research in management. Collectively, this PDW will equip participants with conceptual clarity, methodological guidance, and practical insight for advancing rigorous, resilient, and impactful DEI scholarship.
