Session overview

Support for policies to address inequality and unfair group-based disparities rises and falls over time. At some moments, there is broad support for these policies; at other moments, these same policies face resistance or backlash. Why does this happen? This symposium examines the conditional nature of support for workplace equality. Together, four presentations explore when, why, and how people endorse or resist inequality, drawing attention to the protection of ingroup advantage, the need for personal autonomy, cultural cognition, and beliefs about individual responsibility. We aim to advance understanding of equality support as context-sensitive, and to offer insights for leaders on how to design and communicate inequality-reducing initiatives in ways that foster more enduring change.
