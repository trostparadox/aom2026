Session overview

This presenter symposium examines interactions of shareholder activism and target firms through a system-level lens. The process of shareholder activism encompasses the target selection by activist shareholders, the consequences for targeted firms, and stakeholders’ responses to varying engagement approaches. Amid increasing societal polarization, institutional influx, political volatility, and shifts in governance practices, this process has become markedly dynamic and complex. Drawing on the system-level perspective on shareholder activism, this symposium engages with three frontier areas: how regulatory change affects shareholder activists’ opportunity structures and campaign success, how they make divestment decisions under political uncertainty, and how firms adapt their practices in reaction to shareholder activism. This symposium also fosters rich discussions on future research opportunities grounded in the system-level view of shareholder activism.
