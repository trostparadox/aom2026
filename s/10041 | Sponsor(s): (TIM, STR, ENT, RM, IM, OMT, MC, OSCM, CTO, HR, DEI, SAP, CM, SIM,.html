Session overview

The Space Economy (SE) has entered a new phase of growth and strategic significance, with space-based infrastructures and services increasingly shaping markets, organizations, and societal outcomes. Yet management research on the SE remains dispersed across innovation, entrepreneurship, governance, and organization studies, limiting cumulative theory building. Building on the 2025 Space Economy PDW—the largest PDW in Academy of Management history —this year’s workshop shifts from introducing the SE to consolidating a coherent research agenda. It is designed to help scholars identify high-potential research questions, clarify boundary conditions for existing theories, and develop rigorous empirical strategies for studying this frontier context. The PDW focuses on four core domains: (1) theoretical developments and consolidation opportunities in SE research, (2) innovative methodologies and datasets for studying SE organizations and ecosystems, (3) emerging phenomena (e.g., new business models, platform ecosystems, institutional change), and (4) policy and practical challenges shaping responsible and sustainable SE development. The session features a distinguished industry keynote, followed by a practitioner panel with leaders from pioneering space companies (e.g., Axiom Space, Starlab, Voyager Technologies, VAST Space) and then an academic panel that translates practitioner insights into theory-driven research directions. Interactive roundtable discussions, facilitated by senior scholars across AOM divisions, form the core of the workshop, enabling participants to refine research questions, compare theoretical lenses, and co-develop research designs and data approaches. The PDW aims to catalyze collaboration across divisions and position management scholars to advance an integrated, forward-looking research agenda on the Space Economy. The session is open to all; however, pre-registration is required to secure a spot before capacity is reached and to participate in the interactive roundtable discussions.
