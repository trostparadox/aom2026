Aligning Choice and Need: How Firms Support and Integrate Self-Directed Executive Learning

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 2
HR

People

Author: Salma Qarnain IE Business School

Session overview

Employees are increasingly choosing external, self-directed learning paths for reskilling and upskilling, from which organizations struggle to reap the full benefit. Using two executive learner samples in different online learning contexts—a one-year multi-wave study of a certification program and a two-year cross-sectional study of MOOCs, a comparative study was performed to determine if the relationships between organizational support mechanisms, through novel index Level of Transfer Support, and knowledge sharing were consistent across contexts. Preliminary results suggest that executives act as independent operators who transfer their knowledge to the organization if there is opportunity to use the knowledge and will share the knowledge only if they have integrated their own knowledge first.
