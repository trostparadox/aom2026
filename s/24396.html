Next Step In Sustainable Careers Research: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Research Agenda

CAR
Shaofang Zong (University of Vaasa); Vesa Suutari (University of Vaasa); Beatrice Isabella Johanna Maria Van Der Heijden (Radboud University Nijmegen)
Over the past decade, research on sustainable careers has attracted significant interest from scholars and practitioners alike, leading to a notable rise in scholarly work in this field. However, up until now, this body of research remains quite fragmented. To address this issue and to devise well-grounded recommendations for future work, this study presents a system-atic review of the existing empirical work on sustainable careers. Specifically, this review fo-cuses on three questions: 1) What indicators have been used in empirical studies to measure sustainable careers? 2) Which key antecedents of sustainable careers have been identified? and 3) What theoretical frameworks have been adopted to study sustainable careers? Addi-tionally, we aim to uncover the foundational elements that both indicate and contribute to career sustainability. We also intend to extend the sustainable career framework by proposing how to integrate the outcomes of our review. Finally, we devise a general future research agenda and introduce two additional focal areas that emphasize the temporal character and international context of sustainable careers.
