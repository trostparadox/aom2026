Session overview

In this symposium, we bring together 5 new research studies by top scholars examining women's leadership development from an identity and social dynamics approach. The importance of offering tailored (not one-size-fits-all) development programs is stressed by research showing the importance of demographic, trait, and context in leadership attraction. And from a social dynamics standpoint, this symposium also includes a focus on observers (e.g., followers) and their characteristics in understanding the perception and recognition of women's leadership.
