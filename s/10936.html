Session overview

The objective of this PDW is to explore the value added of Management Consulting intervention through the lens of the stakeholder and qualimetrics approaches. Diagnoses and Methods will be presented by the panelists and participants. We'll propose ways of improving processes and tools to enhance the quality of performance measurement processes that involve many stakeholders, specially tackling with the challenge of AI.
