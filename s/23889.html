Laughing With You or Laughing at You? A Systematic Literature Review of Leader Aggressive Humour

OB
Akanksha mishra (Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee); Rajib Lochan Dhar (Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee)
leader aggressive humour, a negative type of humor that veers on the edge of offensiveness, has drawn scholarly attention because of its contradictory nature. The present review presents a thorough picture of this understudied issue by sourcing articles from Scopus, Web of Science, EBSCOhost, Sage journals, and ProQuest. It enhances our current understanding of leader aggressive humour by portraying detailed picture of its dysfunctional as well as positive outcomes and further assembles the buffering mechanisms to give a wholistic picture. Consolidating the findings in S-O-R framework, this review elucidate how leader aggressive humour transcends to its consequences in organisational settings. The findings are further woven together in a consolidated framework integrating future research directions.
