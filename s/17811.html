Navigating Sustainability Trade-offs in Purchasing: The Roles of Paradox Mindset and AI Usage

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 9:00AM–10:00AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — 108B
OSCM

People

Authors: Owais Khan Aalto University; Andreas Hinterhuber

Session overview

Sustainable purchasing decisions often involve persistent tensions between economic efficiency and environmental responsibility. While prior research acknowledges these tensions, less is known about how individuals cognitively and behaviorally respond to them. Drawing on paradox theory, this study examines how experiencing tensions influences sustainable purchasing behavior through paradox mindset and artificial intelligence (AI) usage. We propose a moderated mediation model in which paradox mindset and AI usage function as parallel mediators between experiencing tensions and sustainable purchasing behavior, while paradox mindset and AI usage also condition the strength of this relationship. Survey data were collected from purchasing professionals and analyzed using partial least squares structural equation modeling. The results indicate that the direct relationship between experiencing tensions and sustainable purchasing behavior is fully mediated by paradox mindset and AI usage. Individuals who perceive tensions more strongly are more likely to adopt a paradox mindset and to use AI-based tools, which in turn promote sustainable purchasing decisions. In addition, paradox mindset positively moderates the relationship between experiencing tensions and sustainable purchasing behavior, suggesting that cognitive acceptance of contradictions strengthens the translation of tensions into sustainable action. This study contributes to paradox theory by demonstrating how tensions can be transformed into sustainability-oriented behavior through both cognitive and technological mechanisms. It also advances research on digitalization and sustainability by clarifying the complementary—but distinct—roles of paradox mindset and AI usage. Practically, the findings suggest that organizations should cultivate paradoxical thinking capabilities alongside responsible AI adoption to support sustainable purchasing decisions.
