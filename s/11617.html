Session overview

Despite the growth in virtual and hybrid work, studies consistently show that most organizations fail to adequately train, develop, and socialize managers, leaders, and employees in this new way of working. This PDW aims to help Academy of Management members meet this critical need by sharing evidence-based, interactive, and experiential activities to teach others how to lead and function effectively in virtual and hybrid settings, such as geographically dispersed virtual teams and remote and hybrid work. The PDW includes activities focused on different aspects of virtual and hybrid work, targeted at academic and practitioner audiences at different levels. Audience members will benefit from insights and lessons learned by the presenters, who have extensive experience using active learning approaches to teach this topic in their classes and workshops. In the first part of the PDW, the audience will participate in a full demonstration of a teaching activity. In the second part, the presenters will describe how to conduct additional activities, including their teaching materials and other tips. The workshop participants will leave with everything they need to teach the activities in their classes and workshops. They will also have the opportunity to ask questions, discuss different ways to apply these activities in training interventions, and share insights about other interactive and experiential activities for training and developing the virtual and hybrid workforce.
