Session overview

The STR Division will sponsor the Dissertation Consortium during the 2026 Annual Meeting of the Academy of Management. The consortium is intended for PhD candidates who will be on the job market during the 2026-2027 academic year (i.e., for jobs beginning in Fall 2027). The main focus of the 2026 Dissertation Consortium will be on providing participating students with an opportunity to discuss their job market papers and research agenda in small groups with distinguished faculty and peers.
