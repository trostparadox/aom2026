Session overview

The CTO Paper Development Workshop was created to give authors of papers that did not make it into the AOM scholarly program an opportunity to get detailed feedback on their work. Space permitting, we also invite authors of working papers close to submission. Because this is a small workshop with focused feedback from mentors and other participants, space is limited to invited participants.
