Session overview

Since the proliferation of social media, student feedback has become increasingly ubiquitous and accessible. Whether harsh or fair, these critiques are being absorbed by faculty. The proposed professional development workshop (PDW) will explore the emotional and practical dilemmas educators face when confronted with tough student feedback. Drawing on research in organizational behavior and applied psychology, participants will create a supportive, judgment-free space for professors to address distressing feedback by openly sharing their experiences, responding to negativity with humor, and developing strategies to use feedback productively. The proposed PDW invites prominent scholars to share their harshest critiques, divulge common themes, and explain how they turned those critiques into practical strategies for improvement. Following the discussant's comments, there will be a series of interactive activities in which participants rank their negative feedback, roundtable discussions in which participants and discussants identify ways to address useful feedback, and hands-on group exercises to employ productive coping skills. The proposed PDW aims to shed light on the difficulties faculty face when receiving difficult feedback and on how to embrace it as a tool for development. The end goal of the PDW is for educators not only to feel empowered to face negative reviews but also to be part of a community dedicated to growth, resilience, and the joy of teaching.
