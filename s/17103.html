Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) creates a dynamic learning environment for scholars interested in enhancing their presentation capabilities. Designed to advance the art and science of academic presentations, this interactive session brings together experts who will share innovative approaches to crafting and delivering impactful research communications and presentations. The program begins with an engaging panel discussion where speakers will explore key presentation strategies and practices drawn from diverse professional domains. Following this, participants will take part in hands-on activities that allow them to apply and practice these techniques directly. The session addresses important aspects of presentation development and delivery, including selection and framing of content, preparation strategies, audience engagement across disciplines, and effective delivery, for both qualitative and quantitative research.
