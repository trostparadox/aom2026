Self-Regulated and Workplace Learning as Catalysts for IWB: The Role of Organizational Context

Authors: Jennifer Liehr Helmut Schmidt University - University of the Federal Armed Forces of Hamburg; Karen Schwien Helmut Schmidt University - University of the Federal Armed Forces of Hamburg

Session overview

Organizations face rapid digital transformation, requiring employees’ innovative work behavior (IWB). This study explores how self-regulated learning and workplace learning activities influence IWB and how contextual factors moderate these relationships. Findings aim to guide organizations in fostering learning-driven innovation to master transformational change.
