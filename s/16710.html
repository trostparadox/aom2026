Session overview

This symposium integrates four complementary papers that examine AI at work through the lenses of guidance, communication, relationship building, and evaluation. Morozova introduces the concept of perceived prescriptiveness to explain when algorithmic guidance is experienced as assistance versus control, showing how individuals selectively comply with or resist AI depending on task human uniqueness. Retkowsky examines AI-assisted email communication, revealing how generative AI disrupts signals of effort, authenticity, and care, prompting new forms of image and evaluation work. Lief, Kwon, and Inoue theorize and test how AI can support workplace relationship building by eliciting candid personal information, leveraging AI’s distinctive mind profile to reduce social risk while preserving human judgment. Finally, Miller uses a field experiment with avatars in live investment conversations to show how AI-mediated representation reshapes trust and evaluation, attenuating or reversing gender disparities by altering who—and what—is seen during interaction.
