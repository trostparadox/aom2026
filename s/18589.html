Session overview

Generative AI foundation models are increasingly seen as commoditizing, creating pressure on model sponsors to develop new ways to capture value. Building ecosystems of complementary innovators is one approach that has been gaining traction. This symposium examines how different foundation model approaches to openness affects ecosystem creation and value capture. Openness can expand downstream generativity and specialization, but it can also weaken appropriability and, unlike app-store platforms, often lacks scalable mechanisms that link complementor success to sponsor returns. Panelists will discuss how these conditions reshape ecosystem governance, incentives, and industry architecture, and when they may push orchestrators toward tighter control, integration, and acquisition.
