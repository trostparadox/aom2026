Session overview

Difficult conversations, whether involving performance, feedback, ethics, identity, politics, or power, are fundamental to leadership and collaboration across all organizational contexts. While scholars analyze these dynamics, academics and practitioners frequently struggle to navigate such conversations effectively in real time. This PDW brings researchers and practitioners together to co-explore what makes conversations difficult and to practice evidence-informed approaches for engaging them with clarity, empathy, and skill. Designed as an experiential learning environment, this PDW emphasizes “learning by doing,” collective sensemaking, and research–practice integration. Participants will leave with a clearer understanding of why conversations derail, concrete tools for navigating them more effectively, and a broader community invested in advancing scholarship and practice on difficult conversations.
