Session overview

In this PDW, we will focus on the challenges experienced by educators from diverse backgrounds when teaching MBA students. The goal of the PDW is to facilitate an exchange of experiences and provide an opportunity to learn from senior and junior faculty members about their strategies for managing MBA students, especially when they face challenging situations that are directly linked to their students’ diverse identities and personal characteristics (such as gender, ethnicity, language, career stage), as well as their own identities. This PDW involves shared learning between workshop participants a) educators who have experience teaching MBA students in a cross-cultural and international setting, and b) those who are expected to teach in an MBA program and who would like to improve the way they manage the students. In this PDW participants are encouraged to discuss practical challenges and strategies based on an outline prepared by the organizers, developing and adding onto that by means of additional ideas by participants (if needed, by using a structured participation method such as World Café, see Brown & Isaacs, 2005). The discussion groups will provide peer-to-peer mentoring between experienced and junior faculty members, focused on identifying and developing innovative and creative teaching strategies. At the end of the discussion session, each group will be asked to report a summary of their discussion and share their takeaways with the other groups so as to promote a greater collective understanding of post-experience student teaching. We hope to encourage participants to maintain ongoing connections.
