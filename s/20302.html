Session overview

The rise of generative AI is transforming how work is organized and what is expected of business graduates. While AI can automate many analytical and routine tasks, it cannot replace the human capacities that enable people to collaborate, lead, and navigate complex stakeholder relationships. Among these, empathy is critical. Empathic graduates are better equipped to lead diverse teams, manage conflict, support wellbeing, drive innovation with users and customers in mind, and act responsibly in situations involving ethical tension and social impact. As organizations increasingly operate in cross cultural, hybrid, and high uncertainty environments, empathy becomes central to effective communication, inclusion, and trust building: competencies that AI cannot mimic through data processing alone. The workshop offers practical strategies for intentionally cultivating empathy within business school courses. Participants will engage in hands on empathy building activities and small group discussions, and explore how to design, facilitate, and debrief learning experiences that strengthen students’ capacity for perspective taking, active listening, and compassionate response. A toolkit of proven approaches will be provided, empowering educators to create more human centered and future oriented learning environments that prepare students for the relational demands of the modern workplace.
