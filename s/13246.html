Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) equips scholars with innovative frameworks and measurement tools to elevate their influence beyond traditional metrics. Participants will explore the Contextualized Scholarly Impact Index (CSII). This multidimensional tool integrates contextual relevance, temporal dynamics, and comprehensive data to assess academic impact more accurately than citation counts or the h-index. Through hands-on demonstrations, attendees will calculate their own CSII scores using freely accessible software. The workshop also introduces the Scholarly Impact Performance (SIP) framework, which expands impact across personal, educational, societal, and global dimensions via the CORE performance model (Capacity, Opportunity, Relevant Exchanges). Emphasizing stakeholder engagement, SIP shifts focus from static evaluations to actionable strategies for sustained influence inside and outside of the Academy. In interactive segments, participants will create their own Personal Impact Development Plans (PIDPs) to align skills, activities, and goals with targeted stakeholders and outcomes, fostering communication, collaboration, and adaptability. This PDW provides essential tools to bridge the science-practice gap, inform policy, and drive meaningful change in management education and beyond. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
