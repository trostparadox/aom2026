Listening to Organizing

SAP
Sunyu Chai (University of Southampton Business School)
What if an organization were not something to see, but a soundscape we have been listening to all along? In this essay, I foreground sound as a crucial yet underexamined dimension in the field of organization studies. It offers two contributions: it develops an auditory ontology of organization that brings otherwise unheard industrial dynamics to the fore; and it argues for a reflexive shift in scholarly practice toward listening with organizing, rather than merely defining what organization is.
