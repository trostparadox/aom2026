Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) seeks to connect with and support emerging international members from emerging economies, the Global South, and other underrepresented regions within AOM and DEI. Our goal is to strengthen global inclusion by creating pathways for scholars who may experience outsider status at AOM. DEI’s Global Ambassadors will collaborate with AOM partner organizations to identify and invite management scholars worldwide. Participants may request mentorship and will be matched with experienced members who can provide guidance on navigating AOM, building scholarly networks, and developing high-quality conference submissions. The session will feature three panelists with strong ties to underrepresented and emerging economies. Panelists will share practical strategies for overcoming structural and institutional barriers, positioning research for global relevance, and successfully engaging with AOM divisions and communities. Following the panel, Global Ambassadors will facilitate roundtable discussions focused on paper development, conference participation strategies, and cultivating a sense of belonging within AOM. Aligned with AOM’s mission, this PDW aims to enhance membership diversity, advance globally relevant management scholarship, and strengthen AOM’s standing as a truly global association. Participants will leave with stronger connections, clearer development pathways, and actionable tools to support future AOM engagement and submission success.
