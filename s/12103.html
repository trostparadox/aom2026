Session overview

Decades of research have explored a plethora of negotiation and communication tactics that improve personal and dyadic outcomes and the boundary conditions that limit or enhance their effectiveness. In this symposium, we contribute to this body of work by introducing beneficial communication and negotiation tactics and identifying hitherto unexplored contextual variables that shape the tactics and behaviors individuals use in negotiations.
