Session overview

As organizations integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into human resource management, they shift from using technology as a tool to collaborating with AI as a decision-making partner. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) examines how responsible AI can be designed, governed, and deployed through Human Resources to enhance workforce resilience, equity, and strategic capability. Drawing on research in strategic human capital management, organizational behavior, and responsible AI, the PDW emphasizes human-in-the-loop governance, skills-based workforce planning, and inclusive organizational policies and practices. Participants will engage in topical roundtables to translate theoretical frameworks into two actionable outputs: a Governance Checklist for AI-human collaboration and a Skills-Gap Assessment for AI-augmented teams. This workshop bridges the gap between AI's technological potential and its ethical and strategic implementation, advancing both organizational and societal good.
