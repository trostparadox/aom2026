Session overview

Scientific collaborations can enrich scholars' experiences since they pave the way for notable research breakthroughs and advance career growth. However, taking part in research collaborations is not straightforward for everyone. Early-career scholars may find reaching experienced mentors outside their social network difficult. Geographically dispersed researchers—especially those in developing countries from the Global South—may be resource-constrained in seeking viable research partners. Even teams endowed with adequate resources may face challenges in terms of time, project, and relationship management. In this professional development workshop, we bring together a diverse set of scholars to discuss the challenges faced during research collaborations. Distinguished panelists will share best practices that can lead to fruitful collaborations for producing impactful research. Doctoral students and early-career scholars would have the opportunity to discuss and interact with experienced mentors on topics including (i) finding co-authors and collaborators, (ii) working with senior scholars, (iii) collaborations between academics and practitioners, (iv) collaboration of scholars from the Global North and South, and (v) topical issues such as the role of artificial intelligence in coauthoring in roundtable settings.
