Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop aims to discuss the contributions of the special issue of Latin American Management History published in January 2025 in The Journal of Management History (https://www.emerald.com/insight/publication/issn/1751-1348/vol/31/iss/1). This PDW presents the main contributions of the special issue, including the research tendencies on ancient civilizations, managerial practices in public and private organizations, managerial professionalization, and data analytics for uncovering temporal trends. This space opens the discussion about how to contribute to the field of management history in emerging economies.
