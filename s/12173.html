Session overview

This panel symposium examines technology emergence as a system-level, field-spanning phenomenon that unfolds through the interactions of heterogeneous actors, institutions, and meanings. Moving beyond industry-centric and firm-focused accounts, the presentations highlight how field-level dynamics such as feedback loops and coordination failures shape nonlinear trajectories of technological acceleration, stagnation, and resurgence. Methodologically, the panel advances innovative approaches—abductive reasoning, mixed methods, and the analysis of naturally occurring data—to capture macro-level dynamics that elude traditional qualitative designs. The panel also foregrounds the moral, emotional, and identity implications of technological change, showing how emerging technologies disrupt the meanings and boundaries of field-level phenomena such as identity and legitimacy. Together, the contributions offer an integrative perspective on how technologies co-evolve within broader sociotechnical fields, reshaping fields, practices, and social systems.
