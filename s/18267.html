Panelists: Jeffrey Sonnenfeld; Jeffrey Pfeffer Stanford University; Erika H James Wharton School; Douglas Brinkley Rice University; Rick Stengel; Michael Beschloss

Session overview

This panel will evaluate scholarly and applied analyses of the national leadership of the United States at this historic milestone of 250 years since the dream was proclaimed. We will examine how the character, style, and values of our leaders have unfolded to match and shape the changing societal context domestically and globally. Each of these panelists has made renowned contributions to the study of leaders' historic, strategic, cultural, and societal legacies over the centuries. The goal of the session will be to hold up a yardstick of whether the dream for the republic has been realized, and what must be done to correct for where the nation has departed from its ideals and aspirations. The discussion will consider leadership across sectors, from government, business, and the independent sector, as well as from different parts of the nation and varied segments of society.
