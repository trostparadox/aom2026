Beyond Loyalty: Reconsidering the Intention of Unethical Pro Organizational Behavior

Session overview

Unethical pro-organizational behavior (UPB) refers to intentional actions that violate societal or legal norms to ostensibly benefit the organization. This conceptual work re-examines UPB’s motivational core by questioning the traditional assumption that prosocial, reciprocity-based motives dominate. Synthesizing recent meta-analytic and primary evidence, it highlights the stronger role of self-interest, psychological entitlement, and status striving, and foregrounds moral disengagement as the central mechanism translating mixed motives into action. The paper advances a dual-motivation framework in which prosocial and self-interested intentions co-occur, are contextually weighted, and jointly shape UPB.
