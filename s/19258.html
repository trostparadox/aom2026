A Marketing Capability Sequence Driving B2B Export Venture Performance

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 3
IM

People

Authors: Mateja Bodlaj School of Economics and Business, University of Ljubljana; Maja Arslanagic-kalajdzic University of Sarajevo; Barbara Cater School of Economics and Business, University of Ljubljana

Session overview

This study examines how marketing capabilities shape export venture performance in B2B markets. Drawing on the resource-based view and the dynamic capabilities perspective, we theorize a capability sequence linking informational capabilities to product development capabilities and, subsequently, to customer relationship capabilities. We test the model on survey data from 260 B2B exporters using covariance-based structural equation modeling and conditional process analysis. Results show that informational capabilities are positively related to product development capabilities, which in turn are positively related to customer relationship capabilities. Informational capabilities also have a direct effect on customer relationship capabilities and an indirect (serially mediated) effect on export venture performance. In addition, export venture intensity strengthens the relationship between customer relationship capabilities and export venture performance. The findings contribute to research on exporter capability building and offer implications for managers seeking to improve performance under conditions of cross-border market complexity.
