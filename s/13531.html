Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) explores the concept of temporal patterns as recurring sequences of events, activities, and behaviors in organizations that shape how time is organized and experienced. The PDW aims to bring together diverse perspectives to enhance understanding and future research directions. We will particularly explore the antecedents and consequences of temporal patterns. Through a flexible “present, discuss, and feedback” format, the PDW seeks to create an open space for scholars to engage with this concept, share methodologies, and explore new research opportunities, ultimately building a community around this topic. The PDW will examine different ways in which temporal patterns have been conceptualized and explore how they are underpinned by distinct assumptions about how they emerge, develop, and change, as well as their antecedents and consequences. By convening leading scholars, the workshop will highlight recent advancements in theory, discuss applications to organizational phenomena, and foster collaborations for future research.
