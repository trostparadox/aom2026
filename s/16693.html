Reconciling Clinical Ethos with Managerial Efficiency Through a Competency-Based Model

Session overview

Physicians constitute a distinct and powerful profession with a long-standing tradition and a unique social identity. Currently, this group is increasingly assuming managerial roles, evolving into hybrid physician managers (HPMs). This hybridization integrates specialized clinical practice with leadership responsibilities. The objective of this study is to present the conceptual framework for a systematic literature review focused on identifying and categorizing the specific competencies required by physicians in these roles. The analysis highlights critical barriers to HPMs’ performance, such as the tension between the medical ethos and organizational goals, as well as educational gaps in medical training. While physician leadership is vital for hospital performance, there is a clear research gap in systematizing the competencies of physicians as managers. Fulfilling this gap becomes the aim of the conceptualized systematic review. The objective of this poster presentation is to discuss the validity and conceptual framework of a planned systematic literature review dedicated to these competences of HPMs.
