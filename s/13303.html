Session overview

Individuals’ working lives are growing longer, less linear and more unpredictable, increasing the need for research that examines how career choices, mobility, and sustainability unfold at distinct stages of the life span. Acknowledging this need, this symposium brings together four papers that advance understanding of career development at selected points in the career lifespan, spanning early career through later working life. Specifically, the papers examine: (a) how early-career professionals experience and respond to challenges to career sustainability; (b) how accumulated career success shapes occupational mobility beyond early career; (c) how occupational aspirations can emerge mid-career through unplanned and informal role experiences; and (d) how health, caring responsibilities, and career guidance influence career change and sustainability later in working life. Taken together, the symposium papers illuminate stage-specific conditions and constraints that shape career processes over time, addressing pressing questions about how careers unfold and offering implications for scholars and practitioners concerned with supporting sustainable working lives.
