Session overview

Making technology and innovation more inclusive is essential in the face of the global challenges in relation to sustainable development. Inclusive and responsible innovation paradigms both respond to overarching societal challenges, involve multiple stakeholders in innovation processes, and are considered a promising way for organizations to contribute to the betterment of society. As an outcome of the substantial discourse in several scientific communities, a broad diversity of perspectives on, and tools for, inclusive and responsible innovation exist, without a clear overarching framework or toolbox. This PDW systematizes a selection of approaches and tools that are relevant for research, teaching, and practice by 1. presenting the principles and benefits of inclusive and responsible innovation, 2. providing an overview of approaches, and by 3. offering hands-on experiences in their usage. In particular, this PDW covers open innovation, co-creation, design thinking, frugal innovation as well as specific tools for inclusive and responsible innovation in an organizational context and beyond. First, six experienced presenters will share their practical experiences with specific approaches and tools related to inclusive and responsible innovation. The second half of the PDW is dedicated to roundtable discussions with the presenters and will allow PDW participants to discuss and bring in their own experiences with selected tools themselves.
