Self-Disclosure in Cyberspace: Impacts on Mental Health and Well-Being

Authors: Han Fang; Weize Huang Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University; Xiaojun Zhang

Session overview

The rise in social media usage, particularly during and after COVID-19, has drawn increased scholarly attention to self-disclosure in cyberspace (i.e., online self-disclosure). So far, online self-disclosure is found to either positively or negatively affect disclosers’ mental health and well-being; however, findings are relatively scattered. This study aims to develop an integrative framework that synthesizes existing evidence regarding the impact of online self-disclosure on mental health and well-being, drawing from a narrative review of relevant articles. Practically, it supports the design of targeted interventions that leverage online self-disclosure to promote the mental health of both organizational members and external stakeholders in digitally-mediated contexts. Keywords: Online self-disclosure; Cyberspace; Double-edged sword; Mental health; Well-being
