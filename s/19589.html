The Hidden Costs of GenAI in Idea Generation: Effects on Psychological Ownership & Idea Championing

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 13
CTO

People

Author: Nils Schweikard University of Mannheim

Session overview

Although using generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) in idea generation has been shown to improve performance, having better ideas alone does not ensure innovation. This study investigates how using GenAI in idea generation influences subsequent stages of the idea journey. Specifically, we examine the consequences for the generator’s attitude (psychological ownership) and behavior (idea championing) towards the idea. In a laboratory experiment, 58 participants were randomly assigned to complete a creative product design task either with or without the assistance of ChatGPT. Subsequently, they were tasked with championing their respective ideas. Our findings reveal that the utilization of GenAI in the ideation process significantly diminishes both the generator’s psychological ownership and their subsequent championing behavior.
