Session overview

This PDW offers an introductory, practice-oriented overview of Adizes Symbergetic Organizational Therapy, an evidence-informed methodology for organizational transformation developed over more than 50 years of global application. The approach reframes organizational “therapy” as a systematic process for reintegrating differentiated leadership roles and stakeholder interests, anchored in a culture of mutual trust and respect. The session is designed to give participants a clear “taste” of how the methodology conceptualizes organizational problems and structures long-term transformation work, without attempting to train or certify them in its full application. Participants will: (1) gain a high-level understanding of the core concepts and language of Symbergetic organizations; (2) be introduced to the eleven-step Adizes organizational transformation program, with illustrative examples of how certified Adizes Associates implement these interventions; and (3) explore implications for research, teaching, and practice in leading complex change. The workshop will also feature a live demonstration of the newly developed “Dr. Adizes AI Twin,” a digital companion built on Dr. Adizes’ extensive knowledge base. Participants will receive special, time-limited access as a learning and exploration aid only, enabling them to directly query and explore the knowledge base, examine representative tools and concepts, and assess the feasibility of applying Adizes methods and diagnostics within their own organizational or client contexts.
