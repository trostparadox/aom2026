Session overview

This PDW examines emerging theoretical and empirical agendas at the intersection of artificial (AI) intelligence and the Carnegie School of organizational research. Distinguished presenters will engage three core questions: (1) how AI reshapes foundational assumptions and mechanisms of the Carnegie tradition; (2) how AI helps resolve longstanding theoretical challenges within this framework; and (3) how advances in AI enable the extension of well-established Carnegie ideas through novel data, methods, and analytical approaches. Small-group breakout discussions, led by organizers and facilitators, will explore AI as both a conceptual lens and a methodological tool, and critically assess its potential to advance research on strategy and organizations. A concluding plenary session will synthesize insights across breakout groups, surface points of convergence and tension, and situate AI-enabled research within a broader Carnegie agenda.
