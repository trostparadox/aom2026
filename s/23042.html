“We Need to Get Our Own House in Order”: Putting Sexual Harassment on The Agenda in UK Trade Unions (WITHDRAWN)

CMS
Renu Gupta (Heriot-Watt University); Cecile Guillaume (University of Surrey); Gill Kirton (Queen Mary University of London)
This article examines how sexual harassment has become an internal priority in UK trade unions. Drawing on in-depth interviews, we analyze the operations that have shaped what appears to be a successful, though still incomplete and contested, agenda-setting effort. Our contribution to gender and industrial relations research is twofold. First, we identify the conditions that make it possible for gender-based violence to be recognized and acted upon as a legitimate trade union concern. Second, we demonstrate the heuristic value of social problems theory for analyzing how women’s issues can gain visibility and organizational traction within unions.
