Session overview

Rising political polarization has increased the salience of CEOs’ political orientations for firm strategy and organizational outcomes. This PDW brings together four scholars and a discussant from diverse theoretical backgrounds to synthesize recent advances and identify promising directions for future research on CEO political orientation in an increasingly polarized business environment. The workshop emphasizes (1) novel organizational and strategic outcome variables influenced by CEOs’ political orientations, (2) key boundary conditions, and (3) connections to related constructs, including ideological intensity and CEO psychology.
