Session overview

We resubmit a new version of the ‘humor in the classroom’ PDW that we have offered at AOM for several years (a previous Best PDW award- winner), as humor in the classroom remains one of the most effective yet underappreciated and poorly understood approaches to learning. Management educators often regard their role and topic as too serious to engage with humor and are afraid of negative outcomes when using humor inappropriately. However, research shows that utilizing humor in the classroom can help to motivate students and build a positive relationship between them and educators. When integrated into course design, humor increases students’ understanding of the topic and improves their learning outcomes. This PDW addresses this gap by demonstrating strategies and tools from a variety of presenters with distinct teaching styles, that allow participants to utilize humor in their teaching strategies. It gives an introduction into basic functions of humor, illustrates how it can be successfully applied in the classroom and online education, and will present a novel ‘Humor Card-Deck’ that was developed based on previous PDWs and already successfully applied in a dozen different courses with more than 700 students at bachelor and master level. These strategies can be replicated in different teaching contexts, do not require extensive training and support AOM members in utilizing humor in their teaching strategies, courses, and exercises.
