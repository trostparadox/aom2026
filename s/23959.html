Governing for Resilience Through ICT-Enabled Interactions in Supply Chains

STR
Tingyu Lu (Northwestern Polytechnical University); Xubing Zhang (The Hong Kong Polytechnic University); Guijun Zhuang (Xi'an Jiaotong University); Yu Chang (Northwestern Polytechnical University)
Interorganizational governance (IOG) is widely used to maintain and enhance supply chain resilience. However, working with exchange partners entails significant challenges because of each firm’s diverse technical knowledge. Drawing on task–technology fit theory and the knowledge-based view, we examine the associations between IOG and resilience. Furthermore, we investigate the moderating effects of interactions enabled by information and communication technology (ICT). We adopt a multi-method approach to test the research model using field survey data on 520 industrial manufacturing firms and a secondary dataset of e-commerce development in China. The results confirm our hypotheses that interactions enabled by interorganizational information systems and societal ICT improve the effectiveness of contractual governance. Moreover, social media-enabled interactions enhance the effectiveness of relational governance. This research adds a vital perspective of ICT and IOG and offers practical suggestions to deploy ICT in supply chain management.
