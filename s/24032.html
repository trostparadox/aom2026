The Impact of Artificial Intelligence Policy on Corporate Greenwashing: Evidence from China

ONE
Chengxin Zhang
Against the backdrop of intensifying global climate change and the strengthening of sustainable development goals, environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations have become an important benchmark for evaluating firms’ long-term value. However, corporate greenwashing behavior has become increasingly prevalent, undermining the credibility of ESG information and weakening the effectiveness of environmental governance. Taking China’s National Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Development Pilot Zone policy as a quasi-natural experiment, this study employs panel data of A-share listed firms from 2009 to 2024 and adopts a multi-period difference-in-differences (DID) approach to systematically examine the impact of artificial intelligence development on corporate greenwashing behavior and its underlying mechanisms.The empirical results indicate that the establishment of AI Innovation and Development Pilot Zones significantly reduces the degree of corporate greenwashing. Mechanism analyses reveal that artificial intelligence development primarily curbs greenwashing by enhancing corporate information transparency and alleviating financing constraints. Further heterogeneity analyses show that the governance effect of the policy is more pronounced among firms operating in less competitive markets and those receiving higher levels of attention from institutional investors.This study not only elucidates the mechanisms through which the AI Innovation and Development Pilot Zone policy influences corporate greenwashing but also provides important policy implications for optimizing AI policy design, improving ESG information quality, and promoting firms’ green transformation.
