A Gender Perspective on TPB in Green Consumption: A Multigroup Analysis From a Developing Country

Authors: Šejma Aydin International University of Sarajevo; Hamza Smajic International University of Sarajevo; Hanan Babic International University of Sarajevo

Session overview

Drawing on the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) and Social Role Theory, this study aims to investigate the differences between men and women in the relationships between TPB antecedents (attitude, subjective norm, and perceived behavioral control) and green purchase intention. It is based on quantitative cross-sectional data gathered from 1,169 consumers from Bosnia and Herzegovina through a questionnaire. Hypotheses were tested using structural equation modeling and multigroup analysis. Results suggest that the positive and significant relationships between TPB antecedents and green purchase intention are not different for men and women. The study offers important theoretical implications from an under-researched context and practical suggestions for companies and their green marketing strategies.
