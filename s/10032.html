Session overview

Organizations are currently facing profound changes. Recent megatrends such as digital transformation, the COVID-19 pandemic, and evolving worker expectations have led to an accelerated shift toward emerging work practices. Today, organizations are moving away from on-site, standard work and are instead increasingly relying on virtual and hybrid forms of work. Yet, despite its widespread adoption, many organizations continue to struggle with making virtual work effective. Thus, there is a growing need for Human Resource Management (HRM) to adapt and develop effective ways to address the challenges and opportunities of virtual work. Virtual work is associated with five key, interrelated areas, which have significant implications for HRM: (1) altered employment relationships, (2) flexible work hours, (3) flexible work locations, (4) global dimensions of virtual work, and (5) technology. This symposium brings together leading voices in these areas, allowing the panel to provide in-depth insights on the future role of HRM in shaping virtual work. By integrating theoretical and empirical insights, this panel symposium should serve as a starting point for scholarly discussion on how to advance knowledge on managing virtual work, fostering engagement, and managing concerns in geographically dispersed work settings. Given the increasing reliance on and struggle with different forms of virtual work, this panel symposium will serve as a foundational reference for scholars studying the future of HRM, encouraging cross-disciplinary dialogue, and offering implications for organizations adapting to evolving work environments.
