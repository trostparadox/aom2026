Toward a Blue Economy: Testing the “Business in Nature” Framework With Corporate Disclosure Data

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 2
ONE

People

Authors: Silu Yang; Rafael Sarda ESADE Business School

Session overview

The ocean is a critical social-ecological system under unprecedented pressure, presenting a "grand challenge" of reconciling economic use with ecological health. The "Blue Economy" paradigm seeks this balance, yet prevailing corporate sustainability practices, often rooted in eco-efficiency, have proven insufficient and prone to decoupling. This study investigates the potential of the "Business In Nature" (BInN) framework to guide a substantive corporate transition. BInN reframes the firm as embedded within the biosphere, requiring strategies that are eco-effective and regenerative. We leverage a unique, large-scale dataset from the Ocean Impact Initiative (OII), which assesses over 2,500 companies across 17 ocean-relevant industries via AI analysis of corporate disclosures. The core of our investigation is the theoretical and empirical linkage between BInN's four conditions (Eco-effectiveness, Extended Stakeholder Integration, Transformative Innovation, Collaborative Governance) and the four managerial dimensions of the OII disclosure framework. We examine the extent to which corporate practices, as revealed through the OII, align with BInN principles and explore the relationship between this alignment and patterns of ocean sustainability performance. This research aims to contribute theoretically by empirically testing and refining the BInN framework within the ONE domain, and practically by demonstrating the OII's utility as a diagnostic tool for businesses and policymakers navigating the transition to a genuine Blue Economy.
