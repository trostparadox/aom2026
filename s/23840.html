How Mindfulness Unlocks Employee Creativity: A Self-Determination Perspective (WITHDRAWN)

OB
Xia Liu (Wuhan University); Huan Liu (Shandong Technology and Business University)
Drawing on self-determination theory, this study focuses on mindfulness—a dynamic, adaptive personal quality—and aims to develop a moderated mediation model that explains how and when employee mindfulness enhances creativity. We tested our theoretical model using a three-wave, two-source field survey with data collected from 232 full-time follower-employee dyads. Employee mindfulness significantly boosts creativity by stimulating curiosity in work, and this indirect relationship is moderated by performance feedback. Specifically, the positive effect of mindfulness on curiosity is stronger when employees perceive higher levels of performance feedback, which subsequently leads to greater creativity. This work advances the literature on mindfulness, curiosity, and creativity in organizational settings.
