Session overview

Technological change continues to reshape firms’ innovation processes, but the ability of organizations to benefit from these advancements increasingly depends on the availability and organization of skilled labor. Firms adopting new technologies or pursuing innovation must navigate challenges arising from labor market competition, skill scarcity, worker mobility, and the need for organizational structures that effectively coordinate specialized knowledge. In this symposium we bring together four papers that examine how labor markets, human capital frictions, and organizational design shape firms’ innovation outcomes and the diffusion of new technologies. Together they shed new light on the ways in which firms acquire, organize, and retain the skills necessary to innovate in rapidly changing technological environments.
