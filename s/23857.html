Opportunities vs. Threats: How AI Collaboration Influences Role Changes and Psychological Detachment

OB
Wenyan Duan (Harbin Institute of Technology); Tung-Ju Wu (Harbin Institute of Technology); Zhuojie Yang (Harbin Institute of Technology)
Collaboration with artificial intelligence (AI) is gradually changing the modern workplace, creating new learning opportunities and role expansion possibilities for employees. However, this transformation also brings challenges: traditional job boundaries may be broken down, leading to confusion among employees regarding their own role positioning. In order to explore how AI collaboration affects employees’ experience in a systematic way, we treat AI collaboration as both a job demand and a job resource, analyzing its dual effect on employees’ quality of work life based on the job demands-resources (JD-R) model. A three-wave field study (N = 357) and a scenario-based experiential study (N = 225) were conducted. The results indicated that AI crafting and psychological detachment serially mediate the relationship between AI collaboration and employees’ quality of work life, constituting a resource gain path. In contrast, AI role ambiguity and psychological detachment form a sequential mediation pathway, representing a resource loss path. AI self-efficacy exerted a moderating effect, amplifying the positive effects of AI collaboration while attenuating its potential adverse effects. This research contributes to JD-R theory in the AI era, offering evidence-based insights to guide the development of adaptive human-machine collaboration models that mitigate adverse workplace outcomes.
