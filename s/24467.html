A Typology of Global South Scholars: Status Configuration and Academic Recognition

DEI
Poonam Singh (Maynooth University); Olga Ryazanova (Maynooth University); Peter McNamara (Maynooth University)
This paper seeks to resolve ambiguity in the definition of Global South Scholar (GSS) by rejecting the assumption of homogeneity and advancing a status-based typology. We define GSS by their country of origin and distinguish four scholar types based on PhD-granting institution and employment affiliation. Drawing on status bias theory, we test the existence and consequences of this typology in the context of research awards given by academic societies. Using archival data from Academy of Management conferences (2016–2022), we examine differences in award recognition across Classical, Immigrant, Emigrant, and Transnational GSS. By theorizing internal stratification within the Global South, the study advances research on status bias and meritocracy and shows how recognition systems institutionalize geopolitical hierarchies while obscuring within-group inequality.
