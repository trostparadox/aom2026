Session overview

Artificial intelligence is reshaping how managers construct and perceive problems, search for solutions, and make decisions. As AI increasingly mediates the cognitive process of problem framing, formulation, and decision-making, traditional biases (bounded rationality, heuristics, escalation of commitment) can take new forms. At the same time, AI offers significant advantages for managers, such as expanding the range of problem framings, improving the detection of relevant signals, accelerating analysis, and enabling more informed and creative solution generation. This symposium brings together scholars across strategy, innovation, decision-making, and managerial cognition who are leading important research on human–AI problem-solving and decision-making. We aim to highlight not only the challenges AI introduces but also the new capabilities and strengths that AI enables for individuals and firms. By contrasting viewpoints through panel presentations and discussion, the symposium aims to clarify core mechanisms, identify boundary conditions, and outline a research agenda that moves beyond outcome-focused evaluations to a deeper understanding of how AI transforms managerial decision-making and action in the context of AI assistance.
