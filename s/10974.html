Clarifying Alternative Work Arrangements: Conceptual Boundaries and Research Implications

Session overview

The purpose of this study is to provide conceptual clarity to the fragmented and inconsistently used terminology surrounding alternative work arrangements. Specifically, we examine and distinguish six commonly used work-related terms—virtual work, work from home, remote work, telework, flexible work, and hybrid work—by analyzing how each is defined in the academic literature. Based on definitions from previous empirical and theoretical studies, we identify the core features and conceptual emphases associated with each term across key dimensions, including technology, location, time, autonomy, and boundary. We further examine which combinations of these dimensions qualify each term as a form of alternative work arrangement and propose refined definitions that reflect these distinctions. By clarifying conceptual boundaries and articulating defining dimensions, this study advances cumulative knowledge, reduces construct ambiguity, and enables more expanded theorizing in research on alternative work arrangements.
