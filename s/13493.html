Session overview

Research on industry creation highlights imagination and experimentation as foundational processes. Imagination enables actors to envision future industry possibilities, motivating investment and guiding entrepreneurial action by creating strategic direction under uncertainty. Experimentation complements this process by testing imagined futures through distributed trials, generating learning and reducing uncertainty over time. Despite their importance, existing scholarship has largely treated imagination and experimentation as sequential rather than interdependent processes. This panel symposium addresses this gap by examining how this relationship changes across firm types and how it evolves across different stages of industry emergence. Further, it explores how digital technologies and artificial intelligence are transforming this relationship. As they enhance efficiency and expand imaginative capacity, these technologies can reshape the pace and trajectory of industry emergence
