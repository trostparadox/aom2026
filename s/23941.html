How Does Firm Digital Transformation Shape Its Innovation Persistence?

TIM
Boxi Nan (Xi'an Jiaotong University); Chengli Shu (Xi'an Jiaotong University)
Innovation persistence stands as a pivotal path for companies to build competitive advantages - especially in the digital era, where companies’ market advantages are perpetually eroded by fierce and ever-evolving competition. However, the mechanism by which firms’ digital transformation affects innovation persistence remains unclear. Drawing on the research by Raisch and Krakowski (2021), this paper argues that digital transformation can enhance firms’ innovation persistence through the dual effects of automation and augmentation. First, digital transformation enhances the stability of firms’ innovation inputs by boosting the automation level of these input processes. Second, it empowers firms’ innovation activities, enabling them to conduct innovative endeavors more efficiently. Using a panel dataset of A-share listed companies in China’s Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges as the research sample, this study shows that firms’ digital transformation is positively associated with their innovation persistence. Moreover, a higher supply chain concentration or higher agency costs weakens this positive relationship, whereas this positive relationship is stronger under higher spatial location competition or among state-owned enterprises. These findings enrich the theoretical references for firms seeking to pursue persistent innovation and build sustainable competitive advantages in the context of the digital economy.
