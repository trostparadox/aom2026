A Configurational Analysis of Chief Sustainability Officers' Performance in Sustainability

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 2
ONE

People

Authors: Taiwo Abraham The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley; Sibin Wu The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

Session overview

This study investigates the configurational conditions under which Chief Sustainability Officers (CSOs) effectively drive organizational sustainability performance. Using fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA) on 239 CSOs from publicly traded companies, we examine how combinations of general and sustainability-specific human capital, Champion and Ambassador job functions, and firm sustainability governance (FSG) predict non-financial and financial sustainability outcomes. Contrary to theoretical expectations, configurations featuring low rather than high FSG emerged as pathways to superior performance, suggesting CSO operational autonomy may be more critical than formal governance structures. The findings support equifinality, demonstrating multiple pathways to sustainability success, and offer implications for CSO role design.
