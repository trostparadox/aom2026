Feeling Invisible? AI Can Make SMEs Vsible; Role of Entrepreneurial Leadership

ENT
Sobia Shabeer (Mukabbir University of Science & Technology, Gujrat, Pakistan); Tehreem Fatima (University of Sargodha)
This research investigates the relation of entrepreneurial leadership (EL) on SME innovation. Drawing on the theory of social cognitive (TSC) and the resource-based view (RBV), this study proposes a novel of roles AI self-efficacy and big data powered by artificial intelligence (BDAI) as mediators among EL and SME innovation. Data was collected from managers of SMEs, who were divided into agricultural and non-agricultural sector, present in Lahore, Pakistan. About 160 and 190 usable responses were received both sectors. We tested serial mediation using model 6 of Process Macro, introduced by Preacher and Hayes in SPSS software. The results showed that AI self-efficacy and BDAI significantly play roles as mediators between EL and SME innovation in non-agricultural sector. However, this mediation was not supported in agricultural sector. Only EL increased the AI self-efficacy and SME innovation in agricultural sector. This shed light on meaningful contribution to understand EL, AI self-efficacy, BDAI and SME innovation sector wise.
