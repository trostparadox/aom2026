Session overview

Workplace inclusion, or the valued participation and integration of individuals into the work environment, has emerged as a critical focus for organizations navigating contemporary diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) challenges. Despite growing scholarly interest, inclusion research remains fragmented across theoretical perspectives, methodological approaches, and levels of analysis. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) addresses fundamental challenges limiting progress in inclusion scholarship while charting promising directions for future research. This 90-minute session unfolds in four parts. Part I provides a research-grounded conceptualization of workplace inclusion as a foundation for the session. Part II examines "Barriers" to studying inclusion with Dr. Anne Burmeister and Dr. Darryl Rice presenting key challenges researchers face. Guided by Dr. Yixuan Li and Dr. Christina Stamper, Part III focuses on "Bridges," exploring how inclusion connects to adjacent organizational constructs and links across levels of analysis. Participants will be grouped into interactive roundtables with guided discussion prompts that relate these frameworks to participants’ own research and identify integrative opportunities. Part IV features "Bold Steps Forward," in which Dr. Stephanie Creary and Dr. Amy Randel share their vision for advancing inclusion research in novel and courageous ways. This portion of the session takes the form of a fireside chat that will allow for the synthesis of workshop insights and address emergent themes from the discussion. This PDW appeals to scholars at all career stages studying inclusion and related topics, as well as organizational phenomena, fostering dialogue across theoretical perspectives, and building a research community while advancing workplace inclusion scholarship.
