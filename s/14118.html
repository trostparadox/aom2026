Session overview

There is ambiguity about how management science intertwines with management consulting, and what delimites transformational research from transformational consulting. There is also the question of whether and when consulting is considered scientific. This professional development workshop is designed to reveal the features of the various modes of inquiry and to define what is common and what differentiates Fundamental Research, Translational Research, Applied Research, Intervention Research, Scientific Consulting and Consulting. The approach is participatory, by asking participants to engage in an interactive exercise to enumerate the features of each of the disciplines above cited, note on which features the disciplines intersect, and which features differentiate one from the other. Participants are also invited to share references they find seminal or revealing that might inform the discussion.
