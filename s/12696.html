Session overview

As the landscape of individual careers evolves, so do the research interests surrounding them. Insights from various management disciplines enrich the literature on career transitions and mobility. This professional development workshop provides an opportunity to bring together leading experts who study careers, career mobility, and career management, drawing from diverse fields studying multiple levels of analysis. The workshop will begin with a panel discussion where these leading career scholars share their interests and insights that shape theory, empirics, and perspectives on career transitions. Then participants will engage in small roundtable discussions with panelists to deepen understanding and create opportunities for interaction. The goal is to identify ways to integrate theories and perspectives to guide future research on career transitions.
