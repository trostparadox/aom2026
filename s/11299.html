Session overview

The general equation of leadership is interdisciplinary and constructed from aspects of leadership representative of the actual practice of leadership. Though an equation is generally thought of as quantitative, here, in our case, qualitative aspects are made part of equations and inequalities. The logic and structure of the equation is robust, but the calculation to generate a single number as a measure of leadership is not possible due to challenges in meaningfully calibrating and measuring qualitative factors of leadership. This challenge is left to future scholars with the hope they will have access to new methods of calibrating and measuring qualitative factors of leadership in meaningful and exact ways. When the emergent phenomenon E enters the mind-brain space through sensory apparatus, it is engulfed by an existing set of factors KWIC + PAF and the union of E and KWIC + PAF forms a superset {KWIC + PAF} U {E}.If all dimensions of E are matched up (based on logic and reason) and sufficiently covered by factors in KWIC + PAF, we say that the emergent event E is covered by the contents of the mind-brain (KWIC + PAF). In practice, however, more individuals may be required to combine and deploy their KWIC + PAF onto an emergent event E in order to generate a full solution for E. It is in this respect that this work views leadership in organizations as a multi-individual practice.
