Same Platform, Different Experience: Positional Contingency in Integrated Workplace Platform Use

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 2
CTO

People

Authors: siyu zhou Shantou University; Jiarui Yin Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya - BarcelonaTech

Session overview

Integrated workplace platforms promise organizational transformation through unified communication, collaboration, and workflow systems. However, features celebrated for enabling coordination often burden frontline workers. We investigate this puzzle through 40 organizational cases and 16 interviews with users of Feishu—an all-in-one workplace platform widely adopted across organizations in China. Our findings reveal that platform benefits and costs are not separate outcomes of different features but arise simultaneously from the same platform properties. Visibility enables organizational awareness while creating surveillance exposure. Connectivity enables agility while eroding work-life boundaries. Standardization enables alignment while constraining autonomy. These constitutive tensions distribute asymmetrically based on organizational position: those who coordinate through the platform experience capabilities as resources, while those whose work is coordinated bear costs more directly. We contribute a positionally contingent understanding of affordance actualization and identify structural tensions inherent to integrated platforms in the workplace that require ongoing navigation.
