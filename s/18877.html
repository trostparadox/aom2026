Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed for university teachers who seek to align their teaching with deeper spiritual, ethical, and personal values. Grounded in the Whole Life Management framework, the workshop supports faculty in integrating spiritual (values, meaning and moral purpose), professional (teaching, mentoring, and academic responsibilities), and personal (well-being and identity) goals. Positioned within the Management, Spirituality, and Religion (MSR) Division's Teaching and Learning Committee, the workshop treats teaching as a morally and spiritually consequential practice rather than a purely technical activity. Through reflective mapping, dialogical engagement, and pedagogical strategy design, participants will experience the same integrative and reflective approaches they can later use in their classrooms. The workshop equips educators with practical tools to enhance teaching authenticity, ethical presence, and sustainability, while modeling spiritually grounded and responsible management education.
