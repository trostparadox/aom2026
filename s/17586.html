Session overview

This paper reviews and critically examines the existing literature on customer incivility, focusing on the current status of research, and proposes directions for future research. The paper, through a systematic methodology, synthesises the antecedents, outcomes and the mechanisms of customer incivility. The proposed framework highlights several interesting and counterintuitive, yet theoretically plausible and untested relationships. The article also situates these relationships within a complex network of interactions and offers a possible explanation for them. Furthermore, the paper aims to motivate research in this field by providing empirical evidence and clarification for the proposed framework.
