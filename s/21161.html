Session overview

Following our tradition in every AOM’s annual meeting program, the Management Consulting Division will sponsor its Doctoral Consortium during the pre-conference period of the Academy of Management 2026 Annual Meeting. The objective of this Consortium is to provide support to students working on subject under the wide umbrella of management consulting in their PhD, Dba, Exec-PhD DM programs. The organizers, faculty members, and panelists will focus on providing participating students with opportunities to discuss their dissertation proposals in small groups, and to learn about various academic and professional steps and tasks they will encounter in the senior years of their doctoral studies and professional career afterwards.
