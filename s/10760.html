Session overview

The global Covid-19 pandemic has changed the onboarding process of many organizations. The research objectives were aiming to determine the factors of a successful onboarding process that lead to a newcomer’s overall satisfaction and well-being while decreasing employee turnover. In this research, a longitudinal study with a qualitative research method was used including 29 semi-structured interviews during the pandemic and 22 interviews post-pandemic that were conducted to understand this phenomenon. We contribute to knowledge by extending the Organizational Support Theory with institutional and external factors in the context of virtual onboarding.
