Session overview

This 90-minute experiential Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces "The DharmaArtha Sutra," a leadership framework synthesizing ancient Eastern wisdom—specifically the Bhagavad Gita, Arthashastra, and Confucianism—with modern transformative leadership. Participants engage in the "Chakravyuha," an ancient multi-layered circular formation used as a metaphor for the modern organization. The simulation brings together Senior Leaders, Middle Managers, and Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to solve a systemic crisis predicated on Nishkama Karma(selfless action) and Sattvic (equanimous) leadership. By navigating a mid-session "VUCA Trap," participants transition from competitive Artha (resource hoarding) to "Pluralistic Inclusion," operationalizing an integrated, spiritual pursuit of resilience. This workshop is the primary experiential adaptation of the upcoming book titled “DharmaArthaSutra” by Rajesh Misca, designed to offer a practical mental roadmap for navigating 21st-century global complexity through an integrative worldview.
