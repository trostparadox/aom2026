Women Artists on Instagram: A Lefebvrian Analysis of Digital Workspace

CMS
Khushi Srivastava (Indian Institute of Management Kozhikode); N G Yamini (Indian Institute of Management Kozhikode); Rajeshwari C (Indian Institute of Management Kozhikode)
This paper examines social media platforms as alternative workspaces amid the mobility, flexibilization, and digitalization of work. We study women artists in India who, facing gendered constraints in domestic spaces, adopt Instagram as a primary site for organizing their art business. Drawing on Lefebvre’s spatial triad, we examine Instagram as a workspace shaped by spatial practices, social experiences, and algorithmic governance. Based on qualitative analysis, we explore how Instagram becomes a workspace for women artists facing gendered constraints. We also examine how women artists experience and negotiate the lived space of Instagram and how this lived experience diverges from the platform’s conceived and perceived spaces. The study contributes to space research by theorizing digital platforms as gendered and contested workspaces.
