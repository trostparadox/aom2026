Session overview

Decades of research have examined how new ideas are generated, but scholarly attention has been expanding to how those ideas are evaluated and what behavioral responses they elicit. Creativity reception is nascent literature with many exciting new directions that are a challenge to synthesize whilst the literature rapidly expands, considering characteristics of sender, receiver, idea, and context in predicting outcomes (Zhou et al., 2019). This creates a need for scholars working on different pieces of this puzzle to understand their colleagues’ efforts and to integrate the knowledge produced. The researchers in this symposium are part of a community that seeks to leverage and integrate insights from the many facets of creativity reception. This symposium explores how sender, receiver, idea, and context each influence how an idea is perceived and what actions receivers take as a result. The interactive capstone discussion will focus on the state, current directions, and future direction for creativity evaluation and responses. The goal of this symposium is to provide insight into the factors and processes that influence how new ideas are evaluated and responded to and the implications of such for theory and practice. Specific topics include who- or what- the evaluator is, how the idea was discovered and presented, how a response such as active support or passive opposition is chosen, as well as roles of problem context and narrative arc of the solution.
