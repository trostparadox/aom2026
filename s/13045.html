Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) examines the application of fuzzy-set Qualitative Comparative Analysis (fsQCA) as a set-theoretic analytical approach for investigating complex causality in management and organizational research. FsQCA identifies configurations of conditions associated with specific outcomes, providing a rigorous alternative to regression-based methods by operationalizing configurational principles such as asymmetric causality, equifinality, multifinality, and conjunctural causation. Adding to the previous PDW format, this PDW combines an introduction to fsQCA with example paper discussion under plural research approaches, followed by a roundtable discussion format that aims for practical guidance for fsQCA research across several management fields. The PDW welcomes diverse perspectives and showcases studies across management disciplines (HRM, Strategy, Finance and Operation Management), methodological traditions (deductive, inductive, and abductive), and data types (qualitative and quantitative). The program includes a concise introduction to fsQCA and interactive problem-solving discussions organized through 20-minute table rotations with development discussion for participants’ work in progress. As such, participants are encouraged to bring research in progress for collective examination of fsQCA’s applicability. By integrating theory and practice, the PDW seeks to advance fsQCA as both an analytical method and a conceptual lens in management research, supporting innovative approaches to complex causal structures and heterogeneous datasets.
