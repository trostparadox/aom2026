Session overview

This interactive Professional Development Workshop brings together mid-career scholar-practitioners with a terminal degree to engage in collective reflection on how experiential knowledge informs organization development and change. Designed as an interactive space, the PDW invites participants to actively work with their experiences at the intersection of academic inquiry, professional practice, and institutional engagement. Through structured small-group exchanges and facilitated plenary dialogue, participants explore shared questions related to professional identity, learning through experience, and the institutional contexts shaping scholar-practitioner trajectories. By engaging with experiences across cultural, organizational, and career contexts, participants identify recurring patterns, shared dynamics, and areas of inquiry that support collective sensemaking. The workshop is one in a series of ongoing ODC-sponsored webinars and initiatives aimed at strengthening the visibility and engagement of scholar-practitioners within the division. In this series, the PDW creates a space in which individual experiences contribute to the emergence of collective insights. Participants will leave the workshop with a clearer articulation of their scholar-practitioner position, an understanding of shared dynamics across contexts, collectively generated insights on how AOM and ODC engage with scholar-practitioners, and strengthened connections within an emerging community of practice.
