Can Conscious Leadership and Workplace Spirituality Shape Meaningful Work in the Age of AI?

Session overview

The main thesis of the PDW will focus on how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is reshaping the work in terms of its design, leadership practices and employee experiences at work. While organizations are increasingly relying on AI to redefine productivity, decision-making and performance, there has been less emphasis and attention on how AI is reshaping meaningfulness and inner experiences of employee work. This PDW will explore on how conscious leadership and workplace spirituality will serve as foundational and critical frameworks for navigating AI-driven work environments. In other words, how can we reshape AI-driven work environments from an algorithmic based system to a more ethically grounded, relationally present and meaning-oriented system? Rather than focusing on AI implementation or compliance-based ethics, this PDW emphasizes on awareness of leaders and human-centered approaches deep seated in intentionality, meaningfulness and alignment of values. Through conceptual framing, and guided discussions and reflections, panelists will engage participants to explore how AI work trends intersect with leadership and employees’ experiences of connection, meaningfulness and purpose. This PDW aims to facilitate MSR scholars and educators to develop research questions, reflect on how we can approach and advance to a more conscious, meaningful and humane workplace in the AI era.
