Session overview

Many faculty members and researchers are discovering opportunities to leverage their expertise, passions, and professional networks beyond their primary academic roles. This professional development workshop features a panel discussion with academics who have successfully pursued “side hustles” ranging from small business ownership to consulting. Panelists will share their experiences, lessons learned, and practical advice for balancing these additional roles with the demands of an academic career. Whether your goal is additional income, professional growth, broader impact, or personal fulfillment, this session will provide insights into the possibilities and challenges of developing a multi-stream career alongside a career in academia.
