Cultivating Opportunities for Disruptive Innovation in the Digital Landscape

ENT
Jaeyoung Cho (University of Wollongong); Subhadeep Datta (NEOMA Business School); Kyungmi Lee (University of Wollongong)
We investigate how entrepreneurs cultivate opportunities for disruptive innovation in the digital era across different market conditions. Through an exploratory case analysis of IDIS—a digital security surveillance venture—we reveal that opportunities embedded in disruptive innovations are both deliberately created and detected; continuous entrepreneurial actions are vital for innovation enhancement and commercialization; and opportunity creation tends to prevail in emerging markets, while opportunity discovery is more common in low-end markets. We contribute by illustrating the dynamic interplay between opportunity creation and discovery in digital disruptive contexts, advancing the disruptive innovation literature with firm-level evidence on how digital innovations evolve from niche footholds to industry-wide disruption, and highlighting how disruptive innovations can originate and thrive in different market conditions.
