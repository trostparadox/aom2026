The Impact of GenAI Adoption and Organizational Resilience on Disruptive Innovation

TIM
yuhong liu (Northwestern Polytechnical University)
Generative AI (GenAI) is reshaping how firms innovate, yet its role in disruptive innovation remains unclear. Drawing on the resource-based view and dynamic capabilities theory, we propose and test an inverted U-shaped relationship between GenAI adoption and disruptive innovation, mediated by precursor resilience and improvisation resilience and moderated by GenAI affordance. Survey data from 270 Chinese manufacturing firms support the theorized curvilinear effect: disruptive innovation increases with GenAI adoption up to a threshold and then declines. GenAI adoption also shows inverted U-shaped effects on both resilience dimensions, and the indirect effects through each resilience pathway are significant. Moreover, GenAI affordance flattens the curvilinear adoption–resilience links, buffering over-adoption risks. These findings frame GenAI adoption as a nonlinear, resilience-driven process and identify affordance as a boundary condition for realizing disruptive innovation.
