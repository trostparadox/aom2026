City-Firm Synergy in Digital Transformation: Evidence from China’s “Little Giant” Enterprises

TIM
Teng Wang (Hohai University); Xinru Liu (Hohai University); Xiaotong Li (University of Alabama in Huntsville)
Through a city–firm synergy perspective, we investigate how urban institutions, resource endowments, and firm dynamic capabilities shape digital transformation. Using a dataset of China’s publicly listed “Little Giant” enterprises, we combine Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA) and fuzzy-set Qualitative Comparative Analysis (fsQCA) to identify four equifinal pathways for promoting digital transformation. Our results show that digital transformation is impeded when city and firm factors misalign. Incorporating spatial proximity, our analysis further reveals that firms near metropolitan centers benefit from stronger institution–resource synergies, whereas distant firms rely mainly on capability-driven pathways. Our study advances understanding of multi-level drivers of digital transformation and uncovers spatially heterogeneous configurational mechanisms that offer practical guidance for strengthening city–firm linkages in digital transformation.
