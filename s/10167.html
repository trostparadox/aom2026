Session overview

Impact is receiving increasing prominence in entrepreneurship research, yet scholarship on this topic remains fragmented across levels of analysis. Prior research has focused on how entrepreneurs experience and construct impact, communities co-create impact through stakeholder relationships, and entrepreneurial action contributes to broader systems change. However, these lines of research examine impact in isolation, limiting our ability to theorize how impact emerges dynamically across ventures, communities, and social systems. This PDW addresses this challenge by advancing a multi-level, integrative view of impact and value creation in entrepreneurship research. Centered on the guiding question “Where is Impact?”, the PDW brings together senior scholars, early-career researchers, and doctoral students to discuss conceptual, methodological, and empirical advancements in studying impact across levels of analysis. The workshop consists of two parts. The first part is open to all and includes a facilitated, interactive panel convening leading scholars in the field. The panel will discuss key tensions, assumptions, and emerging research directions in impact and its measurement in entrepreneurship. The second part, open to pre-registered participants, includes paper development roundtables, where participants will receive developmental feedback on early-stage research ideas from leading scholars and peers. To ensure continuity in both the conversations and community, participants will have an opportunity to join a dedicated online network following the PDW. The first part of the PDW is open to all attendees. Participation in the second part, paper development roundtables requires advance application and acceptance.
