The Creative Destruction of Universities: What will Universities Look Like in 2046?

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 4:00PM–5:30PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Regency A
TIM

People

Host: Tobias Kretschmer Imperial College London

Session overview

Academic and industry panelists will discuss their viewpoints of how they think universities will change over the next 20 years in terms of how they are designed, how research is undertaken, and how teaching is conducted. We hope to encourage a provocative and informative discussion. Specific topics that will be covered is how AI will affect research, or knowledge creation more generally, and teaching, or knowledge dissemination, and how these two activities should be organised in the future, and what what big (or small) changes we foresee in what universities will look like in 2046 as a result.
