Exploring Design Heterogeneity in Corporate Venture Builders

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 6

People

Authors: Davide Moiana Politecnico di Milano; Giuliano Sansone University College Dublin; Tobias Gutmann EBS University of Business and Law; Antonio Ghezzi Politecnico di Milano

Session overview

Over the past decade, Corporate Venture Builders (CVBs) have emerged as a novel organizational mechanism through which established firms systematically create new startups. Despite their rapid diffusion, scholarly understanding of CVBs remains limited and largely confined to practitioner-oriented accounts. Drawing on a comparative multiple-case study of four large Italian corporations, this study investigates why firms engage in corporate venture building and how they design CVB units. Based on 24 semi-structured interviews and extensive secondary data, we identify key design dimensions shaping CVBs and analytically derive two ideal-typical configurations: an internalized and an externalized CVB model. Our findings show that empirical cases occupy intermediate positions along a continuum of these dimensions, reflecting different ways of responding to core organizational tensions. The study contributes by conceptually clarifying CVBs as a hybrid organizational form and by advancing a configurational perspective on how corporates balance tensions when they engage in new venture creation.
