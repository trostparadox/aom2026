Session overview

This symposium is organized to systematically investigate the boundary conditions of product-to-platform transformation. Our objective is to move beyond implementation mechanics to distinguish who is best positioned to platformize, when the timing is optimal, and where market conditions are most favorable. By delineating these specific contexts, we aim to provide a more nuanced theoretical framework for scholars and actionable guidance for practitioners navigating this strategic shift.
