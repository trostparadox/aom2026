Social Cohesion: The Role for Management Scholars; Investigating via a Systematic Literature Review

Session overview

The importance of social cohesion to societal functioning is well-validated across both academic and public policy settings and is of increasing importance against the backdrop of growing inter- and intra-national tensions. However, the role of business in contributing to social cohesion, and the associated role for management scholars, remains a subject of debate. In large part, this is due to siloes within the existing, multidisciplinary body of research. This challenge is addressed by way of a novel, Systematic Literature Review of management-focused research into social cohesion. Through application of the Search-Appraisal-Synthesis-Analysis framework and Co-Citation Analysis, we complete a transparent and comprehensive overview of the most highly cited and impactful research articles for management scholars and map how these conceptualise and measure social cohesion. The aim of this study is therefore to provide an up-to-date review of existing body of research on the role of businesses in fostering social cohesion and to develop a comprehensive map to guide future research.
