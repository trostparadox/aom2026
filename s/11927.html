Session overview

Academia may be a particularly attractive career for neurodivergent individuals. However, research and practice in education have focused more on supporting neurodivergent students than faculty. In this caucus, we bring together neurodivergent faculty and allies to share experiences, challenges, strategies, and research ideas that can facilitate neuroinclusive academic workplaces.
