Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) offers an experiential and comparative exploration of gamification in higher education teaching, with a particular focus on the emerging role of generative artificial intelligence (AI). While gamification has become an established approach for enhancing engagement and motivation in management education, the rapid diffusion of generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot raises new pedagogical, ethical, and design-related questions. Through a highly interactive format, participants engage in a structured challenge to design a gamified teaching activity either with or without AI support. By working in parallel groups, participants experience first-hand the differences in creativity, efficiency, decision-making, and pedagogical intentionality that emerge from AI-supported and non-AI-supported design processes. The PDW is designed for management educators seeking practical, reflective, and evidence-informed approaches to innovative teaching. Participants leave with transferable gamification strategies, a nuanced understanding of AI as a pedagogical partner rather than a substitute for educator expertise, and a reflective framework for integrating AI responsibly within management education contexts.
