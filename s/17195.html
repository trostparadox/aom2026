Hybrid Working: (Re) Conceptualizing and Exploring its Potential in a Changing World

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 9:45AM–11:15AM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Salon 7
CAU

People

Participants: Pascale Peters Nyenrode Business University; Martine Coun Open University of the Netherlands; Clare Kelliher Cranfield University

Session overview

Since emerging following the Covid-19 pandemic, hybrid working has become widespread and stimulated research attention. Yet, it remains unclear how this relates to previous research on teleworking. This caucus will explore this and examine how it might be reconceptualized more broadly and implemented as a co-evolving socio-technical-spatial design multilevel challenge.
