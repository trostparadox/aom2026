Session overview

Digital platforms have transformed audiences into a governing "traffic" infrastructure, fundamentally reshaping how producers navigate livelihoods within a triadic system of platforms, creators, and consumers. This symposium examines how actors navigate markets in which audience traffic rules—how they win audiences, convert engagement into revenue, and manage the control dynamics and risks that audience participation can unleash. Bringing together four qualitative studies across China, Turkey, and South Korea, we show that audience traffic is not merely a background condition of platform markets. It is an infrastructure through which value is produced, meanings are stabilized (or strategically kept unstable), and power is redistributed. Collectively, this symposium offers an audience-centered account of platform exchange: when audience traffic rules, markets scale through engagement—but value creation becomes inseparable from new forms of dependence, contestation, and control.
