Digital Globalization: Connotation, Genre and Prospect

IM
Shiwen Liu (Zhejiang University); Kaisen Huang (Zhejiang University)
The convergence of digitalization and globalization has ushered in a new paradigm termed "digital globalization." This phenomenon not only reshapes traditional multinational enterprises (MNEs) but also fosters novel organizational forms, such as digital platforms, challenging conventional international business theories. Through a systematic synthesis and in-depth analysis of 95 articles on digital globalization published in top-tier international journals between 2007 and 2025, this study first delineates the conceptual framework and defining characteristics of the digital globalization context. It then categorizes MNEs based on two dimensions: whether they are "early internationalizing" and whether they are "digital-native." Furthermore, the paper identifies two prominent research streams—the Digital Innovation Stream and the International Business Stream—and synthesizes their respective foci, key research questions, and central arguments. Finally, the study offers practical implications for MNEs and proposes future research directions, aiming to stimulate broader scholarly engagement in the digital globalization discourse.
