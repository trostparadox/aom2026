Session overview

Healthcare systems are increasingly organized as networked healthcare arrangements in which actors across professional, organizational, and institutional domains collaborate to address complex challenges that exceed the capacity of individual organizations. These collaborative configurations play a critical role in improving care quality and system performance by enabling resource sharing, knowledge transfer, and coordinated action. Despite their growing relevance, limited attention has been devoted to understanding the competencies that enable certain professionals to occupy central positions and effectively coordinate networked healthcare arrangements. Building on a Professional Development Workshop (PDW) held at AOM 2025, which explored key competencies for sustaining effective interorganizational relationships, this proposed PDW advances the discussion by developing and validating a methodological framework to identify the competency-based job profile of professionals who occupy central leadership roles within healthcare networks. Through collective prioritization, small-group discussions, and visual competency mapping, participants will assess required mastery levels for key technical and behavioral competencies. The PDW is designed for scholars at all career stages as well as practitioners interested in healthcare networks, competency modeling, and professional development. By extending prior PDWs within the Healthcare Management Division, this workshop contributes a competency-based perspective tailored to healthcare while also bridging conversations with the Human Resource Management division. The outcomes will inform research on network effectiveness and provide actionable insights for talent management, leadership development, and workforce planning in complex, networked organizational contexts.
