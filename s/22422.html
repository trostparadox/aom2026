Session overview

This workshop brings together researchers to support colleagues in publishing on Social Issues in Management (SIM) topics in high-quality business and society, business ethics, and management journals, such as Business & Society, Business Ethics Quarterly, Journal of Business Ethics, Academy of Management Journal, Academy of Management Review, Administrative Science Quarterly, Journal of Management, Journal of International Business Studies, Organization Science, and Strategic Management Journal. The workshop will be held at the 2026 Academy of Management (AOM) Annual Meeting in Philadelphia, PA. The workshop will feature a team of scholars who have successfully published research on corporate social responsibility, business ethics, sustainability, or related topics in these journals, and/or have served on editorial boards of journals that publish SIM-related research. Each experienced scholar will serve as a mentor, paired with early-stage scholars to help refine their manuscripts-in-progress and guide them toward publication in their target journals. This session is by invitation only. Participation is limited to accepted mentees and confirmed mentors. Mentees must submit a manuscript through the SIM Research Development Workshop call for papers and receive acceptance notification. If you are interested in participating as a mentee, please contact Junghoon Park (Junghoon.Park@lmu.edu) or Tobey Scharding (tscharding@business.rutgers.edu) for information about the application process and deadlines.
