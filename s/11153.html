Session overview

Change and sustainability management leaders struggle to transform organizations due to siloed approaches, cultural resistance, and a lack of systemic tools for embedding ESG values. This interactive, game-based workshop introduces participants to the Integral Design Thinking (IDT) framework — a holistic model rooted in systems thinking, design science, and organizational change. Building on AOM 2025 PDW “Integral Design Thinking for Sustainability: Building Organizational Culture for Lasting Change” participants will engage in an immersive simulation called “The IDT Challenge,” where they assume organizational roles (e.g., Sustainability Officer, CFO, HR Lead, Change Consultant) and use the IDT framework to align values, strategy, and culture. They will assess organizational sustainability, identify design challenges, and co-create interventions through gameplay. Facilitators will guide debriefs linking the game’s dynamics to real-world applications. This PDW is a powerful building block for practitioners and academics alike, as this session promotes experiential learning, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and innovative strategy development. Participants will leave with a toolkit to apply IDT in their organizations and consulting practices, along with insight into shifting culture for lasting change. The PDW builds on the book “Integral Design Thinking for Sustainability Management: A Framework for Organizational Culture Change and Innovation” and contributes to the field by showcasing how serious play can unlock deep organizational insight and transformation. Keywords: Design thinking, Organizational Cultural Transformation, Management Consulting, Environmental Social Governance, Sustainability, Gamification
