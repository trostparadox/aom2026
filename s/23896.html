When AI Becomes the Manager: How Deployment and Disclosure Shape Employee Performance

TIM
Hrishikesh Desai (Arkansas State University)
This paper develops an analytical model to analyze how AI-generated performance feedback affects employee productivity. It focuses on two key effects: the deployment effect (performance improvement from AI feedback) and the disclosure effect (performance change when employees know AI generates the feedback). Using a baseline model and an extended framework incorporating factors such as trust, learning curves, and emotional responses, I demonstrate that AI deployment consistently improves performance, while disclosure effects vary significantly with employee tenure. Long-tenured employees show performance improvements with AI disclosure, while short-tenured employees experience slight decreases. The extended model reveals that high trust and appropriate feedback customization can lead to performance improvements. These findings provide organizations with quantitative insights for optimizing AI feedback implementation strategies across different employee segments and organizational contexts.
