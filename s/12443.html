CAR Plenary with Hughes Award Winner Beatrice van der Heijden on "Happiness, Health & Productivity"

CAR

People

Speakers: Michael B. Arthur Suffolk University; Yehuda Baruch University of Southampton; Jon P. Briscoe Northern Illinois University; Juergen Deller; Ariane Froidevaux The University of Texas at Arlington; Beatrice Isabella Johanna Maria Van Der Heijden Radboud University Nijmegen
Organizer: Janine Bosak Dublin City University

Session overview

This plenary on "How to foster Happiness, Health & Productivity" will feature the 2025 Hughes Lifetime Careers Scholar Award Winner Beatrice van der Heijden who will kick off the plenary with a talk on her own career journey, how she maintained her own career sustainability, her research to date and how to move forward as a community. This will be followed by input on the timely topic of 'career sustainability' from distinguished speakers including Michael Arthur, Yehuda Baruch, Jon Briscoe, Jurgen Deller, and Ariane Froidevaux - followed by audience participation in the final discussion.
