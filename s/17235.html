HCMD Distinguished Scholar Keynote Address, Awards Ceremony, and Business Meeting

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 3:30PM–5:30PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Liberty Ballroom C
HCM

People

Organizers: Deirdre McCaughey University of Calgary; Amber Stephenson The Pennsylvania State University-Penn State Harrisburg; Ingrid Nembhard The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania; Daan Westra Maastricht University; Nicholas Edwardson The University of New Mexico

Session overview

All members of the Health Care Management (HCM) Division and those interested in the Division and its scholarship are encouraged to attend. This session features reports on the state of the division, presentation of division awards, discussion of involvement opportunities, and more. A highlight of this session is the presentation of the Keith G. Provan Distinguished Scholar Award to the deserving recipient, who then delivers a keynote address.
