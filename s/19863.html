Session overview

Technological advancement has always been a key driver in the healthcare sector, as healthcare professionals continue to improve medical treatments, the patient experience and health outcomes. Imaging and diagnostics, electronic health records, wearable health devices and AI virtual assistants are just a few examples of how emerging technologies have been implemented and will continue to be used in healthcare. Preparing future generations of health professionals requires academic programs to keep pace with the technological changes made in the industry, allowing them to quickly adapt and pivot while moving things forward. We see curriculum changes to include data analytics, digital health applications and AI modeling, aimed at creating stronger technology-focused programs and to improve industry operating efficiencies. This PDW examines one such program change: the integration of WebXR/Web3D. The PDW examines the creation, implementation and interim findings of a WebXR/Web3D pilot study as integrated into an undergraduate U.S. Healthcare course. The three-part presentation aims to achieve the following: 1) discuss the current state of the healthcare sector and its reliance on technological advancements, 2) detail the WebXR/Web3D pilot study/assignment, data collection tools, and interim results and 3) demonstrate the WebXR/Web3D approach with real-time participant access.
