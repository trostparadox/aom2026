Hiding in Plain Sight: Multimarket Contact and Brownwashing

SIM
Lejia Zhong (Wuhan University); JIURUI HE (Wuhan University); Xinming Deng (Wuhan University); Guohua Liu (Shanghai International Studies University)
In contemporary business environments, brownwashing has emerged as a strategic disclosure practice that undermines the quality of ESG information and misleads stakeholders. While previous research has largely explained brownwashing as a firm-level response to institutional pressures or reputational risk management, relatively little attention has been paid to how interfirm competitive dynamics shape such behavior. Addressing this gap, this study investigates the role of multimarket contact in driving corporate brownwashing, arguing that mutual forbearance arising from repeated competitive encounters across markets can encourage firms to adopt strategic underreporting as a form of strategic discretion aimed at risk avoidance. Drawing on the strategy tripod framework, we further examine the moderating roles of financial slack, market concentration, and marketization. Using panel data from listed firms in the Chinese home appliance industry between 2016 and 2021, our empirical results show that multimarket contact significantly increases brownwashing. Moreover, financial slack weakens this positive relationship, while market concentration and marketization strengthen it.
