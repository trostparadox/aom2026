Research on the Impact of Digital Transformation in Manufacturing on Sustainable Growth

CTO
Junyi Tang (Harbin Institute of Technology); Ping Li (Harbin Institute of Technology); Xuan Liu (Harbin Institute of Technology)
Digital technologies represented by artificial intelligence, big data, and the Internet of Things are providing strong support for the development of the manufacturing industry. Based on organizational adaptation theory and resource-based theory, this study selects 365 Chinese A-share listed manufacturing companies from 2013 to 2023 as samples, incorporating innovation rhythm, innovation speed, and unpledged slack resources into the mechanism by which digital transformation affects the sustainable growth of manufacturing enterprises. The study finds that: (1) Digital transformation significantly promotes long-term corporate value, suppresses short-term performance, and exhibits a lag period of one to two years. (2) The inhibitory effect of digital transformation on short-term performance is mainly observed in labor-intensive enterprises, whereas the promotion of long-term value is more evident in technology-intensive, capital-intensive, and state-owned enterprises. (3) The promotion effect of digital transformation on long-term corporate value is partially realized through the mediating paths of innovation rhythm and innovation speed; regarding short-term performance, innovation speed strengthens the inhibitory effect of digital transformation, while innovation rhythm produces a masking effect. (4) Unpledged slack resources negatively moderate the impact path of digital transformation on innovation speed. These conclusions provide systematic insights into how manufacturing enterprises can leverage digital technologies to promote sustainable growth.
