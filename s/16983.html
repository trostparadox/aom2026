Linking Task Conflict to Relationship Conflict: The Moderating Role of Conflict Management

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 3
HR

People

Author: Hyunjoo Lee Sungkyunkwan University

Session overview

Drawing on social information processing theory (Salancik & Pfeffer, 1978), this study examines whether task conflict leads to relationship conflict and how this process affects employees’ task performance and withdrawal behavior. Using time-lagged survey data from 56 teams and 501 employees in South Korea, the results show that task conflict is positively related to relationship conflict, which mediates its effects on task performance and withdrawal behavior. Moreover, conflict management weakens the negative association between relationship conflict and task performance, whereas no significant moderating effect is found for withdrawal behavior. These findings clarify how task-related disagreements escalate into relational conflict and identify the conditions under which employees can sustain task performance despite interpersonal conflict.
