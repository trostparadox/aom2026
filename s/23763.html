Dreams to Action: Converting Social Entrepreneurial Intention to Social Entrepreneurial Behaviour

ENT
Priya Tewary
We study the factors that influence the translation of social entrepreneurial intention into social entrepreneurial behavior. All studies on social entrepreneurial intention use self-reported intention as a proxy for the actual venture creation, despite increasing evidence that not all entrepreneurial intentions translate into the creation of a new venture. We propose a research framework including two groups of factors- intrinsic and extrinsic, that are likely to impact this relationship. We investigate this relationship using a mixed methods approach. To test the hypotheses around social entrepreneurial behavior, we use a 2- wave survey separated by 9 months, and complement the findings with qualitative interviews from 82 respondents filling the two surveys.
