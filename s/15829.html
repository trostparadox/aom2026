Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) focuses on cutting-edge research on founder demographics—gender, race, age, social class, and immigrant status —especially on novel methods to identify these characteristics at scale. Although founder demographics play a significant role in venture financing, hiring, innovation, and broader startup performance, most large-scale datasets on high-growth ventures do not directly record these attributes, which limits the research in this area. At the same time, new AI techniques make it feasible to infer demographic characteristics from names, photos, education, and career histories, opening the door to richer and more generalizable methods. The PDW brings together leading scholars studying founder demographics and applying these frontier measurement methods in their research. Through structured discussion with panelists and participants, the PDW aims to expand both the empirical toolkit and the research agenda on founder demographics in entrepreneurship, innovation, and strategy.
