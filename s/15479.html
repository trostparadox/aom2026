Session overview

This caucus will seek to establish a case for hospitality and tourism as a new interest group within the Academy of Management. The convenor will set the context for the discussion and then participants will discuss the need for and the feasibility of such an interest group. The discussion will be in the form of an informal robin roundtable meeting, so as to encourage open dialogue. The participants are reputed global scholars – either from hospitality and tourism – and others who have held leadership positions within the Academy. This caucus invites leading scholars from across disciplines to critique why this gap persists and discuss how to create pathways for strengthening HTM visibility and academic legitimacy within the Academy.
