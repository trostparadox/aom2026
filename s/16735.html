Don't Discount the Hype: AI Narratives and Their Influence on Career Decision-Making

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 1
MOC

People

Author: Timothy Englehart Boston College

Session overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies are the subject of powerful and often extreme narratives that may or may not align with their current capabilities. While AI has become a topic of great scholarly concern, the influence of narratives surrounding AI has received less scrutiny. Narratives surrounding AI often focus on themes around replacing or augmenting workers, suggesting that these narratives may influence how workers approach or conceive their careers. Accordingly, I theorize that AI narratives can shape how individuals make decisions regarding their careers. I do so by considering how individuals align with distinct narratives, how individuals internalize elements of narratives that they are exposed to, and how internalized narratives shape individual decision-making. I offer a set of propositions suggesting that individuals with less familiarity with AI are, perhaps counterintuitively, more likely to be influenced by AI narratives in decision-making because they are more likely to draw on AI narratives when making rational evaluations of future conditions. This work provides theoretical contribution to literature on technology by proposing that AI narratives represent a distinct influence on individual career behavior. It also offers a theoretical contribution to narrative research by proposing a model through which external narratives diffuse to an individual, internalized level.
