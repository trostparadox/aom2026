Session overview

The intersection of parenthood and academia presents unique opportunities and challenges for academics who are (or plan to become) parents. A multitude of career paths are available within academia, theoretically allowing academics agency over how they choose to craft a career amid diverse family demands. Yet, the structure of academia, such as rigid tenure-track timelines and immovable teaching schedules, can make it difficult for academics to successfully balance work and family demands. The goal of the professional development workshop (PDW) is to provide a constructive forum for parent academics to build community over their shared parent and academic identities. The PDW will allow participants the opportunity to obtain practical guidance for crafting a career in academia as a parent and form a network of support as a parent academic. Prominent management scholars will serve as panelists and facilitators to share their perspectives on these topics – derived from both their lived experiences and the literature. Following an opening reflective exercise and panelists’ remarks, participants will be invited to participate in two breakout table sessions with panelists. The session will conclude with a report out of main take-aways and next steps for further building community among parent academics within the Academy.
