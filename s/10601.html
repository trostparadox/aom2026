Session overview

We are planning a doctoral student consortium and a mentor-match. The 2026 DEI doctoral student consortium will include a diverse mix of speaker panels and participative roundtable discussions designed to provide doctoral students with a realistic, open, and honest understanding of how to build a research identity, build their community, and bring their ideas to publication in academic journals. For 2026, the consortium’s theme will center on supporting doctoral students in facing risks and challenges when engaging in DEI work in the early stages of their career and when on the job market. In light of the recent global backlash against DEI efforts, the PDW as a whole will center on providing practical strategies for navigating risks, building a sustainable academic career, and fostering impactful DEI scholarship. Participants will also have the opportunity for more personalized connections and feedback through a mentor-match to be held after the consortium (or at another mutually agreed upon time), in which participants are paired with a scholar based on the participant’s research interests. Participants are required to apply and to register for the doctoral student consortium. Day of registration is not available. To apply, please submit your CV to Renata Casado renata.casado@uwa.edu.au and Charlotte Karam ckaram@uottawa.ca.
