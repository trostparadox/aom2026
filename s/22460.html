Session overview

The ODC Doctoral Consortium is the place for students of organizational change to gain professional knowledge, receive mentorship and feedback on their work, and meet senior colleagues and journal editors in their field. The agenda includes: Welcome and Introductions Resources and Strategies for Finding Your Academic Connections, Purpose, and Flow Coaching and Research Dialogue Constructive Feedback Session: Improving Journal Submissions through Honest Feedback Editorial Panel: Publishing Change Research in Leading Journals Building Your Identity, and Managing Your Career Wrap Up and Reflections We look forward to welcoming you and to learning about your work.
