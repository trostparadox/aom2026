Session overview

This PDW will equip participants with a clear understanding of the landscape for theory work and the nuances of submitting to various journals. Refined and expanded content builds on the well-attended 2025 and 2024 editions by providing first-hand author accounts of publishing in respected management journals. In the first part of the workshop, Professors Joanna Campbell, Gerardo Okhuysen, and Dean Shepherd will share their experiences in publishing theory papers across a wide array of leading outlets, then engage with session participants via a facilitated discussion. In the second part of the workshop, participants will engage directly with editors-in-chief and associate editors of 18 management journals in roundtable discussions to learn about the distinctive criteria for publishing in outlets of interest. Overall, this PDW offers a collaborative environment where established and emerging scholars will connect, ask questions, share insights, and reflect on the intricacies of crafting and submitting theory papers, thereby supporting the development of impactful theory work and strengthening scholarly networks.
