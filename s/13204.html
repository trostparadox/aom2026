Session overview

This symposium advances an agentic view of status dynamics, highlighting how individuals actively shape, negotiate, and recalibrate status relations across organizational and social contexts. The four papers examine distinct yet complementary forms of agentic engagement with status. One paper shows how low-status actors can initiate upward mobility by communicating unconditional respect, clarifying when status advancement becomes possible. A second paper demonstrates how declining a promotion can function as a counterintuitive status signal under specific conditions. A third paper examines rivalry as a competitive context, revealing how asymmetries in perceived rivalry threaten status and provoke incivility with consequences for well-being. The final paper reconceptualizes status inconsistency as a motivational force, illustrating how misaligned status signals can redirect individuals’ engagement with status hierarchies.
