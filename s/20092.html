Session overview

Relationships are foundational to work, influencing everything from how work feels to how work gets done. Yet, the relational architecture underpinning organizational relationships is undergoing a significant transformation. Digitally mediated internal communication tools are increasingly playing a central role in how employees connect with one another and their organization. These shifts raise critical questions for scholars about how high-quality workplace relationships differ in a world of work that features less face-to-face interaction but more accessibility and visibility. This panel will re-examine relational assumptions in a changing world of work, define strategies for cultivating high-quality workplace relationships through digital communication, and explore how organizations and employees must adapt to digitally-mediated work environments.
