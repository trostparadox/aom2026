Session overview

Abstract: The overarching goal of the Behind the Scenes PDW is to facilitate conversations, knowledge sharing, and connections among scholars interested in investigating social phenomena in organizational settings using immersive techniques. Planning, executing, and publishing research using immersive methods such as ethnography can be daunting and elusive. With this challenge in mind, our PDW aims to highlight processes and practices that lead to successful completion of ethnographic and immersive research endeavors that result in interesting and highly cited publications. This year, we revisit the core issue that jumpstarted the initial creation of our PDW: unwritten challenges. While many methods texts and sources exist, many of the challenges facing scholars remain unwritten and unpublished; largely shared orally through networks, conversations, and advisory relationships. We will dig into some of those challenges and explore how they change over time as scholars progress through their academic career. Our goal is to better equip and assure ethnographic scholars, especially those who are new to conducting or supervising organizational ethnographies. Thus, our PDW will address the broad question, ‘what challenges and experiences do scholars face that remain unwritten?’ and how do scholars cope with and overcome these challenges and experiences? The PDW will be structured into three interactive segments, providing participants with the opportunity to hear from and engage in conversations with our panelists and fellow participants. First is a panel discussion, during which panelists will reflect on their experiences. Second is a moderated panel discussion addressing questions submitted by workshop participants. Third, are themed roundtable discussions allowing small groups of participants to discuss their own work with an expert panelist. The PDW covers both traditional and new methods (such as team and digital ethnography) and addresses issues related to training and supporting young scholars in applying various ethnographic methods in their research. Given the increasing interest in qualitative and mixed-method approaches, the PDW will be of great interest to PhD students, junior scholars, and senior members of the field interested in immersive research methods or whose team members or supervisees require support for their ethnographic research.
