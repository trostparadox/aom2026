Session overview

Business schools are increasingly expected to address grand challenges that are systemic, cross-sectoral, and global, yet they remain embedded in institutional systems that constrain change. This panel symposium explores what systems-level leadership looks like in business schools and how it can be practiced and studied more effectively. Bringing together deans and scholars of organizations, strategy, and societal impact, the session connects lived leadership experience with theory on institutional change, complexity, and transformation. Through a moderated dialogue, panelists will surface practical insights for academic leaders navigating rankings, accreditation, and incentive systems, while identifying new research opportunities at the intersection of management scholarship and leadership practice.
