Session overview

In response to the pressing grand challenges facing contemporary organizations and the Academy of Management’s call to elevate purpose-driven, interdisciplinary scholarship, this cross-divisional symposium positions the Management Consulting (MC) Division as a vibrant, integrative hub at the crossroads of theory and practice. The session explores consulting as a shared intellectual and practical domain across the Academy, highlighting its potential to bridge disciplinary boundaries. Attendees will depart with an interdisciplinary practice map as a guide to the core consultative approaches across management fields and insights into their combined application. This consulting practice palette serves as a framework for selecting and blending diverse disciplinary perspectives to address complex organizational challenges. Scholars from multiple divisions— including for example, Diversity, Equity and Inclusion, Careers, Human Resources, Entrepreneurship, Organizational Development and Change, and Management and Organizational Cognition –will engage in dialogue about how their respective fields conceptualize consulting as a process of change, learning, and transformation. The symposium invites reflection on the diverse ways consulting manifests across management subfields—from facilitating sensemaking and inclusion to advancing systems redesign, organizational learning, international development, and technology-driven innovation—and considers how these perspectives might meaningfully inform one another. By fostering interdisciplinary exchange, the session seeks to surface shared theoretical foundations, illuminate different methodological traditions, and identify opportunities for collaborative research and practice. In doing so, it positions the MC Division as a central crossroads where diverse management perspectives come together to advance understanding of consulting as a mechanism for organizational and societal change. Unique to this session, the organizers will employ live sensemaking activities throughout the moderated panel discussion to demonstrate effective consultative skills – a true “show and tell” experience.
