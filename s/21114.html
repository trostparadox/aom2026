Evaluating the Effectiveness of Recommender Systems Explanations Across Purchase Funnel

Author: Chigozie Francis Obasi Temple University

Session overview

The use of explanation on ecommerce platforms has been ubiquitously aimed at enhancing customer trust in the platform and on the products recommended. Explanations come in various types, and most of the time, these explanations are short and concise. The purpose of this study is to analyze the effect of different explanation types (social, feature and no explanation type) on different customer stages in the purchase funnels (awareness, interest and desire) and how explanation length moderates these relations. This study will use a 3x3 factorial experiment design to be done on Amazon Mechanical Turk involving hypothetical tasks, purchase funnel stage characteristics and survey questions. Discussion of this study will center on the result of the regression analysis between effectiveness of explanation, explanation type, explanation length and customer purchase funnel stage.
