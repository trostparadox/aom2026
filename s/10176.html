Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces innovative, evidence-based approaches for developing leadership competencies through Virtual Reality (VR) simulations enhanced with AI-driven avatars. As traditional leadership workshops often fall short in replicating the emotional complexity and immediacy of real managerial situations, VR-based training environments enable learners to safely engage in authentic leadership conversations and decision-making scenarios. AI avatars dynamically respond to participants’ behaviors, model realistic communication patterns, and provide personalized, immediate feedback. Advances in affective AI and avatar realism further strengthen their perceived social credibility, deepening immersion and contributing to sustainable learning outcomes. This PDW highlights theoretical foundations, empirical evidence, and practical implementation strategies demonstrating how immersive VR can enhance motivation, emotional engagement, behavioral awareness, and self-efficacy. Participants will gain insights into pedagogical design, technology integration, and best practices relevant to business education and leadership development. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
