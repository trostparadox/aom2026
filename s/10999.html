Session overview

Despite significant advances in organizational change scholarship and sustained innovation in practice, research and practice continue to evolve largely in parallel rather than in productive dialogue. Scholars generate increasingly sophisticated theoretical and empirical insights, while practitioners confront escalating complexity driven by digitalization, artificial intelligence, and persistent uncertainty, often without timely or actionable pathways for translating research-informed knowledge into practice. This enduring separation constrains both the practical relevance of scholarship and the evolution of scientific knowledge capable of engaging contemporary organizational realities. This PDW represents the third iteration of a multi-year initiative, building on prior sessions at AOM 2024 (Chicago) and AOM 2025 (Copenhagen) that consolidated momentum and positioned the PDW as a continuing forum for shaping the organizational change agenda. For AOM 2026, it shifts focus toward participatory foresight, engaging scholars, practitioners, and educators in jointly imagining, shaping, and committing to the future of the field of organizational change. The PDW is designed as a future-oriented, dialogical space that supports collective sensemaking and forward-looking inquiry. Through structured interaction, participants will surface emerging tensions, question taken-for-granted assumptions, and articulate plausible futures that can inform research agendas, pedagogical approaches, and change practices. Importantly, the workshop emphasizes not only the exploration of alternative futures, but also the identification of concrete avenues for collaboration, experimentation, and impact that extend beyond the session itself. By foregrounding participatory foresight, co-creation, and commitment to action and measured impact, this PDW invites participants to move beyond observation toward active engagement in forging the future trajectory of organizational change.
