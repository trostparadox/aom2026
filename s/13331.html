Dissonance in Unity: How Stakeholder Disagreement Influences Firms' Climate Change Practice?

Author: Luman Yu The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Session overview

This study explores how stakeholder opinion divergence affects firms’ climate change practices after wildfires. Specifically, I argue that wildfires raise local residents’ climate change awareness, increasing collective action and stakeholder pressure on firms. However, divergent opinions weaken this effect by reducing collective action. Moreover, social trust, environmental NGO density, and lung cancer rates can mitigate this negative impact by lowering perceived costs or increasing perceived benefits of cooperation. This study aims to contribute to stakeholder theory by highlighting how opinion divergence within stakeholder groups influences firms’ climate change responses.
