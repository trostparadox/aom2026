Session overview

Many faculty members are invited, often unexpectedly, to consider senior academic administrative roles, such as program director, academic director, department chair, associate dean, and interim dean. These roles can be professionally rewarding and impactful, yet they also involve tradeoffs, steep learning curves, and career risks that are often poorly understood prior to entry. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed to provide candid, experience-based advice and education for faculty who are contemplating, entering, or currently serving in senior academic leadership roles. The session brings together four experienced academic administrators who have served in a wide range of administrative leadership roles at research-intensive, U.S.- based institutions. Through a structured panel discussion, interactive Q&A, and facilitated small-group breakouts, participants will gain practical insight into the nature of these roles, the skills and mindsets required for success, and strategies for navigating common challenges. Particular attention will be paid to decision-making before accepting an administrative role, priorities during the early stages of service, managing relationships with faculty and senior leadership, and planning for a successful transition back to the faculty after an administrative term. The PDW is intended for faculty at all career stages who are curious about academic leadership, have been asked to serve in an administrative role, or are already serving and seeking perspective and guidance.
