Session overview

Publishing management and organizational research has become increasingly difficult in an ever-changing field. Complexities such as intensified performance pressures, shifting journal rankings, evolving methodological expectations, and emerging technologies like generative AI are further amplified when authors mobilize history in their work. This PDW brings together editorial teams from key outlets that specialize in history including Business History, De Gruyter Brill (book series in organizational and management history), Industrielle / Industrial Relations, Journal of Management History, Management & Organizational History, and Qualitative Research in Organizations and Management. The purpose of this session is to demystify the publishing process for history-based research. Drawing on their collective editorial experience, panelists will go behind the scenes of the publishing process and offer candid guidance on what good historical research looks like, why manuscripts succeed or fail, and how authors can position their work for maximum impact. The workshop is explicitly developmental and interactive. Participants will have opportunities to raise questions and receive specific best practices to support their own historically informed research projects.
