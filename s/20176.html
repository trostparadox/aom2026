Session overview

This PDW will allow division members to participate in research discussions moderated by accomplished HR scholars, as well as network with other scholars researching similar topics. Attendees will have an option to participate in two 40-minute roundtable discussions from a variety of research areas. The tables research themes span a range of 15 HR topics: Alternative Work/Work Arrangements, Compensation, Diversity, Global HRM, HR Analytics, AI & Talent Management, HR Innovation & Entrepreneurship, Leadership & Followership, Legal Issues (safety, discrimination, harassment) & Stressors, Onboarding & Socialization, Organizational Culture & Climate, Performance Management (performance appraisal and feedback), Recruitment & Selection, Sex & Gendered Work Disparities, Strategic HR, and Turnover/Absenteeism/Withdrawal.
