Session overview

AI is fundamentally redefining the role of management consultants. As AI rapidly commoditizes analysis, diagnostics, and strategic recommendations, consultants can no longer rely on expertise and planning alone as primary sources of value. In AI-driven transformations, outcomes emerge iteratively, assumptions shift quickly, and implementation pathways must be continuously revised. Under these conditions, consulting work is moving from delivering strategic advice to leading change in real time. This Professional Development Workshop addresses this role shift and argues that change leadership has become a core consulting responsibility in an AI-accelerated world. Many AI initiatives fail not because strategy is absent or flawed, but because organizations struggle to absorb continuous change while navigating uncertainty, trust, ethical concerns, and capability constraints. Consultants are increasingly expected to support sensemaking, adapt engagement design, align stakeholders, build resilience to ongoing disruption and sustain momentum as plans evolve. This highly practical workshop draws on the new Edition of the Association of Change Management Professionals (ACMP) Standard for Change Management as a reference framework for strengthening consulting judgment—particularly in evaluating change impact, organizational readiness, and adaptive capacity in AI contexts. Participants will work with applied diagnostic questions, decision heuristics, and engagement design approaches that help consultants fit AI initiatives to organizational systems, constraints, and capabilities. Rather than teaching a prescriptive methodology, the session emphasizes consulting judgment in action. Participants will leave with concrete, immediately applicable tools and language to strengthen their change leadership role and sustain consulting credibility and impact as AI reshapes organizations and the consulting profession. This workshop is designed as an intensive, case-based session that actively engages participants in examining how consulting value is created and sustained in AI-driven initiatives. Participants work directly with realistic consulting challenges, applying structured analysis and guided discussion rather than relying on abstract models or prescriptive tools. The format promotes rigorous exchange among participants with diverse experience, enabling practical insight grounded in real consulting decisions. By focusing on how consultants exercise judgment to translate AI investments into measurable business benefits and organizational resilience, the workshop delivers high participant engagement and clear, immediate value for those involved in consulting practice, research, and education in an AI-accelerated environment.
