Session overview

A wonderful aspect of the Academy of Management (AOM) Annual Meeting is the opportunity it provides for scholars to reflect on and recalibrate their careers at key transition points. One such transition is the period immediately following the achievement of tenure. While tenure represents a major professional milestone, it is often accompanied by questions about long-term career direction, evolving definitions of success, and the burnout that can result from the sustained pressures of the pre-tenure years. To support scholars at this juncture, this PDW is designed specifically for scholars who have recently received tenure and are beginning to determine which paths they wish to pursue for the remainder of their careers.
