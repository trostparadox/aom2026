Session overview

Where do we find new ideas about history, memory and the past? In this plenary session we will discuss and debate the ideas of Early Career Researchers who are engaged with new and interesting topics work in the field of Management and Organizational History. Far too often there is an expectation that new research emerges from those who are already established in the field. Moreover, there is the perception that gatekeepers and the old guard limit theoretical conversations. To debunk this claim we showcase the cutting edge research of some of the top new researchers in our field. The goal is to create an environment where new research is encouraged, freely discussed and debated. In so doing we look to the future of the division to expand our understanding about history and the past.
