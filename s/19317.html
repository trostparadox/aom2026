The Immersed Manager: Collaboration Technology Embeddedness and Management Innovation in Teams

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 4:00PM–5:00PM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 1
CTO

People

Authors: Melody Jun Zhang The Hong Kong Polytechnic University; Yan Zhang Peking University; Yunyun Emily Yuan The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Session overview

Collaboration technologies (CT) increasingly constitute the context in which managerial work is enacted, shaping how managers interpret and perform their roles. While CT use is known to boost efficiency and team effectiveness, it remains unclear if and how it affects managerial behaviors. Drawing on structuration and role-making theories, we argue that as managers become deeply embedded in CT, they tend to expand their perceived role breadth, which in turn enables management innovation in their work teams. We further propose this role-expansion is strengthened by a high team learning orientation. A three-wave, multi-source study supports our theorizing. Our study opens the “black box” between CT use and management innovation, contributing a novel, role-based mechanism to the management innovation literature and extending CT research to higher-order managerial outcomes.
