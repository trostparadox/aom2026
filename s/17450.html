Session overview

Now in its 14th year, this PDW offers a practical, collaborative space for Academy members interested in teaching social networks and helping students understand how social relationships shape organizational life. This session brings together experienced instructors who will share concrete exercises, tools, and course designs that make social networks actionable and meaningful, showing how everyday connections influence individual and organizational outcomes. It will also introduce new teaching tools, including ways Generative AI can support the design of engaging, experience-based activities. Participants will leave with ready-to-use materials and clear ideas for teaching social networks across undergraduate, MBA, and executive programs, regardless of discipline.
