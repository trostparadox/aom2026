Session overview

o This session offers a welcoming introduction to Critical Management Studies (CMS), designed especially for early career academics and those who are CMS-curious or new to the field. Aiming to make CMS more accessible, we will provide an overview of its core themes, including power, equity, and social justice. The session brings together voices from across the CMS community - from early career scholars to established academics - highlighting the wide range of topics and scholarly work that shape the field. Through engaging discussion, participants will gain insight into how CMS challenges mainstream management thinking and creates space for alternative perspectives. Whether you are just beginning to explore CMS or looking to deepen your understanding, this session offers a supportive and inclusive environment to learn, connect, and be inspired.
