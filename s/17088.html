Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) will address the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business’s (AACSB) standard that requires business schools to generate positive societal impact. While many institutions rightly focus on sustainability research or specialized curricula, like corporate social responsibility, this session demonstrates how pedagogical approaches—specifically experiential learning—also support this standard by transforming business education into a vehicle for societal impact. The workshop is based on the Societal Impact through Experiential Learning (SITEL) framework. This typology categorizes activities based on whether they treat students as investments in future impact (building knowledge and skills), agents of current impact (applying skills to real-world issues), or beneficiaries (promoting equity and pathways out of poverty). Participants will use this framework to map their current teaching portfolios and identify gaps in classroom-based and community-based experiences. Designed as a practical masterclass, the 90- minute session guides participants through a rapid design activity to create or refine a societally relevant experiential activity. Attendees will engage in peer coaching to address common implementation barriers, such as faculty mindset, student buy-in, and logistics. Participants will leave with a completed design worksheet, concrete mitigation strategies implementation challenges, and membership in a virtual, peer-led support group to foster long-term adoption at their home institutions. This PDW is ideal for instructors, program directors, and academic leaders seeking practical tools to align their pedagogy with the United Nation’s Sustainable Development Goals, Principles for Responsible Management Education initiatives, and AACSB accreditation standards.
