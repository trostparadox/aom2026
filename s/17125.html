Session overview

The academic job market in management education is undergoing significant structural change. U.S. universities are preparing for the “enrollment cliff,” while policy shifts affecting international mobility and hiring are reshaping global talent flows. At the same time, business schools increasingly compete in an international labor market for faculty who can contribute to globally oriented research and teaching. Research shows that attracting scholars with international capabilities has become central to the internationalization strategies of business schools and to maintaining institutional competitiveness. This session provides job seekers with insights into these trends and offers practical strategies for navigating technology-driven change, global hiring dynamics, and evolving institutional priorities in management education.
