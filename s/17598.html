Mapping the Intellectual Landscape of Generative AI in Business:A Structural Topic Modeling Approach

Session overview

Since the introduction of ChatGPT, business and academic interest in generative AI has surged. Researchers also began exploring the capabilities of LLMs in various domains. The study applies structural topic modeling to 678 articles to map the thematic evolution of Gen AI in business research and we identified six latent topics: The Role of Gen AI in Strategic Entrepreneurship, Gen AI: Opportunities, Challenges, and Ethical Dilemmas, Gen AI and Marketing Strategies, Process LLMs: Optimizing Business Process Management, Financial Decision-Making Using Gen AI, and Leveraging ChatGPT for Sustainable Innovation. The result listed emergent keywords for each topic, and inter-topic correlations are examined to capture the semantic relationships among the topics. This study helps researchers gain a comprehensive understanding of the scholarly discourse on the Gen AI area and enables managers to strategically allocate resources toward emerging opportunities.
