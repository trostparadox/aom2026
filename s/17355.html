Session overview

Management research has evolved into a significant knowledge foundation for contemporary organizations. Scholars and practitioners recognized the value of creating and sharing knowledge and expertise to support management teams in a rapidly changing economic environment. In recent years, the collaboration of management professionals has been critically important in developing frameworks and assessment tools to address organizational challenges, including resource allocation, time management, risk assessment, strategy development, digital technologies, processes, policies, practices, organizational structures, techniques, leadership, and business models. This professional development workshop (PDW) demonstrates best practices in content creation for management innovation and organizational development, combined with hands-on production of studio vodcasts to disseminate scholarly and practical outcomes. Management scholars and practitioners will acquire valuable skills to design, produce, and sustain high-quality vodcasts that balance professional standards with academic rigor and ethical considerations. Participants will also engage in interactive discussions and examine techniques and methods of content creation to effectively share research outcomes with executives, educators, policymakers, and practitioners.
