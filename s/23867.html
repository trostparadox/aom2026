As the Leader, So the Led: Creative Leadership and Employee Creativity

OB
Si Li (Zhongnan University of Economics and Law); Shou Gao (Huazhong University of Science and Technology); Xiao Chen (Guangdong University of Foreign Studies)
Drawing on the job crafting theory, we developed and tested a theoretical model examining when and how creative leadership increases employees’ creativity through identifying the indirect effect of employees’ job crafting behaviors and the moderating effect of job uncertainty. Data collected from 274 supervisor-subordinate dyads in a three-wave, time-lagged design through the alumni networks of several companies in China confirm the proposed hypotheses, tested in a moderated mediation model. The results suggest that creative leadership is associated with more employee creativity via job crafting, particularly for employees facing lower level of job uncertainty. There also exists a moderated mediating effect. Implications of the model are described and future research directions are suggested.
