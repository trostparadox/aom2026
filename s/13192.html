Session overview

The rigor of research presented at the Academy of Management is often diminished by ineffective visual delivery. When slides are overcrowded with text and bullet points, the audience—comprising scholars, students, and practitioners—struggles to process and retain key insights. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed to transition participants from standard slide design to strategic visual communication. The session focuses on practical techniques that enhance the speaker's ability to transfer knowledge effectively and control the pacing of information. Key topics include the strategic use of white space, the adaptation of APA standards for visual media, and the application of Generative AI tools to streamline design workflows. Utilizing a "live reveal" pedagogical format, the facilitator will critique and redesign participant-submitted slides in real-time. This interactive approach demonstrates how specific design adjustments directly improve cognitive processing for the audience. The objective is to provide attendees with actionable skills to create presentations that are not only rigorous but also visually accessible, ensuring that complex data is communicated clearly and remembered accurately.
