Presenter: Pu Wang School of Economics and Management, Tongji University

Session overview

As AI spreads, resistance shifts from overt protests to hidden practices. This caucus explores how workers employ underuse, data manipulation, shallow compliance, and workarounds to negotiate control, identity, and legitimacy. We discuss these subtle tactics for reclaiming autonomy and agency within AI-enabled organizations.
