Session overview

This symposium examines social network agency as a dynamic cognitive and behavioral process. The session focuses on how individuals actively interpret, maintain, disengage from, and respond to changing workplace relationships. Across four papers, presenters explore how agentic network change shapes perspective taking, how coworkers’ turnover influences organizational commitment, how biased perceptions of one’s own network position affect attitudes and performance, and how deliberate tie dissolution constitutes an important form of network management. Collectively, the papers highlight network agency as situated and heterogeneous, revealing how individuals navigate relational change and its consequences for cognition, well-being, and organizational outcomes.
