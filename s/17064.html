Session overview

Data studies is gaining increasing attention as new data types, forms, and growing volumes introduce both new complexities and new opportunities for studying data-intensive phenomena. At the same time, scholars working within data studies often see data in different ways. This workshop brings these multiple perspectives together to ask what it means to study data as an object of inquiry from different angles. Continuing prior community building efforts and professional development workshops, the workshop highlights how different perspectives within data studies define data, what they examine, and what is evolving in their approaches. The workshop begins with brief position statements by speakers representing five perspectives in data studies, followed by breakout group discussions in which participants discuss their ongoing data research. A concluding panel synthesizes similarities and differences to tease out emerging theoretical insights and open questions for future data studies research.
