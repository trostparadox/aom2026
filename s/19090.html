Session overview

Management consulting faces paradoxes that technical solutions cannot resolve. Organizations demand AI transformation while experiencing digital exhaustion. Virtual collaboration promises connection yet delivers isolation. Family enterprises pursue innovation while protecting values algorithms cannot comprehend. What becomes irreplaceable about human judgment when machines analyze faster and at scale? This 90-minute workshop examines the intelligent paradox—the tension between algorithmic capability and human wisdom reshaping consulting practice. Scholars and practitioners explore four interconnected tensions through presentations and structured dialogue. Augmentation Paradox: As AI handles routine analysis, what constitutes irreplaceable consultant value? Connection Paradox: Virtual collaboration enables global consulting, yet trust formation operates through fundamentally different mechanisms online versus in-person. Innovation Paradox: Family businesses (70% of global enterprises) navigate AI through fundamentally different logic than public companies. Velocity Paradox: Organizations accelerate transformation while 70% fail—not from technical issues but human unreadiness, with 88% of heavy AI users experiencing elevated burnout. Focused presentations ground each paradox in research and practice. Rotating table discussions enable participants to tackle genuine dilemmas. This format serves scholars seeking new research directions, early-career academics identifying dissertation topics, and practitioners navigating these challenges daily.
