Session overview

Advancements in generative artificial intelligence, particularly large language models like ChatGPT, are rapidly reshaping the landscape of higher education. While many faculty members recognize the potential of AI to transform teaching, research, and service activities, few have structured guidance on how to implement these tools effectively and ethically. This professional development workshop is designed to equip workshop attendees (faculty, students, and/or practitioners) with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to integrate AI meaningfully into their work. Participants will engage in hands-on activities that introduce key concepts of prompt literacy, ethical considerations in AI use, and practical applications of generative tools for course design, student engagement, and scholarly tasks. Through real-world examples and collaborative exercises, attendees will explore how to enhance instructional innovation, streamline research tasks, and support administrative responsibilities using AI. Ethical and privacy issues will be addressed explicitly including the AI-generated text detection technologies (pros & cons) and institutional AI adoption guidelines, ensuring participants can make informed decisions about responsible AI adoption in their fields. By the end of the workshop, attendees will have a deeper understanding of generative AI’s capabilities and limitations, practical strategies that they can apply immediately in their teaching and research, and a network of colleagues with shared interests in AI innovation in management education.
