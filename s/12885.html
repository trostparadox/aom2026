Session overview

The purpose of our panel symposium is to stimulate the debate on endogenous shaping as a strategic approach. Distinguished scholars who have studied firms’ strategies aimed at shaping the business environment in which they operate, from diverse theoretical and empirical perspectives, will provide their insights on the current state of research on the topic. The discussion will acknowledge the substantial strides recent work has made in advancing theorization on endogenous shaping, while also drawing attention to remaining shortcomings, unresolved gaps, and ongoing fragmentation in the field. Bringing these distinguished scholars together creates an opportunity to collectively advance this emerging stream of research. In addition, the panelists will highlight promising avenues for future theoretical and empirical research that can further deepen our understanding of endogenous shaping and the co-evolution of firms and their environment.
