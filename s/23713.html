Does the Human Capital of BoP Entrepreneurs Affect Entrepreneurial Success?

DEI
Ye Zhang (Beijing University of Chemical Technology); Shanshan Chen (Beijing University of Chemical Technology)
From a human capital perspective, this paper examines the factors influencing entrepreneurial performance at the base of the pyramid (BoP). Using structural equation modeling, we empirically investigate the impact of BoP entrepreneurs’ human capital on their entrepreneurial performance and explore the potential moderating roles of online social capital and entrepreneurial training. The results indicate that human capital has a significant positive effect on the entrepreneurial performance of BoP entrepreneurs. Moreover, entrepreneurial training significantly strengthens this positive relationship. Online social capital also positively moderates the relationship between human capital and both individual and social entrepreneurial performance, but it does not significantly influence the relationship between human capital and corporate entrepreneurial performance.
