Session overview

Innovation is the driving force behind businesses’ technological and economic growth. Innovations contribute to economic development by creating demand for new products and by enhancing comfort and quality of life (Siwek, 2021). Enhancing employees’ skills and organizational capabilities supports the adoption of innovation strategies and management consulting practices. The innovativeness of enterprises is built on entrepreneurs’ ability to create and promote new products, services, and processes in the market. Moreover, the development of innovative activities contributes to the sustainable growth of jobs in developing and emerging enterprises (Siwek, 2021). Alshaer and Almarri (2024) and Al-Moaid and Almarhdi (2024) discussed how organizations should be flexible and adapt to a constantly changing environment by developing dynamic capabilities to support successful digital transformation projects. The design and allocation of technological, strategic, and cultural resources within organizations lay the foundation for deploying innovation. Human capital and organizational resources are essential components for constructing a strong innovation culture. Studies examining how culture influences innovation have gained attention in recent years (Fuad et al., 2022; Lousa˜ and M´onico, 2018; Tian et al., 2018). Jaskyte (2004) and Tushman and O’Reilly (1997) argue that cultural perspective is vital for understanding innovation. Furthermore, Danks et al. (2017) note that an innovative culture serves as an indicator of organizational innovativeness. Singun (2025) explains that the absence of a robust innovation culture can make it extremely difficult for companies to implement strategic changes. Moreover, a non-creative and unsupportive approach to innovation might decrease organizations’ capacity to develop specialized expertise and sustain their competitiveness in the market. Younis et al. (2023) emphasized the necessity of management to align innovation with organizations’ long-term strategy. A well-crafted strategy supports the development of an innovation culture and the efficient utilization of resources for the creation of new products, services, and processes. This symposium offers academics, practitioners, and policymakers an integrated perspective on how management consulting practices and strategies, digital technologies, and innovation culture interact to formulate innovation in organizations. By bridging theory and practice, the symposium proposes a foundation for organizations striving to develop resilient, future-ready innovation ecosystems (Younis et al., 2023).
