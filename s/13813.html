Session overview

Goals are a foundational concept in the strategic management of organizations. Today, as organizations navigate volatile environments, hybrid work, and algorithmic management, new questions and challenges are brought to the table for scholars studying goals in and around organizations. This PDW aims to reinvigorate this core topic by convening a panel of leading scholars. We will build upon the classics of Carnegie School of Organizational Studies to chart new theoretical territory and showcase frontier empirical methods and data. The overall goal of this PDW is to explore the future of goals in organizations, bridging foundational wisdom with contemporary organizational complexities. This two-and-a-half-hour PDW will follow a four-part format. In the first part, three leading scholars (Pino Audia, Songcui Hu, and Daniel Levinthal) will share high-level insights and perspectives on the theoretical developments on goals in and around organizations. The second part will showcase some research programs from emerging junior faculty (Amy Zhao-Ding, Valerie Caubergh, and Subrina Shen) who study goals in and around organizations employing frontier research methods and data. The third part will consist of a semi-structured panel discussion among the panelists and presenters. The last part will contain roundtable discussions that enable further interactions between the participants who submit brief descriptions of their research ideas related to the PDW theme and panelists/presenters. Anyone can attend the first three parts of the PDW.
