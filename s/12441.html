Session overview

This Conflict Management Division Junior Scholar Workshop is designed for early-career scholars – including PhD students, post-docs, and junior faculty - who seek to navigate their academic career and expand their professional networks. The workshop focuses on three key topics essential to launching and managing a successful academic career: (1) understanding the factors critical to an enduring academic career, (2) navigating intrapersonal challenges that undermine our success as scholars, and (3) successfully acquiring necessary resources in one’s job, such as managing your department chair and leveraging your network. The Professional Development Workshop (PDW) will be led by a diverse group of mid-career and senior scholars, each providing a unique perspective on these topics. The format will include practical exercises and multiple breakout sessions. Advance registration is strongly encouraged due to limited capacity.
