Location-Allocation Problem with Order Decoupling in MTS/MTO Supply Chains with Remanufacturing

Author: Iman Ghalehkhondabi Howard University

Session overview

Hybrid MTS/MTO strategies are widely used by researchers to address demand uncertainty in supply chains and manufacturing systems. This study develops a conceptual mathematical model for a hybrid manufacturing system with decisions regarding the location-allocation of plants in a manufacturing supply chain. The model, which is understudied, considers the possibility of remanufacturing a certain amount of products at the end of their product life. The locations of demand points are known, but stochastic programming is used to address uncertainty in the demand quantity at each point. The MTS/MTO strategy enables the supply chain to benefit from both strategies in terms of total cost. A sample numerical parameter description is provided to demonstrate the potential applicability of the proposed model.
