The Dance of Power and Fraud: Political Turnovers in Emerging Markets

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 7
SIM

People

Authors: XUAN LI Hong Kong Shue Yan University; Chaowen Deng Fudan University

Session overview

Drawing on agency theory, this study examines how impending political turnovers influence the severity of organizational financial fraud. We tested our models in a sample of 3,520 bank-year observations from 502 China’s local commercial banks during the period of 2008-2020. We find that impending political turnovers positively affect banks’ financial fraud severity, driven by politically motivated assertive lending. We further find that the positive impact of impending political turnovers on banks’ financial fraud varies in the states of local politicians, including local politicians’ performance deviations and political contestability.
