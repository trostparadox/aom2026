Session overview

This symposium examines a place-based perspective on management, noting that understanding a city’s unique context can unlock strategies to enhance freedom, sustainability, and growth. There is renewed scholarly research on the role of place in management and organizational design (Wright et al., 2023). Given the prevalence of globalization and interconnected markets, cities are not just backdrops for economic activity. They actively shape the behaviors, values, and strategies of organizations. Institutional theory (e.g., North, 1990; Scott, 2001) holds that local formal institutions, such as city laws and regulations, and informal institutions, like community expectations or historical precedents, create an “institutional environment” that organizations must navigate. The 86th Annual Meeting of the Academy of Management (AOM) takes place in Philadelphia. Philadelphia, therefore, presents a rich case study for the symposium. Philadelphia’s history equips it with several enduring assets and lessons that inform its current management and design strategies. The city’s approach to management and organizational design is deeply influenced by its sense of place.
