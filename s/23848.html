We Were A Family Until We Weren’t: Understanding Psychological Contract Violation Through Social Med

OB
Namita Mangla (ICFAI Business School, Gurgaon); Anamika Sinha (Goa Institute of Management)
Mass layoffs in the technology sector have triggered widespread employee expressions on LinkedIn, offering a unique window into perceptions of psychological contract violation (PCV), coping, and identity reconstruction.We analyzed 1,540 English-language LinkedIn posts collected via Octoparse between February 2022 and November 2023. A mixed text-analytic approach combined lexicon-based sentiment scoring (Pattern, Python) with qualitative thematic deep-dives and interpretation through Constructivist Self-Development Theory (CSDT) and Social Identity Theory (SIT). Results show that 61% of posts carried negative sentiment, 27% mixed, and 12% positive. Fear emerged as the dominant negative emotion and increased over the study period. Coping narratives were primarily socialized, with smaller but meaningful sets of self-authored and self-transforming reframings. We integrate PCV, CSDT, and SIT to show how digital peer networks both amplify feelings of breach and provide resources for identity reconstruction.
