Session overview

Students can use AI to move faster than ever from problem to pitch - yet wicked problems, sustainability demands, and ethical consequences require slower, and better reasoning. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) introduces AI-enabled pedagogy that improves rigor, responsibility, and real-world readiness to both entrepreneurship education and entrepreneurial practice. The session examines how AI tools can be integrated into entrepreneurship courses and real-world entrepreneurial contexts to support experiential learning, creativity, and effective decision making. Participants engage in hands-on activities to experience how AI-supported tools can enhance ideation, opportunity evaluation, strategic thinking, and learning outcomes. The workshop includes a condensed experiential exercise, discussion of concrete course integration strategies, and exploration of how AI can be used effectively and ethically to support entrepreneurial thinking and strategy development in both classroom and applied settings. Participants will leave with practical tools and methods they can implement in their classes and use for research. Organized by Dr. Tamara Stenn and Athina Skiadopoulou, the PDW features an expert panel of educators actively integrating AI into entrepreneurship education and practice, including Dr. Tamara Stenn (Suffolk University), Dr. Craig Armstrong (The University of Alabama), Dr. Audra Quinn (USC Marshall), and Dr. David Stehlik (University of Saint Francis).
