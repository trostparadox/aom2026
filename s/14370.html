Session overview

This PDW explores how generative AI and prompt data are transforming the study of organizations, work, and social behavior. As generative AI becomes embedded in work and social life, prompts—the textual interactions between humans and AI—offer both a new source of behavioral data and novel methodological tools for experimental research. By treating prompts as data, method, and phenomenon, this session examines how they can illuminate the microfoundations of organizational life—how cognitive work, skill formation, and technology adoption unfold in real time. Prompt data create visibility into previously unobserved phenomena while also enabling new research designs where AI interactions serve as treatments or measurement instruments for studying persuasion, collaboration, and organizational behavior. Drawing on lessons from prior computational and methodological innovations, we consider how to build a productive research agenda around this dual opportunity. To advance this conversation, we bring together distinguished scholars whose work on computational methods, experimental design, and technology in organizations positions them to help navigate this moment. Following panel presentations, participants will join roundtable discussions designed for in-depth exploration of future research directions across organizational behavior, strategy, and computational social science.
