Session overview

Global mobility is expanding in scope and complexity, making international work an increasingly central feature of contemporary careers rather than a marginal or exceptional experience. As organizations and individuals engage in a growing range of global mobility forms, traditional typological approaches are no longer sufficient to explain career dynamics, outcomes, or responsibilities. This evolution highlights the need for a criteria-based perspective that focuses on when, how, and under what conditions mobility occurs, complementing existing mobility categories. Across six presentations, the symposium moves from individual mobility decisions through status dynamics, motivation and proactive career behavior to long-term outcomes and conceptual renewal, demonstrating that international careers are dynamic, context-sensitive, and unfold over time.
