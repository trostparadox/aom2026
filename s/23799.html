Spreading Green Behavior at Work: An Actor-Partner Interdependence Model of Motivation and Advocacy

OB
Xiaojing Shao; Moyang Gao (Northeast Agricultural University); ?? ?; Qianqian Zhang (Harbin Institute of Technology)
In consideration of the lack of understanding of crossover exploration of green behavior in employee-coworker binary relationships in the organization. Drawn on the actor-partner perspective, this research developed and tested a dyadic interpersonal model of green influence between employee and coworker. Using a two-wave employee-coworker dyads design, the research collected data of 126 pairs of 252 hospitality employees in China. The results demonstrated that green advocacy mediated the relationship between green motivation and green behavior, which revealed the green actor effect at the individual level. Moreover, employee green advocacy mediated the relationship between employee green motivation and coworker green behavior, which showed the green partner effect at the interpersonal level. Interpersonal conflict negatively moderated the impact of employee green advocacy on coworker green behavior. This study enriches the literature on green influence between employees and coworkers, and reveals the vita impact of interpersonal conflict in green management.
