Session overview

Systems thinking provides a complementary approach towards understanding the connections between interconnected parts and the wider systems within which they operate. This PDW aims to foster a conversation on how to embed systems approaches in management and organization theory, teaching, and engagement with practice, while particularly seeking to address complex societal issues. The purpose is to create a space to reflect upon and discuss fundamental questions, such as: How can systems methods and approaches shape our research, teaching, and forms of scholarly engagement beyond academia? How can scholars publish papers inspired by systems perspectives in management journals? This interactive PDW combines roundtable discussions with an editors’ panel. Thereby, it draws on the expertise of an invited set of established scholars at all career stages who are familiar with systems thinking methods and approaches to discuss the value of systems perspectives for advancing organization and management scholarship, as well as its implications for teaching and real-world impact. Further, there will be a debate on the role of systems thinking during theorization, as well as being an important research phenomenon on its own. Finally, we invite leading journal editors to reflect on future opportunities and potential traps for authors seeking to contribute to management and organization theory and methods with a systems perspective.
