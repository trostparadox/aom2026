Session overview

The city of Philadelphia and the 2026 Academy of Management provides a critical nexus for stakeholders including policymakers, academics, entrepreneurs, and ecosystem agents to bridge the research-practice gap. While the ENT community has long studied entrepreneurial ecosystem from multiple perspectives, this PDW facilitates a direct "Meeting of the Minds" to empower entrepreneurs and equip organizations for an increasingly complex future. Our Ecosystem Community is a diverse, global collective transcending traditional business school boundaries. This session serves as a high-energy platform where top policy experts, ecosystem builders, and entrepreneurship scholars engage in reciprocal learning. We move beyond passive listening to ask: What are the Big Burning Questions that define the next decade of ecosystem research? Format: The workshop begins with provocative lightning talks from diverse voices, followed by two hours of intensive breakout sessions designed for co-creation. We focus on vigorous, creative dialogue to shape the future of entrepreneurial communities, centering on the connectors who drive healthy ecosystems. We continue to experiment with a modified unconference approach where some of the tables will focus on specific themes, whether a country or policy arena. Impact: By bringing together 80+ proactive professionals in Philadelphia, we aim to spark the same intense, joyous collaborations that have, in past years, yielded high-impact publications and innovative cross-fertilized partnerships. Join us to co-create the innovative frameworks and partnerships necessary to advance the global entrepreneurial ecosystem.
