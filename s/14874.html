Session overview

The world of work is rapidly changing. New technological advances in areas such as artificial intelligence and robotics are reconfiguring and replacing work tasks, altering occupational jurisdictions, and even disrupting entire industries. Scholars of work and occupations have a long history of studying the impact of new technologies on work and organizations. How can an occupational lens today help us understand emergent technologies and their impact? And do new technologies also call for novel methodological or theoretical approaches? The goal of this professional development workshop is to bring together leading scholars with workshop participants in an interactive format to facilitate discussion and the sharing of new research findings, as well as insights from the past. An additional objective of this annual PDW – now in its sixth year – is to encourage community building among scholars engaged in theoretical and empirical research on occupations and professions. Through presentations from leading scholars, interactive panel discussions and roundtable breakouts, we seek to advance scholarship in these important areas while simultaneously building community.
