Session overview

We propose a Professional Development Workshop (PDW) to address the developmental gap faced by mid-career academics in the Organization and Management Theory (OMT) Division. Building on last year’s piloting effort, this workshop targets OMT faculty grappling with the complexities of post-tenure life, including expanded administrative duties, mentorship roles, and heightened research expectations. In total, the PDW is planned to take 2 hours divided into two blocs: 1) 90 minutes of formal programming, and 2) 30 minutes of informal networking. The formal program includes an introduction, panel discussions with six esteemed OMT scholars, and interactive roundtable sessions, focusing on career development, leadership, balancing multiple roles, research evolution, and different pathways to a meaningful and impactful career. This format provides a dynamic environment for mid-career faculty to gain practical insights, forge peer connections, and learn strategies for navigating this demanding phase. The workshop also includes a second, more informal segment to facilitate networking, idea development, extended discussions, and community building among mid-career participants and alumni, panelists, and senior scholars, reinforcing a culture of continuous learning within the OMT Division.
