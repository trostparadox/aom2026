The Role of Strategic Agility in Organizational Capabilities for Rapid Transformation

Authors: Jovana Popovic University of Montenegro; Jovana Milic University of Montenegro

Session overview

In the contemporary business landscape, organizations are operating in highly demanding and dynamic environments that necessitate rapid adoption. Strategic agility – defined as the ability to seize, sense and reconfigure resources – is increasingly recognized as a critical factor for successful organizational transformation. However, extant empirical evidence on which organizational capabilities enable such agility remains limited. This study aims to address this gap by investigating a single organization undergoing a systematization process. A qualitative case study approach was utilized, entailing the conduction of ten semi-structured interviews with managers and team leaders involved in the transformation process. The collected data were then subjected to thematic analysis in order to identify the key organizational capabilities that enable strategic agility. Preliminary findings indicate several critical capabilities for strategic agility, including leadership alignment, expeditious decision-making, cross-functional collaboration, digital infrastructure, and cultural readiness. The aforementioned capabilities interact in a manner that facilitates rapid adaptation and transformation. This interaction offers both theoretical insights and practical guidance for managers who aim to enhance agility within their organization. The study contributes to the strategic agility literature by offering empirical evidence of organizational capabilities in action and informs practice on how firms can successfully navigate rapid transformations.
