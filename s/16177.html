Session overview

While the U.S. political landscape has historically been divided into a two-party system, ideological divisions have become markedly more salient in recent years. Within this context, research on political differences is a relevant and timely topic. This symposium brings together multiple perspectives to explore how employees’ political identities influence decision-making and organizational dynamics. We will also discuss strategies for managing political diversity in the workplace, highlighting implications for human resource practices, conflicts, and managerial management. By integrating theoretical insights with empirical findings, this symposium aims to advance understanding of the complex interplay between political identity and employee outcomes, offering actionable guidance for scholars and practitioners navigating increasingly politically diverse workplaces.
