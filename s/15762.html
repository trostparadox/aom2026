Session overview

This symposium brings together ethnographers from a variety of backgrounds to reflect on Howard Becker’s 1998 foundational book Tricks of the Trade. The goal of the symposium is to discuss what about the book remains enduringly useful, and what may need to be updated or reimagined in light of modern forms of organizing and fieldwork. In particular, we consider how four tricks—imagery, sampling, concepts and logic—have traditionally been applied in ethnographic research and how they can be challenged or extended in the context of virtual and hybrid organizations and new forms of ethnography (such as virtual ethnography). Through this symposium, we aim to encourage ethnographers’ forward-looking application of a foundational text.
