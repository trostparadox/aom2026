The Victim Provocation of Experiencing CWBs: A Replication and Extension of Islam and Bowling (2022)

Author: Md Rashedul Islam Valdosta State University

Session overview

Curmudgeon personality refers to the disposition of disliking both positive and negative stimuli. Research found that employees with a higher level of curmudgeon personality and a higher level of extraversion or emotional expressiveness engage in victim provocation, which leads experiencing more workplace incivility from their coworkers and supervisors (Islam & Bowling, 2022). To examine whether this finding is replicable using a cross-cultural sample (i.e. a UK sample) and the research could be extended cross-culturally to another form of counterproductive work behaviors (CWBs; i.e., social undermining at work), two samples were collected from the UK (N = 321) and US (N = 324). Hierarchical regression analyses provided mixed results: no support for the replication; however, the relationship between curmudgeon personality and social undermining from supervisors was moderated by emotional expressiveness only in the US sample. Explanation, theoretical contributions, and future research have been discussed.
