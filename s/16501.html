Session overview

Now in its third edition, this PDW builds on the momentum of the previous two highly successful sessions to foster and deepen scholarly conversations around important strategic themes in the biopharmaceutical and healthcare sectors that are of interest to a broad group of scholars at the academy, as well as practitioners and policymakers. Our goal is to discuss cutting-edge research ideas and how scholars studying these questions can make meaningful contributions, bridging across management, economics, and sociology. The PDW aims to serve as a platform for scholars to build a shared understanding of research in the biopharmaceutical and healthcare industries. It also offers valuable insights on how emerging researchers can apply innovative theories and methods to shape future studies. By continuing to bring researchers together across institutions and career stages, the PDW seeks to strengthen this emerging community and stimulate collaborative, high-impact scholarship. This 2.5-hour PDW will follow a three-part format. In the first part, six distinguished scholars will share insights on important aspects of research in the biopharmaceutical and healthcare sectors. The second part will consist of a semi-structured panel discussion among the presenters. The final part will feature roundtable discussions around different topics and methods. Prospective participants will be invited to apply by submitting a brief description of a research idea they would like to explore with discussants and attendees.
