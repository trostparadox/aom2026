Session overview

Business educators face the challenge of developing students' empathic capabilities while ensuring these capabilities are channeled through conscious, ethical frameworks rather than mindless emotional reactions. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) presents a comprehensive approach to cultivating conscious compassion in business students through the application of the Ethical Lens Inventory framework (Baird, 2013). We will begin with an exploration of the distinctions between empathy, sympathy, and compassion, examining how unconscious biases can lead to "selfish empathy" that distorts judgment (Breithaupt, 2019). Next, participants will engage with the Ethical Lens Inventory to identify their own ethical orientations and blind spots (Baird, 2013). Finally, we will facilitate experiential exercises using business case scenarios that require participants to navigate ethical dilemmas while balancing multiple stakeholder perspectives. The common thread throughout these activities is learning to transform instinctive empathic reactions into deliberate, ethically-grounded compassionate action that serves the well-being of diverse stakeholders in business contexts.
