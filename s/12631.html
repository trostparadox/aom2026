Session overview

This panel symposium proposes a medical-grade reframing of management consulting through intervention-research, organized around the integrative concept of Adjusted Prophylaxis. The core premise is simple: just as modern medicine progressed by moving from persuasive remedies to disciplined clinical protocols—prevention, early detection, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up—management consulting can strengthen its scientific credibility and practical responsibility by adopting analogous standards adapted to organizational complexity. Rather than treating organizations with generic “best practices,” Adjusted Prophylaxis emphasizes context-sensitive prevention and relapse avoidance, recognizing that organizational dysfunctions present differently across governance structures, professions, and institutional constraints.
