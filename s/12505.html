Session overview

This symposium brings together four papers that examine developmental relationships (mentoring, sponsorship, peer coaching, and leadership coaching) across career stages. The papers highlight how these developmental relationships operate through different mechanisms and relational dynamics to shape leadership development, relationship quality, and career outcomes, particularly during periods of career transition and uncertainty. Together, the symposium offers a concise, integrative view of how developmental relationships influence leadership and careers over time.
