Presenter: Michael J. Alleruzzo Carnegie Mellon University

Session overview

College professors face unique workplace challenges. These have been exponentially exacerbated since 2020. This session will focus on a 7-step framework for college professors to build their resilience in the face of their current academic and professional challenges. Participants will analyze what they can do to navigate through and around these challenges in healthy ways. Engagement activities will include a faculty challenge audit tool, structured reflection, and participant discussion.
