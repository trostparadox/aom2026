From HR System Strength to OKR Implementation Climate.

STR
Natalia Startseva (Universität Innsbruck)
Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) is a framework that is used by practitioners to align teams on strategy execution in day-to-day work, however the mechanisms that keep that bridge in place are under researched. This paper develops a notion of OKR implementation climate (shared perceptions that OKR use is expected, supported and rewarded). The argument builds on strategic HRM and shows how five practice bundles (governance and compliance, enablement, social infrastructure, performance and rewards, transparency and accountability) interact with HR system strength (distinctiveness, consistency and consensus) to shape that climate. The paper explains how climate is being implemented by using transparency, goal clarity, commitment and focus, cadence discipline, and learning loops in order improve adoption depth, alignment and cross-team coordination. The paper positions HR system strength as the signal architecture behind OKR climate, develops testable propositions and outlines a research agenda. For practitioners, the claims can be transferred into a successful OKR implementation.
