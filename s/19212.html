Strategic Timing in the Shadow of Giants: Entry Decisions of Indie Developers on Steam

Authors: Zhongning Xu The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Han Jiang The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen; Xufei Ma The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Yue Zhao Peking University

Session overview

This study investigates how low-visibility (indie) developers on digital platforms strategically time their product releases in the presence of high-visibility (AAA) competitors. Using data from the Steam platform, we examine the interplay between product similarity and entry timing decisions. Our results reveal a strategic paradox: while high product similarity encourages indies to release after major competitors to capture market spillover, it simultaneously discourages release delays to avoid signaling product inferiority. These findings highlight the complex trade-off between leveraging market attention and managing reputational signals in hybrid market structures.
