Session overview

This workshop builds on the well-attended and successful session at the AoM in Copenhagen in 2025 during which we created a dynamic platform for scholars to explore cutting-edge research on legitimacy, emphasizing theoretical robustness, methodological rigor, and pathways for future inquiry. This year’s PDW begins with an introduction by the organizers, followed by discussant presentations showcasing notable recent findings and methodological advancements. Interactive thematic roundtable sessions, facilitated by leading scholars, will address key challenges in conceptualization, methodology, and publishing. A panel discussion will synthesize insights from the roundtables, highlighting emerging trends and future directions. Dedicated networking opportunities will foster connections and collaborations. Post-event initiatives, including follow-up summaries, online “Legitimacy Cafes,” and a dedicated LinkedIn community, will ensure ongoing dialogue and global engagement, building a lasting network of legitimacy researchers.
