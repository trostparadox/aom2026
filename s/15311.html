Session overview

The ENT Late Career Consortium empowers seasoned academics to explore new horizons, share expertise, and lead with lasting impact. Designed for faculty 15+ years post-PhD, this forum fosters collaboration, innovation, and reinvention across research, teaching, leadership, and life beyond tenure. This interactive forum brings together accomplished scholars to examine opportunities for leadership, innovation, and legacy. Through dynamic sessions, candid discussions, and peer-to-peer exchange, participants gain practical insights on topics such as: ? Expanding research impact and exploring new publishing avenues. ? Assuming administrative and leadership roles within and beyond the academy. ? Consulting, mentoring, and contributing to the broader academic community. ? Planning for retirement and engaging meaningfully post-retirement. Join us at the Late Career Consortium during the AOM Annual Meeting to connect with peers, share experiences, and discover how decades of expertise can open new opportunities.
