Session overview

Educating for systems thinking requires more than transmitting concepts like feedback loops, boundaries, and emergence. It requires building students' capacity to stay with complexity long enough for systemic patterns to become visible. This staying with is embodied and affective: when students encounter material that destabilizes their prior frames, they often exit complexity through premature closure, abstraction, or withdrawal before insight can emerge. This Masterclass offers educators a framework for embodied systems thinking grounded in learning science. Participants will develop the capacity to design learning sequences that build tolerance for uncertainty, diagnose signals that students are exiting complexity, and facilitate debriefs that connect affective responses to systems concepts. The session combines conceptual input with interactive application to participants' own teaching practices. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
