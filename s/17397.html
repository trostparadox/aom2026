Session overview

The research comprising this presenter symposium seeks to understand the complexity of how emotions, work relationships, and social context constitute one another by leveraging a social functions of emotions perspective. In doing so, the research tackles dynamics at the individual-, dyadic-, and group-level of analysis. The collection of papers reflect theoretical treatments and empirical research that employs a combination of qualitative and quantitative methods in the field and lab.
