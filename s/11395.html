Session overview

This workshop addresses AI-driven gaps in career readiness by integrating problem-based learning (PBL) into management education. It responds to employers’ growing concerns about graduates’ soft skills, particularly problem solving, teamwork, communication, ethical judgment, and critical thinking in AI-intensive workplaces. Participants experience an AI-infused PBL activity from the student perspective, interrogating flawed AI recommendations embedded in realistic managerial scenarios and collaboratively developing justified action plans. The session then debriefs design principles for making AI a catalyst for critique rather than an unquestioned authority, aligning scenarios and prompts with career readiness competencies, and ensuring inclusive, psychologically safe discussion. In the final segment, participants co-design adaptable PBL mini-modules for their own courses, specifying AI use cases, focal skill gaps, facilitation moves, and assessment strategies. The workshop’s impact and reach are extended through structured evaluation and resource packs. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
