Session overview

Recent management research has increasingly drawn attention to the growing role of Artificial Intelligence as both a predictive and a generative methodological tool. Although recent developments in AI show unprecedented progress, there is still considerable uncertainty about the potential avenues and conditions under which AI-based methods should be incorporated into standard research practice. The goal of this session is to introduce a wide and promising set of potential new avenues for using AI in management research, both in terms of how it could advance management theories and methodological practices. After introducing these possibilities, we address the boundary conditions and best practices for using AI tools in academic research. Specifically, we address the fundamental questions surrounding the use of AI in academic research, including how AI can augment existing measurement approaches, improve statistical inference, and support rigorous theorizing and experimentation. We invite scholars who have pioneered the incorporation of AI into their work to reflect on their key insights and experiences and to offer critical perspectives on the opportunities and challenges that lie ahead.
