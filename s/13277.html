Session overview

Featuring leading disability employment scholars from the University of Illinois Chicago, Syracuse University, and Rutgers University, this symposium will explore how expert researchers are framing work around the employment of people with disabilities in a changing landscape, what work is being done in this space, and what opportunities exist for researchers and organizational practitioners in this unique time. The landscape of disability and inclusion workplace research is shifting, with new organizational, legal, and institutional conditions, which creates an important opportunity to better understand the current state of the field and the future direction of research focused on people with disabilities in the workplace. The goal of this symposium is to engage leading scholars in disability and work in a moderated conversation that (1) synthesizes current empirical knowledge on disability and employment, (2) examines methodological, conceptual, and practice barriers that are shaping the field, (3) considers how researchers are framing their work to enable translation and utility across varied organizational contexts, and (4) identifies promising future directions for research and partnership that can continue to move the field forward.
