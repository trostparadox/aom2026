Session overview

Futue of text analysis panel symposium examines the evolution of text analysis methods in management research, from traditional bag-of-words and supervised natural language processing (NLP) approaches to contemporary large language models (LLMs). Panelists discuss methodological trade-offs, researcher experiences, and practical decision-making when selecting NLP methods for different research purposes and domains. The discussion addresses issues of validity, reliability, bias, interpretability, and reproducibility, as well as the implications of open- versus closed-source language models. The session offers guidance for scholars navigating the rapidly changing landscape of text analysis in organizational research.
