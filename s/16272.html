Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) examines the evolving landscape of Generative AI (GenAI) research at the intersection of industry and academia. While the study of work and organizations has historically been driven by academic institutions, we are currently witnessing a profound paradigm shift: industry leaders such as Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are producing high-impact research at a velocity that often outpaces academia. This shift is fueled by an asymmetry in resources, including distinct incentive structures and exclusive access to proprietary data—assets largely unavailable to university-based researchers. How can academia remain relevant in this "new normal"? This PDW provides a collaborative forum where corporate and academic voices converge. By bringing together experts studying AI deployment within organizations, we aim to share and compare recent research from industry and academia while identifying the institutional barriers that hinder a more integrated, cross-sectoral research agenda. -
