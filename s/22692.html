Session overview

Reception for international researchers and scholars interested in the themes of teaching and research in Brazil, who wish to join a special gathering bringing together the Brazilian and international communities. The reception aims to promote the Brazilian Academy of Management and its partner programs, fostering meaningful international connections and creating an environment conducive to the exchange of knowledge and experiences among professionals and academics committed to advancing management research and practice on a global scale.
