The Role Of Meta Organizations In Developing And Implementing Regenerative Business Strategies

Session overview

Regeneration is a concept under sustainability that can help businesses tackle the rising matters in the society and the environment. This is different from traditional sustainability which seeks to minimize harm done to the environment. The main purpose of regenerative business is to develop a thriving environment and society. One of the key growing environmental issues is biodiversity loss. This affects systems like food, water, climate which contributes majorly to survival of both the environment and society. Thus, an indication about the need to ensure the conservation of biodiversity. In addition, implementation of a major systemic strategy such as this, is always done best with a coordinated effort, making the role of meta organizations crucial. Nevertheless, little is known about how meta-organizations contribute to the development and application of regenerative business strategies. This study seeks to explore how meta organizations can help businesses develop and implement effective regenerative strategies that can ensure biodiversity conservation and societal wellbeing. The research will employ the use of case studies and obtain data from 20-30 in-depth interviews with professionals engaged in meta organization and regenerative business efforts. The study’s conclusion outlines the implications for further studies on collaborative ways of organizing for the conservation of biodiversity and regeneration.
