Session overview

In an era of polycrisis-climate disruption, technological upheaval, and social fragmentation, management scholars and educators face unprecedented stress while guiding others through uncertainty. This interactive Professional Development Workshop introduces five evidence-based meditation techniques specifically designed for disrupted times. Participants will experience accessible practices including mindful breathing, body scan awareness, the RAIN technique for difficult emotions, loving-kindness meditation, and "turning toward" complexity. Each practice addresses distinct challenges: immediate stress relief, somatic awareness, emotional processing, cultivating connection, and building capacity for ambiguity. Attendees will leave with a personalized practice plan and resources for integrating these secular, research-supported techniques into their professional and personal lives.
