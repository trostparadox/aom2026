Session overview

There is a growing need for research that takes an HR-centered perspective and focuses on questions that matter for people management. This symposium responds to this need by bringing together five studies that examine how AI influences assessment, compensation, employee development, creativity, and candidate experience in AI-enabled interviews. The first theme highlights employees as active agents who learn from AI, adapt their work with AI support, and experience new forms of assessment and evaluation. The papers in this theme examine how employees seek different kinds of help from AI, how AI-supported problem-solving influences creativity, and how AI-based interview systems affect candidates’ emotional experiences and performance. The second theme focuses on employers and HR systems and investigates how AI changes traditional decision making in compensation and selection. These papers show how AI may reproduce or amplify gender disparities in salary recommendations, how AI-generated personality assessments compare with human evaluator judgments, and how transparency in AI interviews shapes fairness perceptions and behavioral responses. Together, these five studies demonstrate the value of examining AI from an HR perspective. They offer new theoretical insights and methodological innovations, including multimodal LLM assessment, field experiments, and large-scale human AI comparison designs. By integrating these findings, the symposium provides a clearer foundation for future work on AI and HRM.
