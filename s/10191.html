Synergy in Action: A Character Strengths Intervention Model For Teams

Author: Gayathri Janapati Mahindra University

Session overview

Effective teamwork is essential for achieving organisational goals in today’s collaborative and dispersed workplaces. Team members need to recognise and leverage each other’s strengths to adapt effectively to changes and challenges, a process known as team resilience. Research shows that a shared understanding of strengths and knowledge of tasks is essential for achieving productive outcomes, i.e., shared mental models. This study, using a narrative literature review, proposes an intervention-based model that employs character strengths to facilitate team roles, aiming to enhance team resilience and shared mental models. The intervention was designed to be easily adaptable to both established and new teams. The model, if proven effective, can provide implications for its use and integration into team development initiatives. The paper represents a novel attempt to highlight the value of leveraging character strengths to enhance team effectiveness. This equips teams to thrive in today’s ever-evolving work environments.
