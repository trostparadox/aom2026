Mediating Role of National TVET Expenditure Between Economic Uncertainty and Financial Inclusion

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 2
DEI

People

Authors: Muhammad Ali Asadullah University of Khorfakkan; Arslan Tariq Rana; Muhammad Ramzan Air University

Session overview

Abstract TVET is a promising vehicle to address economic issues but little research has examined how TVET contributes to financial inclusion during highly uncertain economic conditions. To develop a better understanding about national TVET investment, we applied public expenditure theory to analyze if national TVET expenditure improves financial including under the conditions of economic uncertainty. Using the data of 17 OECD economies from 2011 to 2022, we found statistical support for our hypotheses. In particular, our findings show a significant decrease in financial inclusion while a significant increase in TVET investment during highly uncertain economic conditions. The findings show a u-shaped relationship between TVET investment and financial inclusion. The findings also support a significant quadratic indirect effect of economic uncertainty on financial inclusion through national TVET expenditure. The findings offer interesting insights about the role of national TVET expenditure as an important tool for enhancing financial inclusion during uncertain economic conditions. Keywords: Economic Uncertainty, TVET expenditure, Financial Inclusion, Public Expenditure Theory, OECD.
