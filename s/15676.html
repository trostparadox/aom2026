Session overview

Hyper-individualism—the assumption that the self is confined to the physical body and thrives by maximizing its own interests—pervades Western management thought and practice. This Professional Development Workshop offers an alternative: understanding Oneness not as mystical transcendence but as an accessible, everyday phenomenon of interconnectedness cultivated through contemplative practice. Drawing on Daoist and Buddhist traditions, contemporary neuroscience, and educational research, this experiential workshop guides participants through meditation, reflective dialogue, and exercises that foster emotional intelligence, ultimately promoting experiences of Oneness. Participants will explore how cultivating awareness of deep interconnection can transform individual decision-making, organizational culture, and pedagogical practices. The intention is to expand our sense of interconnectedness by offering practical tools for moving beyond isolated individualism toward collective flourishing grounded in care, interdependence, and ethical responsibility.
