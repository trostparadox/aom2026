Unpacking the Complexity of Entrepreneurial Ecosystems Through Network Theory: A Systematic Review

ENT
Aziza Sellah (Cadi Ayyad University); Said Ouhadi (Cadi Ayyad University)
Entrepreneurial ecosystems are complex systems characterized by dynamic interactions among their components, with networks playing a central role in resource access and knowledge spillovers. Network theory has become an important framework for understanding the function, evolution and structure of entrepreneurial ecosystems. Despite the advancement made in ecosystem literature, it remains highly fragmented, lacking integrated theoretical and analytical perspectives. This study explores the insights of network theory in unpacking the complexity of entrepreneurial ecosystems, using a systematic methodology to analyze peer-reviewed literature. Findings highlight the contribution of network theory in four dimensions of ecosystem complexity which are: (1) ecosystem configuration, (2) ecosystem dynamic, (3) ecosystem inclusion and (4) ecosystem openness. Based on the findings, an integrative framework analyzing ecosystem complexity through network lens is proposed as well as a future research agenda.
