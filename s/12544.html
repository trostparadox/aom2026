Team Fit, Team Creativity, and Team Performance: A Moderated Mediation Model of Regulatory Focus

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 4:00PM–5:00PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 2
DEI

People

Authors: Jee Young Jeeny Seong Jeonbuk National University; Doo-Seung Hong Seoul National University

Session overview

Despite growing interest in person–environment fit at the collective level, little is known about when and how team fit in terms of skills, knowledge, and abilities (hereafter, collective ability fit) translates into superior team outcomes. Drawing on regulatory focus theory, we develop and test a moderated mediation model in which collective ability fit enhances team creativity, which in turn improves team performance, with team regulatory focus shaping the strength of these relationships. Using multi-source, time-lagged data from work teams, we find that team creativity mediates the relationship between collective ability fit and team performance. Moreover, this indirect effect is strengthened when teams exhibit a strong promotion focus but attenuated under a strong prevention focus. These findings advance fit theory by highlighting motivational contingencies at the team level and offer practical insights into aligning team composition and motivational orientation to foster creativity and performance.
