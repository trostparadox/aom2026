Session overview

This workshop provides a practical, experience-based exploration of technology use in negotiation and conflict management courses across diverse institutional contexts. Through live demonstrations and facilitated roundtable discussions, participants will engage with several classroom-tested teaching innovations, and leave with practical strategies, shared resources, and peer connections to support their pedagogical goals.
