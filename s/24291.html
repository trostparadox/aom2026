More Entry, Less Exit: How Artificial Intelligence Reshapes Corporate Scope

STR
Yuanyuan Chang (Zhejiang University); Feng Wan (Zhejiang University)
Strategic change in corporate scope has long posed a dilemma: constrained by non-scale free resources, entering new markets often requires firms to exit existing markets for resources redeployment. Artificial intelligence (AI) offers an opportunity to mitigate this trade-off. Drawing on the resource-based view (RBV), we propose that AI enhances a firm’s capacity for growth — complementing as a scale free resource, and substituting for non-scale free resources. Thus, AI-oriented firms can enter more markets with fewer exits. Using a panel of 1,568 U.S. manufacturing firms from 2012 to 2023, we find AI orientation increases market entries and decreases exits. These effects are stronger for firms with lower production capacity and higher managerial ownership, highlighting the roles of capability and governance in AI-driven scope expansion.
