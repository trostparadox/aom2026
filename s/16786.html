Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) aims to help scholars publish responsible, relevant, and high-impact research in leading journals within the Technology and Innovation Management (TIM) field. The workshop will bring together journal editors and expert researchers to discuss how to balance rigor, relevance, and responsibility in academic publishing. Through interactive discussions and hands-on feedback, participants will gain insights into how to design research that contributes to both scholarly advancement and societal well-being, aligning with contemporary movements such as responsible innovation and sustainability-oriented innovation research. The PDW will provide practical guidance on navigating the review process, selecting appropriate journals, and framing impactful research questions that resonate with editors, reviewers, and readers alike.
