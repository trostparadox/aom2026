Session overview

The first ten minutes of a class session set the tone for the entire learning experience. This Professional Development Workshop equips educators with practical, research-informed strategies to establish trust, psychological safety, and a sense of belonging from the moment students walk into the room. Drawing from communication theory, inclusive pedagogy, and leadership practices, this session demonstrates how small, intentional actions, modeling vulnerability, activating student voice, and reducing performance anxiety can dramatically improve engagement and learning outcomes. Participants will explore replicable techniques for both in-person and online environments. These practice micro-interventions foster connection and help you learn to create classroom openings that support diverse learners, including first-generation, multilingual, and anxious students. By the end of the workshop, attendees will leave with a toolkit of actionable approaches they can implement immediately to cultivate a supportive, high-trust learning climate that enhances participation, confidence, and academic success.
