Session overview

CEO celebrity is a powerful social approval asset, conferring visibility, symbolic value, and influence beyond firm performance. Yet today’s polarized and fragmented media environment has transformed the nature, durability, and consequences of this form of social evaluation. This symposium brings together leading scholars to examine how polarized audiences, ideologically segmented media systems, and emotionally charged narratives are reshaping CEO celebrity and its impact on organizational outcomes. By examining CEO celebrity in the context of polarization, we aim to spark conversation about how organizations manage symbolic leadership assets in today’s polarized and fragmented social landscape
