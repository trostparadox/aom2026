Session overview

Wicked problems in consulting combine contested problem definitions, non-linear causality, and strong political and temporal constraints. This PDW introduces Systemic Organizational Constellations (SOC) and Structural Constellations (SySt® (Sparrer, 2007)) as a heuristic diagnostic and problem-structuring device for complexity reduction. Grounded in Weick’s (1995) sensemaking-as-enactment, participants learn a replicable six-element Problem-to-Task syntax (Focus/Client, Goal, Obstacle, Resource, Preservation Function/“Hidden Gain”, Task). The spatial configuration makes implicit structures—role order, informal authority, exclusions—discussable and supports the emergence of actionable abductive hypotheses about leverage points and sequencing. The explicit Task element is a minimal viable next step that tests the new configuration through action, helping reduce the implementation gap. The workshop combines a short fishbowl demonstration with tabletop triad practice (Client/Consultant/Observer).
