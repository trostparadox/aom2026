Session overview

Publishing healthcare management scholarship in premier healthcare management (HCM) and management journals remains a key pathway to success for tenure and promotion, and can build one’s credibility as a domain expert. Moreover, dissemination of scientific findings through peer-reviewed publications can benefit HCM scholarship through broadening the reach and translation of findings into practice. While many challenges related to publishing remain constant, such as finding the right journal fit, framing the paper, and navigating peer reviews, emergent issues raise new concerns for both scholars and publishers alike. For example, the rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence (AI) presents new opportunities and challenges for both authors and journal editors. This session will provide an opportunity for scholars across the career spectrum to engage with journal editors from leading journals in HCM (i.e., Medical Care Research and Review, Health Care Management Review, Journal of Healthcare Management) and management (i.e., Academy of Management Journal) to discuss these challenges and emergent opportunities. The session will be designed as a moderated panel to probe journal editors regarding best practices for publishing in their journals. The session will also include time for questions and answers with the panelists. Scholars of all career stages are welcome to attend this interactive PDW, which is proposed as an in-person session; however, the organizers will work with AOM HCM leadership to modify as needed if preferences are for hybrid and/ or an emergent scholar focus.
