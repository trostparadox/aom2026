Session overview

We investigate the dark side of the plaza language narrative imprinting white-collar workforce and devaluating their labor within the collective memory of the society. We employed an interpretative method of discourse analysis on a case study to understand the semantics of the plaza language criticism and how the social distance between white-collar workers and blue-collar workers is reflected in collective memories. In our case study, we picked an episode of one of Turkish television's most-viewed comedy shows. The name of the show is called “Güldür güldür” and the Episode is called “Plaza Dili” (The Plaza Language). Studying this case is an attempt to show the narratives and perceptions about business life in collective memories.
