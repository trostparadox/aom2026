Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) focuses on teaching sustainability for practice across different educational audiences, recognizing that learners at distinct career stages face different motivations, constraints and learning objectives. While sustainability has become a core topic in management education, pedagogical approaches that are effective in one setting often fail to translate across undergraduate, graduate, doctoral and executive education contexts. The workshop brings together four practice-oriented teaching perspectives, each corresponding to a specific audience: undergraduate, graduate, doctoral and executive learners. Rather than presenting research papers, contributors will share concrete teaching strategies, course designs and instructional practices that have proven effective in preparing learners to engage with sustainability-related decisions in real-world organizational settings. Particular attention will be paid to how educators adapt content, pedagogy and assessment to align with learners’ professional experience, time constraints and decision-making responsibilities. The PDW is designed to be highly interactive. Short, focused interventions will be followed by facilitated discussions and participant breakouts, allowing attendees to reflect on their own teaching contexts and to identify transferable principles across educational levels. By the end of the workshop, participants will have a clearer understanding of how to design sustainability education that is both pedagogically sound and practically relevant, regardless of the audience they teach.
