Trajectories of Perceived Organizational Support Among Newcomers

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 3
HR

People

Authors: Gaëtane Caesens; Alexandre Morin Concordia University; Nicolas Gillet

Session overview

To date, most research has been interested in relatively static organizational support perceptions (POS) among samples of tenured workers, thus denying how these perceptions might fluctuate at the beginning of the employment relationship among a population of newcomers. To fill this gap, this research aims to identify trajectories of POS among newcomers over the course of nine months, and to explore the associations between these longitudinal trajectories and predictors (e.g., perceived career opportunities, informational justice, job autonomy) as well as outcomes (e.g., job satisfaction, work engagement, turnover intentions, performance). More precisely, newcomers with an organizational tenure between one day and three months were invited to complete a longitudinal survey over the course of nine months (four measurements time with a three-month interval). Person-centered growth mixture analyses among a sample of 416 newcomers are currently conducted. This research will have important theoretical and practical implications.
