Session overview

This pranayama and meditation session introduces participants to simple yet powerful pranayama (breathing method) practices and meditation that working people can do during breaks at work. After learning these practices, participants can do them at home or during break time in the workplace. These practices improve focus and sleep quality, enhance mental clarity, increase energy levels, reduce stress, and promote a greater sense of peace, well-being, and emotional stability. After the workshop, participants can continue these practices on their own at any time they need support, both during the conference and in their daily lives (e.g., in offices and homes) after the conference.
