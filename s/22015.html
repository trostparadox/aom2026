Session overview

This caucus examines how artificial intelligence is reshaping white-collar work and managerial decision-making. Focusing on organizational capability building, governance, and workforce readiness, the session invites participants to collaboratively explore how AI can enhance productivity while supporting, reskilling professional judgment, and worker empowerment. This caucus is research-anchored and is intended for researchers and practitioner-scholars examining AI-driven workplace transformation. Using a Value– Risk–Capability–Workforce Readiness conceptual framework as a shared point of reference, participants will engage in interactive discussion on how artificial intelligence is reshaping white-collar tasks, workforce skills, and governance challenges, with the aim of surfacing emerging research questions and opportunities for collaborative inquiry.
