Session overview

The Humanistic Management Caucus creates an informal space for building community and advancing collaborative initiatives. The overall intention is to spark collaborations that can grow beyond the caucus gathering through ongoing community engagement. In the session itself, we aim to: 1) Build connections among scholars, practitioners, and educators interested in humanistic approaches to management; 2) Nurture research ideas and potential collaborations through interactive dialogue; 3) Share and develop teaching innovations in an informal, collaborative setting; 4) Create opportunities for spontaneous cross-pollination of ideas across different domains and perspectives. These objectives will be pursued through discussions and collaborative activities at the caucus that can lead to concrete initiatives that extend throughout the year. The intention of these objectives is to contribute to the following outcomes: 1) Advancing interdisciplinary scholarship through collaborative research projects and knowledge exchange; 2) Developing practical pedagogical materials as in case studies, exercises, activities etc.; 3) Strengthening vibrant communities of practice connecting academics, practitioners, policy makers, and civil society members (including media); 4) Securing ongoing engagement through conferences, workshops and research seminars around the humanistic management theme.
