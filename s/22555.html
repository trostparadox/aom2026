Session overview

OMT Meetup is a place for prospective, new, and/or established OMT members to connect one-on-one and in small groups. If you are new to the Organization and Management Theory (OMT) division, are interested in joining OMT, or are a member who has been away for a while, then this forum is for you! It was created to help members feel “at home” in the OMT division. We have designed the forum so that you can meet both leading scholars and new members of our division and discuss research with colleagues who share similar interests.
