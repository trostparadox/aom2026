The Suffering-Opening Delta: A Grounded Theory of Paths of Spiritual Awakening

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 6
MSR

People

Authors: Gaetan Mourmant; Tianyuan Yu Mount Saint Vincent University

Session overview

In this extended abstract, we propose and examine an emerging model outlining three paths to spiritual awakening. A key concept that emerges from this model is the suffering–opening delta. We suggest that this delta represents a critical focal point for understanding why and when a spiritual awakening is triggered. Finally, we discuss managerial implications and directions for future research.
