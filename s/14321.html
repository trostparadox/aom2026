From Serving Employees to Serving Communities: A Multilevel Study of Servant Leadership

Authors: Sarah Kimakwa California State University-Chico; Jorge A. Gonzalez The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley; Hale Kaynak The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

Session overview

This study responds to calls to incorporate community outcomes into servant leadership research. While prior work has largely focused on organizational and employee-level effects, the impact of servant leaders on the communities in which their organizations operate remains insufficiently understood. We examine whether servant leadership positively influences community impact directly, and indirectly through employees. Drawing on servant leadership and organizational support theories, we link servant leadership to community engagement through employee perceptions and behaviors. Using survey data collected from U.S. service-oriented organizations, our model will be tested using multi-level structural equation modeling. We contribute to servant leadership research by shifting attention beyond internal outcomes and advancing understanding of how leadership shapes community impact amid growing societal expectations for business responsibility.
