Copenhagen Business School AoM reception (RSVP Rqd)

On behalf of Copenhagen Business School, we are pleased to invite you to the annual AoM reception at the Hard Rock Cafe, 1113 Market Street - just steps from the Pennsylvania Convention Center. The iconic venue sits in the heart of Center City and offers classic American fare alongside a curated collection of rock memorabilia, including pieces from local legends that add a touch of Philadelphia character to the evening.

The Academy of Management is an important platform for CBS both as a space for academic exchange and as an opportunity to strengthen connections across our international research community. We highly value the conversations, collaborations, and inspiration that emerge from engaging with colleagues and peers from around the world.

We hope you will join us for an informal and welcoming gathering, where we can reconnect, share perspectives, and celebrate our shared commitment to advancing research and education in management.

We very much look forward to seeing you in Philadelphia.

Warm regards,

Peter Møllgaard
President

Copenhagen Business School

 

Please note: this event is for guests over 21 years of age. Registration required. Participation is on a first come, first served basis. Maximum capacity: 150 guests. 

Please note that photos will be taken during the event.
