Session overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) constitutes a fundamental component of firms’ digital transformation, yet its diffusion confronts a critical challenge: standardization. Proprietary AI platforms from technology giants employ algorithms while processing globally sourced data. Widespread AI adoption requires robust governance frameworks and strategic approaches, particularly concerning AI and data trustworthiness—encompassing accountability, compliance, and security—while facilitating interfirm data exchangeability. Standardization serves as an essential mechanism for establishing such trustworthiness and fostering ecosystems across firms, platforms, and users. Recent scholarship shows that AI standards function beyond mere technical specifications, operating as governance instruments that shape regulatory regimes and organizational practices. This PDW advances a novel research stream by bridging AI and standardization studies. It examines how AI standardization shapes ecosystems with requisite market and social acceptance, contributing to the understanding of AI development and diffusion through standardization mechanisms and practices. The workshop addresses how technology firms of varying scales can navigate AI standardization for business and ecosystem development, exploring tensions between heterogeneous institutional logics of innovation, management, and regulation while considering the roles of international bodies and firm-level strategies. Through expert presentations, interactive discussions, and case-based exercises, participants will explore how regulatory criteria and technological interface standards influence ecosystem development without constraining innovation. This interdisciplinary forum convenes scholars and practitioners to co-create insights into emerging frameworks and research opportunities. Participants will acquire actionable approaches for integrating standardization into AI-related research and practice, thereby fostering innovation and competitive resilience in an increasingly standardized AI landscape. Keywords: AI, Standardization, Governance, Ecosystems, Scalability, Interoperability Panel 1 and Panel 2 are open to all AOM attendees.
