Session overview

The ubiquitous presence of AI in all layers of society and associated implications, such as hallucinations, bias, lack of transparency, and manipulation, emphasize a need to (re)think about questions of governance. The goal of this professional development workshop is to advance an ongoing dialogue between scholars from diverse disciplines on the governance of and through AI from an organizational perspective. We plan to address the topics of 1) how organizational actors are challenged in governing AI, 2) how AI itself is a governing mechanism that can control organizational behaviors and operations, and 3) how the existence or the absence of AI governance mechanisms influences innovation and society at large. We invite scholars working on related topics to share their latest work through panel presentations, followed by breakout session discussions, where participants can interact closely with peers and expert mentors. We hope to expand the community around the governance of AI and continue a conversation on how to understand and study this organizational, institutional, and societal challenge.
