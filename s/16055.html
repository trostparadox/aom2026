Session overview

Emerging markets face pressing societal challenges alongside rapid technological change and institutional complexities. This presenter symposium brings together papers that advance a governance focused perspective on technology adoption and deployment for social good in emerging markets. The symposium shows that that the pursuit of technology-enabled social good may involve navigating complex trade-offs that are multiple, interrelated, and not immediately apparent. Across diverse contexts – including clean cooking in East Africa, remote learning and higher education in Brazil, minibus taxi industry in South Africa and mobile-money industry in multiple markets – the papers demonstrate that the societal impact of technology is shaped not just by the technical capabilities but also by how technologies are embedded within institutional meanings and governance arrangements. The symposium has learnings for scholars, managers and policy-makers interested in technology, governance, and social impact in emerging markets.
