Session overview

Inclusive leadership has become a cornerstone in understanding how leaders can create workplaces where individuals feel both valued for their uniqueness and accepted as part of the collective. Although prior research has established inclusive leadership’s positive effects on team performance, voice, and well-being, we know little about how leaders develop and sustain such leadership, what psychological and cognitive foundations enable it, and how inclusive leadership can both promote inclusion and prevent exclusion. This symposium advances the literature by uniting four papers that address these questions. Together, these studies capture inclusive leadership as a dynamic, learnable, and identity-driven process. At the end, the symposium will conclude with a discussion highlighting opportunities for future research and engaging the audience in exploring these findings’ theoretical and practical implications.
