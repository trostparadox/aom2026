Session overview

Multinational enterprises (MNEs) increasingly operate in environments characterized by polycrisis, in which economic, social, environmental, and political disruptions interact and unfold simultaneously. Unlike discrete challenges, polycrisis involves cascading effects and feedback dynamics that complicate strategic decision-making across borders. These conditions are frequently intensified by geopolitical developments such as sanctions, trade fragmentation, climate–security tensions, and shifting regulatory regimes, raising new questions for international strategic management research on non-market strategy and legitimacy. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) offers a developmental and agenda-setting forum for IB scholars to reflect on how theories and methods can be extended to better capture MNE strategy under conditions of polycrisis and heightened geopolitical uncertainty. Bringing together experienced scholars in international business and strategy, the PDW combines expert agenda framing with interactive roundtable discussions. The PDW is purely developmental in nature and aims to foster dialogue, generate shared research questions, and support the development of future international strategic management research on non-market strategy and legitimacy in an increasingly volatile global environment.
