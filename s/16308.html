Session overview

We organize the symposium around three fundamental questions that drive stakeholders’ evaluations: 1) Who these stakeholders are (that is, how stakeholders’ identities and attributes shape their evaluations), 2) What do stakeholders believe (how stakeholders’ values and beliefs influence their judgments), and 3) Who or what stakeholders depend on (how stakeholders’ resource dependence and exchange dynamics affect their assessments of leaders). These three lenses provide a unifying framework for integrating diverse research streams on gender bias that have developed largely in isolation.
