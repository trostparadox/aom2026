Carbon Asset Allocation Strategies and Corporate Performance: An Integrated Perspective from China

STR
Kepeng Li (University of Tasmania); Renu Mishra (RMIT University)
This paper examines how carbon asset allocation strategies influence corporate performance and organizational strategy in Asian markets, with particular emphasis on China's evolving carbon market landscape. Drawing on the complementary lenses of Resource-Based View (RBV) and Institutional Theory, we develop an integrated framework that explains how firms navigate the complex interplay between internal carbon management capabilities and external institutional dynamics. Our theoretical analysis reveals that effective carbon asset allocation transcends mere regulatory compliance, representing a strategic resource that, when properly leveraged, generates sustainable competitive advantages. The study contributes to the literature by demonstrating how the integration of carbon assets with corporate strategies creates value through various mechanisms. We propose that firms successfully managing the dual challenge of economic and environmental objectives develop distinctive capabilities in carbon market sensing, institutional alignment, and value synergy through organizational agility such as institutional entrepreneurship and political ties. The paper provides critical insights for practitioners navigating carbon markets in institutionally complex environments and offers a roadmap for developing carbon asset strategies that align internal competencies with external institutional demands.
