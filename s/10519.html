Session overview

This PDW is designed for members of the Organizational Behavior (OB) Division to expand their scholarly networks around shared research interests. The forum provides participants with opportunities to engage with leading scholars in specific OB topic areas, connect with other Division members, and discuss ongoing and emerging research ideas. The PDW will feature three rounds of 30-minute small-group discussions, each moderated by accomplished scholars with expertise in a focal topical area. Participants may self-select into research themes of interest and may remain in the same theme or rotate across topics between rounds. Research themes span a broad range of OB domains, including: Creativity & Innovation; Dark Side of OB; Diversity; Emotions, Affect, & Attitudes; Future of Work; Identity & Identification; Justice/Fairness, Trust, & Ethics; Leadership; Motivation, Passion, & Calling; Personality & Neurodiversity; Relationships at Work; Social Networks; Teams and Teaming; Time & Temporal Dynamics; Voice, Proactivity, & OCBs; Work-Life Interface. We anticipate in-depth research discussions and the facilitation of many meaningful connections among the participants and members of the OB Division.
