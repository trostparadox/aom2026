Session overview

Academic debate increasingly emphasizes the importance of impactful research yet offers limited guidance on how such impact can be achieved. Like other aspects of academic work, impact must be learned, particularly by early career researchers (ECRs), who often lack practical guidance on generating impact for both theory and society. This session focuses on developing the skills, mindsets, and techniques that help ECRs increase their impact through the emerging concept of micro-level impact. Miedtank et al. (2025) argue that focusing on individuals and groups throughout the research process, by anticipating stakeholder interactions and concerns and reflecting on the implications of theoretical and methodological choices, makes impact more tangible and attainable for ECRs. Building on this perspective, the PDW addresses three themes: (1) the current state of research impact for ECRs; (2) insights from micro-level impact on how to generate impactful research; and (3) the tools and practices through which ECRs can learn micro-level impact. Following brief reflections from experienced scholars, the session will focus on participants’ key challenges and provide concrete guidance for addressing them.
