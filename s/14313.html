Session overview

This professional development workshop addresses ways in which academics can advance their skills and approaches within the classroom —whether one is a novice to instruction or is a seasoned teacher who seeks to polish their pedagogy. Participants will engage roundtable discussions with experts who teach a range of topics - from core classes such as Strategy, Management, and Entrepreneurship, to specialty areas such as People and Politics and People Analytics. Key questions will also be addressed by the discussants, such as how to teach the same topic to different audiences and how instructors can handle the accelerated pace and dynamism within the contexts in which students and academics are embedded. This lively and interactive session will grant participants new knowledge to advance their teaching and provide a forum for collective agenda setting regarding teaching and its connection to research and academic theory. As such, this suits the OMT Division’s commitment to enhancing the effectiveness and profile of pedagogy among its members.
