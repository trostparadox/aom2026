Session overview

Scientific and technological trajectories are intertwined and path dependent, yet we have limited understanding of how initial conditions shape downstream outcomes. In the fourth edition of our symposium devoted to exploring the dynamics of moving from research to development, we turn our focus to path dependence in science and technology. We present four empirical studies that examine how absorptive capacity, inventor involvement, and early career experiences influence the long-term innovation trajectory of firms and scientists. Through this symposium, we hope to spark future research on the path-dependent drivers of science and innovation.
