Session overview

Asian American leaders frequently encounter distinctive leadership barriers, commonly referred to as the bamboo ceiling, arising from cultural stereotypes, leadership prototype mismatches, and limited access to sponsorship and organizational support (Sy et al., 2010; Gündemir et al., 2019; Kim et al., 2022). While they are expected to demonstrate leadership prototypes such as assertiveness, emotional presence, and upward influence, organizations expect them to remain culturally congruent (Fiske et al., 2002; Lu et al., 2020). Emerging scholarship and practitioner work suggest that coaching, particularly culturally informed and ethnorelative approaches, can help them identify and navigate tensions by supporting stereotype management, identity integration, and relationship-building (Boyatzis et al., 2006; Rosinski, 2003). This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) will facilitate a conversation between senior researchers and experienced coaches to discuss how to better support Asian American leaders in navigating leadership challenges and advancing their careers in the U.S. and Western workplaces. The PDW will begin with a moderated panel using directed questions, followed by an interactive group coaching activity and roundtable discussions that allow participants to reflect, exchange experiences, and co-create practical strategies for culturally responsive leadership development.
