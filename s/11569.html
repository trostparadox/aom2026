Session overview

This workshop builds on successes of prior workshops exploring research frontiers in the increasingly important areas of climate mitigation, adaptation, and regeneration. Through a combination of panel presentations by distinguished scholars and interactive roundtable discussions with workshop participants, we seek to identify how to advance research and how to address some shortcomings in existing approaches – both empirical and theoretical – in climate change research. Roundtables will provide a venue for participants to discuss their work in progress and receive feedback from peers while building a community of scholars in the field. The proposed workshop is likely to be of interest to researchers working on topics such as sustainability, corporate social responsibility, nonmarket strategy, organizational resilience, and cross-sector partnerships, providing an informed perspective on cutting-edge research on these topics. While participation in the first half of this session is open to all AOM attendees, individuals must pre-register to partake in the final research roundtables, in which participants will read and discuss each others' work.
