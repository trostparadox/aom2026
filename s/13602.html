Session overview

Qualitative research offers powerful approaches for developing new and extending existing theory on the evolution of technology and innovation. With the rise of digital technologies, rapid progress has been made in the availability of qualitative data sources and the tools used to analyze them. Against this backdrop, this PDW brings together scholars who employ novel qualitative methods across diverse empirical settings. Based on keynotes and roundtables, the PDW aims to (1) inspire future research by showcasing recent advancements in qualitative methods and research designs, and (2) provide developmental feedback on ongoing and planned qualitative projects. We welcome manuscripts addressing phenomena relevant to technology and innovation scholarship at multiple levels of analysis, ranging from individuals and teams to organizations, ecosystems, and networks.
