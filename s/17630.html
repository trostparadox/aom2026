Session overview

Participants are invited to experience guided artistic practices— to make meaning, create from their imagination and collaborate to build together in a nurturing environment. A variety of artistic practices, such as drawing, photography, ceramics, sculpture, theater and poetry — will offer quick experiment - for non-artists. In times of great change and chaotic divisive public dialog, it is a call for collective sensemaking with the senses attuned to individual and shared experiences, guided by experts. Management education and business schools have mostly privileged intellectual and analytical reasoning. Our theories reward efficiency, often at the cost of experimenting and nurturing needed for innovation. The use of arts has been relegated to package or market products. The activists use it to protest the institutional dominance of oppressive structures. Art is powerful and sensory knowing and being is increasingly crucial in our multimedia world. As more organizations face complex and uncertain futures, creative approaches are more practical and even necessary (Taylor & Ladkin, 2009; Sutherland, 2013). Building on Nancy Adler’s (2006) call for 21st-century leaders to be like artists, capable of envisioning a beautiful future in order to build it, this workshop provides embodied artistic experiences facilitated by management scholars and practicing managers who are also committed artists. The facilitated activities will engage the participants’ imagination and intuition, in addition to their intellect, to co-create a dialog on creativity, aesthetics, beauty and humanism in organizing (Barry & Meisiek, 2010; Hatch, 2012). The workshop follows on previous successful art-based explorations at the Academy. No prior artistic experience required.
