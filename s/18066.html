Session overview

The papers in this session specify a (Bayesian) process model of strategic uniqueness: strategic uniqueness is generated initially through unique priors, either by theorizing (Felin) or disagreement across mentors (Guis). But this initial heterogeneity of priors is just the beginning, as learning can catalyze strategic uniqueness through theory-based practices (Yang) or computational anomaly detection (Conti). For strategic management, this reframes entrepreneurship as a setting where the micro-foundations of competitive heterogeneity become visible.
