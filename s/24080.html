Flexible Organizational Culture Promoting Organizational Change and Performance

ODC
YongSun Chang (Chosun University)
This study comprehensively examined the relationship between flexible organizational culture, organizational change, and organizational performance at the corporate level. This study was conducted using data collected by the Korea Research Institute for Vocational Education and Training in 2017. The results revealed that flexible organizational culture and organizational change enhanced organizational performance, and that flexible organizational culture exerted a positive influence on organizational change. Furthermore, this study partially confirmed the mediating effect of the organizational change between flexible organizational culture and organizational performance. Moreover, changes in core business demand and the diversity of performance appraisal partially moderated the relationship between flexible organizational culture, organizational change, and organizational performance. This study showed that flexible organizational culture and organizational change are key concepts that determine a company’s competitiveness through its ability to respond to external environmental changes.
