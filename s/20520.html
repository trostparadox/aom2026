Advancing Mindfulness at Work: Building Community Across Sanguine and Skeptical Perspectives

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 11:30AM–1:00PM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Salon 9
CAU

People

Organizers: Christopher James Lyddy Providence College; Darren Jason Good Pepperdine University

Session overview

Mindfulness research has rapidly grown within organizational science. This caucus will connect mindfulness scholars interested in maturing this discourse by developing balanced understanding that integrates sanguine and skeptical views of mindfulness. Participants will discuss the future of mindfulness research, build research collaborations, and develop scholarly community.
