Session overview

The finalists for the IM division’s Best Dissertation Award will introduce their work. The committee for the IM Division Award for the Best Dissertation in International Management has selected the following five finalists for this year’s award (in alphabetical order): • Aparajita Agarwal (INSEAD), "Digital platforms as institutional intermediaries: Implications for inclusion, relational dynamics, and strategic advantage in emerging markets", Ph.D. granted by the University of Pennsylvania, supervised by Zeke Hernandez and Tyler Wry. • Yejee Lee (Auburn University), "When, where, and how: Firm responsiveness to social and environmental sustainability shocks", Ph.D. granted by Indiana University, supervised by Stephanie Wang. • Yu Li (SKEMA Business School), "Walking a tightrope: Challenges for corporate sociopolitical activism amidst stakeholder divides", Ph.D. granted by the University of South Carolina, supervised by João Albino-Pimentel and Stan Markus. • Laurenz Tinhof (WU Vienna), "A matching perspective on foreign direct investment", Ph.D. granted by WU Vienna, supervised by Jonas Puck, Thomas Lindner and Sinziana Dorobantu. • Xin Wang (HKUST Guangzhou), "Multinational enterprises' adaptation strategies under geopolitical risk", Ph.D. granted by the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, supervised by Fiona Kun Yao.
