Presenters: Shannon Fletcher University of Washington; Ashley Nichols Boise State University

Session overview

Conflict in the workplace can be challenging- demanding time, interpersonal skills, and a solid understanding of conflict management. In today’s fast-paced environment, these resources can feel scarce. This interactive workshop offers practical insights and proven frameworks to help you confidently facilitate high conflict situations. We will begin by exploring key principles from the SCARF model (Rock, 2021) and the Workplace Conflict Matrix (Thornton & Fletcher, 2024), along with the physiological impact conflict has on the body. Then, through experiential learning exercises, you will gain actionable strategies and practical tips for guiding conversations toward resolution. By the end of the session, you will leave with tools to transform conflict into opportunity – boosting workplace productivity, strengthening relationships, and fostering collaborative teamwork.
