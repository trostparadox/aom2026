Session overview

The HR Division Late-Stage Doctoral Student Consortium will take place at the Academy of Management annual meeting, in-person in Philadelphia, on Saturday, August 1st, 2026. Highlights of this year’s Consortium include a keynote address by Dr. Peter Bamberger (Tel Aviv University); a presentation and panel on navigating the job search process; an assembly with editors of major journals in HR (AMJ, AMR, JAP, PPsych, JOM, HRM, HRMR); roundtable sessions with leading scholars/educators in the field on research, teaching, and career development/management; and a research feedback session on a dissertation/research project (as well as breakfast and lunch). Ideal candidates are doctoral students who are members of the HR Division and have finished all required coursework and expect to complete their PhD in one (or possibly two) years. It is encouraged – though not required – that candidates have their dissertation/thesis proposal approved by their supervising committee before (or soon after) the conference. There is no attendance fee. To participate in this session, applicants will have to: 1) sign up for this PDW session and 2) complete a pre-registration form by May 4th, 2026. In the pre- registration, applicants will upload an abbreviated (1-2 page) CV with contact information, education, research/teaching interests, publications, and conference presentations for distribution to all participants. Each applicant’s faculty advisor or doctoral program supervisor will need to endorse the applicant. Once selected for the consortium, the participants will submit their paper/dissertation (maximum of 40 pages) via the pre- registration form or via email to the PDW organizer (Troy Smith; troy.smith@ou.edu).
