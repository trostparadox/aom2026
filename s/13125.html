Organizers: Martina Pizzinato UCL School of Management; Colin M. Fisher University College London

Session overview

The study of creativity has a long, established history within management studies (Harrison, Rouse, Fisher & Amabile, 2022). This caucus aims at connecting creativity scholars in the field to: (1) discuss the future of creativity research; (2) encourage collaboration; and (3) foster a scholarly community.
