Session overview

After a period of rapid adoption of diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) efforts, many firms are now backtracking just as quickly from their support for DEI. This symposium will highlight cutting-edge research on this emerging phenomenon and facilitate meaningful discussion regarding how disengagement from DEI should be studied. It features three papers that address the antecedents and consequences of DEI disengagement using perspectives such as collective action framing, symbolic management, and organizational reputation. It also features a panel of expert scholars who will discuss how this topic can be studied. The ultimate aim of the symposium is to accelerate research on this practically and theoretically important topic.
