Session overview

Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming organizational landscapes, yet many leaders make critical decisions about AI implementation without fundamental understanding of how these technologies actually work. This gap between hype and reality leads to poor resource allocation, unrealistic expectations, workforce disruption, and missed opportunities for strategic advantage. This professional development workshop addresses this challenge through experiential learning designed specifically for organizational leaders, management consultants, and decision-makers who need conceptual AI literacy before implementing AI solutions or restructuring their workforce. Through hands-on activities where participants role-play as AI systems, this workshop builds scaffolded understanding from foundational symbolic AI through modern agentic systems. Participants will experience five levels of AI intelligence: rule-based decision trees, the Turing Test illusion through ELIZA, machine learning pattern recognition, neural network processing, and contemporary AI agents. Rather than teaching specific tools, the workshop establishes conceptual foundations that enable participants to evaluate any AI solution and adapt to future developments. The final synthesis activity applies these concepts to real organizational scenarios, demonstrating how experiential understanding translates to practical decision-making.
