Session overview

Bridging the divide between academic research and practical application in healthcare management remains a critical challenge. Over the past two decades, implementation science has emerged to address this issue, though many management scholars remain unfamiliar. This workshop seeks to provide a foundation for implementation science in the Academy of Management by examining how four key models—Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), Total Quality Management (TQM), Diffusion of Innovation (DOI), and Complex Adaptive Systems (CAS)—have informed the development of implementation science, which is the study of methods and strategies to promote the integration of research findings and evidence into healthcare practice and policy. Through interactive presentation and discussion, participants will gain tools to apply these frameworks to their research and management challenges, enhancing their capacity to design and implement effective change strategies. The workshop will help bridge the gap between theory and practice by focusing on practical application, ensuring that academic insights translate into real-world healthcare management and research improvements. Participants will leave with actionable strategies to implement in their research and management challenges, fostering a more theoretically driven, evidence-based approach to research in healthcare management.
