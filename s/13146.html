Session overview

This interactive, practice-oriented workshop showcases the four finalists for the new MED Award for Innovative Educational Practices. The session offers participants direct exposure to proven, high-impact innovations that are ready for immediate adoption in management education. Through facilitated roundtable discussions designed to encourage close engagement, comparative insight, and structured action planning, participants gain transferable strategies for transforming their own teaching practice. Each finalist presents concrete implementation guidance, evidence of learning impact, and insights on adaptation. The distinctive roundtable format enables participants to engage deeply with three innovations through small-group dialogue, extract actionable pedagogical frameworks, troubleshoot likely implementation challenges, and develop concrete plans tailored to their own courses. Rather than abstract principles, finalists provide step-by-step accounts of their innovations, including design decisions, student responses, resource requirements, and guidance for contextual adaptation. Participants leave with ready-to-use templates, clear implementation roadmaps, and connections to a growing community of innovative educators. The session concludes with the announcement of the MED Award winner and the launch of the next year’s competition. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
