Session overview

Nonprofit organizations operate in an environment where expectations for accountability and impact measurement can sometimes be at odds with mission commitments. While funders, donors, and other stakeholders often agree on the importance of demonstrating effectiveness, disagreement frequently arises around how impact should be defined, measured, and communicated. Additionally, there may be misalignment on the mission or purpose, as well as how realistic funder expectations are. This tension creates practical and ethical challenges for organizations that seek to evaluate effectiveness without undermining mission integrity or oversimplifying complex social outcomes. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) provides an interactive forum for examining these challenges through a research-informed and practice-oriented perspective that aligns with PNP’s emphasis on engaged scholarship. The interactive session engages participants through intentional and inclusive facilitated exercises that explore trade-offs, build shared understanding, and enable collaboration across the group. Participants will collaboratively develop a shared analytic framework that clarifies mission-driven – and accountable – measurement for nonprofit organizations. The workshop is designed for scholars and practitioner-scholars interested in nonprofit organizations, ethical leadership, organizational effectiveness, and accountability. It aims to foster dialogue, generate insights, and support future research and teaching related to nonprofit accountability.
