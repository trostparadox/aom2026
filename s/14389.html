Technology-Mediated Professional Learning: Communication and Collaboration in ESP

Author: Ruei-Teng Hung Chaoyang University of Technology

Session overview

As digital technologies and artificial intelligence reshape professional work and learning, educators must renegotiate how communication, expertise, and collaboration are organized in practice. This challenge is particularly evident for English for Specific Purposes (ESP) teachers who are subject-matter specialists rather than trained language teachers and who face tensions between disciplinary expertise and English-medium instruction. From a Communication, Digital Technology, and Organization (CTO) perspective, professional learning is a technology-mediated process of organizing rather than simply acquiring skills. Accordingly, this study examines a technology-mediated professional learning workshop for ESP teachers, focusing on how digital mediation, communication self-efficacy, and interaction quality relate to readiness for interdisciplinary collaboration and perceived ESP teaching capability.
