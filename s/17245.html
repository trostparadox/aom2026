Session overview

Large language models (LLMs) have reduced the cost of extracting information from unstructured text while greatly expanding the scope and granularity of extractable information. This development holds particular importance for scholars of innovation, strategy and entrepreneurship, where progress in content-based analysis of publications, patents, and company data has significantly expanded the research frontier. Yet, extracting accurate, reliable, and reproducible information from text remains challenging, while accepted standards are still developing. In this PDW, we showcase applications of LLMs in innovation, strategy, and entrepreneurship research, while discussing best practices for validation and reproducibility. We address topics such as how to structure processing workflows and prompts, explore methods to validate results, and share unexpected obstacles when working with LLMs. Most importantly, we aim to connect scholars currently experimenting with LLMs to initiate a dialogue that will shape best practices in the future.
