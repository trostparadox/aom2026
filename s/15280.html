Session overview

This PDW seeks to inspire scholars how to renew and valorize their expertise in order to safeguard their career sustainability. We present the scientific knowhow on how experts meet new expertise demands to stay happy, healthy and productive. This adaptive ability is known as flexpertise and enables scholars and practitioners to deal with new work demands within and across the boundaries of their own fields and occupational domains. The PDW offers a broad range of perspectives on how flexpertise may foster career sustainability including: (1) the ability to perceive new demands and opportunities to renew one’s career, (2) strategies for expertise redevelopment (upskilling, reskilling, deskilling), (3) strategies for exploiting one’s expertise in new contexts (including job crafting, expertise transfer, and portfolio careers), (4) building confidence in a new field, (5) adjusting one’s identity as an expert, and (6) strategies for building social recognition for one’s renewed expertise and contributions to science and professional practice. The PDW will start with a summary of expertise and flexpertise research, followed by an individual exercise by which attendees draw a graph of their own expertise development over time (their so-called ‘expertise cocktail’). During 2 speed dating rounds, attendees will collect input on how to further develop and valorize their expertise cocktail aiming to foster their own career sustainability. This is followed by a Q&A session with a panel of flexperts who can illustrate the aforementioned six perspectives on how they have renewed their career, including the obstacles they were able to circumvent.
