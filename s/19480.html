How Digital Technologies Build Resilient Industrial Chains Through Green Innovation

Authors: Sha Xi Tianjin Chengjian University; Jiayi Sun Tianjin Chengjian University

Session overview

This research aims to explore how digital technology can improve the regional industrial chains resilience, paying special attention to the intermediary role of green innovation. Based on the theory of regional innovation system and absorption capacity, the panel data of the Yangtze River Delta region in China from 2011 to 2022 is analyzed. We construct the digital economic development index and the multi-dimensional industrial chain resilience index. The research results show that the progress of digital technology has a significant and direct positive impact on psychological resilience. More importantly, green innovation plays a key mediating role, accounting for about 30%-40% of the total effect. Digital-driven more environmentally friendly production improves environmental compliance and market adaptability, thus enhancing long-term flexibility. By integrating technology management, sustainable management and flexible research, we emphasize the strategic coordination of digitalization and greening. These findings provide a theoretical basis for integrating environmental goals into digital transformation, and provide policy inspiration for the design of regionally appropriate innovative systems to enhance ecosystem resilience.
