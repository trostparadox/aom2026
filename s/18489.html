Session overview

Consistent with their dynamic nature, leader-follower relationships evolve in ways that systematically alter leader behavior, leader/follower perception, follower outcomes, and even the validity of leadership assessment. Yet, much of the empirical literature continues to assume that leadership identities, perceptions, and measurement tools remain stable and interpretable even when individuals and organizations are under strain. This symposium challenges that assumption by examining how leadership processes and assessments shift under conditions of identity threat, stress, and evaluative uncertainty. Each presentation examines leadership as a dynamic phenomenon influenced by factors beyond the leaders themselves.
