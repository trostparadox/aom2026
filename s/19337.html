Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is aimed at scholars interested in advancing responsible innovation in science commercialization and academic entrepreneurship, with a particular emphasis on how research and teaching can add ethical, societal, and sustainability value when they intersect with inventors’ and organizations’ practice in timely and impactful ways. Responsible innovation emphasizes the anticipation, inclusion, reflexivity, and responsiveness of innovation processes, ensuring that scientific and technological advances generate not only economic value but also positive societal and environmental outcomes. Expanding this PDW to explicitly foreground responsible innovation responds to growing interest across management research, teaching, and practice in how innovation can be designed, governed, and commercialized responsibly. Building on the workshop’s established focus on the intersections of research, teaching, and practice, we will explore how organizational and management scholars can conceptualize, model, and empirically examine science commercialization ecosystems that are ethically grounded and societally oriented, while also developing pedagogical and practice-based approaches that embed responsibility from the earliest stages of innovation. As university funders, policymakers, and stakeholders increasingly call for demonstrable societal impact and responsible stewardship of public research, this PDW is designed to support early career researchers (ECRs) and senior scholars alike in aligning their research agendas, classrooms, and engagement activities with responsible innovation goals. The PDW thus serves as a one-stop forum for participants to build capacity for responsible innovation across research, teaching, and organizational practice.
