Session overview

The OMT Junior Faculty Consortium provides a forum for early-career scholars interested in organization and management theory to get practical advice and build relationships that will help them thrive in their academic careers. The program will feature feedback sessions, panels, and facilitated discussions focused on: 1) developing research for publication with the help of experienced scholars, 2) strategies for impact and growth as a researcher and teacher, and 3) navigating the pre-tenure years of building a successful faculty career in diverse institutional settings.
