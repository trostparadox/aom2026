Session overview

The Organizational Behavior (OB) Mid-Career Faculty Workshop is designed for faculty members who consider themselves mid-career. This typically includes scholars who have earned tenure but are not yet full professors (i.e., are at the associate-level) or the equivalent in non-tenure-granting institutions. The workshop provides a platform for attendees to connect with senior Faculty Fellows, receive guidance, and ask questions about navigating the challenges and opportunities of this career stage. Discussion topics will be determined based on input from attendees but may include career enablers (e.g., joint appointments, sabbaticals, changing institutions), research advancement (e.g., finding new collaborators, securing grants, expanding publication portfolios through writing and editing book, leadership roles, service and engagement (e.g., department chair, center director, professional service) and promotion planning.
