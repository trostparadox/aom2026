Session overview

The Entrepreneurship Division Social is an off-site event for those who have pre-registered and paid for their entry ticket. Tickets will NOT be available at the door. The purpose of the social event is to provide a venue for members of the Entrepreneurship Division to socialize, collaborate, and connect. For those unable to attend this event, we encourage participation in a general ENT division social which will be held after the ENT business meeting on Monday.
