Session overview

Public policy shapes organizations and organizations shape public policy. Research on each relationship is growing across organizational theory, strategy, economics, and sociology. However, the growing interest in these relationships has also led to findings that are fragmented across social science disciplines and, even within management, often split into parallel streams, making it hard to build cumulative understanding of the mutual influence between public policy and organizations. This presenter symposium brings together four papers to highlight a cross-disciplinary research stream on the two-way relationship between public policy and organizations: Edward Walker (sociology), Manuela Collis (economics), Jingya You (strategy), and Rajiv Kozhikode (organizational theory). Together, their papers examine both sides of the relationship, providing new evidence for mechanisms that explain how policy impacts organizational behavior and how organizations influence policy. We conclude with an integrative discussion to outline a research agenda and invite dialogue across academy divisions and beyond.
