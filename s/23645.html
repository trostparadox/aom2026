Session overview

The Fellows of the Academy of Management invite all AOM members to sign up for the inaugural Fellows Café Friday (7/31, 2-3 pm), Saturday (8/1, 11am – 12 pm) and Sunday (8/2, 2-3pm). You may sign up for one table in each of the three sessions (three total) using this link: https://pitt.co1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0HATXLOHsN77pvE What is the Fellows Café? The Fellows Café provides attendees with an opportunity to engage directly with AOM Fellows in small-group discussions on specific research topics and, more broadly, on navigating academic careers. Each table seats 10-12 participants, keeping conversations intimate and accessible. Whether you are a doctoral student, early-career scholar, or seasoned researcher, participants are encouraged to ask questions, exchange research ideas, and benefit from the collective experience and insights of distinguished Fellows. Open to all AOM Annual Meeting attendees, the Fellows Café is intended to foster meaningful dialogue, mentoring, and community building to connect members across career stages and scholarly interests. All sessions will be held in the Philadelphia Convention Center, Room 204B. Please join us!
