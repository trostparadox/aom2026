Session overview

This symposium examines the (survival of) corporate purpose in the face of socio-political change. We ask whether purpose operates as (a) a resilient anchor that stabilizes decision-making and stakeholder relations, (b) a strategic liability that invites backlash, internal polarization, or symbolic decoupling, or (c) an evolving commitment that is revised as firms adapt to new political and regulatory conditions. The conversation will invite recent global and domestic examples, bridging scholarship and practice.
