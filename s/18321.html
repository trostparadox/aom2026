The Algorithmic Class Ceiling: How AI Interviews Exacerbate Social Class Inequities in Hiring

Monday, Aug 3 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 3
HR

People

Authors: Yuchuan Liu Nanjing University; Zhengqing Chen Nanjing University; Yanshu Ji Nanjing University

Session overview

While organizations increasingly adopt AI interviews to mitigate human bias against applicants from lower social class origins, we reveal a paradoxical “algorithmic glass ceiling.” Drawing on applicant reactions model, we propose that the dehumanized and non-reciprocal nature of AI evaluation alienates applicants from lower-class origins because the “interpersonal void” of AI interviews erodes their interview satisfaction and subsequent performance. Results from a survey study demonstrate that lower-class applicants are significantly less likely to advance to the next hiring round in AI interviews compared to traditional human-led formats. This relationship is driven by a decline in interview satisfaction. We uncover how automated hiring systems create new hurdles for social mobility in the modern workforce.
