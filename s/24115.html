Evolution of Decision-Making Autonomy for Managers of Subsidiaries of a Multinational Company

IM
Sondes Turki
This research investigates the evolution over time of decision-making autonomy of senior managers in subsidiaries regarding human resource management policies and practices, via resource theory, and within a multinational company that has undergone strategic organizational transformation. Based on a multiple case study, the data consist of 30 semi-structured interviews as well as various organizational documents. The results confirm the dynamic nature of decision-making autonomy in human resource policies and its evolution following the implementation of a radical organizational transformation. Autonomy is shaped not only by the content and process of the organizational transformation, but also by the involvement of subsidiary managers, who are responsible for human resources, in this process.
