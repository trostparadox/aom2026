Session overview

Tools such as canvases, AI-based applications, and conceptual frameworks play a vital role in entrepreneurship and innovation. Influential tools in entrepreneurship, such as the Lean Startup heuristic, the Business Model Canvas, and the Lean Canvas, have become essential to entrepreneurship education, startup programs, and venture development. While these tools have long shaped entrepreneurial practice, scholarly research has only recently begun to examine them systematically. This raises important questions about the nature of tools in entrepreneurship, how they should be designed and validated in real-world settings, and how research on these tools can be published in high-impact journals. Following a keynote address, this PDW uses a World Café format to create an interactive, hands-on learning environment. Participants will rotate among facilitated discussion tables focusing on the following topics: (1) the nature, (2) design processes, (3) evaluation, and (4) publishing of tools in entrepreneurship. Through structured discussion, peer exchange, and guided reflection, participants will develop actionable insights for advancing research on tools in entrepreneurship.
