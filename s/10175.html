Session overview

This presenter symposium examines strategic interactions between business and public policy, focusing on how firm actions and policy interventions generate underappreciated system-level effects, and how corporate governance and stakeholder engagement mediate these dynamics. The four presentations span multiple domains, including government mandates and board diversity, ownership structures and corporate responses to democratic erosion, the microfoundations and ethical boundaries of corporate political activity, and employee mobilization as a nonmarket strategy. Collectively, these papers contribute to research on nonmarket strategy, corporate governance, stakeholder management, and issue management.
