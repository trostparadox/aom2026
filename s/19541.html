Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is designed for educators and case writers who want to enhance the relevance and impact of their teaching cases in diverse contexts. The workshop presents strategies for adapting globally-developed management cases to diverse cultural, institutional, and market environments, helping participants make cases more locally relevant, pedagogically meaningful, and impactful for students across regions. The PDW is organized around four guiding topics. The session begins by examining why local context matters, exploring the limitations of globally-focused cases and the importance of aligning cases with local cultural norms, institutional structures, and market realities. Participants learn to select cases and map contextual factors, ensuring that adaptations preserve key learning objectives while increasing relevance and engagement. The workshop introduces a localization framework with step-by-step guidance for adapting narratives, data, decision points, and dilemmas, supported by research-informed strategies and practical examples. A mini group activity allows participants to apply the framework to a sample case, with panel feedback and a Localization Checklist to guide ongoing adaptation. Takeaways: Participants leave with practical tools, a structured approach to case localization, and shared language for applying these methods in their own teaching, increasing the impact, inclusivity, and educational value of globally-adapted cases.
