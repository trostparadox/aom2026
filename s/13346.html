Accelerating Internationalization with AI: The Role of Procedural Rationality and Politicization

Author: DunHuei Hsu Ming Chuan University

Session overview

Although prior research highlights the business value of artificial intelligence (AI), how firms leverage AI to enhance managerial decision-making and accelerate international expansion remains underexplored. Drawing on the resource-based view, this study examines the effect of organizational AI capability on internationalization speed and the moderating roles of procedural rationality and politicization within strategic decision-making processes. Organizational AI capability is conceptualized as a firm-level strategic resource supported by tangible, human, and intangible resources that enable effective AI deployment. AI-driven analytics enhance information processing, reduce uncertainty, and improve market forecasting and foreign market selection, thereby accelerating internationalization. However, the effectiveness of AI capability depends on how strategic decisions are made. Procedural rationality strengthens the positive effect of AI capability on internationalization speed by promoting systematic information analysis, cross-functional information sharing, and learning from prior internationalization experience. In contrast, politicization weakens this relationship by distorting information flows, reducing reliance on AI-generated insights, and slowing strategic responses to foreign market opportunities. The empirical context consists of Taiwanese publicly listed firms with at least one foreign subsidiary, combining survey data from key informants responsible for AI-related strategic decisions with secondary data from the Taiwan Economic Journal and firms’ annual reports. Theoretically, this study advances international management research by conceptualizing AI capability as a strategic resource shaping internationalization speed and by clarifying the moderating roles of procedural rationality and politicization. Managerially, the findings offer guidance on how managers can leverage AI capabilities and decision-making processes to accelerate cross-border expansion.
