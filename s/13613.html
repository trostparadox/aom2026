Session overview

In a time of profound transformation in academia—marked by shifting opportunity structures, intensifying performance expectations, and growing identity pressures—scholars need structured ways to make sense of complexity and design sustainable careers. Building on insights from our 2025 showcase symposium in Denmark, this Professional Development Workshop (PDW) explores how scholars can intentionally shape and adapt their scholarly trajectories through a Life Design approach supported, where helpful, by generative AI. Integrating research on career construction, entrepreneurial thinking, positive psychology, and human–AI collaboration, the session introduces an AI-supported Double-Diamond model for reflective practice and identity development. Participants engage in structured exercises ranging from values clarification and vision work to situational diagnosis and future scenario prototyping, using AI as a reflective partner that expands cognitive space, emotional integration, and strategic clarity. The 90-minute workshop blends conceptual grounding with hands-on experimentation to help scholars at all career stages cultivate agency, resilience, and intentionality. By combining narrative reflection, entrepreneurial action principles, and AI-enabled synthesis, the PDW offers a practical toolkit for designing meaningful scholarly pathways and embedding Life Design practices into mentoring, supervision, and professional development.
