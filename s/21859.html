After the Loss: Organizational Response to Status Loss in Haute Cuisine Organizations

Authors: Niccolò Ulderico Re Politecnico di Milano; Antonio Ghezzi Politecnico di Milano

Session overview

In the wake of status loss, formerly high-status organizations face a critical dilemma: whether to accept a lower-status position or attempt to regain their prior standing. This dilemma is particularly challenging because status is an ambiguous resource. While high status enhances visibility and economic performance, it also intensifies stakeholders’ expectations, constraining organizational action and complicating value capture. When a negative status shock occurs, organizations must recalibrate their relationship with status, balancing its benefits against its constraints. Despite growing interest in status dynamics, we still know little about how high-status organizations respond to status loss over time. In this exploratory qualitative study, we examine haute cuisine organizations that have lost a Michelin star. Our analysis reveals four distinct response trajectories, characterized by different combinations of institutional participation and organizational change. These trajectories illustrate how organizations react following a negative status shock. This study contributes to the literature on status dynamics by theorizing heterogeneous organizational responses to status loss and by showing how status decline can act as a catalyst for divergent patterns of organizational change.
