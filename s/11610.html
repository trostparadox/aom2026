Session overview

This PDW offers an experiential inquiry into inner leadership, drawing on Ayurvedic psychology and Vedantic wisdom traditions as contemplative and epistemological resources for leadership and organizational studies. Participants explore how attention, states of being (consciousness), and intention shape perception, stress, ethical judgment, and professional action. The session introduces the ABC-Model—Attention, Being, Creation—as a concise framework linking inner development to leadership practice. Through brief embodied exercises and reflective dialogue, participants examine the mind as a filter and explore intention-based action (karma yoga) as an alternative to fear- and control-driven leadership in contemporary organizational contexts.
