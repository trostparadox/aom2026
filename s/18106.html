Session overview

"Human-AI collaboration" has become a ubiquitous phrase in discussions of LLMs in business strategy (Csaszar, 2025) — yet specifics remain frustratingly vague. What does collaboration actually mean? How should it be designed? The paper in this session will provide a deeper understanding of the design challenges and architectural requirements for effective human-AI collaboration for strategic decision-making.
