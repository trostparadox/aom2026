Session overview

Division Chair: Brian Wu, University of Michigan Participant: Daniel Levinthal, Wharton School Participant: Constance E. Helfat, Tuck School of Business This plenary features Connie Helfat and Dan Levinthal in a dialogue on the two-way relationship between AI and the foundations of strategy. On one hand, core strategy concepts—capabilities, corporate scope, knowledge, imitation, positioning, firm boundaries, and industry structure—offer powerful lenses for understanding AI’s impact on firms and competition. On the other hand, AI provides a consequential new context that may challenge and refine how scholars think about these foundational constructs. The session will invite STR members to consider how the field can engage AI without losing sight of strategy’s enduring intellectual agenda.
