Session overview

This workshop brings together mid- and late-career researchers active in Social Issues in Management (SIM) research for a networking conversation. The workshop will feature a team of scholars who have been successful in navigating career and research challenges, including funding, methods, and Co-I relationship issues, dissemination issues (academic, practitioner, and social/public profile), and AI. Experienced scholars will be discussion facilitators at each table, but are not meant to be the authority; they are an idea generator and reflective listener. This workshop offers a unique opportunity to expand your professional network within the SIM community and explore new directions for impactful scholarship.
