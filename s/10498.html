Session overview

This two-part professional development workshop (PDW) is designed to provide tips on publishing in scholarship of teaching and learning (SoTL) journals and support authors in the preparation of their manuscripts. In Part I, open to all attendees, a panel of journal editors and associate editors from Academy of Management Learning & Education, Harvard Business Impact, Journal of Management Education, Management Learning, and Organizational Management Journal will provide general advice on manuscript development and answer questions about publishing in their respective journals. Part II will be restricted to pre-registrants, authors who submitted SoTL manuscripts on which they wanted detailed developmental feedback. These authors will meet in pre-assigned pairs for in-depth discussions with experienced reviewers (including current and former SoTL journal editors and associate editors). The reviewers assembled for Part II represent a variety of countries, institutions, journals, and research areas. We will accept manuscripts at any stage of completion, as long as they contain sufficient substance to warrant a constructive review. We will encourage the authors of papers not accepted for inclusion in the 2026 Academy of Management’s Management Education and Development Division conference program to attend both parts of the PDW and, especially, to register for Part II. Manuscript titles and abstracts will not appear in the program. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
