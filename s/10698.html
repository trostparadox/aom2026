Session overview

This inaugural PDW examines how artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming innovation by reshaping how individuals, teams, and organizations search, identify problems, and generate and evaluate solutions. Moving beyond viewing AI as a mere tool, the PDW centers on human–AI hybrid processes, and in particular, the tension in which humans manage AI while simultaneously being subtly managed by it. By considering both predictive and generative forms of AI, we highlight how these technologies reshape human expertise and knowledge in identifying opportunities, generating alternatives, and assessing innovation. The PDW brings together scholars working from both sides of this tension to examine how human–AI hybrid processes challenge conventional assumptions about how organizations innovate. The PDW consists of three parts and will feature leading scholars from disciplines including strategy, technology, innovation, operations, data science, and entrepreneurship. The PDW blends panel discussions and interactive roundtable sessions. Part I will feature a panel on micro- and meso-level perspectives and implications. Part II will highlight a panel on macro-level perspectives and implications. Part III will consist of roundtable discussions in which pre-registered junior participants engage with matched faculty to discuss their research projects in depth. Overall, the PDW brings together a diverse array of scholars to provide insights and develop a research agenda on integrating different forms of AI into innovation processes.
