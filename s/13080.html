Session overview

As AI becomes embedded in managerial work, a central question emerges: does it erode or enhance critical thinking? This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) reframes AI not as a substitute for human judgment, but as a cognitive partner that can enhance diagnostic rigor, sensemaking, and reflective decision-making in organizational contexts. This PDW is designed primarily for scholar–practitioners and management consultants, with secondary benefits for doctoral students, junior faculty, and organization leaders seeking to strengthen critical thinking and judgment in AI-enabled work. Participants engage in structured, hands-on activities using their own real-world challenges (e.g., research design dilemmas, consulting diagnoses, leadership decisions) to practice problem framing, hypothesis development, bias detection, and adaptive judgment (please bring laptop). The PDW combines brief expert framing with facilitated breakout sessions and whole group sensemaking. Participants leave with a portable set of AI-enabled prompts and guardrails that can be immediately applied in research, teaching, consulting, and organization leadership. By bridging perspectives from MCD, CTO, and TIM, the workshop fosters cross-disciplinary dialogue on responsible AI use, human judgment, and professional identity in an AI-augmented workplace.
