Session overview

Universities continue to expand the role of professors of practice and other educator-track faculty in delivering management education. Building on last year’s well-received PDW, this workshop offers an inclusive, interactive forum for educator-track faculty to connect, share experiences, and examine the evolving expectations of their roles within business schools. Framed around the overarching theme of Thriving as Educator-Track Faculty in Evolving Business Schools, participants will engage in facilitated roundtable discussions and hands-on activities organized around four interconnected areas: adapting professional expertise to academic roles, navigating institutional expectations and career pathways, maintaining and leveraging external partnerships, and collaborating across institutions and divisions. Aligned with the MED Division’s mission to Research, Innovate, and Inspire, the PDW focuses on professional integration, scholarly expectations, and career navigation for educator-track faculty. The session concludes with a synthesis of shared insights, a collaborative idea repository, and structured networking to support continued mentorship and cross-institutional connection.
