Session overview

The rapid technological changes in society have raised the urgency of better linking scholarship with practical relevance. This plenary explores several important questions: How is (and should) entrepreneurship research be interfacing with practitioners, what are the emerging priorities among different audiences, and what opportunities are there to elevate the relevance and impact of these interfaces? This plenary will seek to tackle these questions by engaging practitioners and academics seeking to engage in work that is mutually beneficial and/or reinforcing. We explore pathways to values- based pluralism and how the rapid technological shifts provide unique design opportunities for the academic industry, its diverse stakeholders, and forward-thinking scholarly inquiry. The plenary will examine the following themes: 1. Audience and purpose. How do researchers and practitioners better engage in research questions, designs, and output channels that address emerging needs? 2. Future horizons and tradeoffs. What will scholarly-practice interfaces look like in the coming years, and what tradeoffs should be considered in pursuing different paths forward?
