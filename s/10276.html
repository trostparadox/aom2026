Session overview

The aim of this symposium is to clarify and deepen academic understanding of the increasingly important practice of Student Knowledge Exchange (SKE). While the broader field of Knowledge Exchange (KE) is well discussed in the higher education (HE) literature, SKE represents an emergent and distinctly democratising form of KE, one that involves external partners, but centres on students. This panel symposium brings together a diverse group of international experts for a moderated and interactive discussion on the pedagogical implications, innovations, and broader impacts of SKE within contemporary higher education.
