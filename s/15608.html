Session overview

This PDW provides opportunities for scholars in the middle-to-late (i.e. post-tenure) stages of their career to learn from senior scholars (who have “been there”) and from each other (who are “going there”) about navigating the path post-tenure. A panel of senior scholars will share their insights on how to tackle evolving career demands, expectations and opportunities that emerge as they progress beyond the early career stage. Following a panel discussion, the panelists will join participants in roundtable discussions, which will provide opportunities for asking questions, sharing experiences, and developing new connections. In addition to discussing focused questions (e.g., next steps towards promotion to full professor, navigating different post-tenure career paths, balancing larger service roles), a key aim of this PDW will be to identify how the HR Division can better support members at this career stage. Note that the PDW is intended only for participants post-tenure and requires pre-registration. Seating is limited.
