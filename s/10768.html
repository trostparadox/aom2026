Session overview

There is growing consensus that AI-enabled platforms are qualitatively different from earlier generations of digital platforms. By introducing adaptive learning mechanisms, algorithmic agency, and new forms of value creation and coordination, AI-enabled architectures reshape how platforms orchestrate complementors, govern data flows, structure interdependencies, and expand into new markets. At the same time, these developments introduce unprecedented strategic challenges, as ambiguity in how AI technologies, platform roles, and value propositions are interpreted across actors amplifies divergent interests and complicates governance. Yet despite the growing literature on the leadership–platform nexus, the strategic leadership implications of AI-enabled platforms remain theoretically and empirically fragmented. This PDW brings together leading scholars to discuss how AI transforms strategic leadership at the individual, firm, and ecosystem levels. The PDW highlights three themes: (1) socio-cognitive and emotional dynamics in which leaders frame and legitimate AI across heterogeneous actors, (2) AI-driven diversification through algorithm-enabled expansion into new markets, services, and domains, and (3) governance challenges related to power concentration, accountability, and decentralization. Conceptual presentations are followed by interactive roundtable discussions. This PDW supports the development of a research agenda for strategically leading AI platforms in ways that accommodate ambiguity, distributed agency, and evolving platform and ecosystem configurations.
