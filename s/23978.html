Employee Lobbying: Data Assets and Friendly Employee Treatment

STR
Xinyi Wang (University of International Business and Economics); Wei (Luke) Zhang (PSL University); Xiaojia Zheng (University of International Business and Economics)
With increasing digitalization and data breaches, data protection has become a vital issue for firms. Based on the resource-based view, this paper proposes a novel strategy wherein firms leverage employee treatment to maintain competitive advantages in the digital era. Using the sample of listed firms in China, we find that firms with richer data assets exhibit significantly better employee treatment. Drawing on the ability-motivation-opportunity framework, we find that this relationship is negatively moderated by cyber risks and cooperative culture, and positively moderated by firms’ predation risks. Furthermore, the enhancement in employee treatment amplifies the value of data assets by fostering corporate innovation. This paper contributes to the literature on the resource-based view and has significant practical implications for strategic management in the digital economy.
