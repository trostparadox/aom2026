Beyond the Myth of Activist Homophily: Exploring the Paradoxes of All-Female Angel Networks

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 2:30PM–3:30PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 3
DEI

People

Authors: Salma FATTOUM University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Western Switzerland (HES-SO); Janice Byrne; Janine Swail The University of Auckland

Session overview

Policy makers increasingly assume that women investors will support women entrepreneurs through activist homophily, thereby reducing gender gaps in access to capital. Based on a qualitative study of 21 members of an all-female angel investor network in France, this paper challenges this assumption. We show that, despite operating in a women-only space designed for empowerment, female investors often reproduce (and sometimes amplify) gendered evaluation standards that disadvantage women founders. Drawing on a multilevel view of gender as a social structure, we demonstrate how the professional identity of the “rational investor” overrides gender solidarity, leading to social distancing strategies and the individualization of structural barriers. Our findings question representation-based solutions to inequality in entrepreneurial finance and highlight the resilience of institutionalized investment norms, even in gender-segregated settings.
