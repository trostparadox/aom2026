Session overview

This symposium presents four studies that advance understanding of career mobility across organizational boundaries. The studies share a theoretical orientation: returns to cross-boundary mobility depend on individual characteristics, organizational context, and the degree of cross-sector experience itself. Human capital theory provides a foundation for understanding skill transfer (Gibbons & Waldman, 2004), while research on career imprinting (Higgins, 2006; Marquis & Tilcsik, 2013) and transfer learning explains why such transfers often fail. Social capital perspectives illuminate how relationships developed in one sector may not translate to another (Broschak, 2004). Status characteristics theory reveals how identical experiences yield different returns depending on who holds them (Foschi, 2000).
