Session overview

This symposium asks what digital innovation, transformation, and entrepreneurship mean in the age of AI. These three concepts became well known in contexts such as platforms and the so-called 'SMACIT' technologies (social media, analytics, Internet of things). However, considerable technological developments have occurred since as is widely seen in research in information systems (IS), organization theory (OMT), and entrepreneurship (ENT). We bring together scholars working in these areas to discuss conceptual cross-fertilization across these areas and to outline what these three terms might come to mean in this day and age.
