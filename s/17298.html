Session overview

Securing research funding remains a critical pathway for Health Care Management (HCM) scholars to achieve tenure, promotion, and domain expertise. In a shifting political landscape characterized by federal budget uncertainties and evolving priorities at agencies like the NIH and AHRQ, diversifying one’s funding portfolio into non-governmental streams is essential for research sustainability. This session explores alternative pathways, including private foundations and philanthropic organizations (e.g., Arnold Ventures and Commonwealth Fund), non-profit organizations (e.g., Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute (PCORI)), state and local government grants (e.g., State Departments of Public Health or Medicaid agencies, and hybrid industry partnerships (e.g., pharmaceutical companies, National Institute for Health Care Management (NIHCM)), which offer vital resources but introduce unique challenges. For example, industry-funded research requires navigating complex manuscript approval processes, while PCORI requires specific patient-centered engagement models. This PDW provides an opportunity for scholars across the career spectrum to engage with a panel of experts to discuss these non-traditional opportunities. The session is designed as a moderated panel to probe best practices for aligning HCM scholarship with the missions of alternative funders. Time will be included for a robust Q&A.
