Session overview

Emerging economies’ dependence on the classic export led, manufacturing based development model that was premised upon relatively predictive and open global markets, unpriced carbon, and limited stakeholder intrusion is currently under strain. Instead, a qualitatively new phase of development shaped by three overlapping disruptions: digitalization, decarbonization, and the democratisation of business are fast reshaping the rules of the game. This new order raises fundamental questions about the assumptions and boundary conditions underpinning existing theories of emerging economies and prompts a direct inquiry: to what extent do extant management scholarship and practice in this domain require reassessment, and along which dimensions. Through multidisciplinary lenses of management, the session aims to highlight emerging theoretical challenges as well as applied opportunities for businesses and societies. Participants will engage with leading scholars, learn from cross-domain insights, and co-create a roadmap for future research and practice, especially in the Indian context. The workshop combines theoretical perspectives, real-world case studies, comparative cross-country insights, and interactive discussions to illuminate how responsible, adaptive, and future-ready organizations can be developed in an era of continuous disruption.
