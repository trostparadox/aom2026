Session overview

Blockchain-based ecosystems and decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs) have emerged as a frontier case for understanding how organizing evolves when governance is partially delegated to digital infrastructures. Yet, despite growing interest, core organizational questions remain unresolved: How is coordination achieved without hierarchy? How does authority emerge—and become legitimate—when decision rights are distributed across communities, protocols, and expertise? How do accountability and judgment operate when “rules” are encoded, but contested in practice? This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) uses blockchain as an empirical setting to advance broader debates in organization and management research on collective agency, ecosystem orchestration, algorithmic governance, institutional work, socio-materiality, and expertise and valuation. The PDW features a closed-loop format designed to foster theory–practice interaction, followed by development tracks and meta-integrations that articulate transferable insights beyond the blockchain domain (e.g., platforms, AI governance, hybrid agency, digital innovation). Participants will leave with refined research questions and conceptual insights emerging from a sustained, dialogic engagement between theory and practice, informed by empirical insights from ongoing governance experiments in decentralized digital organizations.
