Session overview

Firms increasingly link corporate strategy decisions—including acquisitions, divestitures, and restructuring—to their sustainability efforts. Yet the net impact of these strategic choices on sustainability outcomes remains uncertain. Does corporate strategy function as a vehicle for substantive sustainability transformation, or does it primarily enable symbolic alignment with stakeholder expectations? This panel symposium brings together leading scholars who have examined corporate sustainability from different perspectives and will engage in a structured, comparative debate. Organized as an Oxford-style debate, the symposium contrasts competing views on how firms deploy corporate strategy in response to sustainability challenges and examines the conditions under which such strategic actions generate substantive sustainability outcomes rather than symbolic or incremental responses. Through direct engagement and synthesis, panelists clarify the theoretical mechanisms linking corporate strategy and sustainability and identify promising directions for future research at the intersection of corporate strategy and broader societal impact.
