Session overview

In this PDW four leading public intellectuals and management scholars will share their perspective on how we can transform business, education and specifically business education to support flourishing of human beings and all life on the planet. As the polycrisis is intensifying, massive disruptions are already on its way in the form of AI, electrification, climate change, health and disease etc. Business and Education are mainly still operating under assumptions from the past. How can we as management scholars and teachers contribute to support the ongoing transitions to create a world that works for everyone as Buckminster Fuller called it. For many professors and students such ambitions sound wildly naive while others are drowning in cynicism. The speakers will provide perspectives that PDW participants will explore and workshop to identify workable approaches for research, pedagogy and public engagement.
