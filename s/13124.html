Creating Value with AI in Management Consulting in Academia: Leveraging AI for Organizational Impact

Tuesday, Aug 4 2026 · 8:00AM–9:30AM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Parlor P1
MC

People

Panelists: Donna L Ogle Rockford University; Gokhan Turgut Universite de Quebec a Montreal; Dan Bumblauskas Drury University; Arnum Cohran Evolution Strategies, LLC; Russell Ogle Russell Ogle Consulting, LLC

Session overview

This session is designed to offer members of the Management Consulting Division a rich, interdisciplinary examination of AI’s role in driving value creation across research, teaching, and broader organizational impact within management consulting academia. This panel explores how artificial intelligence delivers real value in management consulting as an academic discipline by enhancing teaching effectiveness, accelerating research insights, and enabling transfer of skills and knowledge to organizations. Each panelist focuses on how AI-driven tools and practices translate into tangible outcomes, fostering academic excellence and societal impact.
