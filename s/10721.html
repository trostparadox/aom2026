Session overview

Navigating the early years of a tenure-track position presents unique challenges for junior faculty as they strive to establish themselves in academia. Even in research-oriented schools, there are expectations for teaching and service that junior scholars may not have been prepared for. Balancing these demands while developing an academic identity can feel overwhelming, especially in the competitive and evolving landscape of business schools. With the added complexity of institutional expectations and the realities of the academic job market, junior faculty often face barriers to achieving sustainable success in their roles. While the institutions themselves may provide some support, the broader academic community and especially AOM are important resources for academic faculty that can help address these challenges. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) is tailored for junior faculty transitioning into tenure-track positions. The workshop aims to provide practical strategies to help participants thrive in their early academic careers, as well as opportunities to share and discuss their experiences with each other, building a sense of belonging. Topics include understanding the diverse needs of academic institutions, aligning personal and institutional goals, developing effective teaching and research strategies, and preparing for tenure evaluations. Participants will also gain insights into managing competing priorities, building professional networks, and navigating institutional politics. Through interactive discussions and networking, this PDW will empower junior faculty to move beyond survival mode, establish a strong foundation, and confidently advance in their academic careers.
