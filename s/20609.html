Session overview

Integrative reviews have become a new, important approach to building theories. However, many academic colleagues lack sufficient insights into how to conduct such reviews, such as how to generate appropriate questions that motivate the review, how to integrate fragmented insights for a coherent theory, and how to navigate the publication process. The need for greater understanding motivated us to propose this PDW. We aim to offer a constructive forum that helps participants deepen their understanding of integrative reviews as a theory-building approach, learn from diverse experiences of publishing integrative reviews across journals, and engage with our panelists on questions or challenges encountered in their own publication journeys. This PDW will feature panelists’ firsthand experiences from their roles as authors and editors, sharing their unique stories about publishing review articles in leading management and psychology journals. Panelists will also engage in a roundtable session with the participants to explore topic-focused questions. The PDW will conclude with a report-out of key takeaways and actionable insights for conducting and publishing integrative reviews.
