The Impacts of Brexit on UK International Trade With EU and Non-EU Countries: A Firm-Level Analysis

Authors: Rekha Rao-Nicholson Greenshoots Pvt Ltd; Israa Kishk University of Northampton

Session overview

Following Brexit, a substantial body of literature emerged on its potential impacts. Using firm-level analysis, our study examines how Brexit impacted the UK’s international trade with EU and non-EU countries. Unlike previous studies that used simulations and aggregate trade flows to estimate the effects of Brexit on UK international trade, this study examines the actual impact of Brexit using firm-level data from 2008 to 2024, covering UK-based companies engaged in foreign trade. Two main research questions are addressed: First, what is the impact of Brexit on the UK firms’ trade with the EU? Second, what is the effect of the UK firms’ trade with the rest of the world? We find that, following Brexit, UK firms’ sales to the EU decreased relative to sales to the rest of the world, whereas UK firms’ assets in the EU increased relative to those in the rest of the world. The examination of the impacts of Brexit on UK international trade helps address the post-Brexit uncertainty affecting firms and provides policymakers with valuable insights to develop suitable policy tools.
