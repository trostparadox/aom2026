Session overview

This PDW will provide a forum for discussing relationships among research methods and theoretical advancement. Research spanning qualitative, quantitative, and critical management studies, as well as the micro, macro, and meso traditions of management scholarship, is welcome. The session will consider a range of possible method-theory linkages including how new developments in methods can lead to new theorizing, how combinations of methods and/or methods and theory might produce new insights, how new designs, measures, tools, and technologies as part of their methodological toolset can offer significant potential to advance theory, how methods can reinvigorate theoretical bodies, and how addressing challenges and current questions in research methods might have theoretical implications. Participation is by invitation. 5-page proposals are due by 30 June. Proposals should be sent to Don Bergh (dbergh@du.edu).
