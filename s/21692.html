Session overview

This Professional Development Workshop is designed as a generative, book-in-progress PDW supporting an edited volume currently under active development and in conversation with Palgrave Macmillan, targeted for the Palgrave Studies in Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Indigeneity in Business series. The proposed volume, Sacred Futures: Reimagining Spirit, Power, and Resilience in Organizational Life – Decolonial Wisdoms for Grit, Inclusion, and Transformative Leadership, responds to a growing global urgency: the need to rethink diversity, equity, and inclusion beyond compliance-driven or depoliticized frameworks, and toward spiritually grounded, decolonial, and justice-centered organizational scholarship. Rather than functioning as a paper presentation workshop, this PDW invites participants into the process of co-creating a scholarly book. The workshop is structured to help scholars conceptualize, refine, and ethically position potential book chapters aligned with the volume’s intellectual vision and MSR’s core commitments. Participants will engage with decolonial, spiritual, and indigenous epistemologies that challenge Western-centric rationalist assumptions; receive structured guidance on theory, method, voice, and positionality; and join an international scholarly network committed to spiritually engaged, culturally humble, and socially transformative MSR research. This PDW explicitly advances MSR’s developmental agenda by centering knowing beyond rational knowing, plural wisdom traditions, transformative pedagogy, and organizational and societal renewal.
