Modding Community as a Source of External Consultancy Knowledge for Innovations Enhancement.

Author: Magdalena Hofman-Kohlmeyer University of Economics in Katowice

Session overview

User innovation has migrated from specialist “lead-user” toolkits to mass-scale, digitally networked arenas where millions of consumers routinely extend, hack and remix commercial offerings. In the computer game market this phenomenon is called „game modding”. Casual players are not the only final recipient of a product but also participate in its development. They play the role of active consultants for the gaming market. Players also engage in co-creation of brand communication. They introduce the branded product or logotype to the existing games. Presented research was aimed on answering the question: What drives consumer to co-create innovations? In order to meet the assumed goal the author at first caried out 10 in-depth interviews with computer game players and experience representatives of game industry. Second, the author conducted qualitative content analysis of statements on online forum (2 890 posts) related to 20 branded game modifications. In analysis of gathered online forum posts the authors were inspired by inductive approach of Gioia, Corley and Hamilton (2013) methodology. research results reveals four following domain-specific drivers of players engagement in innovations development: fun improvement, brand familiarity, financial benefits and legal concerns.
