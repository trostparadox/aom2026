Session overview

The Intelligent Virtual Agents and Behavioral Dynamics in Immersive Worlds PDW is a highly interactive workshop designed for OB scholars who seek to better understand how AI-driven intelligent virtual agents (IVAs) operate within immersive virtual reality (VR) work environments. This PDW focuses on how OB scholars can conceptualize, study, and theorize IVAs, while explicitly embracing an interdisciplinary orientation. Drawing on perspectives from OB, CTO, and related fields, international experts will share conceptual frameworks, empirical insights, and methodological approaches for examining IVAs in immersive organizational contexts. The workshop follows a structured agenda centered on three expert presentations. Each presentation is followed by a brief question-and-answer exchange with the audience, and subsequently transitions into facilitated roundtable discussions to encourage in-depth engagement and peer exchange. The workshop concludes with an overarching plenary discussion that integrates key insights across presentations and highlights implications for future interdisciplinary research on human–IVA interaction in immersive organizational contexts. The intent of this PDW is to foster a collective learning experience in which presenters, discussants, and participants jointly develop shared conceptual language and actionable research strategies. Attendees will leave equipped with frameworks and tools to advance interdisciplinary research on IVAs in immersive organizational settings that pave the way for future research avenues.
