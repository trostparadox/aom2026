Session overview

The most influential views of the firm do not incorporate the natural environment in their theorizing. Thus, I propose a new corporate governance framework that prioritizes reducing pressure on the Planetary Boundaries. Living in a “polycrisis” demands appreciating each crisis’s distinct nature. While geopolitical tensions and wars are expressions of conflict within our species. The people involved may be quite different – in terms of nationality, culture, and values – but still belong to the homo sapiens species. Another set of crises extends beyond us and concerns the profound alterations to the natural environment. Building on this distinction, I suggest Earth Value Maximization Theory (EVMT) to explain how some boards of directors prioritize nature. This is not introduced as a competing alternative to other views of the firm. Instead, EVMT is envisioned as an essential first-order condition that visionary boards of directors adopt to preserve nature. As the integrity of the Earth system is a necessary condition for human and non-human lives, maximizing Earth value is a precondition for boards of directors.
