AI as a Reflective Partner: Enhancing Investment Decision Quality

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 5
ENT

People

Authors: Lorena Stelzer University of Paderborn; Sylvia Hubner-Benz University of Paderborn

Session overview

Early-stage investment decisions are characterized by high uncertainty, limited information, and time pressure, making them particularly susceptible to heuristic reasoning and systematic cognitive biases. While prior research has increasingly promoted AI-driven decision-making systems as neutral and objective substitutes for human judgment, emerging evidence suggests that algorithmic systems can reproduce or shift biases rather than eliminate them. This study advances a human-centered perspective that conceptualizes artificial intelligence as a reflective partner rather than a decision substitute. We argue that AI can improve investment decision quality by scaffolding in-the-moment reflective cognition, prompting decision-makers to scrutinize assumptions, evaluate evidentiary strength, and consider alternative explanations while preserving human agency. Methodologically, the study employs a between-subjects experimental design comparing unaided investment decision-making with AI-supported reflective decision-making in early-stage venture evaluations. Decision quality is assessed relative to an expert benchmark and complemented by measures of bias and decision rationale quality. The study contributes to entrepreneurial decision-making and human–AI collaboration by demonstrating how AI-supported reflection can enhance judgment under uncertainty without undermining human authority.
