Session overview

The human mind can be a source of flourishing or suffering for oneself and others. As technological advancements amplify the scale and speed of individual action, individual decisions increasingly carry significant organizational and societal consequences. This context raises a critical question for conscious leadership development: how can individuals be supported as self-leaders who can skillfully regulate their inner experiences so that the mind becomes a consistent source of clarity, discernment, and constructive action rather than distress or harm? This 60-minute session introduces participants to Miracle of Mind (MoM) meditation, along with its spiritual foundations and emerging research. Following direct experiential practice, participants will examine the theoretical implications of this contemplative approach for leadership development and collaboratively co-design empirical research questions. Through this exploration, the session aims to foster a community of scholars and practitioners and discuss how the MoM may be integrated into research and teaching related to leadership development.
