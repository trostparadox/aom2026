Session overview

We have successfully organized the Behind-the-Publication PDW at the Academy of Management over the past four years, accumulating over 500 participants. Each year, we bring in a new set of panelists to share their experiences with publishing at top management journals. For AOM 2026 in Philadelphia, we continue with the same format that was well-received in AOM 2025 in Copenhagen, with a moderated Q&A session as well as an audience Q&A. Moreover, we would like to thank the OB division for sponsoring this PDW session since 2022. We hope this PDW will offer even more benefits to the OB community in 2026.
