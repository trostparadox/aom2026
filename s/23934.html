The Double-Edged Sword Effect of Data Assetization on Exploratory Innovation

TIM
Yapu Zhao; Qian Xu (College of Science & Technology Ningbo University); Shengyang Wang (Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics)
In the digital era, China’s “Specialized, Refined, Distinctive, and Innovative” (SRDI) enterprises, epitomizing focused niche leaders, face the strategic imperative of leveraging data for exploratory innovation. While data assetization is considered as a key to digital upgrading, its impact on the exploratory innovation crucial for these firms remains paradoxical. Integrating the Resource-Based View (RBV) and Attention-Based View (ABV), this study theorizes and tests an inverted U-shaped relationship between data assetization and exploratory innovation within Chinese SRDI enterprises. We posit that moderate assetization builds dynamic capabilities, but excessive formalization induces cognitive rigidity and attention lock-in. Furthermore, we demonstrate that this inverted U-shaped relationship is shaped by two key contingencies. Supplier concentration steepens it by simultaneously strengthening the enabling and constraining forces across both phases of the curve, whereas industry competition intensity flattens it by exerting a countervailing pressure that moderates these forces. These findings advance the understanding of the digital paradox in specialized SMEs and offer critical insights for managers and policymakers navigating the data-driven transformation of industrial niche leaders.
