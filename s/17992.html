CAU

People

Participants: Abel Adekola Wilkes University; Kedir Assefa Tessema Wilkes University

Session overview

Geopolitical and institutional shifts are redrawing where higher education collaboration concentrates, yet many partnerships still rely on inherited, asymmetric designs. This caucus convenes scholars to map emerging partnership geographies (e.g., BRICS and regional hubs), critique the architectures now taking shape, and prototype alternatives that advance symmetric interdependence and epistemic autonomy.
