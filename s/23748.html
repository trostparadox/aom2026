Digitalization Meets Traditional Culture: Institutional Complexity and Rural Entrepreneurship

ENT
daobang yang (Guangdong University of Technology); ZIQIAN WEN (Guangdong University of Technology)
The interplay between digitalization and traditional culture provides a unique context to unpack institutional complexity in emerging economies’ rural entrepreneurship. Rural entrepreneurship is a vital engine for regional vitality and poverty alleviation, contributing to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Yet the deep embedding of rural informal institutions introduces uncertainty, forming an unresolved "institutional black box". Using 2018– 2020 panel data from 1,532 Chinese counties, we find digitalization significantly promotes rural entrepreneurship, with robustness confirmed via double machine learning and the Bartik instrumental variable approach. Confucian culture positively moderates this effect by aligning with formal institutions, whereas clan culture exerts a negative moderating role due to formal institutional conflicts. This study advances Institutional Complexity Theory and offers actionable insights for emerging economies.
