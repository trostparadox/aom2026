Session overview

Serious games are increasingly used as structured idea management systems to address complex sustainability challenges by engaging diverse stakeholders in experiential learning and collective problem solving. A defining feature of effective serious games is coopetition: participants collaborate to achieve shared sustainability goals. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW) examines how the Greenopoly game helps to create more sustainable ideas for different questions. Drawing on the development and empirical testing of the Greenopoly game, this workshop conceptualizes serious games as gamified idea management systems that integrate sustainability principles. Empirical simulation studies comparing Greenopoly with traditional brainstorming demonstrate that Greenopoly significantly outperforms conventional methods in both the quantity and sustainability potential of generated ideas, while maintaining comparable levels of innovation and implementation feasibility. Participants also report higher engagement, satisfaction, and deeper understanding of sustainability and circular economy principles.
