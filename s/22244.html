Differences Between Entrepreneurs’ Well-being Across the Entrepreneurial Process

Philadelphia Marriott Downtown — Poster Wall 6
ENT

People

Author: Sophia Marie Quick University of Mississippi

Session overview

This paper highlights the differences experienced between entrepreneurs of different types at different stages across the entrepreneurial process and how those differences may affect entrepreneurial well-being. Solo, partnered, and family entrepreneurs experience unique challenges that require more specific forms of support, and often determines which areas of the entrepreneurial process they tend to excel in or require additional help. The implications of this research indicate that entrepreneurs are unique and although they may face more taxing challenges than other forms of employees, there are specific, actionable ways in which organizations built to support entrepreneurs can better provide help where their clients need it most.
