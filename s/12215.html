Session overview

Organizational life frequently presents individuals and teams with seemingly contradictory demands. In this symposium, we adopt a paradox lens to examine how individuals and teams can effectively navigate such tensions and contradictions by engaging in paradoxical thinking. This involves acknowledging the tension between paradoxical elements, understanding that combining both elements overcomes the downsides of either element alone, and integrating them to discover new solutions. We demonstrate that doing so has positive implications for how individuals, teams, and organizations can manage the conflicting demands presented by interdependence, the use of generative AI, and workplace offences in organizations.
