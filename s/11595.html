Session overview

Research on career transitions is burgeoning but remains fragmented, siloed, and imbalanced. To contribute to a more coherent understanding of career transitions, this symposium takes a lifespan perspective and brings together five studies examining both internal, self-related and external, contextual factors in different predictable and idiosyncratic career transition across the lifespan. Utilizing diverse and rigor research designs (i.e., longitudinal study, experiment, scale development and literature review), we introduce new perspectives to explore key mechanisms, challenges, and adaptive strategies that characterize different types of career transitions, spanning from school-to-work transition to retirement transition. We hope to advance theoretical frameworks on career transitions and offer opportunities to stimulate an integrative dialogue for individuals, organizations, and career counseling practitioners.
