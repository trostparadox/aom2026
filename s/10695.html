Session overview

Artificial intelligence is reshaping global strategy by transforming how firms build capabilities, enter foreign markets, and orchestrate cross-border structures and governance. While generative AI has attracted enormous attention, broader forms of AI, including predictive analytics, automation, and optimization, are redefining global competition, international scaling, and the rise of AI-centric multinational enterprises. At the same time, firms operate across increasingly diverse institutional environments marked by geopolitical frictions, ethical concerns, and regulatory uncertainty. Scholars in international business, global strategy, and innovation have begun to examine these dynamics, but existing conversations remain fragmented (Volberda et al., 2021). This PDW brings together leading scholars to explore how AI reshapes three core dimensions of global strategy: inside-out capability formation, outside-in deployment across markets, and boundary-spanning structures and governance. Through short conceptual pitches and roundtable discussions, the session highlights emerging theories, methodological approaches, and research opportunities related to AI-driven internationalization, AI-native ventures, AI-enabled decision-making, and global governance. The PDW is linked to the Special Issue Globalizing Intelligence: Orchestrating Artificial Intelligence for Global Advantage in Global Strategy Journal (submission deadline: September 30, 2026).
