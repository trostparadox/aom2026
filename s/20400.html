Session overview

Workplace nature contact and biophilic design (bringing nature into the workplace) are rapidly increasing as topics of occupational research and a multi-billion-dollar industry. Nature offers beneficial effects for physical, mental, and social functioning; however, these effects are not universal, and careless integration of nature can lead to harm for employees and organizations. This panel brings together interdisciplinary experts to discuss the paradox of nature at work – where it helps versus harms – and to bring management scholars and practitioners into the forefront of this emerging area of research and practice.
