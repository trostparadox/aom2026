Urban Commons and Citizenship in Small Business Communities

Authors: Cheng Wang California State University-Sacramento; Dali Ma Drexel University; Richardson Dilworth Drexel University

Session overview

Based on a longitudinal fieldwork study of the biannual Baltimore Avenue’s Dollar Stroll Event in Philadelphia from 2017 – 2022, this study unfolds the dynamic of collective action in the creation of urban commons and enactment of urban citizenship in the context of small business communities. Drawing on five years of repeated field observations, event documentation, and semi-structured interviews of community actors, this study demonstrates the dynamic nature of urban commons governance and urban citizenship practices. This study reveals how formal rules of the business events and informal social norms in the community together facilitate the revitalization of a business community and encourage civic engagement. These findings highlight that in urban cities, small business communities serve the dual role of economic actors and civic actors, and neighborhood business event is an important means to achieve both roles. This study offers theoretical advancement and practical implications for urban sociology, entrepreneurship, and community studies.
