Session overview

Generative AI offers genuine affordances for case-based teaching, including dynamic scenario generation, expanded facilitation capacity, and accelerated feedback cycles. Yet these benefits are efficiencies by default. Without deliberate design, they risk displacing the productive struggle through which learners develop managerial judgment. This How-to Workshop offers educators a diagnostic framework for navigating this tension. The framework organises the instructor's work into three functions: curriculum and content development, discussion orchestration, and feedback and assessment. GenAI intersects with each differently, creating distinct opportunities and risks. Participants will apply this framework to their own teaching through structured exercises and peer exchange. Working individually and in small groups, they will complete a diagnostic audit of their course, identifying where GenAI threatens learning and where it offers genuine benefit. They will prototype a concrete redesign strategy for one high-stakes teaching moment and articulate a specific commitment for what they will change. While case-based pedagogy anchors the session, the framework transfers to any active learning approach predicated on productive struggle. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
