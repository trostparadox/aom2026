Integrating Entrepreneurial Education Ecosystems Into Basic Schooling: A Theoretical Review

MED
Bruno Luis Avila Freischlag; Daniel Paulino Teixeira Lopes (Centro Federal de Educação Tecnológica de Minas Gerais); Bruno Anicet Bittencourt; Eduarda Corrêa Moravia; Cacilda MARIA Almeida (Sebrae Minas Gerais)
This study investigates the key dimensions that define entrepreneurial education ecosystems (EEEs) within basic education and explores their implications for research, policy, and prac-tice. Through a systematic literature review of approximately 400 studies, five core dimen-sions of EEEs emerged: (1) Governance; (2) Stakeholder Engagement; (3) Infrastructure and Resources; (4) Educational Design; and (5) Entrepreneurial Culture. The analysis highlights how these dimensions can enhance practice in primary and secondary education by promoting horizontal governance, fostering territorial partnerships, integrating experiential and interdis-ciplinary learning, establishing educator networks, developing continuous professional devel-opment for teachers, and adapting curricula to local contexts. Theoretically, this study ex-tends existing entrepreneurial ecosystem frameworks to the basic education level by propos-ing five propositions grounded in the key dimensions identified in the analysis, thereby offer-ing structured guidance for the further development and consolidation of theory in this do-main. Practically, it offers actionable recommendations for educators, school leaders, and policymakers. Overall, this review contributes to understanding how entrepreneurial educa-tion can be effectively embedded in basic education to create innovative, inclusive, and con-textually relevant learning environments.
