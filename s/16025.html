Session overview

Second generation immigrants comprise a rapidly growing segment of the workforce, yet their experiences in the work environment remain elusive in organizational DEI literature. Existing research has focused mainly on first generation immigrants, leaving little understanding of the workplace experiences that second generation immigrants, or the children of immigrants, experience. This PDW examines how identity complexity, acculturation expectations, and microaggressions shape the workplace experiences of second generation employees. Using the Conservation of Resources (COR) theory, we explore how repeated exposure to subtle bias and identity-based pressure contributes to over-commitment, stress, and burnout. This further emphasizes the need to better understand second generation immigrants as a distinct group within organizations, that should be addressed in DEI research, and through appropriate human resources practices. This discussion-based and interactive workshop aims to bring interested scholars together to explore organizational and DEI research streams as well as strategic human resources practices that can minimize the potential negative experiences faced by second generation immigrants.
