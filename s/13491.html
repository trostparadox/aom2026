Session overview

We follow up on our highly successful "OMT meets STS" PDW at AOM 2025 in Copenhagen by once again assembling a group of reputed scholars operating at the interstices of organization and management theory (OMT) and science and technology studies (STS). This time, our proposed professional development workshop has a thematic focus: imaginaries, innovation and futures. Building on Koselleck's notion of "the future" as a product of modernity’s rupture from cyclical time and its increasing orientation toward progress, prediction, and control, as well as Jasanoff’s concept of sociotechnical imaginaries —collectively held and institutionally stabilized visions of desirable futures realized through science and technology—this workshop explores how imagined futures shape innovation, and how innovation, in turn, shapes what futures are imaginable. The PDW aims to open a multidisciplinary dialogue between OMT, STS and many related fields (SAP, CTO, ENT, MH, CMS among others), inviting participants to theorize innovation not only as a technological or organizational process but as a temporal and narrative practice.
