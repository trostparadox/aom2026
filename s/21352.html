Session overview

This ongoing study investigates how spatial and relational proximities within innovation ecosystems evolve into location capabilities that enhance firms’ internationalization performance. Using a multiple case study approach in the innovation ecosystem of Florianópolis (SC, Brazil), the research identifies new dimensions of proximity — digital (spatial) and temporal (relational) — and explains their role as location-specific resources that firms internalize, transform, and exploit. The findings contribute to the International Business literature by expanding the concept of location capability as a dynamic capability, to proximity theory by adding new dimensions, and to innovation ecosystem studies by clarifying how ecosystem resources translate into firms’ specific advantage (FSA) for internationalization.
