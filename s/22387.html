Session overview

The Critical Management Studies (CMS) Division of the Academy of Management is pleased to host a ‘Meet the Editors’ plenary session, offering a valuable opportunity to engage with leading journal editors in our field. This session provides insights into editorial priorities, submission processes, and navigating peer review, helping demystify the publishing landscape for scholars at all career stages. It is a chance to connect more closely with outlets shaping critical scholarship and to better understand how to position your work. Reflecting CMS’s commitment to challenging mainstream management approaches, the session highlights research that critically examines organizations, power, and inequality, fostering theoretically rich and socially impactful scholarship.
