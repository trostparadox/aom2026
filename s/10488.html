Session overview

The interest in informal communication in the workplace and the relationships that run on it continue is flourishing, though more is to be done to understand the impact they can have on employees. In this symposium, four papers investigate communication and relationships at work to understand how employees can react to being on the receiving end of interaction and the motivations in communicating to and building relationships with their coworkers. Specifically, these papers look how feeling coworkers are gossiping about you can elicit distinct psychological states in employees, how the credibility of gossip can shape emotional and behavioral reactions to the target, when and why employees are motivated to disclose disruptive family events to coworkers or supervisors, and the different friendship expectations workers hold when building relationships within the workplace.
