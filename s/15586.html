Session overview

Our goal is to assemble a focused session that examines how firms engage with OSS communities, how organizational structures and managerial practices shape that engagement, and how participation in OSS feeds back into firm-level innovation and capability development. Relevant work may include research on firm–community coordination, governance mechanisms that support productive collaboration with external contributors, cross-boundary knowledge flows, and the strategic or organizational implications of contributing to externally governed innovation ecosystems. While we hope to gather presenters working directly on these themes, we also anticipate that the scope may broaden slightly to include closely related topics on firms’ interactions with open innovation or community-driven development contexts.
