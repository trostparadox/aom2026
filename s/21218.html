Session overview

The objective of this doctoral activity is to create a dynamic learning environment that fosters knowledge sharing and addresses key questions to help participants advance in their academic journey. The Consortium aims to enhance the quality of doctoral proposals while also serving as a valuable networking space, connecting doctoral students with peers and experts in the fields of economics and business.
