Session overview

Leadership scholarship increasingly recognizes that organizational effectiveness cannot be separated from human experience, emotional awareness, and relational capacity. Yet dominant leadership cultures often privilege analytical cognition, task execution, and control, reinforcing fear-based norms that limit creativity, learning, and flourishing. This professional development workshop introduces the LOVING Leader Framework, an adaptive leadership model that frames love as a disciplined leadership practice and positions the integration of heart and head as the primary pathway to human and organizational flourishing. The framework integrates six interrelated practices: Listening, Opening, Vulnerability, Integrating, Nurturing, and Gift. Grounded in Emotional Intelligence as its primary theoretical foundation and informed by Adaptive Leadership, love-centered leadership is understood not as a competency set or performance strategy, but as a humanizing relational force that reshapes how people experience work, leadership, and themselves. Empathy functions as both the foundation and the fruit of this leadership paradigm, emerging through practice rather than prescription. Participants will engage in guided practices, reflective inquiry, and structured peer dialogue designed to surface fear-based leadership norms that are culturally reinforced within organizations. These norms narrow attention, privilege task-dominant cognition, and undermine the relational and emotional capacities necessary for creativity and flourishing. Practice is treated as the primary mechanism for capacity building, enabling leaders to integrate analytical and relational intelligence over time. This PDW is designed for scholars, educators, and practitioner-researchers interested in leadership development, organizational culture, Emotional Intelligence, and integrative pedagogy. Participants will leave with a transferable framework and practical tools for research, teaching, and leadership practice that support sustained human and organizational flourishing.
