Session overview

Interested in getting published? Wish you knew what the editors of the leading journals would advise on getting published? Your wish is our command! Come join us as we ask the editors of our leading journals in international business, management, and strategy to share their personal approaches to getting published, along with their observations as editors. Our PDW will allow a rare opportunity to hear publishing advice from some of the most respected scholars in our field. We will also include time for participants to ask questions to our editor panel. All are welcome.
