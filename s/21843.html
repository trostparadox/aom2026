Session overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly reshaping business education, research, and administrative processes, yet adoption across schools of business remains uneven. Employer evidence shows that AI is already delivering measurable workplace impact, increasing pressure on business schools to accelerate AI-relevant curricula and capability building (Digital Education Council, 2025). Deans play a pivotal but under-examined role in translating this disruption into coordinated institutional action by aligning school-level initiatives with university strategies and developing faculty and staff AI competencies. This Professional Development Workshop (PDW), designed as a Practical Masterclass, targets Deans, associate Deans, program directors, and senior faculty leaders. Participants engage in structured, team-based SWOT analyses across Accounting, Marketing, etc. to diagnose discipline-specific AI opportunities and risks in teaching, research, and operations. These insights are synthesized into a master, cross-disciplinary SWOT framework. The PDW also introduces an AI literacy framework and a systems-oriented leadership model linking Dean decision-making, training investments, and outcomes. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
