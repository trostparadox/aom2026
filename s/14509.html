Geopolitical Risk at Home: A New Motivational Lens on Export Behavior

Saturday, Aug 1 2026 · 11:30AM–12:30PM
Loews Philadelphia Hotel — Poster Wall 7
SIM

People

Authors: Nianyu ZHOU Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University; Mengshu Cui Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University; Chenguang Hu Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University; Patrick Peng Wang Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University

Session overview

This study examines how firms respond to rising home-country geopolitical risk by engaging in export-based institutional escape. Drawing on the institutional escape perspective, we argue that home-country geopolitical risk motivates firms to expand exports to mitigate external institutional uncertainty. Using cross-country data from 27,610 business owners across 32 countries during 2019–2021, we find that home-country geopolitical risk is positively associated with firms’ export intensity. Moreover, this relationship is strengthened when business owners hold political connections and when firms operate in high-technology sectors. We contribute to the geopolitical risk literature by expanding the focus to home-country geopolitical risk and highlighting the motivational role of geopolitical risk in international trade.
