Session overview

The goal of this PDW is to conduct a doctoral student consortium (DSC) to connect, inspire, and inform doctoral students about elements leading to success in their scholarship, teaching, service, and lives as academics, especially in the area of Social Issues in Management (SIM). To follow tradition, we anticipate kicking off the DSC by hosting a dinner the evening before the daylong event. The DSC is designed to be interactive, with ample time for students to ask questions of the panelists and interact with each other, building the SIM community. SIM-affiliated scholars will share their knowledge, participate in panel discussions, and engage with students. Participating students will develop a toolkit for successfully finishing their doctoral programs and entering their professional career.
