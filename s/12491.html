Use of Optimization Techniques in Measuring Path Dependence

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 10:00AM–11:00AM
Sheraton Philadelphia Downtown Hotel — Poster Wall 4
OMT

People

Author: Abhishek Srivastava Ball State University

Session overview

We argue that the concept of optimal path is embedded in the idea of path dependence, whether we consider an evolutionary perspective or an agentic view of a firm (e.g., resource-based view, dynamic capabilities). We present a network of paths that fit a deterministic view and contrast it with the arrangement of organizational decisions and actions for path creation that assumes greater freedom for managers. We provide a hypothetical example of how innovation capability may build over time with accumulation of resources in a certain sequence. We compute the optimal path for a firm to maximize overall synergy using Simplex algorithm in the Solver add-in module of Excel, which would be easily accessible for organizational scholars. We provide another hypothetical example of acquisitions of several target firms and use the non-linear evolutionary algorithm to find the optimal path for an acquiring firm that minimizes the dissimilarity across targets. The computation of optimal paths may be used in organizational research for comparison with the actual path followed by firms in research studies to evaluate the role of environmental factors or effectiveness of decision making.
