The Impact of Corporate Data Asset Disclosure on Investment Efficiency

STR
Zhou Tong; Ying Zhang
Against the backdrop of the digital economy and the market-oriented reform of data fac-tors, this study, based on the annual report texts of Chinese A-share listed companies from 2007 to 2023, explores the intrinsic relationship between data asset information disclosure and corporate investment efficiency by constructing disclosure indicators through text analysis and conducting empirical tests with relevant models, aiming to clarify the mechanism, transmis-sion pathways, and constraints of data asset disclosure on investment efficiency; the results show that data asset information disclosure significantly enhances corporate investment effi-ciency (especially in suppressing over-investment), with financing constraints playing a partial mediating role (alleviating information asymmetry to improve the external financing envi-ronment), media attention negatively moderating the relationship (strengthening the inhibitory effect on investment deviation), management myopia positively moderating it (weakening the promoting effect), and these effects being more significant in enterprises with higher digital finance development and better overall information disclosure quality; the study expands the research on the economic consequences of non-financial information disclosure and provides empirical references for regulators and enterprises, while its limitation lies in the insufficient semantic depth mining of disclosed content in indicator construction, with future research di-rections including optimizing measurement dimensions through refined NLP technologies and extending to industry-specific or international comparative studies.
