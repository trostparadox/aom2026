Safe to Grow: How Leader Secure-Base Support Boosts Performance and Career Development Potential

OB
Yuan Zhou (Shandong University); Fengyu Zhai (Shandong University)
Building on the conservation of resources theory, this study examines how leader secure-base support promotes employees’ job performance and career development potential through personal learning. We collected three-wave data from 334 leader-employee pairs in China. The results show that secure-base support enhances employees’ personal learning, which subsequently improves their job performance and career development potential. In addition, core self-evaluation moderates this process such that employees with lower core self-evaluation gain more learning benefits from leader secure-base support. These findings illuminate a resource-based learning mechanism through which leader secure-base support fosters employee outcomes and clarify when such support is most effective.
