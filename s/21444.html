Session overview

Entrepreneurial ecosystems enhance innovation and sustain the economy and society by providing new product or service solutions and job creation. One of the most critical factors for the growth of emerging entrepreneurial ecosystems is funding. Crowdfunding is one of the technology-based alternative entrepreneurial finance models for equity-based investment. However, there are limitations of centralized crowdfunding platforms, integrating blockchain technology adds value by enlarging the funding pool, increasing trust, and lowering operational costs and time. This research aims to explore the perceptions and attitudes towards the adoption and use of innovative blockchain-based crowdfunding platforms. The findings reveal that perceptions and adoption are shaped and require a collaborative effort on technological design, user experience, and policymaking. This research contributes to the limited literature on this specific topic on understanding how blockchain may reconfigure crowdfunding ecosystems and support emerging entrepreneurial ecosystems by providing context-sensitive, rich data and actionable findings.
