Uncovering University-Industry Knowledge Domains: A Topic Modeling and Dense Retrieval Approach

RM
Aisada Nielsen (Kiel University)
This paper presents a methodological approach for identifying knowledge domains at the project level in university-industry collaborations (UICs) by combining BERTopic with dense retrieval. Using a dataset of publicly funded UIC projects in Germany between 2010 and 2020, linked with patent and website texts, our approach identifies top-k latent topics for each project. Integrating dense retrieval improves the semantic alignment between project descriptions and the actual knowledge domains involved, while also reducing topical noise by filtering out irrelevant or weakly related domains. This approach enhances topic specificity and enables longitudinal tracking of thematic engagement, allowing us to trace domain-specific knowledge convergence over time. We demonstrate the potential of dense retrieval to advance text mining in organizational research by enabling more precise identification of knowledge domains and offering a transferable framework for studying knowledge flows in innovation contexts.
