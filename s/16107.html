Session overview

Research on the drivers and deterrents of inclusive, age-diverse workplaces—and older workers specifically—has advanced significantly over the past years. Building on this foundation, this symposium aims to contribute a more nuanced understanding. It brings together studies that provide a focused literature review on long-term trends in work, aging, and retirement research, challenge prevailing (but potentially oversimplified) perspectives, introduce new theoretical perspectives, and utilize advanced research designs. We hope to inspire new lines of scholarly inquiry and equip practitioners with the nuanced insights needed to develop effective, targeted interventions.
