The Impact of Green Human Resource Management on Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivation

Friday, Jul 31 2026 · 1:00PM–2:00PM
Pennsylvania Convention Center — Poster Wall 3
HR

People

Author: Meiyu Fang National Central University

Session overview

As global enterprises strive toward Net Zero targets and ESG goals, Green Human Resource Management (GHRM) has emerged as a critical system for driving employee green behavior. This proposal examines how GHRM influences both intrinsic and extrinsic motivation through the mediating effect of green psychological climate (GPC). Drawing on Self-Determination Theory and Signaling Theory, the research proposal hypothesizes that perceived organizational greenwashing moderates the relationships between GHRM and motivation via the mediation of green psychological climate. The perceived organizational greenwashing weakens the positive impact of GHRM on motivation by eroding the credibility of the green psychological climate. The proposal plans to employ a two-wave, time-lagged survey design, targeting employees in Taiwan’s sustainable enterprises (e.g., ISO 14001-certified or ESG-reporting firms). This research proposal aims to provide nuanced insights into how organizations can foster genuine employee engagement in sustainability.
