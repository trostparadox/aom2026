Session overview

This symposium explores how entrepreneurs redefine and reorder social arrangements in the process of creating and institutionalizing novel technologies, categories, and practices. The rise of new ventures (including Airbnb, OpenAI, Tesla, Uber, among many others) in the past decades was associated with entrepreneurs reshaping the social rules of the game and reconfiguring how people work and organize in modern societies. As entrepreneurs introduce novel technologies, categories, and practices, they contest and often redraw established norms, arrangements, and what is considered legitimate. We will spotlight how entrepreneurial actions and processes reconfigure the established rules of the game and redefine what becomes mainstream. This symposium seeks to advance scholarly understanding and discussion of how entrepreneurs’ work of creating and introducing novel technologies, categories, and practices entails theorizing, enacting, and institutionalizing social change.
