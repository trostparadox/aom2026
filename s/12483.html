Session overview

As the health care sector continues to evolve in response to changing population needs, policy environments, financial pressures, and technological advances, there is a growing demand for research that addresses critical and emerging issues. This PDW invites participants to engage in discussion of four of the “hot topics'' in health care management research and practice: 1) the rise of alternative care forms and investors: the role of VC and private equity; 2) the future of equitable healthcare work: neurodiverse teams, equitable systems, and research to practice frameworks; 3) evolving team structures and the role of AI in team based care; and 4) social network analysis in health care. The session will be organized into two facilitated discussion rounds. In each round, participants will join a topic-focused research table to engage with others who share similar interests. Conversations will center on the current state of research in the area, common challenges in conducting this work, and promising directions for future studies. Each table will be guided by one or two established scholars actively conducting research on the featured topic. Participants will select their preferred topic at the start of the session. The PDW is designed to foster in-depth scholarly exchange, generate new research ideas, and support the development of collaborative relationships.
