Following the Flagship: Leveraging Leading-Chain Enterprises for SMEs Digital Transformation

TIM
Qinyue Xu (Central South University); Chang Wang (Central South University); Qi He (Central South University)
Although the flagship leading-chain enterprises involvement can contribute to the digital transformation of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), little is known about the mechanisms underlying this process. Drawing on the Motivation-Opportunity-Ability (MOA) framework, this study explores the relationships and boundary conditions between leading-chain enterprises involvement and SMEs digital transformation by analyzing a sample of three hundred and seventy-seven Chinese manufacturing SMEs. The results demonstrate that leading-chain enterprises involvement serves as a critical external opportunity for SMEs digital transformation. Notably, the effectiveness of leading-chain enterprises involvement is contingent upon SMEs motivation and ability. SMEs digital awareness, market pressure and absorptive capability amplify the impact of leading-chain enterprises involvement in their digital transformation. Overall, these findings contribute to the existing literature on SMEs digital transformation, offering practical insights for leveraging the important role of leading-chain enterprises in propelling SMEs digital transformation process.
