AI as a Co-Entrepreneur? Generative AI and Entrepreneurial Opportunity Recognition

Sunday, Aug 2 2026 · 8:30AM–9:30AM

Authors: Ishraque Hossain Chowdhury The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley; Sibin Wu The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley; Bahjat Mohammad Ogielat The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

Session overview

Opportunity being the central concept of the field of entrepreneurship requires scholars to look for ways to improve the opportunity recognition process. The introduction of Artificial Intelligence (AI) means that we need to understand the ways in which this can improve the opportunity recognition process. Based on the external enablers’ framework, we theorize that Gen AI chatbots will act as external enablers that improve the opportunity recognition quantity and quality. This process would be mediated by entrepreneurial alertness. Moreover, digital literacy and domain experience of the entrepreneur would moderate this relationship. The arguments provide an explanation on how AI can be an external enabler and provide support for increased prompt engineering education for entrepreneurs.
