“Beyond the Dyad: The Social Ripple Effects of Workplace Romance from a Network Perspective”

Session overview

Workplace Romance (WR) has garnered significant attention in the media recently. From an academic viewpoint, it is still considered an underexplored phenomenon and a contested area in contemporary organisations. While prior research has focused on the antecedents and consequences of WR on partners, the impact of WR on reshaping the relational dynamics between coworkers is still unexplored. Drawing on a social network perspective, we aim to investigate how WR impacts the strength of ties between coworkers and the participants mediated by trust and coworkers’ evaluations. We aim to argue that WR extends beyond the dyadic level and has various spillover effects on co-workers. Specifically, the visibility of WR invites scrutiny at various levels by multiple parties, which recalibrates trust towards the partners, influencing existing ties. Using the study's findings, we aim to offer specific organisational and policy implications. Keywords: Workplace Romance, Coworkers Evaluation, Tie Strength, Trust, Citizenship Behaviour
