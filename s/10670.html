Session overview

Despite growing evidence of migrants’ contributions to innovation and entrepreneurship, their connection to management remains underexplored. This gap contrasts with reality, as organizations are the primary contexts in which migrants influence innovation and entrepreneurship. The limited evidence restricts management scholars’ understanding of the organizational effects of migration, a highly debated contemporary issue, but also presents an opportunity to advance research in the field. This two-part PDW brings together migration research experts to address this gap by reviewing the literature on key topics, assessing the challenges of research in this domain, and providing recommendations to guide future work. In the first part, participants will hear from leading scholars about current literature and emerging perspectives. In the second interactive part, breakout activities will facilitate idea exchange and involve participants in shaping a community-wide future research agenda. These activities aim to foster management scholars’ interest in migration research and promote greater engagement of migration researchers with the management literature.
