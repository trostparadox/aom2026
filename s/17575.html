Session overview

In a business world based on technology and AI, social or otherwise known as "soft" skills are becoming increasingly important. Such competencies communication, conflict management, critical thinking, teamwork and decision-making are relevant for representatives of all professions, as they enable them to work more effectively and achieve better results Traditional teaching methods are usually focused on developing a narrow range of competencies. The inclusion of interactive teaching methods in the study process allows for the combined development of both module-specific competencies and soft competencies. We offer a PDW (Professional Development Workshop) on the topic of Interactive teaching methods for social competencies development. This would be a practical and engaging workshop, where we will present our experience in developing soft competencies through innovative teaching methods. During the workshop, we will demonstrate how digital educational escape rooms (DEER) and Challenge-Based Learning (CBL) can be applied to the development of soft competencies. Participant evaluations are invaluable. They help us understand what attendees find most beneficial, identify opportunities to strengthen future PDWs, and demonstrate the impact and value of the PDW program to the Academy.
