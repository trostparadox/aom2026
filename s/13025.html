Session overview

This symposium brings together cutting-edge research on an institutional perspective of emotions. Adopting a co-constitutive approach that posits emotions and institutions as endogenous to one another, leading scholars in this space analyze the construction of national humiliation in China’s past, the spatial dynamics of hope in the Israeli–Palestinian war, the institutionalization of happiness and the Chief Well-being Officer in American organizations, and how the concept of affect challenges current understandings of emotions in institutional life. Together, this symposium advances a robust research agenda, offering important insights for institutional scholars, organizational theorists, and practice-oriented researchers interested in the social nature of emotions.
