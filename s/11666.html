Session overview

In this symposium, the papers reveal how bringing nonwork identities and values into the workplace reshapes interpersonal dynamics – eliciting support, scrutiny, stigma, or influence depending on how identities are expressed and interpreted. By uncovering the nuances of modern approaches to integrating work and nonwork identities, this symposium demonstrates that work-nonwork identity integration is neither uniformly beneficial nor risky, but instead produces unique consequences shaped by identity type, context, and social judgment.
