Session overview

Disruptive technological change forces managers to make decisions under conditions of rapid change, ambiguity, and uncertainty. With the advance of AI and its ability to analyze vast amounts of data in mere seconds, decision-making has increasingly become a human–AI collaborative effort. Even with the growing evidence that AI can enhance analytical performance and pattern recognition, it remains unclear how AI performs in environments marked by complexity and uncertainty - core elements of strategic decision-making. Strategic decisions are challenging because they require forward-looking causal reasoning and timely action to navigate the uncertainty inherent in these environments. Yet, despite advances in AI research, we still lack an understanding of how AI interacts with managerial cognition, disruptive change, and strategic timing. This panel aims to clarify how managers recognize and respond to disruptive change, and to examine the role of AI in strategic decision-making, particularly in interpretation, timing, and action under uncertainty. By bringing together diverse scholars, this panel aims not only to be comprehensive but also to articulate a forward-looking research agenda.
