Session overview

MC Division hosted in 2024 and 2025 two PDWs during the AOM annual meeting focused on “MC in Africa and Middle-East (AME)”, including MC case studies from Nigeria, Taïwan, Senegal, Lebanon, Sudan or Gabon. Considering this, we acknowledge these previous PDW and build upon its foundation to go further in the study of MC in African and Middle-East Countries. This year, based on new original case studies of MC interventions in Egypt, Gabon, Lebanon and Morocco, the PDW aims both at highlighting and capturing the local cultural traits to foster the sustainable performances of the consulted companies. Thus, we could position the PDW as innovative strategies in management consulting adapting to cultural and economic diversity in Africa and Middle East. This approach would allow us to explore new dimensions while respecting past contributions.
