Paradoxical Leadership and Innovative Work Behavior: A Hybrid SEM–ML Study

OB
Muhammad Saeed (Harbin Institute of Technology); Songsong Li (Harbin Institute of Technology); Weiqian Zhang (Harbin Institute of Technology); Zawar Hussain (School of Education, Xian FanYi University, Xian, China)
Paradoxical leadership (PL), which balances competing organizational demands, is vital for fostering innovative work behavior (IWB). However, the proximal behavioral mechanisms linking leadership to innovation remain underexplored. Drawing on paradox and self-determination theories, this research examines the influence of PL on IWB through work autonomy (WA) and creative deviance (CD). We tested this model using three-wave, time-lagged field data from 427 software developers in Pakistan. To analyze the data, this study applied structural equation modeling integrated with machine learning algorithms using the bnlearn library. Findings reveal a significant direct effect of PL on IWB, with WA and CD serving as partial mediators. Machine learning analyses confirmed PL as the most influential predictor and identified mild nonlinear patterns. These findings clarify the behavioral mechanisms by which leaders balance control and flexibility to foster innovation, and highlight the significance of integrating theory-driven modeling with predictive analytics.
